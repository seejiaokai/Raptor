Question 1 — what the switch looks like

A tick box before the date, or the date itself?

I recommend B. A tick box looks like something you can tick, and it matches the "all day" tick in the same window. With A, nothing shows that the date can be tapped, so people would never find it. On a keyboard, Tab reaches the tick box and Space ticks it.

Reading 2 — the default follows the dates

The tick sets itself when the dates change

  • If you ticked or unticked it yourself, and then the dates change so that the default changes (one day becomes two, or two become one), the tick takes the new default.
  • If the dates change but the default stays the same (three days become four), your own choice stays.
Reading 3 — unticked means no date anywhere

No screen prints the date

Readings 4 and 5

A published day, and who may change it

  • 4. Ticking or unticking it on a day that is already published reads "1 pending" on that day, because the printed words change. That is the same pending count the app already shows when a remark changes; nothing new on screen.
  • 5. Whoever may change the input may tick or untick it: a member on his own inputs, a scheduler on any.

One more thing it means: one-day duties and other commitments already saved will stop printing their date once this is built. A published day that carries one will read "1 pending" once. That is only the demo data, which is cleared before the database.

How to answer

For example: Q1 B, readings 2–5 yes. Say "no" to any reading you want changed, and how.

THE CHECKS
Crew rest and the working day
Warning:
Crew rest (<12h)
For a flying member: at least 12h (Crew rest) from the end of his day before to his earliest in-time, or to anything earlier that day.
How it works
Rest runs from the end of the day before to the first thing today: his in-time or rally, his B where neither is typed, or anything earlier, such as a meeting.
The day before ends at its last commitment of any kind. A flight ends at landing plus the debrief (2h), or at take-off plus the debrief where the landing is not a time. Anything else ends at its written end.
An input counts only if it is a duty or commitment, other than Personal or SANS Availability, with typed times.
Exactly 12h is legal. A day with no flying asks for no rest.
A LATE SHOW remark keeps the warning. The ring is dashed while rest still clears by step, solid after.
Monday checks last Sunday. A Sunday that breaks Monday shows a "Breaks Monday" box.
A flying line with no times yet is not measured, but a typed brief, in-time or landing on it still counts.
An SC SPARE standing by carries no crew rest either way, but one that flies carries it as any flyer does. A shift reports at its start, or at its B or R where one is earlier than the start.
Advisory:
Tight turning — crew rest
His rest would be short if he reported the usual 3h before take-off (Default rally before take-off), but his in-time or rally, or his B where neither is typed, is later, so it clears.
Warning:
No break day — too many days in a row
On the programme more than 6 days in a row.
How it works
Any tasking counts: a flight, a duty, a sim, a ground or programme row.
Leave or a downchit resets the count.
It counts last week's tail, and the warning lands on the day to clear.
Note:
Long work day
More than 12h from first commitment to last.
Two things at once
Warning:
Conflict — two events at once
Two commitments overlap for one man. Touching end to end is not a clash.
Advisory:
On shift — also down for a ground event
An SC MAIN man is also down for something set to Advisory under SC MAIN clashes. A commitment input across the shift is red; a meeting stays amber. Once the shift flies, every clash with it is red.
Note:
ⓘ info only
A ground or programme item marked ⓘ is printed but never checked, and earns no OIL.
Note:
Past midnight
A row whose end reads earlier than its start runs into the next day.
Flying lines
Note:
Flying wave
An ordinary flying wave — it counts toward the day’s flying tally, and every line is fully cross-checked.
Warning:
In-time / Rally — timings out of order
In-time, rally, brief, take-off and landing are not in that order.
How it works
The order is in-time, rally, brief, take-off, landing. Equal times are fine.
The B on the line is what is checked. A blank B is checked as the suggested brief, take-off less 2h20.
Out of order is red, but it never stops a day being published.
A formation's rally is its R box alone. An In-time line is an in-time whatever its words, even one that says RALLY, and its first clock is used (09:00H or 0900).
A formation's name makes the line that formation's; otherwise it is the whole wave's.
A clock later than take-off means the day before.
A line with no clock, RALLY AFTER IN TIME included, sets no time.
Advisory:
In-time — check the reporting instruction
The app cannot read the clock on an In-time line.
Advisory:
No time for the flight brief
Something sits between the brief and take-off. A blank B counts as the suggested one (Brief before take-off).
Advisory:
No time for the flight debrief
Something sits inside landing plus 2h (Debrief after landing).
Advisory:
Tight turn
Less than 2h from landing to the next take-off (Tight turn, or Dekit plus Step if longer).
Advisory:
Double turning
Two or more sorties in one day. One line names everyone; it is routine, not an error.
Advisory:
Flight times — take-off and landing are the same
A line's take-off and landing are the same time. A flying line still earns its OIL from the report before it and the debrief after it, and none if it has neither. A standby shift with no length earns none. One that flies with its R or B before its START earns from that time.
Sims
Advisory:
No time for the sim brief
Something sits inside an EP sim's 15 min brief (EP sim brief), or an AMT's BRIEF row.
Advisory:
No time for the sim debrief
Something sits inside a sim's 30 min debrief (Sim debrief), or an AMT's DEBRIEF row (AMT debrief, where that row has no end).
Qualifications and currency
Warning:
Qualification — illegal seat
A man in a seat he may not hold, such as a WSO in the front seat. Any pilot may fly the rear seat.
Warning:
Illegal aircrew combination
An OCU pilot with a CAT A–D WSO, or the reverse. An instructor in either seat clears it.
Advisory:
Crew solo — only allowed under syllabus
An OCU pilot with an OCU WSO. Allowed only on sorties the syllabus names.
Advisory:
Crew combination — CO approval required
A CAT D pilot with a CAT C WSO, a CAT C pilot with a CAT D WSO, or two CAT Ds. The crew needs the CO's approval.
Advisory:
OCU without IP
An OCU formation with no instructor (IW, IP, IR or FI).
Advisory:
Incentive passenger — crew pairing needs approval
Ground crew in a rear seat. The ride needs approval. Ground crew are checked for clashes, the long day, days in a row and their own absences, and a front seat is always a warning. None of the flying rules apply to them: crew rest, turns, pairing, AAR, SC currency or briefs.
Warning:
IRT without an IR examiner
IRT in the mission needs an IR in the formation; IRT in an aircraft's remarks needs one in that aircraft.
Warning:
AAR currency — not qualified
AAR in the remarks needs the front seat's currency: night on a night wave, day otherwise. NAAR or DAAR says it outright.
Warning:
AAR — back seat not cleared to instruct
A pilot not current may fly AAR as training only with an instructor pilot (IP, IR or FI) in the back seat marked I for it on Quals (an I in place of the tick on DAAR or NAAR). That clears the front seat's currency warning, but an instructor pilot without the mark is flagged instead.
Warning:
SC currency — wrong shift
A standby shift wholly inside 07:00 to 19:00 (SC day window) needs SC DAY; one reaching outside it needs SC NIGHT. A SPARE is checked the same as a MAIN.
Note:
Night after day
NAAR needs DAAR, and SC NIGHT needs SC DAY. On Quals, removing the day one removes the night one.
Standby (SC, AVALON, BB)
Note:
SC
A SPARE line is only checked for overseas / medically down, SC currency, a pilots-only front seat and not holding another SC seat or SC desk in the same hours — nothing else, unless it flies. A MAIN line is checked in full. Add as many of each as you need.
Note:
AVALON
Overnight. Every line — MAIN or SPARE — is only checked for overseas / medically down, SC NIGHT currency, a pilots-only front seat and not holding another AVALON seat or the AVALON desk in the same hours. Nothing else, unless it flies. Add as many as you need.
Note:
BB
Times are yours to set. The same checks as AVALON — overseas / medically down, SC currency for the hours typed, a pilots-only front seat and not holding another seat or the BB desk in the same hours. Nothing else, unless it flies. Add as many as you need.
Note:
A shift's hours
A standby shift takes exactly its written hours: no step, dekit, brief or debrief.
Note:
A standby row that flies
A shift with its B or R typed flies, every row on it; a row ticked FLIES on the Scheduler Board flies on its own. A row that flies is checked as a sortie is, counts as a jet and a sortie, and earns OIL by default. A row with nobody on it counts nothing; one that does not come in is cancelled with CX.
Advisory:
Standby line flies — no start or end
A standby shift that flies has no START or END, so its crew rest and OIL cannot be worked out.
Advisory:
In-time window cut — busy between report and shift start
A standby row that flies has a typed B (the crew's in-time) or R (its rally), whichever is earlier, and something sits between it and the shift start. That time also starts his crew rest, his long day and his OIL.
Note:
Arming an SC seat
The crew list judges each man against that shift's hours only.
Leave, downchits and inputs
Note:
An input closes its hours
Every input closes the man for the hours it covers, from the moment it is filed. One a scheduler has taken off the programme closes nothing.
Warning:
On leave + flying
A man on leave is planned for a flight, sim, duty or ground slot.
Warning:
Downchit + flying
A medically down man is planned. On ATT B he may still stand a duty, sim or ground slot, but not fly.
Warning:
Personal input clash
Any other input clashes with a planned slot: a course, a meeting, an appointment, overseas duty.
Note:
A seat with no times
Still checked against an absence covering the whole day; a part-day absence waits until the seat has times.
Note:
Half days
Leave and medical can be AM (to 12:00), PM (from 12:01) or all day, and a half day closes only its own half. A sortie counts from its step (Step before take-off), so AM leave still clashes with a wave that steps before noon.
Note:
Standing SC SPARE
A man on local leave may still stand SC SPARE. Overseas leave or duty (OL, OD) or a medical code (HL, OML, ATT C, ATT B) may not: that is a warning. A row that flies has no such allowance.
Advisory:
SANS availability — planned outside the availability filed
A SANS man planned outside the event or the hours he filed. Filing nothing raises nothing.
Note:
LATE
An input last changed after its cut-off (Inputs late cut-off) wears LATE: a mark, never a warning. Downchits never do.
OIL
Note:
Weekend and holiday work earns OIL
Work on a weekend or public holiday earns OIL once the day is published: a full day or a half, by Full day of OIL.
How it works
Weekend or holiday work earns OIL once the day is published.
A man's day runs from his first report to his last end, gaps included: 6h01 or more is a full day (FO), less is a half (HO).
A flight runs from its in-time or rally, whichever is earlier (Default rally before take-off where neither is entered), to landing plus the debrief.
A row the scheduler adds with no times, or no end time, earns nothing, but a request answered yes earns over its own times. An Off day earns nothing.
SC SPARE, AVALON and BB rows that do not fly earn only if switched on in OIL Earn. A row that flies earns by default, from its B or R if earlier than its START, otherwise from its START, to its END.
Until its wave flies, a desk made from an AVALON or BB template earns only if switched on in OIL Earn. Once the wave flies, the desk earns by default.
A Duty & commitments input earns once its OIL question is answered yes.
A published day keeps its OIL until it is published again.
Warning:
No OIL earned — a row has no usable times
On a weekend or holiday, a row with someone on it and no usable times earns nobody on it any OIL. The warning names the row.
Advisory:
OIL waiting — this day is not published
A weekend or holiday with someone to earn is not published yet. Nobody earns until it is.
Advisory:
No OIL — no Leave War period covers this date
No Leave War period covers this weekend or holiday, so its work earns nobody OIL. The day offers to create the period, before and after it is published.
Advisory:
Became a holiday after publishing — republish for its OIL
A day published as a working day that the Leave War now calls a public holiday earns nothing until it is published again.
Advisory:
No longer a holiday — republish to withdraw its OIL
A day published as a holiday that the Leave War no longer calls one keeps its OIL until it is published again, which withdraws it.
Advisory:
Published before these seats counted — republish to credit them
A weekend or holiday published before the app counted an ALL AVAIL on a duty desk, sim or extra people line shows a pending change. Publish it again and the men behind that puck get their OIL.

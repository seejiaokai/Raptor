Empty space above the first event

Pick a student with nothing marked and the chart lands on its first event, ST-01. Today it lands with ST-01 in the middle of the chart box, as you asked on 9 Sep ("centralise the view if its possible"). That leaves empty space above the chart: about a third of a desktop screen, and on a phone the top half of the chart box.

I tried a version that stops the chart at its top instead, so ST-01 sits near the top with no empty space. It went against your 9 Sep words, so I took it out and am asking you.

  1. A, keep it centred (as now). Every landing puts its event in the middle, the first event too.
  2. B, no empty space above the chart. Every event that can be centred still is. An event near the top of the chart sits as high as the chart goes instead, so nothing above it is empty.

My recommendation: B. On a phone, half a screen of nothing reads as a chart that hasn't loaded, and the first event is where every new student starts. B still centres everything below the top rows. It costs one small change, already built once: two of the Tracker's own checks would say "centred, unless that shows empty space above the chart".

What changed

1. The chart by keyboard, on a desktop (your D980)

One Tab reaches the chart. The arrows follow its lines: → goes to the next event, ← back to the one it needs first, ↑ ↓ to the event above or below. Enter opens the marking pop-up with the keyboard inside it, and Escape comes back to the same event. The event the keyboard is on wears the app's plain blue ring. A mouse press, or a tap on a phone, draws no ring.

2. A man deleted through Post out leaves his running courses (your D981)

He leaves every Tracker course where he still has events to do. Where every event of his is done or N.A., he stays, his record kept. His marks are never erased.

3. The Currency & flex bars' words (your D982)

Dark words on the green, amber and red bars; white words stay on the grey one. The colours and the words themselves are unchanged.

4. A longer name in the key round the course ball

A name on the left of the key was cut off at the card's edge. Each name now gets the room its side of the card has.

5. Coming back to the Tracker lands on your last mark

After a Log out and back in, the chart opened at its top corner, away from the mark you had just made. It now lands where picking the student lands: the last mark, else the first event.

6. No "saved" while a save has failed

When a save failed, Raptor's band said "Not saved — keep this page open" while the Tracker's corner still said "saved". The corner now stays quiet until the save really lands.

7. A ball dropped above or left of the chart stays on it

In Edit chart layout, a ball dropped in the empty room above or left of the chart was cut off once you left, and ⤢ Fit couldn't bring it back. A drop there now stops at the chart's edge.

8. Two fixes with nothing to see

  • One zoom per wheel step. In Edit chart layout, every ball you moved made one mouse-wheel step zoom one more time. After two moves a step zoomed ×1.57 instead of ×1.16. Now it's ×1.16 every time, and a Connect or Text click acts once.
  • Undo stores nothing nobody made. The first Undo of a mark saved a pace, an empty lull list and dates for that student that nobody had set. Export and the database would have carried them. Now Undo puts back only what was really there.

Your look on your iPhone and iPad

These can only be checked on Apple's browser. There is none on the PC.

  1. iPhone, Tracker chart: tap an event. The marking pop-up opens, and no blue ring appears round the event. The ring is for a keyboard only.
  2. iPhone, Info half: add a student with a long name (say "HIS STUDENT"). The key round the course ball shows the whole name on both sides.
  3. iPad upright, Tracker Info half: tap Last Flown (Syllabus). The calendar opens right under its box, not somewhere else on the screen.

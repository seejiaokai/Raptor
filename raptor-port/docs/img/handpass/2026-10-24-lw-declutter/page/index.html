Round 3 · D1311

The window, redrawn: eight changes

  1. The row labels go (Date, Decide, Selected, Document, Remarks, Duration, Which leave, Also). Each row's buttons already say what they are; a screen reader still hears the names.
  2. The day line leaves its box (Q2 A on this morning's pictures page): the same words, no frame around them.
  3. Decide and Selected become one row: Approve · Refuse · Move · Delete. "Note" goes: on an approved leave the Remarks box is simply there (your D1307).
  4. Document and Remarks share one row: the Remarks box, now dark (your D1308), with "Remarks" inside it until you type; then its "till" date and the app's one Document button.
  5. "Just this day" goes (it is always the default); "Pick a range" moves to the end of the Duration row.
  6. The leave buttons take the row's full width: same height, wider, easier to hit. A size change, so your word (D487).
  7. The day's own leave is lit (your Q6): a ring on the LL button on an LL day. The title's "NOW LL" goes, as the ring says it.
  8. "Approve what I enter" becomes the app's switch (your D1302, remembered until changed), and Grant OIL, Post out and Post in move behind one ⋯ (they were the "Also" row, used rarely). Since then (D1326): the ⋯ is a button in the fourth place of the CSE · OD · UNA row, not in the title bar.

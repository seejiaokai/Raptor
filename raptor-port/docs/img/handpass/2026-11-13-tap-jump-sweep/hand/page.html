Recommend fixing

1 · Inputs → Calendar: pressing a day

PC (your 125% screen). On a phone the day opens over the calendar and nothing moves.

Fix When you press a day, its panel opens at the right and the whole calendar gets narrower; the filter boxes also drop to a new line, so the calendar falls about 55px. The day you pressed (Fri 13) slides 257px left, and your pointer is now over Sun 15. A second click opens the wrong day.

The fix I recommend: the calendar keeps its size and place; the day's panel opens over the side of the calendar away from the day you pressed. It already has a grip, so you can still drag it aside. Only the Inputs calendar on a PC changes.

2 · Leave War day window: "Pick a range"

Phone and PC, the same on both: it rose 377px.

Fix Pressing "Pick a range" opens a month calendar under that row. The window grows upward from the foot of the screen, so the button shoots to the top. To close the calendar you have to find the button again. This is the same cause as the below-zero line you have already approved the fix for.

The fix I recommend: the same cure as that fix. The calendar opens above the "Whole day · AM · PM · Pick a range" row, so the row and the leave buttons stay under your finger.

3 · Schedule: the CAT / TYPE / QUALS tabs

View-only Sched, Edit Schedule and Inputs; PC and phone.

Fix With CAT open, its letters (A, B, C, D, OCU) sit between CAT and TYPE. Press TYPE and the CAT letters close, so TYPE slides 217px left and the TYPE choices open where it was. Your finger is now on "SUP". A second tap would highlight every SUP on the schedule. Each tab also shifts about 4px when it opens.

The fix I recommend: the three tabs always stay where they are, and the open tab's choices appear after QUALS, in one place for all three. One tab is open at a time, as now.

4 · Leave War window for several people: "Delete"

PC (a drag across people). On a phone the test browser cannot do that drag, but the window is the same, so it will behave the same.

Fix Pressing Delete adds the line "Delete 3 days for Reaper, Anvil, Cinch? Tap Delete again." under the buttons, and the button rows rise 35px. The second tap that the line asks for lands on the line, not on Delete. This is the same fault as the below-zero line.

The fix I recommend: the same cure: the question line stands above the buttons. The day window does not need it: there one Delete acts at once.

7 · Leave War on a phone: pressing a day low on the screen

Leave The page scrolls up 77px so the day you pressed stays in sight, just above its window. Your finger then rests on the window's line of plain text, so a second tap does nothing. A day already high on the screen does not move.

8 · Edit Schedule on a phone: the AIRCREW tab

Leave The tab is the handle of a drawer and travels with it to its new edge, as a drawer's handle should. Under your finger is the drawer's empty space, so a second tap does nothing. Press the tab again to close the drawer.

# Words for a cold read

1. Where: on the OIL Earn bar of the Scheduler Board, the label beside a switch that stops the whole day earning OIL.
   Words: "No OIL this day"

2. Where: the words that show when a mouse rests on that switch.
   Words: "covers anything added later too"

3. Where: on the OIL Earn bar of the Scheduler Board, a button that stops the whole day earning OIL, before it is pressed.
   Words: "Stop OIL for this day"

4. Where: the same button once pressed.
   Words: "Let this day earn again"

5. Where: the note under the OIL Earn bar while that button is pressed.
   Words: "Nothing on this day earns OIL. The marks underneath are kept and come back when you let the day earn again."

6. Where: a passing note after tapping a man's puck in OIL Earn while that button is pressed.
   Words: "Nothing on this day earns. Let the day earn again first."

7. Where: a passing note at the foot of the screen after tapping, in OIL Earn, a man's puck on a row that has a start time but no end time.
   Words: "Fable: no OIL worked out — this row has no end time"

8. Where: the same passing note for a man's puck on a row with nothing to measure.
   Words: "Fable: nothing measurable to earn from here"

9. Where: in place of note 7, a grey refusal note.
   Words: "Blocked: no OIL worked out — this row has no end time"

10. Where: the foot of a long list in a day's window (the Leave War's day, the Inputs calendar's day) while more lines are below.
    Words: "↓ 8 more"

11. Where: a notice on each day of a week whose saved copy cannot be read.
    Words: "This week's saved copy could not be read, so it can't be shown here. It is kept as it was saved. Report it on the Help page."

12. Where: in place of notice 11.
    Words: "This week's saved copy could not be read, so it can't be shown here. It is kept as it was saved."

13. Where: a grey refusal note when someone tries to save a change into that week.
    Words: "Blocked: this week's saved copy could not be read, so it can't be edited. Nothing was saved."

14. Where: a grey refusal note when someone tries to save a change into a week published by an older version of the app.
    Words: "Blocked: this week was published by an older version of the app, so it can't be edited. Nothing was saved."

15. Where: the Logic page, the explanation under "Default rally before take-off".
    Words: "An empty R box suggests take-off less this time, and "+ In-time" fills in the same time. With no in-time or rally entered, a flyer's OIL counts from here. His long day and Insights hours count from step. It also sets when the tight crew-rest advisory shows."

16. Where: the Logic page, a sentence added at the end of the explanation under "Step before take-off".
    Words: "With no in-time or rally entered, a flyer's long day and Insights hours count from it too."

17. Where: on a phone, the words for the ✕ at the end of the Tracker's Find box.
    Words: "Clear the search and close Find"

18. Where: on a phone, the words for that ✕ once the Find box is already empty.
    Words: "Close Find"

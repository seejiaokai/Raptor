Round 2 · what was looked at

The crew list on all seven days of the demo week with no seat picked, and on Monday with each kind of place picked: a front seat, a back seat, a duty desk, a sim seat, a sim passenger seat, a ground row, a programme row, and an SC seat (an SC wave added for it, with a man on its SPARE line so the standby look shows). For every one I recorded each man's look, his reason, the order and the counts.

  • Right everywhere: every "N free" count matches the men drawn free; pilots and WSOs always run free, then faded, then crossed out; every crossed-out man shows his reason once a place is picked, or the reason is said once under the heading where the whole column shares it ("WSO — cannot fly front seat"); an SC seat cuts the list to the SC-current men and gives "inside this shift" reasons; the weekend lists everyone free.
  • Not looked at: a passenger seat on a jet (none on the demo Monday), AVALON and BB seats, the phone's crew drawer, and Edit Schedule's own crew panel (the same list, drawn one puck wide).
  • Text to delete: none found. The list's words are its headings and its reasons.
Round 2 · finding 1 · the SANS section

List the SANS men free first, and give the section its count?

The SANS section is the only part of the crew list not ordered free first: its men stay in callsign order, so free and crossed-out men are mixed, and it has no "N free" in its heading. With a duty desk picked on Monday, Bolt (crossed out) comes before Jester (free), and you count the free ones by eye. Drawn: the same order as the pilots and WSOs (free first, then crossed out, callsign order within each), and the heading "SANS AVAIL · 8 FREE".

Recommended: yes, as drawn.

Round 2 · finding 2 · the strip over the list

Drop the dangling dot in "Planning FCP ·" when the line has no callsign yet?

When you pick a seat on a line that has no callsign or mission yet (every new line starts blank), the strip over the crew list reads "Planning FCP ·" with nothing after the dot. Drawn: "Planning FCP". A line that has its callsign keeps it after the dot, as today.

Recommended: yes, as drawn.

Question 1 · dragging the panel wider (D1113)

Drag the panel's left edge to widen it; double-click the edge to put it back?

The panel's left edge becomes a grip, like the one between the issues list and the crew list: a short bar at its middle that turns blue under the pointer. Drag it left to widen the panel from today's 320 up to 40% of the screen; the width stays on that computer. Both crew columns and the issues list share the extra width. The day's area gets narrower to make room: on your screen, from 1162 wide today to 868 at the widest.

Recommended: yes, as drawn, with a double-click on the edge putting it back to 320.

Question 2 · your question: the reasons beside the pucks

Once there is room, does a crossed-out man's reason move beside his puck?

Yes, if you agree: the reason stands beside the puck wherever at least 92 is left next to it, and drops under it where not. That is the rule the ALL AVAIL window already uses when you drag it wider (D40), so both lists behave the same. At today's 320 the reasons stay under the pucks; from about 400 wide they sit beside, and the list gets much shorter.

Recommended: yes, as drawn.

Question 3 · the ATT B man with no seat picked (D1114)

Show an ATT B man in full colour, with "ATT B: no flying" under his puck?

Today, with no seat picked, a man on ATT B (grounded, but able to work) is faded like a man already tasked, though he is free for a duty desk, a sim seat or a ground row. Here Havoc is on ATT B and has nothing else that day. Drawn: his puck in full colour, the crew list's own small grey words "ATT B: no flying" under it (beside it once the panel is wide), and his place straight after the free men. He is still not counted in "5 free", because the count is of men who can fly. Once a seat is picked nothing changes: a flying seat crosses him out with his reason, and a desk, a sim seat or a ground row shows him in full colour, as today.

Recommended: yes, as drawn.

Question 4 · the line through a crossed-out puck

Draw the line across the puck only, not across the empty column?

Today the line through a man who can't take the seat runs on past his puck to the panel's edge, across empty space (and through the column beside it, for the WSOs). Drawn: the line crosses the puck only. With the reasons beside the pucks (question 2) this matters more, because a full-width line would strike through the reason's words. This is the first finding of the look over the whole crew list you asked for (D1115); the rest of that look comes as round 2.

Recommended: yes, as drawn.

Already ruled · Edit Schedule's AIRCREW heading (D1116)

The day picker stays in sight when you scroll down

The cause: as the page scrolls, the crew panel holds 8 from the top of the window, which is behind the blue top bar, so its heading "AIRCREW · ‹ MONDAY ›" sits under the bar. The fix holds the panel just under the bar instead. Nothing else moves; the crew list under the heading scrolls as today. No question here: this is built as you asked.

What your recording shows

On the phone today, a pinch is Safari's own zoom of the whole page. The frozen date row is fixed to the page's top edge, and it keeps the column widths it measured before the zoom. So after the pinch its dates no longer stand over their columns, and then it slides up out of sight.

The fix: the pinch becomes the app's own, as on Quals and the Scheduler Board. The grid is zoomed, not the page, and the frozen date row is zoomed with it. Quals' frozen heading row already does exactly this, live since 21 Oct.

Question 1

What does a pinch zoom, and where can it start?

  • A (recommended). The grid only: dates, Event rows, Manning rows, names with their LVE figure, and every cell. The top bar, the Grid / OIL tabs, the View row, the grid's bar (Manning, Bidding, Legend) and the month buttons stay their size. A pinch that starts anywhere on the Leave War, the bars included, zooms the grid. The whole page never zooms, so the frozen date row can't be thrown off again.
  • B. The grid only, but only a pinch that starts on the grid. A pinch on the bars above still zooms the whole page, as Safari does today. The frozen row would then need a second fix for a page zoom, and the bars would still grow off the screen.
Question 2

How far can it zoom out?

The board and Quals zoom out until their whole width fits. The Leave War is a whole year wide, so that rule would shrink it to dots. It needs its own limit. Counted on the phone held upright:

  • A. As far as the − goes today: about 18 days and 35 people on screen.
  • B. A little further: about 22 days and 42 people.
  • C (recommended). Until a whole month fits across: about 30 days and 51 people. The letters are small, but each leave's colour and code still read, and it is the most useful overview. Turned sideways, the same zoom shows about two and a half months.

The July leave in these pictures is sample leave added for the pictures only, so you can judge a busy month. "Drawn" means the zoom was set by hand, since today's − cannot reach it.

Question 3

How far can it zoom in?

The names column grows with the zoom, so zooming in a long way leaves little room for days.

  • A (recommended). As far as the + goes today: about 6 days and 15 people on screen.
  • B. Twice normal, as on the board and Quals: under 3 days on screen. The names column takes about two thirds of the width.
Question 4

Does the OIL tab pinch too?

The OIL tab has its own − / +, separate from the grid's. Your ruling to remove the buttons named the grid.

  • Yes (recommended). A pinch zooms the OIL table the same way, Ctrl+scroll on a desktop, and its − / + go together with the grid's, so the Leave War zooms one way everywhere.
  • No. The OIL tab keeps its − / + and no pinch, as it is today.

Already settled, so not asked

  • The − / + on the grid's bar: they go only once the pinch works with the frozen date row (your D1276). The test is your look on your iPhone, held upright, turned sideways, and turned while zoomed in. If the dates drift, the − / + come back before anything goes live.
  • Desktop: Ctrl+scroll or a trackpad pinch over the grid zooms the grid only (D1228). It uses the same limits as the phone.
  • Opening size: a phone still opens one step out, and a desktop at normal size (D159).
  • Kept while the app is open: the zoom stays when you leave the Leave War and come back, as the − / + does today. Signing in again starts at the opening size.
  • One finger is unchanged: it still scrolls. A pinch never picks a block of days or opens a day, even when the first finger was held down (fix A7a from the overhaul's red team).

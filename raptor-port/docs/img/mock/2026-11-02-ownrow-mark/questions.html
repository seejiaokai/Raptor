What changed

An entry of several days filed on the Inputs page now opens whole, like leave placed on the Leave War: its dates in the title, its days marked, and “All 4 days” or the day you tapped. On “All”, Grant OIL gives OIL on every day of it, and a course, an overseas duty or anything in the ⋯ is filed over every day. The entry itself is still changed on the Inputs page.

On your own row, the days a window acts on now wear a stronger cyan fill and ring, so they no longer disappear into your row’s cyan (your pick A).

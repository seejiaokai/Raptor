# The notifications register — every place the app tells someone something (D1151)

**What this is (owner, D1151, 21 Oct 26 — "Can u create a document to note down all the areas in which we should have a
notification and how it should work … I want u to automatically see which areas needs a notification and automatically
fill it in and tell me if I want to reject").** One list: each place the app does, or should, tell a person something
without being asked — who is told, when, what the line says, what a tap opens, and whether it is built. Backlog item:
`[NOTIFY-REGISTER]` in `../../OUTSTANDING.md`.

**How it is kept.** The agent adds a line by itself whenever work shows a place that needs one, marks it *proposed*, and
names it in that job's closing report; he strikes any he does not want (the line then says *struck* and why, and is not
built). A proposed line becomes *ruled* only on his word (a D-number), and *built* when it is live. Nothing here is built
because it is listed.

**How the bell speaks** (D769): the thing first, in bold, with its date; then what happened and who did it; its age at
the right; a tap goes to it. A notice is written inside the change it reports, so an Undo takes it back (D148), and it is
never rewritten once made. The words follow the writing guide (`design-practices.md` §1b); new words are read cold before
they are built.

# The notifications register — every place the app tells someone something (D1151)

**What this is (owner, D1151, 21 Oct 26 — "Can u create a document to note down all the areas in which we should have a
notification and how it should work … I want u to automatically see which areas needs a notification and automatically
fill it in and tell me if I want to reject").** One list: each place the app does, or should, tell a person something
without being asked — who is told, when, what the line says, what a tap opens, and whether it is built. Backlog item:
`[NOTIFY-REGISTER]` in `../../OUTSTANDING.md`.

**How it is kept.** The agent adds a line by itself whenever work shows a place that needs one, marks it *proposed*, and
names it in that job's closing report; he strikes any he does not want (the line then says *struck* and why, and is not
built). A proposed line becomes *ruled* only on his word (a D-number), and *built* when it is live. Nothing here is built
because it is listed.

**How the bell speaks** (D769): the thing first, in bold, with its date; then what happened and who did it; its age at
the right; a tap goes to it. A notice is written inside the change it reports, so an Undo takes it back (D148), and it is
never rewritten once made. The words follow the writing guide (`design-practices.md` §1b); new words are read cold before
they are built.

**Undo retracts every line (his D1693, 5 Nov 26 — to build, [NOTICE-UNDO-RETRACT]):** the undo of what made a line takes it back — unread, it simply goes; read, it is replaced by its own words struck through, then "taken back · <who>" — "~~Posted out · now SANS~~ · taken back · Saber", unread (D1699, his idea; the Leave War's taken-back lines the same look, "~~LL approved~~ · taken back · Saber"; the drawing `docs/mock/2026-11-05-taken-back-sans/` picture 8) (the Leave War's way since D1216, now every line's: the posting line by its sheet's Undo post out, the input lines, the holiday's OIL line). **And a posting changed after it ran (his D1710, 5 Nov 26 — BUILT 5 Nov 26, [PO-CHANGE-RETRACT], `leavewar/sync.ts postOut`):** a ran posting given another date or another outcome on its Post out sheet takes back its lines the same way, inside the posting's own command, the admin who changed it told nothing; the changed posting tells again when it runs. A date moved earlier counts too (the agent's reading — the old line would name a day the posting no longer ran on, beside the new one); saved unchanged, or a posting not yet run, touches no bell.

**Across squadrons (his D2194, 18 Nov 26 — the rule for every future line):** no line about one squadron's own business
reaches another squadron's people; a line crosses only where the matter concerns both — a borrowed man, a man moving
between them, a detachment the other's men join, a place both share — and each squadron is told only what touches its
own people and days. No holiday list is shared (his D2195), so a holiday never crosses squadrons. A master admin's bell
carries his primary squadron's lines by default, every other squadron's switched off until he switches it on (his D2198,
18 Nov 26 — the switch to be drawn first; not built). An admin a master admin gives "Plan" of another squadron gets that squadron's admin lines while he holds it, on by default, his to switch off; on any bell, a line from a squadron other than the person's primary shows which squadron it is from, 142 or 149 (his D2202, 18 Nov 26 — drawn first; not built). **Built of it, 21 Nov 26 (the multi-squadron reshape):** who is told — a squadron's admin lines (B10, B12) go to its admins of today, a plain admin holding Plan of it among them, a master admin through his home squadron only (`state/accounts.ts adminsOf`, the one audience the commit gate checks too); no holiday line crosses squadrons (D2195). Not built: the switches, and the mark of which squadron a line is from — drawn and built before any screen gives Plan, so nobody holds Plan yet.

Squadrons, the second half: ten small things

You approved the second half's design on 22 Nov. Writing the build plan, and three readers checking it, turned up ten small things you have not seen. Each is drawn here on the real app. Pink "New" marks what is new. Blue "Approved" marks what you have already approved. Tap a picture to see it full size. Nothing is built until you answer.

Look at now

1Admin → Squadron config with 149 picked (pictures 1a, 1b). Its day, wave and duty templates belong to the squadron in view.
Recommended: it follows the squadron picked, as Logic does. Only an admin of that squadron can change it; anyone else sees it greyed, with "This is 149's set-up. Only an admin of 149 can change it."
2The Tracker after you pick another squadron (pictures 2a to 2d). Once open, the Tracker stays on the squadron it opened with until you sign out.
Recommended: as drawn, four cases.
  • 2a: you may still change that squadron's Tracker. It shows, greyed: "This is 149's Tracker. Pick 149 to change it. To open 142's, sign out and in."
  • 2b: you can no longer open it (your Plan of 149 was taken). It hides: "You can't open 149's Tracker any more. Sign out and in to open 142's."
  • 2c: you tapped to the member view. It hides: "This is 149's Tracker. Leave the member view to open it."
  • 2d: you pick another squadron with chart changes not yet saved. It asks first: "Save them before switching to 142?" with Stay, Discard them, Save them.
  • What it costs: a master admin or a Plan holder working in two squadrons' Trackers signs out between them. Letting the Tracker change squadron in place would mean rebuilding its core, which I recommend against for this batch.
3The bell lines when your own account changes, every wording (pictures 3a, 3b). You approved the shape on 22 Nov ("Given Plan of 149: full access to 149 · Saber"); these are the rest.
Recommended: as drawn.
  • Changed by someone: "Made an admin of 142", "Made a member", "Suspended" (read once he can sign in again), "Enabled", "Given View of 149", "View of 149 ended", "Plan of 149 ended", "Made a master admin", "Master admin taken off", each ending "· Saber".
  • Changed by the app itself, with no name: "View of 149 ended: you moved to 150", "Plan of 149 ended: archived", "Made a member: you moved to 149" (an admin who moves is a member of his new squadron), and "Suspended" (when posted overseas).
4Someone with no sign-in, opened on Admin → Users (picture 4). Access belongs to a sign-in, so he has none to give.
Recommended: 149's buttons greyed, with "Give him a sign-in first to give access."
5Eight squadrons on your iPhone (pictures 5a to 5d).
Recommended: the bell's Show buttons stay in one row that slides sideways. A person's squadron lines, the filter's Squadron ticks and the Schedules menu each grow a row per squadron.
6Signing in on a shared computer, two moments you have not seen (pictures 6a, 6b).
Recommended: as drawn.
  • 6a: after the last person's change saves, the page reloads so the next person starts clean. His name stays typed in; he presses Sign in once more: "Saved. Press Sign in again."
  • 6b: the change does not save, for example with no connection: "The last change has not saved yet — check the connection. Sign in waits until it saves."
7A Leave War window or an Input window left open when you pick another squadron (pictures 7a, 7b). Today it shows "This change could not be saved." though nothing was tried.
Recommended: "This is 142's. You picked 149 — close this to go on." Its buttons greyed, Close and ✕ still work.
  • The greying is new: today a held window looks normal and simply does nothing when pressed.
8A request to join 149 can only be answered by 149's admins (picture 8). Example: Jett asked to join 149, and Hunter, 142's admin, types Jett's sign-in onto a 142 man.
Recommended: refused, with "He asked to join 149 — only an admin of 149 can answer it."
9Tapping an access request from another squadron in the bell (picture 9). Example: Hunter, in 142, holds Plan of 149; Jett asks to join 149.
Recommended: one tap moves him to 149 and opens Admin → Users there, with "1 waiting for access to 149".
10The words after Undo of an access change (picture 10).
Recommended: "Undid: Plan of 149 given · Hunter", in the bubble the app shows after every Undo.

Told, not asked

  • Losing a right clears the bell, whatever the way. As you ruled, 149's lines leave Hunter's bell the moment his Plan of 149 ends. This now also holds when it ends by an Undo, by archiving or by an overseas posting. An Undo that gives the right back gives its lines back. A right given back later brings none of the old lines back, with one exception: a posting dated that same day shows again, because the app keeps a posting's day, not its time.
  • An admin made a member loses his own squadron's admin lines too, not only the other squadron's.
  • Made a master admin, his View and Plan buttons go, since "Full access" replaces them. He keeps the bell lines of the squadrons he planned, and those squadrons stay switched on in his bell.
  • "+ New squadron"'s first-admin list leaves out master admins, who already have full access, and anyone moving squadron the next day.
  • Archive stays outside Undo, as today.
  • About the pictures: nobody in the demo holds only View of 149, so picture 1a was drawn on Kestrel's sign-in with the name at the top changed to Hunter. Picture 8's role box shows today's long words; the short ones you approved come with this build. Picture 2b hides the whole Tracker, its bar too.
  • The approved design (pictures 1 to 10, R and M of 22 Nov) is unchanged and stays in the project.

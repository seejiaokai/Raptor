Squadrons, the second half of batch 1

The first half of batch 1 is live: 142 and 149 kept apart underneath. This half builds the screens you see. Nothing of it is built yet. Each picture is the real app with the new part drawn in. Blue "Approved" marks what you already approved on 17 or 19 Nov, redrawn on today's app. Pink "New" marks what is new since. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Look at now

1The Admin → Users filter row you asked me to draw (pictures 7a to 7c).
Recommended: as drawn.
  • Each admin sees his own squadron's people. Under the search: Pilots · WSOs · Personnel · All (the same switch as on Quals), then a CAT button and a Role button. Each opens a short list.
  • Role offers Member, Admin, Admin + Plan of 149, and Master admin, one each. So "Admin" does not show a master admin; "Master admin" does.
  • A CAT or Role button that is set turns blue, so a short list never looks like missing people. Filters only hide rows: the list stays A to Z.
  • A master admin's row starts with "142 · All squadrons". 142 is the squadron picked in his Schedules menu. All squadrons shows everyone, grouped under each squadron.
2What "+ New squadron" opens (picture 3c). Only a master admin sees the button.
Recommended: as drawn.
  • Three boxes: the squadron's number (a faded "150" shows what to type), its place, and its first admin.
  • The first admin is picked from the admins of the squadrons there already are. He gets Plan of the new squadron, so it never starts with no admin. Once its own people are added, he or a master admin makes one of them its admin.
3The bell line when your own account changes (picture 9b). You ruled that a person is told; this is how it reads.
Recommended: as drawn.
  • "Hunter · Sun 22 Nov / Given Plan of 149: full access to 149 · Saber". The same shape for made admin or member, View given or ended, master admin made or taken off.
  • A tap opens nothing: nobody may open his own row on Admin → Users.
4Plan taken away without a move. Example: on Monday Saber takes away Hunter's Plan of 149. Hunter stays 142's.
Recommended: 149's lines leave Hunter's bell that moment, read or not. His own lines stay.
This is the same as a man who moves squadron: on that day the old squadron's lines leave his bell. Without it, Hunter would keep tapping lines that open pages he can no longer use.
5Does a man keep his View and Plan of another squadron when he is archived, posted out to an overseas squadron, or suspended?
Recommended: archived or posted overseas, both end, as they do when he moves squadron. Suspended by hand, both stay.
Example: Hunter holds Plan of 149. Posted overseas, he is archived and his account suspended: his Plan of 149 ends, and if he comes back a master admin gives it again. Suspended for a week instead, he cannot sign in, and once he is enabled he has Plan of 149 as before.

What this half gives you

  • The "142 ▾" button and its Schedules menu for anyone who may see more than one squadron. For View it moves the schedule pages and Logic, read only. For a master admin or an admin with Plan, it moves every page, Admin → Users too, each his to edit.
  • The Scheduler Board shows its squadron's number, and prints and exports carry the number of the squadron printed.
  • Admin → Squadrons: the squadrons, the places and their time zones, and "+ New squadron" for a master admin.
  • Request access asks the squadron first; only that squadron's admins are asked.
  • Admin → Users by squadron: the home squadron line, the access buttons (None, View, Plan, Master admin), "also plans 149", Add a person's home squadron, and the filter row.
  • The bell: a master admin's or Plan holder's squadron buttons, the line when your account changes, and the line when a posting out could not run.
  • A sign-in on a shared computer waits for the last person's change to finish saving.

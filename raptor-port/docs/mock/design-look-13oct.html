Three things you asked for on one page: ways to redo the Leave War’s clash note on a phone, the shorter passing notes before and after, and a look over what was built on the night of 13 October against the design guide, with everything a keyboard cannot yet do. The OIL question before a swap

The idea is right and could only belong to this app: nothing moves until the question is answered, and the two buttons say what each does. Its frame is what lets it down.

L12The two answers sit in a box inside a box

What you meet: inside the window an outlined box; inside that a darker strip with a line under it that stops halfway across; inside that the two answers, pushed in from the left. It reads as broken. Next

The keys calendar step two must carry, written before it is built

Step two puts the one calendar into an input’s own window, the holiday form, the Leave War’s range pickers and “Jump to a date”. There the calendar sits inside its form instead of floating over it. Its keys:

  • One Tab stop for the dates. Tab moves on to the form’s next box; nothing is trapped, because nothing floats.
  • Arrows a day or a week · Page Up / Down a month · Shift + Page a year · Home / End the week’s ends.
  • Enter or Space picks. For a run of dates: the first Enter is the start, the second the end, a third starts over, as a tap does.
  • Escape with a run half-made drops the half; otherwise it closes the window or sheet the calendar is in. In “Jump to a date” Enter picks and jumps.
  • The month title: Enter opens the lists with the keyboard in the month list; Enter in a list returns to the dates of that month.
  • Today is the Today button, one Tab from the title. No extra key.
  • Where the keyboard lands: on the picked date, else today, else the 1st. An arrow at the month’s edge rolls into the next month.
  • A faint date can be reached by the arrows, so you keep your place, but Enter does nothing on it.
  • Escape with the lists open closes the lists only. Enter on a date picks, and never sends the form it sits in.
  • Delete or Backspace clears a box that can be empty.
  • In a Leave War sheet, Tab stays inside the sheet, as the sheet already keeps it.

So step one and step two matchK1 to K4 above are the same keys. Building them once, in the calendar itself, gives them to every place it is used.

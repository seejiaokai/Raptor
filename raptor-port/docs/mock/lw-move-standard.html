What I read into your words

correct any
  • Delete is the one word for taking something away — the one-day Clear and the list's Clear become Delete. Dashed, not red, so on the list it never reads as Refuse beside it.
  • Pick a range widens the selection: Decide, Move and Delete then act on every day in it, as a drag across those days does.
  • Move's rules don't change: it lands only where its half of the day is free, a refusal says why, and an OIL award or leave filed on the Inputs page stays put.
  • The block's "Post out (PO)…" reads PO, like the one-day sheet.
  • Every sheet follows one rule for who may move, so the one-day sheet and the block never differ (today a block can move a bid in a draft war and the one-day sheet can't).
  • A refused bid can move too (the one-day sheet already does this when it's alone on its day); it lands undecided, as every moved bid does.
  • In a block, what can move goes; what can't stays — an approved leave on a published war, an award — and the banner says what stays. A landing that can't take a moving record is still refused whole.

Your four answers

  1. Which order? A — what's there first (section 2), or B — new leave first (section 3). I recommend A.
  2. One word, "Delete", for Clear and Delete? I recommend yes.
  3. A member gets Move on his own bid's one-day sheet while bidding is open? Dragging already allows it. I recommend yes.
  4. The Move button? A, B, C or D (section 7). I recommend B.

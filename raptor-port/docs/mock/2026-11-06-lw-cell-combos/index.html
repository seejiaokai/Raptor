What I need from you

Redrawn after a design review and your question "so whole day, AM and PM will always be top and bottom half?". My first drawing (A) did make every two-entry day look like a morning and an afternoon, and its corner flags made the "!" too quiet and the same colour as the leave. C fixes both.

  1. Two entries on one day (pictures 1–6). Recommended: C — a whole day always reads as a whole day. Beside a half, the whole day keeps the whole cell and the half sits on it (5, 6). Two whole days are one box split in two, the halves touching (1–4). A morning and an afternoon stay two separate boxes with a gap, as today (15). Both codes stay whole. It changes your D1167 (side by side), so it needs your word.
  2. Marks on a day (pictures 7–12). Recommended: C — "!" keeps its amber tab, with a dark outline so it stands out on an amber leave (8, 12). More than two entries show as a small stack of cards behind the box, in place of "+2" (7). On a posted-out day the box itself carries the posting stripes, in place of the grey PO (9). His last day in is a yellow line down the day's right edge, in place of the yellow PO, on every last day, empty or not (10, 11). Letters stay whole; where a mark takes room, they drop one size. It changes the PO tags (your 19 Aug 26 last-day tag, D1720's grey tag) and the "+n", so it needs your word.
  3. Zoomed right out (the last strips). Recommended: the boxes show their colours only, and the "!" stays.

Also fixed, without a question: ATCH, PERS, APPT, OML and the app's own FO* / HO* drop one letter size where they were a hair too wide, and letters sit clear of the Inputs page's cyan edge (13–14).

Answer by number, for example "1 C, 2 C, 3 yes". After you pick, I build it, measure all 23,940 combinations again, and walk the grid on both sizes — the look, what a tap opens and how each entry is changed from there (D1725).

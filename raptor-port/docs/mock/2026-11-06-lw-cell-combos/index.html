What I need from you

  1. Two entries on one day (pictures 1–6). Today they stand side by side and the letters are cut — "FO|TC", "TC|TH", as on your iPhone. Recommended: A — one above the other, both codes whole, as a morning and an afternoon are drawn. B keeps the first entry's letters and shows the second only as a colour edge. A changes your D1167 (side by side), so it needs your word.
  2. Corner tags over letters (pictures 7–12). The "+2", "!", grey PO and yellow last-day PO sit on the letters. Recommended: Flags — on a day that holds a box, each tag becomes a small corner flag in its own colour (grey at the top: more on this day; amber: needs an admin; grey at the foot: posted out), and his last day in becomes a thin yellow line down the day's right edge. The letters keep their full size; the count and the words show when the day is tapped, and a day with nothing on it keeps its "PO". The other choice, "Words kept", keeps "+2" and "!" and moves a whole day's letters below them, smaller. Both change how the PO tags look on such a day (your 19 Aug 26 last-day tag, D1720's grey tag), so they need your word.
  3. Zoomed right out (the last strip). Your iPhone draws two-letter codes at a fixed smallest size, so zoomed out they spill over their boxes while the rest are too small to read. Recommended: at the two smallest zoom steps the boxes show their colours only.

Also fixed, without a question: ATCH, PERS, APPT, OML and the app's own FO* / HO* are a hair too wide for a whole-day box; they drop one letter size (pictures 13–14). Picture 15 fits today and stays as it is.

Answer by number, e.g. "1 A, 2 yes, 3 yes". After you pick, I build it, measure all 23,940 combinations again, and walk the grid on both sizes — the look, what a tap opens and how each entry is changed from there (D1725).

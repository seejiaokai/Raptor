Small things I decided, shown in the pictures
  • The pucks run A to Z, as the Inputs list names a shared input’s people.
  • On a phone the row’s two times stay together at its top. Today, on a tall row, they drift apart.
  • On a desktop, Personal Inputs gets room for two pucks side by side, as the Ground Programme has.
  • Personal Inputs counts it as “1 input”, not 4.
One thing you may notice When the pucks exactly fill their line, the row keeps one empty line under them. Every row in the app does this today. It is where a puck dragged onto the row lands.

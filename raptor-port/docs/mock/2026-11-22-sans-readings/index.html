Why I chose this. A schedule published for Thu 16 Jul while the tick was off shows Hex as an ordinary WSO. If ticking him again on Friday reached back to Wed 15 Jul, that published Thursday would change behind you and read “1 pending”.

The other way. Ticking him again makes him SANS straight through from Wed 15 Jul, with no gap. Thursday would then show him as SANS, and a Thursday already published would need publishing again.

3. How his SANS ends

There are three ways a posted-out man stops being SANS. They differ in what happens to Wed 15 Jul, the day he was SANS.

“Undo post out”: the SANS days are wiped

On Thu 16 Jul an admin opens Hex’s posting on the Leave War and presses “Undo post out (PO)”. Wed 15 Jul goes back to an ordinary day for him, as if the posting was never made.

An admin takes the tick off on Quals: the SANS days are kept

On Thu 16 Jul an admin clicks Hex’s SANS box on Quals, as in reading 2. Wed 15 Jul stays a SANS day. He is an ordinary WSO from Thu 16 Jul.

An admin archives him: the SANS days are kept

On Thu 16 Jul an admin archives Hex on Admin → Users. An archived man leaves the crew list on every day, so these pictures show his seat in the flying wave instead.

Why I chose this. “Undo post out” says the posting was a mistake, so the SANS days it gave him never happened. Taking the tick off, or archiving him, says he really was SANS up to that day and then stopped. Days already published while he was SANS are left alone.

Kept for later, not decided. If an admin has already taken the tick off and the posting is then undone, the earlier SANS days are still wiped today. Whether they should be is a question in the backlog.

4. Two places that go by today

Everything drawn on a day now asks “was he SANS on that day?”. These two do not. They ask “is he SANS today?”.

The SANS highlight chip

Today is Thu 16 Jul. Hex has been SANS since Wed 15 Jul. On Edit Schedule, press TYPE, then SANS. The chip lights Hex on every day of the week in view, Tue 14 Jul included, where he was still an ordinary WSO.

Why I chose this. A highlight chip picks people by what they are now and lights them across the whole week. The CAT and QUALS chips work the same way. The purple line on the puck is what tells you about the day itself.

Who can be given SANS availability

Today is Mon 13 Jul and Hex’s posting is dated Wed 15 Jul, two days ahead. On the SANS calendar an admin opens Thu 16 Jul and presses “+ Commitment”. Hex is not offered, although Thu 16 Jul is after his date. From Wed 15 Jul he is offered.

What it costs. A man posted out as SANS from a date ahead cannot have SANS availability filed for him until that date arrives. Nor can he file his own.

Why I left it. The app worked this way before this job: a posting takes effect on its date, and filing asks who he is today. Letting him be filed ahead, for days on or after his date, would be a new change. If you want it, I draw it for you first.

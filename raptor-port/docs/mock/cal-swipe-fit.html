The app’s own calendar, on a phone

Your direction of 13 Oct: one calendar for the whole app, built like the iPhone’s but in the Raptor look. Drawn here on the app’s real screens at phone size. “Today” is the app as it stands. “Drawn” is the same screen with the new calendar drawn on it. Nothing is built. Tap a picture to see it full size.

1 · The new calendar

What is yours in it: the month and year as a title you tap to scroll to another month or year; a Today icon right of the year; two arrows at the right; large round dates; no Reset and no tick. A tap picks.

A

Picking one date

What you seeThe box reads the date in words. A tap brings the calendar up over the form, as the iPhone’s does. Today sits on a soft blue disc. The picked date is the bright one. A tap on a date picks it and the calendar goes away. A tap outside it closes it and changes nothing.

The Today iconIt is the one the schedule’s “Jump to a date” already has: a calendar page with today’s number on it. A tap goes to today’s month.

In “Every Thursday”The Thursdays stand out, as you approved. Any date can still be picked.

B

Picking a run of dates

What you seeWhere a first and a last day are picked, the calendar stays inside its form, as it does now, so the run can be seen while the rest is filled in. Two taps make the run. It is said in words beneath, as now.

One thing goes“Clear” under the run. A tap on any new day already starts the run over.

C

The same calendar everywhere

The phone’s own date boxesAll of them change: “Every Thursday”, a new person’s Post in, Admin’s dates, a medical input’s upchit date, and the Leave War’s posting dates. The scrolled years are what the posting dates needed, so a date years away is two scrolls and a tap.

The app’s three small calendarsThe Leave War’s (a leave bid, a new period, the bidding dates, an event, an OIL credit, the OIL tracker), the one in an input’s own window, and the schedule’s “Jump to a date” all become this one.

Not changedThe months that are pages of their own: the Inputs calendar, the SANS calendar and the Calendar window’s month.

2 · The swipe (approved 13 Oct)

The same swipe the Inputs calendar and the SANS calendar already have: a finger moved left brings the next one, right brings the last one, and the new month slides in from that side. It never presses a date’s button. The blue mark only shows the finger’s path.

↔

Months on Month, years on Holidays

The arrows and Today stay. Up and down still scrolls. A desktop with a mouse is unchanged.

Three things to confirm, each with my pick
  1. Where one date’s calendar opens. Over the form, as the iPhone’s does (drawn in A). It then works the same in every form, wide or narrow. The other way is inside the form, under the box.
  2. The first day of the week. Monday, as every month in the app runs. Your iPhone’s starts on Sunday.
  3. The order of building. Two steps. First the Calendar window (the swipe, “Every Thursday”) and every date box that is still the phone’s own: your asks, and the boxes that can run off. Then the app’s three small calendars, as a second step with its own check, because leave bids and OIL credits are picked through them. The other way is everything in one go, which is one long build and one long check before anything goes live.

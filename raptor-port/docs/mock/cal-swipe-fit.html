The Calendar window on a phone

Your two asks from 13 Oct, on the app’s real screens at phone size. “Today” is the app as it stands. “Drawn” is the same screen with the change drawn on it. Nothing is built. Tap a picture to see it full size.

1 · The From date box in “Every Thursday”

What goes wrong: the From box is the iPhone’s own date box. An iPhone gives that box a width of its own, so it pushed past the window’s right edge in your photo. It was already told to fit on 8 Oct, and your photo shows it did not hold.

A

The app’s own date field

What you seeThe box reads “Thu 5 Nov 2026”. A tap opens a small month right under it, the same one “Add a holiday” already uses. The Thursdays stand out. A tap on a date picks it and the month closes again.

Until“A date” under Until gets the same field.

RecommendedThis one. Nothing of the iPhone’s is left in the window, so it is the same width on every phone and cannot run off the edge. It also reads as a date in words, where the iPhone’s box reads 05/11/2026.

The costOn the smallest phones the window scrolls a little while the month is open. It closes as soon as a date is picked, and Save is back in view.

B

Keep the iPhone’s own date box, forced to fit

What you seeThe window as it is today, with the box held inside it.

The costThis is a second try at what was done on 8 Oct. My test browser is not an iPhone and the box already fits there, so only your own phone can prove it. If it fails again it is another round.

2 · The swipe

The same swipe the Inputs calendar and the SANS calendar already have: a finger moved left brings the next one, right brings the last one, and the new month slides in from that side.

↔

Months on Month, years on Holidays

Stays as it isThe arrows and Today. Up and down still scrolls. A desktop with a mouse is unchanged.

A swipe that starts on a date’s buttonIt turns the month and does not press the button, so a day is never changed by accident.

Where it worksAnywhere on the month, and anywhere on the Holidays list, the empty room under a short list included.

The blue markIt is only drawn here to show the finger’s path. Nothing new appears on the screen.

Four questions, each with my pick
  1. The From box: A or B? A, the app’s own field, on From and on “A date”.
  2. With A, which dates can be picked? Any date, the Thursdays standing out, as drawn. That is what the box allows today. The other way is Thursdays only.
  3. The swipe as described above? Yes: the Inputs calendar’s feel, anywhere on the month or the list, and a swipe never presses a date’s button.
  4. The app’s other date boxes that are still the phone’s own (Admin, the post-in date on Users, the Leave War’s bid sheet, the upchit date on a medical input). Leave them out of this job. I put them on your iPhone look list, and any that run off become a small job of their own.

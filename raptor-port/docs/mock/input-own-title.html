Your question: how do you know the kind once the title is changed?

In the pictures above you could not tell on the schedule’s row, or on a phone’s day card, without opening the input. So here is the kind kept in sight, small, in both places. It shows only when the title is different from the kind’s own name.

Everywhere elseThe input’s window always shows the kind in its Type box. The Inputs list shows both. The month’s bar has no room, so the kind is in the day you open from it. Yours to choose: kind kept in sight as drawn here, or title alone as in the pictures further up.

What is settled

Which kinds take a titleSettled. Training, Course, Meeting, Fly with, Personal, Appointment, Duty, Event, OD and Other. Never leave or medical: those keep their official names.
Where the title showsSettled. Wherever the kind’s name stands as a name today. The kind still decides every rule: OIL, clashes, crew rest.
“Other”Settled. It takes the same Title box. Its remarks go back to being plain remarks.
RemarksSettled. Left as they are. Several days still read “till” and the last date; the title is not printed a second time.

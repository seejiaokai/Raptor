The other places that glow

You took the glow off the just-added ring on 15 Oct. These are the other places on the Scheduler Board that still glow. Each is shown as it is today beside the same thing with only its glow taken off. Nothing in the app has changed.

Real app pictures: a real element given the app's own class, its flash held at its brightest. The "no glow" picture changes one style setting and nothing else. The dashed box in the "where" pictures is drawn in to show the place; it is not part of the app. Tap any picture to see it full size.

Question 1 · the drag

The landing flash after a drag or an Undo, and the picked-up look

One setting draws both: the flash a puck or a row wears for half a second when it lands, and the look of a row while it is dragged. Its glow is faint. Most of what you see is the light wash and the ring, which stay either way.

Take the glow off the drag's flash and picked-up look? My pick: yes. The difference is small, and the ring then matches the just-added ring you chose.
Question 2 · a tapped warning

The crew a tapped warning lights, and a flying line's time boxes

Tap a warning in the day's issues list and its crew light up: red for a clash, amber for an advisory, grey for a note. A flying line whose landing time is its take-off time lights its two time boxes the same way.

Take the glow off the lit crew and the lit time boxes? My pick: yes. The ring alone still picks them out of a full board, and it is the look you chose for your own flagged puck on 24 Sep (no glow).
Question 3 · OIL Earn

A puck that earns OIL, in OIL Earn

With OIL Earn on, every puck that earns OIL that day wears a green ring with a glow. On a busy weekend that is many pucks glowing at once.

Take the glow off the OIL ring? My pick: yes. Many pucks glowing together reads as busy; the green ring alone says "earns".
Question 4 · a puck picked out

Your own puck, and a puck lit by a highlight chip

Your own puck wears a purple ring with a purple glow ("this is you"). A puck lit by the CAT, TYPE or QUALS chips wears a cyan ring with a cyan glow.

Take the glow off your own puck and the highlighted pucks? My pick: yes, for the same reason: a ring that does not glow, everywhere. Your own puck keeps its purple fill and ring.
No question · the keyboard

The keyboard's ring has no glow

The ring that shows where the keyboard is, is a plain 2px outline. There is nothing to take off, so both pictures are the same.

If you say yes

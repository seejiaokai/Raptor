2 · The rule for the design guide

The wording, checked by one fresh reader (they found four places where my first draft added rules you never made, all taken out):

A page's buttons sit in one row: the first row under the top bar and the tabs. What the page adds, the dates or filter it is read by, its settings: all in that row, at its button height (36 where it holds only the page's own buttons), even when the row holds one button. One or two buttons never take a second row of their own. A heading that only repeats the tab picked above it is put to you to delete. On the narrowest phone a button keeps its size and its words get shorter, drawn for you first; if it still won't fit, the layout is drawn and put to you. Not counted: a window's own buttons, one item's own controls, and a bar that shows for a while (the "Not saved" warning, the bar that waits for an OIL answer). A fold standing alone on a row is put to you.

This is the plain-words version. The guide's own text says the same with its ruling numbers: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-guide-one-row-draft.md.

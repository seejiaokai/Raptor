When an OIL question is left unanswered

The three pictures you asked for, on the app’s real screens, phone and desktop. “Today” is the app as it stands. “Drawn” is the same screen with the change drawn on it. Nothing is built. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Approved 12 Oct 26“Yes to all” — every recommendation below is taken: A for the swap, 2 for the moment after, and the four points that follow. These pictures are what gets built.

1 · A swap whose OIL question is not answered

The example: Saturday 18 Jul. Ranger is on Range brief, his own input. Tally is on Hangar open day. The scheduler drags Tally’s puck onto Ranger’s.

A

The swap waits for the answer

What he seesThe OIL question comes up over the schedule. Behind it nothing has moved. The buttons are “Don’t swap” and “Swap”. Swap stays grey until he picks Yes or No.

Not answeredNothing moved, so there is nothing to put back.

RecommendedThis one. It does what you ruled — no answer, no swap, and the app says so — but no puck ever jumps and jumps back. Also, by the app’s rules a swap that is made and then undone would, for that moment, tell Ranger by his bell that he was taken off, and on a published day it would drop the sign-offs. With A neither happens.

B

Swap first, take it back if not answered

What he seesThe swap is made at once, as today. The question says “Tally and Ranger swapped”, and Cancel becomes “Take the swap back”.

TodayThe same screen, but Cancel leaves the swap in place with no OIL answer at all. That is the gap you found.

The costThe pucks move and then move back, which is the part you said is hard to see.

The moment after — three ways to show it

Whichever of A or B you pick, the end is the same: Ranger back on Range brief, Tally on Hangar open day. These are three ways of making that plain. The words shown are A’s. With B they would read “Swap taken back”.

1

A note, and both pucks flash

The note is at the foot for about three seconds. Both pucks flash once, the same flash a puck makes when it lands.

The costIf he is looking elsewhere for those three seconds, it is gone.

2

The flash, and a bar that stays

Both pucks flash. A bar stays at the foot until he taps it: “Not swapped — OIL not answered”, with “Answer OIL” and “OK”. “Answer OIL” brings the question straight back.

RecommendedThis one. It cannot be missed, and the way to finish the swap is one tap away. It is the same bar the app already uses for “Set as default”.

3

A note, and both rows outlined

The two rows wear a blue outline for a few seconds, the outline a row wears when it has just been added.

The costIt is the loudest, but that outline already means “new row”, so it would carry two meanings.

2 · The one list

Every input of the day that needs an OIL answer

What it holdsOne line an input, drawn as the input’s own card, with Yes and No on it. Yes shows what it gives: FO for a full day, HO for half a day, as the single question offers today.

Yes to all · No to allThey fill every line. He can still change any one line before Save.

Several peopleAn input for several people is one line with one answer (Ranger, Tally).

LaterCloses it. What is left unanswered goes to the schedulers’ bells.

Design notesA chosen Yes is lit; a chosen No is quiet grey, so a long list reads at a glance. On a phone the list is only as tall as what is in it.

3 · A day made a holiday afterwards

The example: three inputs are filed on Friday 17 Jul, a working day. Then Saber makes that day a public holiday, National Day. With the demo’s own overseas duty on that day, four inputs now need an OIL answer.

The admin is told at once

What he seesThe moment he saves the holiday, the list opens by itself, with one line above it: “National Day is now a public holiday.”

What he answers thereTells nobody. Only what he leaves goes on.

TodayThe holiday saves and nothing is said. The inputs wear the small “OIL?” tag and that is all.

The Leave War’s Event row makes the same holiday, so it would open the same list.

The schedulers’ bell: one line a holiday

What they seeThe bell lights. One line: the holiday and its date, how many inputs still need an answer, who declared it. A tap opens the same list.

Who elseWhoever answers for each input is asked by his own bell, as today. Nobody else.

TodayAnother scheduler’s bell says “Nothing new”. Checked on the real app after making the holiday.

What I need from you

Answer by number. A plain “yes to all” takes every recommendation.

  1. The swap: A (it waits for the answer) or B (swap first, take it back)? Recommended: A.
  2. The moment after: 1, 2 or 3? Recommended: 2.
  3. A person added to an input with no answer yet is treated the same way: “Not added — OIL not answered”. Recommended: yes.
  4. Nobody else is told when a swap is not made, since nothing changed. Recommended: yes.
  5. The list opens by itself when the holiday is saved. That is how I read “if the admin already answers for everyone”. Recommended: yes.
  6. One bell line a holiday, counting only what is left, and a tap opens the list. Recommended: yes.

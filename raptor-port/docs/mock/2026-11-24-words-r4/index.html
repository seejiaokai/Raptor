Question 1 When an input is changed to another kind, should the bell say what it changed to, where it says "Renamed" today?

Saber opens Ranger's LL on Wed 15 Jul on the Inputs page and changes it to OL. Ranger opens the bell.

Today
Ranger's bell shows "OL · Wed 15 Jul", then "Renamed — was LL · Saber". Nothing was renamed: the kind of leave changed.
New
It would show "LL changed to OL · Saber". The same for any kind, for example "Training changed to Meeting · Saber".
I recommend
Yes. The Leave War's own bell line already says it this way: "LL bid changed to OL · Saber". "Renamed" stays for a real rename, for example "Renamed — was Training". The new line is one word longer than today's (4 words for 3).

Question 2 When a leave is made a half day, should the bell say AM or PM, where it prints clock times today?

Saber changes Ranger's LL on Wed 15 Jul from the whole day to the morning only. Ranger opens the bell.

Today
Ranger's bell shows "LL · Wed 15 Jul", then "Now 00:00–12:00 am · Saber". For the afternoon it shows "Now 12:01–23:59 pm · Saber".
New
It would show "Now LL (AM) · Saber", or "Now LL (PM) · Saber" for the afternoon. If the day moves as well: "Now Thu 16 Jul (AM) · Saber". For an input that has a title: "Now AM · Saber".
I recommend
Yes. On 30 Oct you chose AM and PM after the code, "LL (AM)", wherever the Leave War writes a half day. This carries the same form over to the bell line a change on the Inputs page makes, which that answer did not cover. The clock times go: they only ever meant "the morning half". The new line is no longer than today's.

Question 3 When more than two things change in one save, should the bell say that more changed?

In one save Saber moves "Training" from Wed 15 to Thu 16 Jul, renames it "Range brief" and changes its remark. Ranger is in it and opens the bell.

Today
Ranger's bell shows "Range brief · Thu 16 Jul", then "Now Thu 16 Jul, Renamed — was Training · Saber". The changed remark is never mentioned, and nothing shows that more changed. The bell only ever says the first two changes. That limit was my choice when the bell was built; you were not asked.
A
"Now Thu 16 Jul, renamed — was Training, and 1 more change · Saber" ("and 2 more changes" for two). It takes two lines on an iPhone, where today's takes one.
B
Say every change: "Now Thu 16 Jul, renamed — was Training, remark changed · Saber". Also two lines here, and longer with each further change.
C
Leave it as today.
I recommend
A. The line stays short, Ranger is told that more changed, and a tap opens the input as it now stands. With A or B, a word after a comma starts with a small letter ("renamed", "now") unless it is a name.

Question 4 Two messages read differently depending on how you got to them. Should each be given one wording?

A time the app cannot read

Saber types 2560 into a take-off box on Edit Schedule. Then Saber types 2560 into the start time on an input's row on the schedule.

Today
The take-off box answers "2560 is not a time. Try 0900 or 09:00." The input's row answers "That is not a time — try 0900 or 09:00".
New
Both answer "2560 is not a time. Try 0900 or 09:00." It names what was typed and has no dash.

Someone put on the seat they already hold

Saber drags Ranger onto the seat Ranger already holds. Separately, Saber taps the ALL AVAIL placeholder onto a seat it already holds.

Today
The drag answers "Already in that seat". The tap answers "ALL AVAIL — already in that seat". (A tap can only do this with a placeholder, never with a person.)
New
Both name who it is: "Ranger — already in that seat".
I recommend
Yes to both. The dash in the seat message stays for this round. It belongs to a family of seat messages that all read "Ranger — reason", and they are better changed together when the dashes are looked at.

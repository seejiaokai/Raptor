A hidden warning: struck out, not counted, no flag on the puck

"It should just show a strike out and it's darker. Ready to be reactivated again for flagging." · "If there are 3 issues initially and a scheduler clicks 1 to hide it should just show 2 issues."

These are pictures of the real app. The example is Tuesday of the demo week: four issues, and the scheduler hides the last one, "Static has a long work day". In each pair, "today" is what the app does now and "new" is what the build will do. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Edit Schedule

Today the count stays at 4 and the line moves under "1 hidden". New: the count reads 3, and the line stays where it was, struck out and darker. The ↺ beside it flags it again.

Scheduler Board

The same change in the board's issues list. A hide made on either screen shows on both, as it does today.

The puck

Today Static's puck keeps its grey ring and its "L" chip even after the warning is hidden. New: his puck is plain. Saint's puck beside him keeps its red ring, because his warning is not hidden.

View-only Sched what everyone else sees

Once the day is published with the warning hidden, members see the count without it and Static's puck plain. If they open the list, the hidden line is there, struck out, with no ↺: only a scheduler can flag it again.

When every issue on a day is hidden

Saturday has one issue. With it hidden the count is zero, but the line still has to be reachable to flag it again. So the bar stays, in a quiet grey, reading "✓ No issues". It says nothing about a hidden one; you see it only when you open the list.

What the build does, from your three rulings

  • Hidden for everyone, until someone taps ↺. A reload or a sign-in no longer brings it back.
  • Only a scheduler hides and flags again. Undo takes a hide back, as today.
  • It comes back by itself if the situation changes. If Static's day gets longer or shorter, that is a new warning and it shows.
  • On a day already published, a hide waits for the next amendment. The day reads "1 pending" and the four sign-offs fall. View-only Sched keeps the flag until the amendment goes out.
  • The published and printed schedule drop the flag too.
  • A man with two warnings keeps the flag of the one still showing. Hide Saint's clash and his puck goes from red to the amber of his other warning.
  • The bar's colour follows what is left. Hide the only red one and the bar turns amber.
  • A hidden crew-rest warning also drops the dotted "breaks tomorrow's crew rest" mark on the day before. It is the same item.

Three calls I made — approved as drawn (D475)

  1. View-only Sched shows the struck-out line (without the ↺). The other way is to leave it off View-only Sched altogether, so only schedulers ever see it.
  2. A day with every issue hidden keeps a quiet "✓ No issues" bar that opens the list. On the board the heading reads "No conflicts flagged ✓" with the struck-out line under it.
  3. The look of the hidden line: struck out, grey, a dashed outline on Edit Schedule, and the ↺ at full strength so it is easy to find.

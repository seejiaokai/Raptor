Six choices for the Tracker leftovers

Everything else in this batch is a plain fix and I'm building it now. These six change how the Tracker behaves, so they're yours. Each shows what happens today and what I recommend. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Reply with the number and your pick, for example: 1 yes · 2 yes · 3 a · 4 yes · 5 keep · 6 fold
1

Failures on an event marked N.A.

Marking an event N.A. hides its red failure ticks on the ball. But the Failures card still lists those failures and counts them in the total.

  • Yes: the card leaves them out too, like the ball does. They're kept, and they come back if the event is graded again. Recommended
  • No: keep counting them on the card.
2

Which failure is ST-02 and which is ST-02X

A failure recorded today, then a second one back-dated to an earlier day. Today the card labels them in the order they were typed in: the later day becomes the plain code. Pressing − takes back the one typed in last, which here is the earlier day.

  • Yes: the labels follow the days. The earliest failure is the plain code, the next adds an X. − takes back the one with the latest day. Your 9 Sep rule said "oldest first". Recommended
  • No: keep the order they were typed in.
3

Undo after changing a pace, end date or lull period

↶ and Ctrl+Z take back marks and the Last Flown and Upchit boxes. They don't take back a change to the pace, the two end dates or a lull period. So Ctrl+Z pressed right after changing the pace quietly takes back an older mark instead. The ↶ button's hover text names it, but the key says nothing.

  • a: make those changes undoable too, like Last Flown and Upchit. ↶ and Ctrl+Z then take back the pace change you just made. Recommended
  • b: keep them out of undo, but Ctrl+Z shows a line saying what it took back.
4

A "Done on" day in the future

Tomorrow's date is accepted as the day a flight was done. Last Flown moves to tomorrow and the Currency card reads −1d.

  • Yes: refuse a future day in Done on, Failed on and the two Last Flown boxes. The box goes back to what it had, with one line saying why. Upchit and the end dates are often in the future and stay as they are. Recommended
  • No: accept any day, as today.
5

Course order after importing a file with students

Importing a file with students puts any course the app didn't already have after the app's own courses, so your own courses stay first.

  • Keep: it's right for a handover. Your database route (export, wipe, import) carries the charts, not the demo students, so course order never comes up on it. Recommended
  • Change: a file's courses come back in the file's own order.
6

Edit chart layout on a sideways phone

On a phone turned sideways, the editing tools take up the whole screen. The chart gets 24px of height, so there's nothing to drag or pinch. In both options, the double-click note, the hint line and the Flow / Info / Show All tabs step aside while you edit. Upright phones and computers stay as they are.

  • Fold: every tool is one tap away and none is hidden off the edge. On 15 Aug you turned down a phone bar that scrolled sideways and hid controls. Recommended
  • Scroll: one row, swipe it sideways for the rest.

Raptor's own top bar is the biggest piece at that size (the top 150px of each picture). It belongs to the whole app, so I've filed it separately.

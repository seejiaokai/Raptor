My suggestion, and four questions

What changes:

  • Two parts: "Settings" (everything you can change, grouped by the job: crew rest and the working day, flying lines, sims, rows with no end time, standby, inputs, OIL, Insights), then "The checks" (every warning, advisory and note, grouped the same way).
  • One line under each setting says what changing it does, e.g. "Moves the suggested B on every line with no brief typed".
  • One line under each check says when it shows. Four keep a folded "How it works": crew rest, days in a row, the brief and the order of times, and OIL.
  • Gone: the code names and the grey column of engine text, the history, the "fired this week" count on every row (the "Fired this week" filter stays), the duplicated settings, and two settings nothing in the app reads (minimum turn, default sortie length).
  • One row of controls: the page name, search, a Filter button (All, Warnings, Advisories, Notes, Fired this week) and Edit, following your one-row rule.
  • Fixed while there: the amber "changed" strip today says the schedule is checked against the new values "not the published ones". Strictly, a published day keeps its brief, report, debrief and OIL values until it is published again. The strip would say so.
  1. The two parts and their groups, in this order: build as drawn? (My pick: yes. Rename or reorder any group freely.)
  2. The checks part: keep every check listed with one line each (my pick), or list only the four with "How it works"?
  3. The "fired this week" counts: drop them from each row and keep only the filter (my pick), or keep them?
  4. The two unused settings (minimum turn, default sortie length; nothing reads them): remove them from the page (my pick)?

"All as recommended" means all four picks. Then I write every line of both parts, get a fresh reader to check the wording, and build it with its own check.

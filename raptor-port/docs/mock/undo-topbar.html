These are pictures of the real app. The "after" pictures move the app's own buttons in the page, so they look exactly as the build will. Tap any picture to open it full size.

What I read into your words

  1. One place, one look, the desktop's order at both widths (your D348). Left to right: Undo · Redo · (the changes clock, Edit Schedule only) · Sync · the bell. On a phone the same order in icons, with the bell at the far right.
  2. On every page where you can change something Undo takes back: Edit Schedule, the Leave War, Inputs, Quals, Admin and the Logic page. View-only Sched and Help change nothing, so they get no pair.
  3. The Leave War's pair leaves its Period row and moves up into the top bar.
  4. On a phone, the Sync chip shrinks to its dot wherever the pair shows, as it already does on Edit Schedule, so the right end of the bar reads the same on every page: dot · bell · Undo · Redo.
  5. The changes clock stays on Edit Schedule only. You asked whether every page should have it. I'd say no: the window is laid out by the schedule's days, and tapping a line takes you to that spot on the schedule. Changes made on Inputs, the Leave War or Quals already show there as lines on their day.
  6. The scheduler board covers the top bar, so it gets the same group in its own bar: Undo · Redo · History · Sync · the bell, then its own Done and Close. On a phone, to make room, the layout switch and Sort move down to the second row beside the search box. On a desktop, Sort all moves to before Undo.
  7. The Tracker's own Undo and Redo move to the top bar too. They still take back the Tracker's own marks and chart edits, because the Tracker keeps its own history. Only their place changes.

Your answer

  1. Approve as drawn, or name what to change.
  2. The Tracker (reading 7): move its pair to the top bar too? I recommend yes. It is what "all" means, and the Tracker's buttons then sit where every other page's do.
  3. The board (reading 6): Sync and the bell join its bar as drawn, with the layout switch and Sort moved to the phone's second row? I recommend yes. The other way would be leaving Sync off the board, since it only matters when you publish in a meeting, but then the board's bar would no longer match the top bar.

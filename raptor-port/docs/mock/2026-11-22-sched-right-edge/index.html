Question 1 — the right edge, crew panel folded

Today a black strip runs down the right side of the window. It is kept empty for the CREW tab and the next-day arrow. In the proposal the strip is gone: the next day shows that much further, and the tab and the arrow sit on top of it. The scroll bar at the foot runs to the edge too.

  • Nothing you can see today gets covered. The tab only sits over the strip that is black today, so you only gain.
  • The day arrows still move one day a press. I pressed the right arrow from Monday to Sunday and the left arrow back, before and after, at six window widths. Each press moved exactly one day, and each day stopped in the same place as it does today.
  • At the very end of the strip the last column still stops clear of the tab, as it does today.

What does not change

Question 2 — a small find at the left edge

I found this while taking the pictures. It is in the app today and has nothing to do with the right edge. When you come to Edit Schedule from View-only Sched, the first day opens hard against the window's left edge, with the left arrow sitting on it. Press either arrow and from then on a day stops a little way in, clear of the arrow. So the same day sits in two different places.

If you would rather leave it, say so and I will only write it down as a known oddity.

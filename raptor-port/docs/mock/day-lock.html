These are pictures of the real app with the lock drawn into its own look. Saber is you. Ranger stands in for a second scheduler. Tap any picture to open it full size.

What I read into your answers

  1. You edit a day by taking it. Press Edit on the day, or pick several days at once. While you hold it, other schedulers see "Saber – editing" and can only read it.
  2. Nothing to save. Every change is saved as you make it. The day still stays unofficial until you publish it, as today. Done editing gives the day back.
  3. Idle means no change on that day for 30 minutes. Looking at it does not count. You get a warning at 25. A phone that dies or loses signal frees the day after the 30 minutes.
  4. An admin can take a day over. Today every scheduler is an admin, so any scheduler can. It asks first, the holder is told, and the change history keeps it.
  5. Other people's changes arrive by themselves every 30 seconds. Fast sync makes it every second, and Refresh now is there as a backup.

The week, while you hold Monday and Ranger holds Tuesday D355

Each day carries a strip under its sign-off. Teal: you hold it, saved as you go, with Done editing to free it. Amber: someone else holds it, "Ranger – editing", read only for you and faded, with when Ranger last changed something. An admin also sees Take over.

Taking a day, or several D355

A day no one holds says so, with ✎ Edit Monday. To take several at once, ✎ Edit days… in the row under the top bar lists the week: tick the free ones, then Edit. A day someone else holds is shown and cannot be ticked. On a phone the button reads ✎ Days.

The warning at 25 minutes D356

Only the holder sees it, and only after 25 minutes with no change on that day. Looking does not count. Keep editing starts the 30 minutes again; Done editing frees the day now. If nobody answers, the day frees itself at 30 minutes. Closing the page or signing out frees it at once.

An admin takes a day over D355

Take over asks first and says how long ago the holder last changed something. Nothing the holder did is lost: it was saved as they went. The holder's screen turns read only and tells them who took the day. The change history keeps a line for it.

The scheduler board D355

The same strip sits at the top of the board, for the day on screen. Stepping to another day on the board shows that day's strip. ✓ Done closes the board; the day stays yours until Done editing or the 30 minutes (question 3 below).

Seeing other people's changes D356

Other people's changes arrive by themselves every 30 seconds while the page is on screen, and straight away when you come back to it. The Sync chip reads "Sync · 30 s". Tapping it opens a small menu: when it last checked, Refresh now, and the Fast sync switch for publishing or a meeting (every second).

What happens behind it (for the database design)

  1. The schedule is saved one day at a time. Today a whole week is saved as one piece. If Saber held Monday and Ranger held Tuesday, their saves would overwrite each other. So the IT team's tables keep each day as its own piece, and the lock sits on the day.
  2. A member's input still lands on a held day. A leave filed for Monday while Saber holds Monday goes in as it does today (on a published day, as a pending change). Saber sees it arrive within 30 seconds. The lock is only between schedulers.
  3. Publishing a day needs the day free or yours. If Ranger holds Tuesday, Publish day on Tuesday says "Ranger is editing Tuesday".
  4. One change that touches two days needs both. Moving a man from Monday to Tuesday, or loading a template onto two days, asks you to take the other day first, if it is free.
  5. The safety check stays underneath. If two saves ever meet on the same day, the second one is refused and reloaded, never silently lost.

  2. ANSWERED 29 Sep 26 — yes (D451): a change on a free day takes it; the Edit button stays. Tapping a free day without pressing Edit: should it ask "Edit Monday?" with one button, or do nothing until you press Edit? I recommend the ask. Otherwise a drag on a free day just does nothing, and it looks broken.
  3. The board's ✓ Done: should it only close the board (the day stays yours until Done editing or the 30 minutes), or close the board and free the day too? I recommend close only. People move between the board and the week all the time, and freeing the day at every close would mean taking it again each time.
  4. Undo after you have given a day back: if the day is free, Undo takes it again by itself and undoes; if someone else holds it, Undo says who. I recommend this. The other choice is that Done editing clears that day from your Undo.
  5. Everywhere else (Inputs, the Leave War, Quals, the Tracker): no lock. If two people change the same thing at the same moment, the second is told "Ranger changed this a moment ago" and shown the newer version, to do again. I recommend this: these are small records, and a clash is rare.
  6. The Sync chip opens a menu instead of switching fast sync on at one tap. That is one tap more for fast sync, but it gives Refresh now a place, on a phone too. I recommend it.

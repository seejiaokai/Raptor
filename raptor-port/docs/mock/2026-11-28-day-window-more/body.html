Q1

A group leave: when does Cutter leave the group?

Example: Cutter, Ghost and Dash (WSOs) share an LL on Mon 21 Dec. Hex (admin) opens Cutter's day, presses "Cutter" on the card, then closes the window without changing anything.

  • A (recommended): he leaves the group only when a change is made for him. Closed with no change, all three stay on one leave. This changes your 27 Nov answer.
  • B: he leaves the group the moment "Cutter" is pressed, your 27 Nov answer. Closed with no change he stays on a leave of his own, and one Undo puts him back.
Q2

A group's course or overseas duty: Move and Delete on its card?

Example: Cutter, Ghost and Dash (WSOs) are on one CSE on Mon 14 Dec. Hex (admin) taps Cutter's day.

  • A (recommended): yes. "3 pax" moves or deletes it for all three, "Cutter" for him alone, the same as a group leave.

Q3

Q4

Show the Auto-approve switch when an admin changes approved leave?

Example: Hex (admin) turned Auto-approve off last week. Today he changes Cutter's (WSO) approved LL to OL. You ruled the result follows his switch, so it goes back to pending. The switch is not on that card, so nothing warns him.

  • B: keep it hidden, as first drawn.
  • C: an admin's change always stays approved, whatever his switch says.

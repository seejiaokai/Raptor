For someone whose phone asks for less motion

Your phone is set to reduce motion, so the real app would show the claw still. It moves here so you can judge it.

How it is built: the claw is drawn by the browser from the logo's own three lines, written into the first page the phone receives, so it shows before the rest of the app arrives. Nothing extra to download (no animation library), so it adds nothing to the load. I will measure the load time before and after the build.

After the move to Power Apps: Power Apps most likely shows its own loading screen first, and the claw would follow it while TALON itself loads. I will confirm this in the Power Apps hosting trial.

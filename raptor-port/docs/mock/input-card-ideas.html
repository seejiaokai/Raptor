A shorter input card that lets long words wrap

These are live cards, not pictures: they wrap for real on your screen. Each idea shows the same six inputs, from a plain Duty to a 40-letter title with a long remark, and its measured height.

The name is cut where the hours begin

Short, but a long title ends in “…”. A long remark does wrap. The kind is in a different place on a titled card and on a plain one.

Idea A · recommended

One line of facts, then the words at full width

Top line: who, the kind, LATE, the hours. These are all short, so nothing ever fights for room. Below it the title and the remark have the whole card and wrap as far as they need. The kind sits in the same place on every card, titled or not. The small print says “By Saber”, as you asked: no “for Ranger”.

Idea B

Words on the left, facts in a column on the right

The hours and who placed it line up down the right edge, easy to run an eye down. The words keep their own column and wrap inside it. The cost: the words’ column is narrower, so long text takes more lines.

Idea C

One flowing paragraph, the hours in the corner

Everything reads as one sentence and wraps round the hours. The shortest of the three. The cost: the parts are harder to find at a glance, because nothing stays in a fixed place.

Yours to choose

1 · Which cardRecommended: A. A plain input stays two lines, as today. Nothing is ever cut. And it answers “one look”: the kind always follows the name of the person, on every card.
2 · The kind: small grey, or a pillEither works in A. Flip the switch at the top. On A’s facts line the pill has room, which it did not in the first drawing.
3 · The small print: do we need “By” at all?Recommended: “Others only”. Flip the third switch. A card says “By Saber” only when someone other than its own person placed it. A man’s own input says nothing, because the card already starts with his name. That turns most cards into one or two lines. Who placed it and when is still in the input’s window and in the change history, for every input.
4 · The same card on the day and on the listRecommended: yes. Flip “List” at the top: the only difference is the date before the hours. A tap opens the input; no pencil, no cross.

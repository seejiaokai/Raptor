What exists today for taking a man out

  • Nothing takes a man out of an ALL AVAIL or ALL crowd by hand.
  • A man leaves the crowd only by being busy at that time: another item on the schedule, or an input such as leave or a course.
  • In OIL Earn, on a day that earns, a tap switches a man off earning, and the man stays in the count. On a working day there is no OIL Earn.

Taking a man out by hand

The pictures in this part use the plain window of question 1 and the control of question 5.

Question 5 How should the control show: a ✕ beside the man you tap, or a ✕ on every row?

Monday's SODB for ALL AVAIL, before it is published. Nothing like this exists today (see the note above the questions).

What you see
A tap on Reaper picks Reaper, as today: Reaper lights up blue across the schedule and the others dim. A ✕ then shows beside Reaper only. The ✕ is the one the warning list uses to hide a warning. Only a scheduler sees it, and never on a day as it was issued.
I recommend
Beside the man you tap. Most of the time the window is only read. A ✕ on all 43 rows is a lot to look past. The other way saves one tap for each man taken out. On a desktop, at the window's narrow width, the ✕ sits 3 px nearer the puck than the warning list's does, so it fits. On an iPhone the ✕ looks as drawn but needs a finger-sized area to press.

Question 8 How is a man put back?

Reaper has been taken out of SODB.

What you see
Reaper stays in place, crossed out. Where the ✕ was, the round Reset arrow shows, the one a hidden warning uses to flag it again. A tap on it puts Reaper back and the count goes back to 43. Undo does the same.
I recommend
By the arrow left where the man was, or by Undo. It works the way a hidden warning does, which stays in its list, crossed out, with the same arrow. Reaper never disappears without a way back.

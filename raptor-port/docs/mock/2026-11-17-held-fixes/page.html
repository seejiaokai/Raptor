Q1 · A WSO in a BB or AVALON front seat with no times

What you meet today. On a new BB or AVALON line, START and END are blank until typed. Drop a WSO in its front seat and the crew list says "WSO — cannot fly front seat" at the moment of the drop, but nothing stays: no line in the day's issues, no ring, no Q chip. Type the times and the warning comes. A flight with no times already warns ("… (no times yet)").

Worked example: Tuesday, a new BB wave. Echo (a WSO) goes in the first MAIN line's front seat. The issues still read "4 issues · 2 warnings". Type 19:00 and 07:00 and the list gains "Echo — Illegal seat: a WSO in the front seat of BB SHIFT MAIN 19:00–07:00".

Proposed. The warning comes with no times too, and the seat wears its red ring and Q chip. The words are the line's own, with the hours swapped for the words a flight already uses:

Echo — Illegal seat: a WSO in the front seat of BB SHIFT MAIN 19:00–07:00 (no times yet)

Q2 · "+ Block" and "BB desk"

What I found. On a fresh copy of the app the menu reads "Standard · 3 roles", with no desk. "BB desk" shows only when the Standard template's "For wave" is set to BB in the duty templates window (the menu's pencil). The walker had set it. "BB desk" then says what the block becomes, the BB wave's desk, the way "SC desk" and "AVALON desk" do for the other two.

Worked example: Monday's board, "+ Block": "Standard 3 roles". Open the pencil, set Standard's "For wave" to BB, press Done, and "+ Block" reads "Standard 3 roles · BB desk".

Recommended: close the item with no change. Nothing needs new words.

Q3 · The singular "warning"

What you meet today. The issues bar now reads "17 issues · 6 warnings" (live since pull request 756), but two places still use the singular: the day's details window (the ⓘ) and Insights' red tile.

Worked example: Monday's ⓘ reads "6 warning 7 advisory 4 note" with the bar beside it reading "6 warnings". The board's Insights shows a big 36 over "13 warning". The 36 is the week's issues and 13 of them are warnings, but nothing on the tile says so.

Proposed. Plurals wherever the number is not 1, and the tile names its number the way the issues bar does:

6 warnings · 7 advisoryies · 4 notes
36 over issues · 13 warnings (the tile prints it in capitals)

Q4 · The banner on a look at the Original

Q5 · "N changes" beside "N pending"

What you meet today. The chip in a day's head shows "N pending" while changes wait to go out, and "N changes" once nothing waits: the day's change history, who changed what. Side by side on the week, the two look alike, and "2 changes" can read as two changes not yet sent.

Worked example: Monday is published with one edit waiting: "1 pending". Tuesday is published and its two edits already went out as AL1: "2 changes", in the same place on its head.

Proposed. "N changes" wears History's clock before the count, the clock the top bar's changes button wears. The words and the window it opens stay. Another way, not drawn: rename it, for example "History · 2"; that changes a word you see on every day, so I recommend the clock.

Q6 · A week whose saved copy cannot be read

What you meet today. Nothing is lost in any of these, but two of them say the wrong thing. On a desktop, the next-week preview at the week's end draws the demo days when next week's saved copy cannot be read, with no notice. And deleting a person while the week cannot be read is refused in the amber note, not the grey "Blocked:" note every other refusal of one change takes (your D1667, D1668).

Worked example: next week's (20 Jul) saved copy spoiled. Edit Schedule on 13 Jul, scrolled to Sunday: the preview shows "Monday Jul 20" with its demo programme as if nothing were wrong.

Proposed, the preview. The notice Edit Schedule shows over an unreadable week (D1785), one word swapped:

This Next week’s saved copy could not be read, so it can’t be shown. The saved copy is kept as it was. Report it with “Report a problem” on the Help page.

Proposed, the delete refusal. The grey note, in D1785's words. It ends longer, 12 words to 29, because it now says why and what to do:

Blocked: the saved copy of the week of 13/07/2026 can't be changed — could not be read, so the delete was not made. Report it with “Report a problem” on the Help page.

"so" is known: this refusal comes only from the unreadable week. Its twin for another stored week, "The week of … can't be read — the delete was not made", would take the same words. No control reaches this refusal without hiding the week behind its notice, so it was raised through the app's own note with its real words.

Not drawn tonight: print and the spreadsheet export of such a week (they wait with the PDF work you held, D1644); a week refused because its records belong to another week, which needs words of its own and is hard to stage; and the Leave War's silent bid refusals on such a week, which belong to the Leave War's own round.

Q7 · The Original on View-only Sched

What you meet today. On View-only Sched, a published day's picker offers the latest issued version and the working draft. Once the day has an amendment, the Original is no longer in the list, so a member cannot look at what first went out.

Worked example: Monday published, then amended as AL1. The picker offers "AL1 — as issued" and "Working draft — not issued". Nothing reaches the Original.

Proposed. One more entry, worded as its neighbour is (the picker's own dash kept, as its family has it). With more amendments, each issued version is listed the same way, newest first.

AL1 — as issued
Original — as issued
Working draft — not issued

Q8 · The late cut-off box

What you meet today. Logic → "Inputs late cut-off" opens the Inputs calendar settings window. A number typed in the days-before box is kept only by Save or Enter. Press ✕ (or Escape) and it is gone without a word.

Worked example: type 10 over 14, then Tab. The box shows 10, and the example under it already says inputs are due by Fri 27 Nov, but the Logic row behind still says "14 days before the week starts". Press ✕: the window closes, the row still says 14, and nothing told you the 10 was dropped.

A (recommended). The box keeps the number the moment you leave it, as the Logic page's own boxes do. The row behind reads 10 at once, and Save and Cancel go, since nothing is left to save; Undo takes it back. The members' switch in the same window would then save as it is ticked.

B. The window keeps Save. ✕ or Escape with a changed box asks first, in a card hanging from the ✕, worded as the input window's own question, one word swapped:

This input window has unsaved changes. Close it and lose them? [Discard and close] [Keep editing]

A is fewer steps and matches the page you opened it from. B keeps the window as built and adds a question.

Reorder rows: a finger drag

The Tracker's ⇅ Reorder windows (courses, syllabi, crew) with a finger drag, as you asked (D922), drawn on the real app before anything is built. Pictures are the Syllabus order window. The dashed orange box only marks what a picture is about. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Drawn in, not in the app yet: the six-dot grip, the white circle (your finger), the blue ring on the carried row and the blue landing line. The ring on the dropped row in picture 3 is the app's own landing mark, the one a note dropped on a day gets.

How it would work

  • The finger takes a row by its grip only. A finger anywhere else on the list scrolls it, so a long crew list still scrolls.
  • Carried to the top or foot of a long list, the list follows the finger. A mouse already does this (since 15 Oct). The demo lists are too short to scroll, so there is no picture of it.
  • The ▲ ▼ stay, and stay the keyboard's way to move a row.
  • The same in all three windows: courses, syllabi and crew.

1 · The grip

Should the grip become the app's six dots ⠿? Today these rows show ☰.

My pick: yes. Every other drag in the app (the board's rows, the Leave War's Rearrange, a day's notes) uses ⠿, and in the Tracker ☰ already means "list" on the ☰ Show All button. The grip also gets a bigger invisible area for a thumb; it does not look bigger.

2 · A mouse drag on a desktop

Should a mouse drag get the same look as the finger? Today a mouse drag fades the carried row and draws a box round the row it will take the place of.

My pick: yes, the same look. One way of dragging in the app: the carried row ringed and one landing line in the gap, as the design guide says for every list (one drop, one landing line). The box today means "take this row's place", which reads differently from every other list.

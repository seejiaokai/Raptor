The bell after an Undo: the new words

Nothing in the app has changed. These are drawings for you to approve. Each "Today" picture is the real bell. Each "New" picture is that same bell with only its words changed. A line nobody had read still simply goes, and the person who pressed Undo gets nothing. Tap any picture to enlarge it.

What to look at now: eight questions

  1. 1. Should the crossed-out "taken back" look go from every bell line?I recommend: Yes, everywhere.
  2. 2. Should the change history keep its "taken back" words?I recommend: Keep them.
  3. 3. Should a read line about an input, or a public holiday, leave a new line when undone?I recommend: Yes, in this job.
  4. 4. Should the last SANS line with "taken back" get the new words too?I recommend: Yes.
  5. 5. Should a Redo say "again"?I recommend: Yes, with the words in the Redo list below.
  6. 6. When a posting's date is moved earlier, should an admin get two lines?I recommend: Keep both.
  7. 7. What should a tap on "no longer a public holiday" open?I recommend: Nothing. It only marks the line as read.
  8. 8. When one change did two things to an input, what should the line say?I recommend: The first change as the title, the second as the sentence.

Three cases still have no words

  • A bidding stretch whose exact days changed so it both lost and gained dates, such as 1 Jan – 31 Mar changed to 1 Feb – 31 May. Its Undo closes some dates and opens others.
  • A posting changed to no outcome at all ("off the manpower").
  • One save that changes both a posting's date and its outcome.

The eight questions in full

Question 1 Should the crossed-out "taken back" look go from every bell line?

Ranger reads that the LL on Mon 20 – Tue 21 Jul is approved. Saber then presses Undo.

Today
Ranger's bell shows "Your leave · Mon 20 – Tue 21 Jul", then "LL approved" crossed out, then "taken back · Saber".
New

Yes, everywhere. The new line says what is true now, so Ranger does not have to work it out from a crossed-out one. This replaces the crossed-out look you chose on 5 Nov, for every line and not only the SANS one.

Question 2 Should the change history keep its "taken back" words?

Saber undoes Ranger's approval, then opens the change history.

New
It would show the same.
I recommend
Keep them. The change history is a record of what was pressed, not a message to one person.

Question 6 When a posting's date is moved earlier, should an admin get two lines?

Question 7 What should a tap on "no longer a public holiday" open?

Hex, an admin, reads "Public holiday · Mon 10 Aug / 3 inputs need an OIL answer". Saber then undoes the holiday.

Today
That line just vanishes. A tap on the original line opened the OIL list for the holiday's dates: every input on them still waiting for an OIL answer, each with Yes and No.
New
It would show "Mon 10 Aug is no longer a public holiday. · Saber". The list it used to open is now empty, except for inputs on a weekend inside a longer holiday, which still need an answer.
I recommend
Nothing. It only marks the line as read. A tap opens nothing, as on a line about a deleted person. It is the one line that would not open what the original opened.

Question 8 When one change did two things to an input, what should the line say?

In one go Saber moves Training from Wed 15 Jul to Thu 16 Jul and adds Hex to it. Ranger reads the line. Saber presses Undo.

Today
The line just vanishes (question 3).

I recommend
The first change as the title, the second as the sentence. Today's lines already name only the first two changes, so this keeps that rule.

Inputs (only if question 3 is a yes)

On an iPhone, a date or time in a long line may wrap onto a second line.

# Bell lines after an Undo: the final words (after the fact check and the cold read)

Nothing is built. Every title is followed by "· Saber" in the title row, as in the SANS lines he approved. "(title alone)" means no sentence under it. A line its reader had not read still simply goes, and the person who pressed Undo gets nothing. No fact or behaviour of the draft was changed; anything doubted is marked "DOUBT" in the note column and goes back to the builder.

## Eight questions for you

**1. Should the crossed-out "taken back" look go from every bell line?**
- The case: Ranger reads that his LL on Mon 20 – Tue 21 Jul is approved. Saber then presses Undo.
- Today Ranger's bell shows: "Your leave · Mon 20 – Tue 21 Jul", then "LL approved" crossed out, then "taken back · Saber".
- It would show: "Your LL on Mon 20 – Tue 21 Jul is no longer approved. · Saber", and under it "It's pending again."
- Recommendation: yes, everywhere. The new line says what is true now, so he does not have to work it out from a crossed-out one. This replaces the crossed-out look you chose on 5 Nov, for every line and not only the SANS one.

**2. Should the change history keep its "taken back" words?**
- The case: Saber undoes Ranger's approval, then opens the change history.
- Today it shows the Undo as "Undo — a decision on a bid". "Taken back" appears for two other acts: an approved leave put back to a bid ("Ranger · LL approval taken back — back to a bid · 20 Jul–21 Jul") and Undo post out ("Leave War · Ranger · posting out taken back · 20 Jul"). [checked: quotations corrected; an Undo's own history line has no "taken back"]
- It would show the same.
- Recommendation: keep it. The change history is a record of what was pressed, not a message to one person.

**3. Should a read line about an input, or about a public holiday, leave a new line when it is undone?**
- The case: Ranger reads "You were added" for Training on Wed 15 Jul. Saber presses Undo.
- Today the line just vanishes from Ranger's bell. He still expects to be in Training.
- It would show: "You're no longer in Training on Wed 15 Jul. · Saber"
- Recommendation: yes, in this job. You already ruled on 5 Nov that every undone line should leave one. It has not been built yet, so this adds building, not only new words.

**4. Should the last SANS line with "taken back" get the new words too?**
- The case: an admin unticks SANS for Ranger on the Quals page. Ranger reads "You're no longer SANS." Saber then presses Undo.
- Today Ranger's bell shows "You're no longer SANS." crossed out, then "taken back · Saber".
- It would show: "You're SANS again. · Saber", and under it "The SANS availability you filed for Fri 17, Sat 18 Jul is back."
- Recommendation: yes. Otherwise it is the one crossed-out line left in the bell.

**5. Should a Redo say "again"?**
- The case: Saber moves Ranger's LL to Thu 23 – Fri 24 Jul. Ranger reads it. Saber presses Undo, then Redo.
- Today the third line reads "LL moved to Thu 23 – Fri 24 Jul", word for word the same as the first, so it looks like a repeat by mistake.
- It would show: "Your LL moved to Thu 23 – Fri 24 Jul again. · Saber"
- Recommendation: yes, with the words in the Redo table below.

**6. When a posting's date is moved earlier, should an admin get two lines?**
- The case: Ranger was posted out as SANS on Mon 20 Jul and Hex, an admin, read that line. Saber moves the date to Fri 17 Jul.
- Today Hex's bell shows "Ranger · Mon 20 Jul", then "Posted out · now SANS" crossed out, then "taken back · Saber". A fresh "Posted out · now SANS" line follows.
- It would show "Ranger's Post out date moved to Fri 17 Jul. · Saber", then the fresh "Posted out · now SANS" line.
- Recommendation: keep both. The first says what Saber changed, the second says the posting has happened.

**7. What should a tap on "no longer a public holiday" open?**
- The case: Hex, an admin, reads "Public holiday · Mon 10 Aug / 3 inputs need an OIL answer". Saber then undoes the holiday.
- Today that line just vanishes. A tap on the original line opened the OIL list for the holiday's dates: every input on them still waiting for an OIL answer, each with Yes and No. [checked: the list covers all the holiday's dates, not one day]
- It would show "Mon 10 Aug is no longer a public holiday. · Saber". The list it used to open is now empty. [checked: true of weekdays; inputs on a weekend inside a longer holiday still need an answer]
- Recommendation: a tap opens nothing and only marks the line as read, as a line about a deleted person does. It is the one line that would not open what the original opened.

**8. When one change did two things to an input, what should the line say?**
- The case: in one go Saber moves Training from Wed 15 Jul to Thu 16 Jul and adds Hex to it. Ranger reads the line. Saber presses Undo.
- Today the line just vanishes (question 3).
- It would show: "Training moved back to Wed 15 Jul, 09:00–10:00. · Saber", and under it "Hex is no longer in it."
- Recommendation: the first change as the title and the second as the sentence. Today's lines already name only the first two changes, so this keeps that rule.

1. A Save that can never work

Hunter is an admin of 142 and also plans 149. He picks 149, changes a chart on 149's Tracker and does not save it. He switches to the member view. Then he picks 142.

Today
The app asks Save them before switching to 142? He presses Save them. The app answers This change could not be saved. and does not say why. The reason: the edits are on 149's Tracker, and in the member view he counts only as a member of 142, so this Save is refused every time. (A member saving a chart on his own squadron's Tracker works as always.) His edits are kept and he stays where he was. Back in the admin view, red words sit beside ✓ Save changes, cut off to Th… on a desktop and This cha… at iPhone size. They stay until he saves, reloads or picks another squadron.
After
In the member view the box does not offer Save them. It says the edits are saved in the admin view, and its third button is Back to the admin view, the words the app already uses for that switch. The button returns him to 149's Tracker in the admin view with his edits still there, where ✓ Save changes works. Stay and Discard them do what they do today. The refusal and the red cut-off words no longer appear in this case.

Logout asks the same question today and gets the same change.

Recommended: yes. A button that cannot work should not be offered. The new line follows the Member view. band you chose on 23 Nov for a window held in the member view.

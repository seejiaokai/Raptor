The new words, on the real screens

Nothing in the app has changed yet. The red rings only mark where to look. Tap any picture to see it larger.

Six to approve. I recommend approving all six.
  1. Admin → Users filter: a "Plans 149" tick finds your people who plan another squadron.
  2. Request access: the Squadron box starts on "Pick…".
  3. Undo of a master admin: says which change and who.
  4. Undo of one Save that changed two things: names both.
  5. Hunter's bell: tells him when his Plan of 149 ended.
  6. A window left open in the member view: says what to do.

1. The filter on Admin → Users

Hunter is an admin of 142 and also holds Plan of 149.

Today
142's filter has a "Plans 142" tick that finds nobody. No tick finds Hunter.
New
One tick for each squadron somebody on the list plans. "Plans 149" finds Hunter.

2. Request access

Kite is new and belongs to 149.

Today
The Squadron box already says 142. If Kite doesn't look, the request goes to 142's admins and 149's never see it.
New
It starts on "Pick…". Pressing Request access without choosing shows "Pick your squadron", and nothing is sent.

3. Undo of a master admin

Saber makes Hunter a master admin, then presses Undo.

Today
"Undid: the master admin mark", with no name, and the same words in both directions.
New
"Undid: Made a master admin · Hunter" or "Undid: Master admin taken off · Hunter".

4. Undo of one Save that changed two things

Saber changes Hunter's sign-in and gives him Plan of 149 in one Save. One Undo takes both back.

Today
"Undid: Plan of 149 given · Hunter". The sign-in went back too, and nothing says so.
New
"Undid: Plan of 149 given, sign-in name changed · Hunter". It fits on one line on a phone.

5. Hunter's bell

Hunter holds Plan of 149. Saber makes him a master admin, which ends the Plan, and later takes master admin off. The Plan does not come back.

Today
His bell never says the Plan ended. Its last word on 149 is still "Given Plan of 149".
New
The two master admin lines say what they mean, and a new line says "Plan of 149 ended: master admin covers every squadron · Saber". View of a squadron ends the same way, in the same words.

6. A window left open in the member view

An admin has a Leave War or Input window open and switches to the member view.

Today
The window says "This change could not be saved." although nothing was tried.
New
"Member view. Switch back to the admin view to change this, or close it." The window is greyed, and Close and ✕ still work.

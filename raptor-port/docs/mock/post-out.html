1 · On the Leave War, you say what the posting is

D229 · D294 · D298

Tap a day on his row, then PO. After the date, the one "Archive on PO date" switch becomes four short choices — Overseas Sqn · Delete · SANS · Transfer to Sqn (the last one later) — with one line saying what the chosen one does on the date. Nothing happens until that day.

2 · Overseas: archived, and his account suspended

D280 · D285

On Admin → Users his account reads suspended. Its buttons are Enable and Delete account (today's "Switch off / on" in your words). If he tries to sign in, he's told his access is suspended.

3 · He's back: restore him, and the app asks you to check his quals

D284

His quals and CAT are kept exactly as he left them. When you restore him on Quals (and enable his account), a line asks you to check them, with a button straight to his row. It changes nothing by itself.

4 · Another workplace, still flies with us: he becomes SANS

D283

With Show SANS off, the Leave War still shows him in his old place, posted out from the date and no longer tracked. With Show SANS on, from that day he sits in the SANS group and his leave is tracked there.

5 · Leaving flying for good: deleted — account and person

D287 · D290 · D297

Delete asks twice and says what goes. Afterwards he's on no list and his callsign is free. Every day he already flew keeps his puck, published or not; days still to come lose him. Underneath, his record is kept as a hidden "deleted" mark, never erased.

6 · Names: an archived man's callsign can go to someone new

D286 · D295

When someone asks for access with a callsign only an archived man holds, Approve opens on New person with a line saying so. An archived man can be renamed right on the archived list; and if you restore him while his callsign is in use, Restore asks for another callsign on the spot — one step, no juggling.

7 · Your member view: tap your name badge

D292

Tap SABER · ADMIN and it becomes SABER · MEMBER: Edit Schedule and Admin go, and you can change only your own row and inputs, exactly as a member. Tap again to switch back; every sign-in starts as admin. On a phone the switch is in the menu, under your name.

8 · Guest flyers from another community — a mark on the puck

later · D288 · D289 · D296

A puck is a fixed width and already shows only about 9–10 letters, so a guest's mark can't be text. Three ways to mark him without taking a letter, including next to a warning chip. Wherever there's room (lists, pickers, a tap or hover) he reads in full: Viper · F-16.

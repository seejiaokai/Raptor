Two questions about an input's window, drawn on the real app before anything is built. Every picture is the app as it runs today; the parts drawn in are named under each picture. The dashed orange box only marks the part the question is about. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Already decided, not a question (your D917): a new input opens on OL. The pictures in question 2 show OL picked, as it will open.

1 · The pencil beside the title

Which pencil? P1 inside the title box, at its right end, or P2 as a small button beside the box.

My pick: P1. It takes no room: the box keeps its full width and nothing moves. A tap anywhere on the box, the pencil included, puts you in it with the kind's name ready to type over, as a tap on the box does today. P2 is a second button that does the same thing as the box, and it makes the box about 40 px shorter.

Either way: the pencil shows wherever the title box shows (an Event, a Training, a Meeting and the other commitment kinds, never leave or medical), on a new input and a saved one, and not on someone else's input that you can only read. It is a line drawing in the app's own icon style and grey, so it does not look like an emoji on your iPhone. A long title stops short of the pencil and scrolls inside the box, as it does today.

2 · Your idea: time and remarks above the calendar

Do you want A? In an input's window, When and Remarks move above the calendar (your first idea, D913). Nothing else changes and the calendar keeps showing.

My pick: yes, A, for every kind of input and every way the window opens. Most new inputs already have their dates, picked on the Inputs calendar, so the calendar is the part you have finished. For a commitment (an Event, a Training) the window is too tall for a phone today: Remarks sits under the Cancel / Add bar and you have to scroll to find it. With A, everything you still have to fill in is on screen, and the calendar is below it. For OL the window fits on the phone either way, so A changes little there.

What A costs: the line that repeats the dates ("Oct 15 → Oct 16") moves to the foot with the calendar. With "+ Input" on the Inputs list, where no dates are picked yet, you fill in the time before the dates. The calendar is still in full view below, and Add still refuses an input with no dates.

What the blink is

When you flick the grid up quickly, the dates row reaches the top bar and should stay pinned there. For one picture on screen (a sixtieth of a second) it is missing, and the next picture has it back. The browser moves the page before the app hears about the scroll, so today's app is always one picture late.

These are real pictures the test browser drew during a quick scroll, the same scroll in both builds. The pinned row's place is outlined in amber. Tap a picture to see it full size.

How to try it (questions 1 and 2)

  1. On your iPhone, open the app as usual, go to the Leave War, Grid tab, and scroll to the very top.
  2. Flick the grid up once, quickly, so the dates row runs into the top bar. Watch the strip just under the top bar. Do it three or four times.
  3. Do the same on Quals with its column heading.
  4. Then open the trial link above, sign in, and repeat. It opens with the demo data and touches nothing on the live app.

One reading to check again if it is built: on the phone-size Leave War, pictures that took just over a thirtieth of a second went from 6 to 20 in a long scroll, all right at that mark; the slowest were no slower. The test browser is the Chrome engine. Your iPhone's Safari draws differently, which is why only your iPhone can answer questions 1 and 2. The trial uses the same browser feature that already moves these rows sideways on your iPhone; where a browser lacks it, the app keeps today's way.

If you say build it (question 3)

The change is to how the pinned row is layered over the grid, so it is checked everywhere that layering shows:

  • the pinned names column, zoom by pinch, Rearrange, the month strip, and the windows that open over the grid;
  • the trial's loose ends: a screen reader must not read the hidden pinned row, a pinch must still hide it, and the pinned row must move if something above the grid changes height;
  • the full check: a walk of the Leave War and Quals with pictures, the everyday tasks end to end, and two separate code readers.

Nothing goes live without your "merge live".

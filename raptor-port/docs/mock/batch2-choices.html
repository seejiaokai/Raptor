The small choices on the Inputs pages

Each one shows the real screen as it is today, what is wrong with it, and what I recommend. Tap any picture to see it full size.

How to answer: reply “yes to all”, or give the numbers you want done differently. The numbers match the list I sent you in the chat.

Your find this morning — fixed, waiting for your look

✓

The dates button lit up and no calendar appeared

What was wrongOn a phone the calendar opened one screen’s height down the page, below the bottom of the screen. The button turned blue and nothing else seemed to happen.

FixedThe calendar now opens straight under the row of buttons. A finger can pick two dates and press “All dates”. Checked at three phone heights, as an admin and as a member.

The choices

1

A passing note covers the buttons at the foot of a window

What happens nowOn a phone, notes such as “OIL answer saved” or “Input updated” appear at the bottom of the screen. That is exactly where an open window keeps its buttons, so for a few seconds the note sits on top of them.

RecommendedOn a phone, while a window is open, show the note at the top of the screen instead.

2

A filtered day says it has no inputs when it does

What happens nowWith a filter on (here: one person), a day that has inputs for other people opens saying “No inputs on this day. Tap + Input to add one.” Nothing tells you the filter is hiding them.

RecommendedSay “No inputs match on this day.” with a “Clear filters” button beside it.

3

The dates calendar stays open after the second date

What happens nowOn the List, after you tap a start date and an end date, the calendar stays up. It closes when you tap outside it or press one of its two quick buttons.

RecommendedLeave it open. You can see the range you chose and still press “Next 2 weeks” or “All dates”. A tap outside closes it. (Escape will close it too — that part needs no choice.)

4

A shared input’s OIL line shows only the first man’s answer

What happens nowAn input for three people says “credited on its non-working day” — the first man’s answer. One of the three has since answered No for himself, and the main line still reads as if everyone is credited.

RecommendedHave the line count them: “credited for 2 of 3 — Ranger: no”.

5

Taking a request off the programme says only “Accept undone”

What happens nowWhen a scheduler takes a member’s request off the programme, the note reads “Accept undone”. It does not say whose request or which one. Accepting it says the full thing: “Ranger’s Sports day added to the ground programme”.

Recommended“Ranger’s Sports day taken off the programme”.

6

A member filing for several people could not leave himself out

No choice neededI tried this on today’s build to take its picture, and it already works: with another man picked, a member can tap his own puck off. I am taking this one off the list.

7

An old OIL answer stays on record after the input’s dates move

What happens nowAn input covers a Saturday and its OIL question is answered. The input is then moved to weekdays. The Saturday answer stays stored with the input. Nothing on any screen shows it and no OIL is credited for it. If the input is moved back onto that Saturday, the old answer stands again. There is no picture because nothing on screen changes.

RecommendedLeave it as it is.

8

With “file for other people” switched off, the filer gets a muddled message

What happens nowRanger filed an input for himself and Echo. An admin then switched off members filing for other people. Ranger opens his input and reads “Only Ranger — who filed it — or an admin can change this for everyone.” He is Ranger, and he cannot. He is also offered “Take me out”.

Recommended“Filing for other people is switched off — an admin can change this.”

9

Escape while typing a note closes the whole day

What happens nowIn a day opened on the Inputs calendar, you press “+ Note” and start typing. Pressing Escape to give up on the note closes the whole day window, not just the note box.

RecommendedEscape leaves the note box only. A second Escape closes the day.

10

The two-documents viewer cuts off its own buttons

What happens nowOn a laptop-sized screen, an input with two documents opens a viewer slightly taller than its box. “Edit input” and “Close” sit half hidden on its bottom edge until you scroll inside it.

RecommendedKeep the two buttons pinned in sight at the foot, the way an input’s own window keeps Cancel and Save.

11

Dragging across several days opens the window at once

What happens nowOn the month, dragging across 7, 8 and 9 July opens “New input · Jul 7 → Jul 9” the moment you let go. The original drawing had a small bar to confirm the days first.

RecommendedKeep it as built. It is one step shorter, and the window shows the dates so a wrong drag is plain to see and Cancel undoes it.

New — seen while fixing your find

12

The days in the List’s dates calendar are small for a finger

What happens nowYou had the days in an input’s window made bigger on a phone. The List’s own dates calendar was not part of that, so its days are still small and it uses about half the width it has.

RecommendedMake its days the same finger size as the window’s, filling the width. I have not changed it, because the size of the app’s buttons stays as it is without your word.

1His bell when what he filed doesn't match what he's planned on

When this happens

Ranger is out of the office and files his SANS availability on his phone, ticking Fly, OFT or AMT for a day. Saber plans him separately, often days later, and sometimes on something he didn't offer. It happens a few times a month, and only for SANS men.

What the app does today

  • Before he files, his bell reminds him to file for the day (picture 1).
  • Once he has filed anything for that day, the reminder goes. His bell says nothing more, even when what he filed doesn't match the plan (picture 2).
  • Saber sees the mismatch on the crew list and the warning list.

3The amber line for nothing filed, and the “S”

When this happens

Saber puts Ranger on a Sat 18 flight (VL BFM, take-off 13:00) and Ranger has filed nothing for that day. It's common in the days after he's posted out as SANS, before he gets to his filing.

What the app does today

  • The crew list strikes him (picture 16).
  • The day's warning list has nothing about him (picture 17), and his puck on the flight has no amber ring (picture 19).
  • When he has filed and it doesn't match, his puck turns amber with an “A”, the same letter used for “advisory: shift + ground” and for a CAT A man's own chip (picture 21).

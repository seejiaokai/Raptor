What the fix does

On the edit week and the board, a change that hasn’t gone out yet gets a dotted line around the puck, in the next amendment’s colour. The warning rings are drawn in the same place — red, and the thinner amber (an advisory) and grey (a note) — so the dotted line wins and the ring disappears. With the fix, a warning ring always keeps the puck’s edge, and the waiting change shows as a small hollow tag in the corner — the corner where a published change already carries its solid tag. A puck with no warning keeps today’s dotted line.

Why not the first idea

The first mock-up moved the dotted line out onto the seat around the puck. Enlarged, both marks show. At real size, Outlaw’s dotted red ring and the dotted line sit half a pixel apart and blur into one thick ring, and the line runs into Relay’s puck beside him — the two pucks of a crew sit 3 pixels apart. Moving it further out makes that worse.

Also found

The board draws a sanctioned late show as a plain solid ring, and never draws the dotted “this day causes tomorrow’s breach” ring — the edit week draws both. The board has always worked this way; the fix doesn’t change it. Filed as a question for you.

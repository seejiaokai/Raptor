What the fix does

Today a changed puck gets a ring in its amendment’s colour — solid once it has gone out, dotted while it waits. That ring sits exactly where the warning rings sit, so it can cover one: checked in the app, the green AL3 ring on Tally covers her amber advisory, and a waiting change’s dots hide the dashed and dotted red rings. With the fix, a change is only its tag in the corner — solid once out, hollow while waiting — and the edge of a puck carries nothing but warnings. Times and remarks keep their marks as they are.

The trade: a change is quieter — a small tag rather than a ring round the whole puck.

Why not the first idea

The first mock-up moved the dotted line out onto the seat around the puck. Enlarged, both marks show. At real size, Outlaw’s dotted red ring and the dotted line sit half a pixel apart and blur into one thick ring, and the line runs into Relay’s puck beside him — the two pucks of a crew sit 3 pixels apart. Your way removes the problem instead of moving it.

Also found

The board draws a sanctioned late show as a plain solid ring, and never draws the dotted “this day causes tomorrow’s breach” ring — the edit week draws both. The board has always worked this way; the fix doesn’t change it. Filed as a question for you.

Question 3

"Lock for members" off and on: approve as drawn?

One switch for everyone, on the page, an admin's only (D1247). On a desktop it is round 14's switch: off (1), members may type their own row; on (2), they read only. Worked example: you turn it on after setting everyone's balances in January, and Ranger's boxes become plain figures with "Locked by admin". On a phone the switch is the padlock button. Round 16 drew only its "on" look, so the "off" look is new: an open padlock, unlit (1); on, a closed padlock lit in cyan (2).

Question 4 (your question)

On 1 Jan, do CCL, FCL, PL, EL and CL keep their figures?

LVE rolls over by itself on 1 Jan (D1235): Ranger's 15 left on 31 Dec, of which 2 carried forward, lose those 2, keep 13 as carried forward and gain his grade's 36, so 49. You have not ruled on the five other kinds. Recommended: A, they keep their figures and the new year's amount is typed from the official leave app, by you or by the member while unlocked, because each person's amount differs (childcare leave depends on his own case) and the app cannot know it. B sets them back to 0 on 1 Jan, so a stale figure never shows, but every one of them must then be typed again.

What changed from round 14, and why

  1. It opens from its own Balances tab (your round 19 tabs, D1254), so round 14's "‹ Grid" button goes.
  2. No year picker on the page. These are today's balances, and "Days taken" counts the period picked in View at the top (JAN – DEC 26), as Approvals does, so two year controls can never disagree. My change, for your yes or no.
  3. App bal comes down by itself as leave is approved (D1479), so it reads 15, not round 14's 17.
  4. Pending figures are amber (D1251).
  5. New: a pending CCL, FCL, PL, EL or CL day shows in amber right after its box (D1251 says every pending figure on this page is amber; round 14 had no place for one). Each box keeps its column whether or not something is pending.
  6. Each name is the schedule's own puck, the size the Approvals list uses (D1468, D1476).

Also gone: the "No grade 2" chip (D1249). Halves read .5 (D1246). Unchanged from round 14: the grade box with "+36" (D1233), the LVE and other-leave columns, OIL read only with › (D1255), "Grades…", "Lock for members" (D1247) and "Find a person".

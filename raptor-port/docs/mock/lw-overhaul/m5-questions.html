Leave War M5a: the build plan, two questions

M5a's build plan is written from your approved drawings (D1479 to D1489). It builds the person window, the Balances tab, the lock, and the leave year exactly as drawn. Two things in it are not ruled yet. Nothing else waits on them.

Look at now

  1. A grade changed after 1 Jan: from the next 1 Jan, or back to this one? Recommended: from the next 1 Jan.
  2. The grid's "drag down the LVE figures to give days" bar: keep it or let it go? Recommended: let it go.

Question 1

A person's grade is changed after 1 Jan. When does the new grade count?

Worked example: Recon is a Junior pilot (+33) on 1 Jan 2027, so 33 days are added to his LVE that day. On 1 Mar 2027 you make him a Senior pilot (+36).

A, recommended: from the next 1 Jan

His 2027 figures stay as they are. On 1 Jan 2028, 36 days are added. The same if you change a grade's "+ days" in the Grades window during the year: the new figure counts from the next 1 Jan.

One cost: a wrong grade put right after 1 Jan (on 3 Jan, say) also waits for the next 1 Jan. That year's figure is put right by typing his App bal.

B: back to this 1 Jan

The change reaches back, so on 1 Mar his App bal jumps up by 3 days for 2027.

Question 2

The bar that gives several people extra LVE at once: keep it?

Today an admin can drag down the LVE figures beside the names, and a bar comes up: "3 people · +LVE · days · Save". You asked for its blue ring on 6 Sep. No ruling says whether it stays, now that App bal is typed from the official leave app and comes down by itself (D1479).

A, recommended: let it go

An extra day granted officially already shows in the official figure you type. With one way to change a balance (its box on the Balances page, where every row is a box), the figures have fewer places to disagree.

B: keep it

It stays on the grid, and the days it gives are added to App bal from the day given.

Built as recommended, yours to strike

Readings in the plan

Each follows from your rulings and drawings. Strike any you disagree with. None holds up the build.

  1. CCL, FCL, PL, EL and CL come down by themselves as their leave is approved, the same as App bal.
  2. Leave already approved when App bal was typed takes nothing off. A leave lengthened later takes off only its new days. A deleted one gives its days back.
  3. A balance changes only three ways: its box, approved leave, and 1 Jan.
  4. A person whose figures were never typed counts from 0 at the start of the war's first year. Each 1 Jan since adds his grade's days, and his approved leave comes off.
  5. "3 taken" is his approved LVE days from 1 Jan up to today. Days taken counts approved days in the View period up to today.
  6. The person window lists his leave (LL, OL, OIL taken, CCL, FCL, PL, EL, CL). Medical, a course, an overseas duty and an Other stay on the grid. MED's tile still counts medical days.
  7. "New since your last visit" means a change someone else made to his leave. His own changes are never dotted. The dots go when he closes his own window.
  8. Every balance change is a line in the change history, for example "LVE balance 18 → 20 · Ranger". His bell is not told when an admin changes his balance (written down as a proposal only).
  9. The Balances page lists the people on the grid, in the grid's order. A SANS man while SANS is off the war is not listed. Nor is an archived man.
  10. A typed figure takes halves only (17, 17.5). A figure below zero may be typed.
  11. Deleting a grade makes its people "No grade" from that day. Past 1 Jans keep what they added.
  12. 1 Jan only adds days for a 1 Jan after the figure was last typed. A figure typed on 3 Jan already includes that year's days.
  13. On a phone the tabs keep their icons at your iPhone's 402 wide and drop them under 400.
  14. My leave's "pending" counts the days the way Approvals does, so weekends are not counted. Today it counts them, so the two can differ.
  15. A tap on the LVE figure beside a name opens his person window. Today it opens a breakdown of that one figure.
  16. OIL can no longer be typed anywhere. How an opening OIL balance from the old system is given at go-live is a question for later: as an award it would show on the grid on its date, and it could expire.
  17. Leave on a date no leave period covers yet still comes off: weekdays count, Saturday and Sunday don't, no holidays known. Today such a date counts nothing. Creating the period later doesn't change leave that was approved before the figure was typed.
  18. Days given back in a year (a leave deleted or shortened) first cancel days taken that year. Any left over go back to this year's leave, never to carried forward. If those days had come from carried forward, the war can show up to that many days more than the official app after 1 Jan, until App bal is typed again.
  19. On 1 Jan, a pending day from the year just ended costs nothing more, because the carried-forward days it would use have ended. So it shows no pending figure on the tile, though the request still waits in his list. Example: 10 left (4 carried forward), 29–30 Dec pending. On 28 Dec: App bal 10, Pending −2, War bal 8. On 1 Jan: App bal 39 and War bal 39.
  20. Days taken counts leave the way the balances charge it (weekends free). Medical, a course and an overseas duty count as calendar days, so a two-week course reads 14.
  21. Your New Year example (D1473: 23 left, 3 carried forward, on leave 31 Dec to 4 Jan) reads 21, 52, 52 on 31 Dec, 1 Jan and after 4 Jan, not 22, 53, 52. Your later ruling (D1479) takes leave off when it is approved, not as the days pass. The end figure is the same.
  22. Leave for the last days of the old year, approved only after 1 Jan, still counts in the old year. If the old year's carried-forward days covered it, it costs nothing now.
  23. A tap on a name on the Balances page opens his person window, as every tap on a person on the Leave War does (D1148). This wasn't drawn.
  24. A pilot's bid that stretches his approved leave past 15 days in a row makes the weekends count. His tile's Pending shows those weekend days. My leave and Approvals count only the bid's own days. Example: 1–12 Feb approved, 13–21 Feb bid. The tile shows Pending −11; Approvals shows 5. This is the one place the two can differ.

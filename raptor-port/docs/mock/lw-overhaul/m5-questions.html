Question 1 · from your D1472 ("All as recommended", 28 Oct)

Which leave comes off App bal to make War bal?

The one fact everything depends on: App bal is the number he copies from the official leave app. The official app takes leave off on the day he applies for it there, not when the leave day arrives. So the number he copies already has every leave he has applied for taken off, and the Leave War should take off only leave the official app does not know about yet.

DateWhat happensOfficial app balance
2 JanBids leave for 19–21 Jan on the Leave War. It's approved.20
3 JanApplies for 19–21 Jan in the official app.17
5 JanTypes 17 into the Leave War as his App bal.17
6 JanBids leave for 26–27 Jan on the Leave War (approved later). Not yet applied in the official app.17
laterBids 2.5 more days on the Leave War. Still pending.17

What War bal should be: the 17 already has 19–21 Jan off, so the Leave War should take off only 26–27 Jan (2) and the 2.5 pending: 17 − 2 − 2.5 = 12.5.

The question: how does the app know 19–21 Jan is already inside the 17?

A · By when the leave was put on the Leave War (recommended)

19–21 Jan was put on 2 Jan, before he typed 17 on 5 Jan, so it is already counted: skip it. 26–27 Jan was put on 6 Jan, after: take it off.

War bal = 17 − 2 − 2.5 = 12.5. Correct.

Its one weak spot: a leave he bid on the Leave War before typing App bal, but had not yet applied for in the official app, is skipped too. War bal reads too high until he applies for it there and types App bal again, which starts the count afresh, as you ruled.

B · By the leave's own dates

19–21 Jan falls after 5 Jan, so it comes off. So does 26–27 Jan.

War bal = 17 − 3 − 2 − 2.5 = 9.5. Wrong: 19–21 Jan is taken off twice, once by the official app and once by the Leave War.

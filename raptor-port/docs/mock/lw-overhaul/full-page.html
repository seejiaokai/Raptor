EIs there a better way to show the events? your question

Yes: one thin bar per event, across the days it runs (B). I drew three ways on the same busy squadron: three events on Tue 6 Jan, events running several days (FAM 5–7 Jan, a no-leave week 26–30 Jan, an exercise 9–13 Feb), and one typed as a name only (Sports Day, shown as SD). Manning is folded on the phone (G4); months, then dates (G1, G2). Names are made up.

What an event already shows without any tag: its kind colours its whole date column: a public holiday green, an off day grey, a no-leave day orange (26–30 Jan in the pictures). So the events that change leave are seen at a glance in every way below. What a tag adds is the other events: an exercise, a FAM week, a sim day.

  • B · An events lane. Each event is one bar across its days, in its kind's colour. Where the bar has room its name is written in ("NL No leave", "EX Exercise"), otherwise its short form. The app stacks the bars into as few lanes as the busiest stretch of the period needs: three here, because Tue 6 Jan has three; a quiet period needs one. Nobody picks "Event 1, 2 or 3" any more: a new event goes in the first free lane, as the Holidays list already does. Every event, its days and its kind are on screen without a tap.recommended
  • A · Tags in the date, and a fold (G3 + G3b, round 20). The day's first event under its date, a number for more; "▸ Events" opens the three rows.
  • C · Leave events only. A tag only where an event changes leave (PH, OFF, NL); any other event is a small grey dot under its date; a tap on the date shows what it is.

Why B, checked against your questions today:

  • "Hard to view with six counters and three events?" B costs 23px more than A shut and 30px less than A open, and shows everything A open shows.
  • "How do I see all of a day's events?" On the grid, straight down from the date: Tue 6 Jan shows FAM, OPS and SIM. A tap on the date still opens the day panel with all of them at its top.
  • "On the phone, no way to bring out the events?" Nothing to bring out: they are always there, three lanes at most here.
  • An event of several days reads as one event: FAM is one bar from Mon 5 to Wed 7. In A it is written on three dates, and on Tue 6 its tag hides OPS, the day's own working event, behind "3". (Round 20's words said two events would show one under the other; its picture showed a number. The picture is what was drawn.)
  • A name only: Sports Day shows "SD" in its bar, made from the name, as today.
  • C is the shortest, but on a phone you cannot tell an exercise from a FAM week without tapping each date: the same problem as your fold question.

How B is used. A tap on a bar opens that event (its name, kind and days, with Edit), as a tap on an event does today; a tap on an empty spot in the lane adds an event on that date; a tap on the date opens the day. The lanes scroll away with the grid like today's Event rows; the date heading that stays at the top keeps each column's colour. The redesigned Settings' "Event rows" line goes, since the app makes the lanes. Your colleague's app draws its events as bars in lanes too (point 18 of round 1's comparison); B keeps our short forms, kinds and colours. The Event window, its presets and short forms, and the planned Events list stay as they are. If you choose B: G3 and G3b are not built; G1, G2 and G4 are unchanged.

B3OIL on the balances page: which is the better design? your question

Read only, with a tap through to the person's OIL tracker. OIL is the one balance the app works out itself: it is earned from the published schedule and awarded by an admin with a reason (D1209, D400), so every day of it has a record behind it. A typed box would put in a figure with no record: the OIL tracker and the balance would then disagree, and nobody could tell which is right. Typing is for the leave kinds the official leave app holds, which the Leave War only copies.

Your question: the same order as the OIL tracker and the Leave War? Yes. The balances page lists everyone in the grid's order, group by group, and the OIL tracker already does: both read the one order the grid uses, so a Rearrange on the grid moves all three together (checked in the app's code).

The one case a box would serve, an opening OIL balance when the squadron starts, is covered without one: it is given once in the OIL tracker as an award, "Opening balance from the old system", so it has its record too.

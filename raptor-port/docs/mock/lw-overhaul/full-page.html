5The stages go, and what takes each one's place your point 17

Your question: which is more intuitive, saying what is open (as your app does today) or saying what is locked (as Gryphon does)? My answer: say what is open. You open about three months at a time, so one line, "Open for bidding: 1 Apr – 30 Jun", says it; moving on to July is changing that one line. Saying what is locked takes two lines (before and after the open stretch), and both must change each time. A new period also starts with nothing open, so nobody bids early by mistake. The stages (draft, bidding, closed, published) still go: the open dates are the only switch. The grid keeps today's green outline round the open dates, and a closed day its quiet dim with a small lock.

Recommended: they go. A member sees every request's state live, and a locked stretch's reason in the day panel. No separate "being bid / being approved" label: the lock's reason says it.

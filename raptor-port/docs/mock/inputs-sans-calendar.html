Where the room comes from. Stretching the month to the foot of the screen gives one more input a day. Thinner bars give two more. The slimmer tools row gives almost nothing: 8 points over five weeks, under 2 a week row.

An input for a group of people

Your hybrid. These are the real “+ Input” window from the app, with only the person part drawn in.

Decided with it: a member can do this for duties and commitments only. It is one shared input for the group. The man himself, whoever filed it, and an admin can change it.

To check on your phone when built: the pucks are small to press at this size. If they are hard to hit, they go one step larger.

Decided, with no picture of its own

  • Windows. Every window of this job can be dragged, and the page behind it still works. A click outside does not close it. On the Leave War this also covers the panel for picked people’s days.
  • Still needed. Required, less those the Leave War shows available, less the SANS committed to fly. Worked for pilots and WSOs apart. “Available” never counts a SANS man.
  • A no-fly day needs nobody to fly and reads 0, but still shows and takes OFT and AMT.
  • A public holiday or an Off day shows its tag in place of the day button, and takes no required figure unless you type one on it.
  • The late cut-off. Each calendar has its own, behind its gear. The Logic page lists both and opens the same setting. A cut-off is the end of its day.
  • No list for SANS, and no “SANS needed” row on the Leave War.
  • In the check of the build, each picture on this page is set beside the built screen at the same size.

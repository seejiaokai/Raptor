OIL for an input with several people

You asked two things: can the person filing choose who gets OIL, and what would a man see if he may answer for himself. Here are the real screens of today, and the two other ways drawn on those same screens. Tap any picture to see it full size.

The example: Saber files a Training for Ace, Drifter, Ranger and himself on Saturday 17 Oct, 09:00 to 12:00. Then Ranger opens it on his own phone.

Your first question

?

Can the person filing choose who gets OIL?

TodayNo. He is asked once, Yes or No, and the answer is written for everyone in the input. He cannot leave one person out there.

How one person is left out todayTwo ways. The man changes his own answer afterwards (way A below). Or the scheduler switches him off for that day in OIL Earn.

The three ways

A

A man may answer for himself — as built today

What Ranger seesHe opens the Training. He cannot change it, but at the foot he has his own line, “Your OIL”, with a Change button. It asks him the same Yes or No, for himself only.

What Saber sees afterwards“credited for 3 of 4 — Ranger: no”.

The costTwo people can change who earns: the filer for everyone, and each man for himself. If the filer answers again, every man’s own answer is overwritten. An ALL AVAIL in the input cannot work this way, so the input has two behaviours.

B

The person filing chooses who earns — your idea

What Saber seesThe same question, and under it the input’s people. A tap on a name leaves that person out. He can open it again later with Change.

What Ranger seesThe input’s OIL line and nothing to change — the same as way C. He asks Saber, or takes himself out.

RecommendedThis one. Whoever filed it decides, once, in one place, and can see who is in and who is out. The scheduler can still switch any one person on a day in OIL Earn. With ALL AVAIL in the input the row shows the named people and “ALL AVAIL”, and the people behind it follow the answer.

C

One answer for everyone, nothing more

What Saber seesToday’s question, Yes or No for all. No list of people.

What Ranger seesThe input’s OIL line, nothing to change.

The costTo leave one person out, the scheduler must switch him off in OIL Earn on the day. The filer cannot do it when he files.

The pictures marked “today” are the app as it stands, taken on a phone-sized screen on 11 Oct 26. The drawings are those same screens with the change drawn in; nothing is built.

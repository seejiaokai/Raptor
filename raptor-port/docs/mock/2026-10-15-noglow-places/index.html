Question 4 · the guide's wording

What the design guide will say

In plain words, the new point says:

  • No mark in this app glows. A ring, edge, stripe, box, line, panel or dot that says something about a thing is drawn as it is, in its colour, with no soft light round it. That covers every place in all three rounds.
  • A pale band of colour round a mark counts as a glow too, blurred or not.
  • The Rearrange button and the Show SANS switch show "on" with the filter chip's pale blue fill. A new on/off button takes the same look unless you say otherwise. The app's other "on" looks (Fast sync, the week buttons, OIL Earn, the bell) stay as they are.
  • A dark shadow that lifts a window, a menu or a dragged thing is depth, not a glow, and stays.
  • The app's own sign is not a mark: the logo and the page's faint wash keep their light.
  • A new glow on a mark is yours to rule, never added without your word.

The guide's front list gains one line: "no mark glows". Its colour table gains the pale blue "on" look under cyan. The other documents that still describe the old glows are corrected in the same change. Then the places themselves are built on the same branch, checked on screen (the SANS calendar's pucks included) and read by a fresh reader before you say "merge live".

The exact wording, as it will go into the guide

No mark in this app glows (owner D164, D860, D877, D879, D880, D881, D882 and this round, 24 Sep and 15 Oct 26). A mark the app draws to say something about a thing (a ring round a thing picked, highlighted, just added or just landed, the crew a tapped warning lights, a puck that earns OIL, his own puck or row, a seat a man can take, an event he can fly next or the one a search found, the stripe that says SANS, the box round the days open for bidding, the line where a dragged row will land, today's day card, a panel that pops up, a status dot) is a line, edge or fill drawn as it is, its colours and a stripe's shading included, with no soft light spreading out from it or into it. Its colour, its width and whether it is solid, dashed or dotted carry the meaning; a glow adds none and blurs the edge it marks. A shadow in the mark's own colour is a glow, and so is a pale, see-through band of its colour round it, blurred or not (a halo); a solid ring drawn as a shadow is a line. The Rearrange button and the Show SANS switch show that they are on by the filter chip's "on" look (a pale cyan fill, cyan words and a cyan edge), never by a glow or a halo; a new on/off control takes the same look unless he rules otherwise, and the app's other "on" looks stay as they are. A dark shadow that lifts a thing off the page (a window, a menu, a thing being dragged) is depth, not a glow, and stays. The app's own sign is not a mark: the claw logo on the sign-in page keeps its light, and so does the faint wash at the top of a page. A new glow on a mark is his to rule, never added without his word. (Followed by which of his rulings each place comes from, and which parts began as my reading and were approved by you.)

Approve the wording? My pick: yes, with your answers to questions 1 to 3 as recommended. If you keep any place, the wording names it as your exception.

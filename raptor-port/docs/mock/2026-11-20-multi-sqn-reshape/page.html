1 · A squadron meeting when its filer moves (Q1)

Example. Hunter, 142's admin, files a squadron meeting for all of 142 (ALL AVAIL) on Fri 24 Jul. Later Hunter moves to 149, counting from Thu 16 Jul. Today the meeting leaves 142's schedule and appears on 149's, because the app follows the man who filed it.

Recommended: it stays on 142's schedule, where it was filed. A squadron's meeting is that squadron's business. It also means a master admin, or an admin with Plan of another squadron, who files a meeting while looking at another squadron's schedule files it there, not into his own.

Until you answer: as today.

2 · Sign-off boxes for a day after a man's move (Q2)

Example. On Wed 15 Jul Hunter opens 142's week of Mon 20 Jul. Ranger moves to 149 from Thu 16 Jul. Today Mon 20 Jul's sign-off boxes still offer Ranger, while the crew list for the same day already leaves him out.

Recommended: the boxes offer only that day's 142 people, the same as the crew list beside them.

Until you answer: as today.

4 · When a moved man loses his access to other squadrons (Q4)

Example. Ranger is 142's and holds Plan of 150: full admin of 150. On Wed 15 Jul at 10:00 Hunter saves Ranger's move to 149, counting from Thu 16 Jul.

  • Recommended: his 150 access ends at the start of Thursday, the day his move counts. For the rest of Wednesday he can still change 150's schedule, people and leave. A transfer saved months ahead works the same way: his access ends on the transfer's date, as the transfer sheet you approved says for his old squadron.
  • The other way: it ends at 10:00 on Wednesday, the moment the move is saved, while he is still 142's for the rest of the day; and a transfer saved months ahead would take his access away months early.

Why I recommend it: it ends on the day the move counts, like everything else about a move. If a move is taken back the same day, his access simply stays, so I no longer need to ask you what should happen then. It also leaves fewer places for a fault.

Nobody can see either way today: no screen saves a move or gives View or Plan yet. If you have not answered when this part is built, it is built as recommended and changed if you choose the other.

5 · The guest switch (Q5)

Example. Each squadron has its own "Guest view" switch on Admin. Kestrel, 149's admin, ticks 149's. Today that lets nobody in: a person waiting for access sees "Waiting for an admin". Only 142's switch works, and it always puts the person in 142's week.

Recommended: wait for the step where "Request access" asks which squadron. Then that squadron's switch decides, and the person lands in its week. A stop-gap now, one switch for the whole app or any squadron's switch letting him in, would let one squadron's admin let a guest into another squadron's week.

Until then: as today.

6 · A moved man's row on both Leave Wars (Q6, and Q3)

Today. Ranger moves 142 → 149 from Thu 16 Jul. From that day his whole row leaves 142's Leave War, his months there before the move included, and 149's Leave War counts him for the whole year, 1 to 15 Jul as well (6a).

Recommended (6b): on 142's Leave War his row stays as it is up to Wed 15 Jul, then shows the striped "not here" look the Leave War already draws, with no "PO" in it, because no posting was set. On 149's his row starts on Thu 16 Jul, striped before it, as for a man posted in. Admin → Users shows no "posting out" tag on him (6c). The plain stripes with no "PO" are new: today the stripes carry "PO" after a posting, and are blank only before a man's first day.

Leave already approved in 142's Leave War for a day after his move, say Mon 20 Jul, stays stored there, out of sight under the stripes. 149's Leave War does not have it until the transfer step carries it across. The schedule still shows the leave itself.

7 · The band at midnight (Q7)

Example. Ranger has a Leave War window open at 23:58 on Wed 15 Jul and has typed in it. His move to 149 counts from Thu 16 Jul. Today at midnight the window stays open and says nothing, while his row disappears from the grid under it (7b, today).

Recommended: one band across the window's top, and one button, "Close", which opens 149's Leave War. His typed words stay visible, so he can copy them; nothing is saved into either squadron, and nothing is dropped without a word. The same for a Tracker chart being edited at midnight.

You are 149's from today. This can't be saved in 142's Leave War.

You are 149's from today. This can't be saved in 142's Tracker.

On the iPhone the band takes two lines; on one line the words would be cut. Until you answer: the app waits for the window to close before moving him, without the band.

8 · Leave stretched across a man's move (Q8)

Example. Ranger is 149's from Thu 16 Jul. Hunter, 142's admin, files leave for Ranger on Tue 14 Jul, then stretches it to Fri 17 Jul and taps Add. For now only Ranger or an admin of both squadrons (a master admin, or an admin holding Plan of the other) may change leave that runs across his move (until the transfer step lets each squadron's admin change its own days, as you have ruled). Today the app says only "Blocked: nothing was kept", without saying why (8a).

Recommended: the window stays open and the note says why (8b). The same words show when Kestrel, 149's admin, changes that leave from 149's Inputs list. These words last until the transfer step changes who may edit such leave.

Not saved: Ranger is 149's from Thu 16 Jul. Only Ranger or an admin of both 142 and 149 can change leave that runs across his move.

The app's note shows in bold everything before a colon, so I recommend starting it with "Not saved:". The drawing has the sentence without it, which is why it is all bold there. Until you answer: today's general words.

Told to you: no answer needed

  • Who counts as a squadron's last admin. Where a squadron has admins of its own, only they count. Where it has none, someone from another squadron who holds Plan of it counts. So 150, run only by Hunter through Plan, keeps him as its admin.
  • A posting that waits for another admin can sit where nobody there can fix it. If Hunter (142) is 150's only admin through Plan and his own posting out is set, it waits until 150 has another admin.   • Linking a sign-in to another person takes away its access to other squadrons. If a sign-in is moved from Ranger to Viper, any View or Plan it held was Ranger's and does not go to Viper; Viper's squadron's admins, an admin with Plan of it, or a master admin give him what he needs. Undoing that move gives the sign-in back to Ranger without the access.

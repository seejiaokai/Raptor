Read-only. Sources: `C:\Users\User\projects\Raptor-msq` on `claude/msq-b1-screens-135` (pull request 826, NOT merged), its `.claude/decisions-full/*.md`, the area pages, `DECISIONS-ARCHIVE.md`, `OUTSTANDING.md`. I also read `origin/main`'s full rows (D2328–D2346, D2390–D2439, D2560–D2580 are on main and not on this branch; only D2332 and D2345 touch squadrons). I searched the 25 most recently pushed remote branches for squadron rulings not on main or this branch, and found none.

**How "built" was judged.** I counted the ruling's number in `raptor-port/src` on `origin/main` (live) and on this branch (HEAD). I also read each row's own "Not built" or "Design only" note, but a note can be older than a later build. Main has what pull request 806 brought (batch 1's first half and the reshape). The branch adds the Schedules menu, the person's access row, the bell's Show buttons and squadron mark, View-only tabs, the "All squadrons" filter and access-request taps. Borrowing, shared waves, detachments and Transfer (batches 2–4) are design only.

**One fact behind every "move" row.** The rules for a man's move (dated home squadron) are built and live on main: `state/units.ts setHome`, used by the tests. **No screen moves a man yet.** Admin → Users' only home choice is "Add a person"'s Home squadron, and the Transfer step is batch 4 (D2262's and D2263's rows say "no screen moves a man yet"). So every move row below is in the app's rules but cannot be reached on screen today.

Legend for the last column: **no** (a deliberate plan onto another squadron, or not a squadron's page, or already separate), **yes** (it shows one squadron's thing on another squadron's page with no deliberate plan), **unclear** (with the reason).

## What I was unsure of

1. **Who moves a squadron's place (row 43).** D2119 says the new place "takes effect the moment a master admin saves it";
   the code and `state/units.test.ts:228-237` let its home admin move it too. By D2241 a Plan holder may wherever a
   master admin may, so he is right either way; whether a plain home admin may is not plainly ruled.
2. **A shared place's time zone and a Plan holder (row 44).** D2196 names only a master admin for a place more than one
   squadron sits on. Hunter, 142's admin with Plan of 149, is an admin of every squadron at Home base. D2241 reads "for a
   squadron"; a shared place is several squadrons' — whether he may is a reading, not a ruling.
3. **A Plan grant held by a member account.** `setGrant` never asks whether the account is an admin
   (`state/units.test.ts:217` gives Osprey, a 149 member, `admin` in 142), and a demotion leaves an admin's Plan in
   place: Saber gives Hunter Plan of 149, a 142 admin then makes Hunter a member — Hunter keeps a level-`admin` grant in
   149, `levelFrom` reads him "admin" there, and made an admin again his Plan of 149 comes back with no master admin

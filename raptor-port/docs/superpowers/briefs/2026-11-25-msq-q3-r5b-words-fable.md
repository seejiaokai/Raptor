## What still has no words

**1. The settings note (E1)**
- a. **On a phone the settings window lies under the Calendar.** The note names a window he cannot see, and Save and Cancel are out of reach until the Calendar is closed. Either the refusal closes an unchanged Calendar so the named window shows (the host's call on behaviour), or the note needs a lead such as "close the Calendar, then save or cancel…".
- b. **The twin from the SANS page:** "Blocked: save or cancel SANS calendar settings, then pick 149." The Calendar opened from the Leave War has no settings window to name.
- c. **Both hold something:** a new holiday in the Calendar and changed settings beneath. One note at a time; the order is not fixed. The Calendar's should come first, since it is on top.
- d. **The other endings and the out-of-sight form:** "…then switch to the member view." and "…Inputs calendar settings on Inputs, then pick 149." follow the settled halves, but none is drawn.

**2. The Undo note (E7)**
- a. **The row, form and Retry leads do not join to this ending.** "Save or cancel the open row, then press Undo again" is untrue if he saves: the save becomes the newest step, and "Undo again" undoes that, not "+ New squadron". The same holds for "add or cancel the new person" and "finish or cancel giving access". For this ending, offer only the way that adds no step ("Blocked: cancel the open row, then press Undo again."), or word it afresh. The host should also check what Retry leaves on top of the Undo list.
- b. **Ctrl+Z:** "press Undo again" is near enough, but nobody has said so.
- c. **A window on a page out of sight:** "…the open window on the Leave War, then press Undo again." is not drawn.

**3. A man of another squadron (D5)**
- a. **The closed row gives no hint of who has no sign-in.** Most of the sixty have none, so Kestrel opens row after row to read "No sign-in". A quiet "no sign-in" on the closed row (the app's own lowercase words today) would save that, but D2625 lists what such a row shows, so it is his call.
- b. **Other rows with no words yet:** a suspended sign-in; a master admin of 142 (today's "master admin covers every squadron"); a member with a sign-in when Hunter, not Kestrel, opens him.
- c. **His own squadron's page:** does a 142 man's row there read "also views 149", as it reads "also plans 149"? The built line lists Plan only.
- d. **More than one squadron:** "also views 149, 150", in the built list's shape.

**4. The archived callsign (D8)**
- a. **"Add person" greyed until the question is answered has no reason.** Every greyed button on this page says why (D6, D11). Suggest, under the button: "Answer “Is this him?” first."
- b. **Archived men on several squadrons:** line 4 must name each: "A new Jester. 142's and 150's admins are told when you add him."
- c. **Kestrel is also an admin of 142, or the archived man is 149's own:** nobody else is told, so the line is "A new Jester." alone, or no line.
- d. **After the press:** does "If this is him, ask 142's admin" stay, and how does he take the answer back? Presumably retyping the callsign brings the question back; this is not stated.
- e. **The request path:** D2632 covers "a request or a new person". On an access request there is no "Add person", so "when you add him" needs a twin ("…when you approve him.").

**5. The sign-off box (D12)**
- Still open from the first review: whether the signed box and the published day carry "· 142", and what "Add" says with nobody picked.
- New: how a screen reader says the dot in a browser's own list. One check on his iPhone's VoiceOver.

Your "One page" (D1944): the scheduler's 334 rulings, one line each, turned into one page in the same shape as the Tracker and Inputs pages. It keeps your standing rules, what you like, and what a fresh AI would get wrong; a rule the app's tests fully check is listed by number only. Nothing is deleted, your words stay in the full record, and nothing is live until you say "merge live".

A flying standby line

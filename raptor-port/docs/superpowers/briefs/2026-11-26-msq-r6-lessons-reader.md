## Lesson 103 (D2644)

His words: "the cancel button is different in size and doesnt seemed to be aligned."

- **Keeps to his words? Mostly.** Size: his. "On one line" is a narrowing of "aligned": two buttons can sit on one
  line and still be out of alignment (tops or centres off by a few pixels), and that is just as much what he flagged.
  Suggest keeping his own word. "The app's own pair" is not in his words; it is lesson 68 (D1496) applied — fine to
  keep, but it should say so rather than read as new from this correction.
- **Readings marked?** Yes — "measured, never by eye" and "any button a drawing adds beside one the app already has"
  are marked. Both are fair widenings.
- **Contradicts / duplicates?** No contradiction. It overlaps lesson 68 (every part drawn the way the app draws it —
  sizes included — found on the running app), lesson 22's reading ("Save and Cancel too") and lesson 63's reading
  ("by measuring, not by eye"); consistent with lesson 53 (a row of buttons stays on one line) and §"A page's controls
  are one row". It narrows lesson 68 for a pair of buttons, so per the head rule it should say so, and lesson 68
  should carry a pointer to it. Line 29's "buttons keep their size" (D487) is about built controls — no clash.
- **Stretch?** Minor: "on one line" in place of "aligned".

Replacement wording:

> **Lesson 103 (26 Nov 26, D2644 — "…").** A button a mock-up draws beside another is the same size as it and aligned
> with it, as the app's own pair is. *(The agent's readings: it is lesson 68 applied to a pair of buttons; the pair is
> measured from the app, never copied by eye; it holds for any button a drawing adds beside one the app already has.)*

And at the end of lesson 68's reading: "; a pair of buttons, lesson 103; a list of people, lesson 104."

## Lesson 104 (D2645)

His words: "i dont remember my drop down of personnel design is so ugly."

- **Keeps to his words? Yes, with one ambiguity to mark.** "Never as the browser's plain list" fairly names what he
  called ugly. But "the app's own drop-down look" is not settled by his words. He says *my* drop-down of personnel —
  he may be remembering a specific people list in the app. The detail entry chose the top bar's squadron list as the
  model for the redraw. The app has a people drop-down of its own already: the SANS calendar's **Highlight** button
  opens a custom list of people's pucks ("Highlight one SANS person"; the Tracker's search list is another). That
  may be the one he remembers. Which look is "the app's own" is the agent's reading and should be marked as such, and
  the SANS calendar's list looked at before N10 goes to him.
- **Readings marked?** The built-app note is marked, and correctly keeps changing the built lists his to rule (N10).
- **Contradicts / duplicates?** It departs from lesson 68's reading — "each part is drawn the way the app already
  draws that kind of part… found on the running app". Today the app draws these lists as the browser's plain list, so
  round 5 obeyed lesson 68 and he corrected it. The two now pull apart; the lesson should say it narrows lesson 68,
  and lesson 68 point to it. It also meets lesson 68's other half: a drawing of a built screen that shows the app's
  own drop-down where the build has the browser's list is drawing a change, so it is named to him as one. Lesson 22's
  reading ("a person picker" the same everywhere) agrees with it. No other guide rule covers drop-downs.
- **Stretch?** Small: "a list of people to pick from" generalises from one box (PLANNED BY), but his "drop down of
  personnel" is itself general, so it is fair.

Replacement wording:

> **Lesson 104 (26 Nov 26, D2645 — "…").** A list of people to pick from is drawn in the app's own drop-down look,
> never as the browser's plain list. *(The agent's readings: it narrows lesson 68, which drew the app as it is — the

> built lists is his to rule (round 6's N10), not taken from this lesson.)*

## What is built

### `src/state/pick.ts`

`pickSquadron(u, opts?: GuardOpts)` runs the checks in §4.2's order. Written as one flow, in this order, each check
made again after anything that waited:

1. `u === UNIT` → nothing, as today.
2. **The guard:** `refuse('pick', u, opts)` → the note is said by the seam; the press ends having changed nothing.
3. **A save still going:** where `saveSettling()` and not `saveRefused()`, wait for the saver's own answer — never a
   timer of the door's own (D87) — then **start again from step 1**. Landed: it goes on. "Not saved": step 2 refuses
   with the Retry note.
   - **A waiting press is dropped when he presses anything else meanwhile** (the plan's reading, named for him): any
     later press of a door, and any pointer-down or key-down on the page while the wait stands. Dropped means nothing
     happens when the save answers — no entry, no note.
   - **This wait is shared by K5 and K6.** Build it once, as a small helper in `src/state/guard.ts` (or a new
     `src/state/doorwait.ts` beside it — your call, say which and add its line to your report for `docs/file-map.md`):
     one waiting press at a time, replaced by any later door press; resolves "go on" or "dropped". It imports nothing
     from `src/ui`.
4. **The Tracker's question** (`askBeforeEntering(u)`), exactly as today — its words, its three answers.
5. **After Save or Discard: step 2 again** (`u === UNIT ||` the guard) — a window or a failed save may have arrived
   while the question stood; and if a save is then going, step 3 again.
6. **The entry — `enterPicked(u)`: `closeShown()` then `enterSquadron(u, holdNow() === 'war', true)` then `notify()`,
   one synchronous run, never earlier** (a Stay, or a refusal after the question, must not have closed anything).
   `closeShown()` already runs each open edit mode's `end()` (K1b) — check Logic's and Quals' modes end here and add
   nothing if they do.

**With nothing to refuse, no save going and no question to ask, the pick stays synchronous, as today** — `UNIT` has
changed when `pickSquadron` returns (existing tests and callers rely on it).

Also: `pickWillAsk(u)` answers false where the guard would refuse; new `pickRefused(u): boolean` =
`u !== UNIT && !!refusalAt('pick', u)`.

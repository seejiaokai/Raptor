## 1. The eleven points

- **A. One board with zooms, or the grid plus the calendars — a third way, nearer Astra.** Keep the calendars. On a
  phone a member's "Mine" is his list with the month calendar he already knows; it must not wait for the squadron's
  year to load (Astra's point, and right). On a desk the squadron grid stays the year grid. What I hold: they are
  **views of the same entries with the same window and the same gestures**, never a third set of rules. A Month or
  Week zoom of the grid is not in the first build; it is added later only if the desk asks for it.
- **B. Leave on a date open for bidding — hold mine, with Astra's name for the other case.** One "Leave" action, and
  the date decides what it is: on a date open for bidding a member's leave is a **bid**; on any other date it is
  **Record approved leave** and stands at once. An admin may record approved leave on any date. This is what stops a
  way round the bidding: there is no second leave button beside "Bid" while bidding is open. It matches how he
  described the war in D1166 (open three months ahead, everyone approved, then closed, then word of mouth), but his
  ruling was about closed dates only, so on open dates it is his to rule (question 2).
- **C. The schedule's Personal Inputs list — agree with Astra.** A short **To review** holds only commitments still
  waiting for the scheduler. An accepted item carries its own Reject on its row; rejected ones sit behind a filter.
  It removes the sentence "To take an input off, press Reject on it in Personal Inputs below". It changes D2773 (a
  Personal Inputs line refusing a drop), which then has nothing to refuse.
- **D. Names — agree with Astra.** "Unavailable" stays. It is on the bell, on View-only Sched and in every scheduler's
  head; a new word buys nothing. Only the new short list gets a name, "To review".
- **E. What changed since I last looked — a third way; I drop the chip.** The conflict leads to the entry (Astra).
  What a conflict cannot show is a change that breaks nothing: Cutter free again, a man away who was not yet planned.
  Those are carried by things the app already has: the LATE mark, the pending mark on a published day, and the
  publish window, which lists what changed in who is away on that day since it was last published. No new chip on
  the day's head. With no live push on the new platform a chip would also promise a freshness the app cannot give.
- **F. A group's box at rest — not settled; drawn both ways.** I still lean to no mark at the year grid's size (a
  half-day box has no room) with the group lighting together; Astra to a small mark. We each moved once already;
  a third round of swapping is not agreement. Both go to him as drawings on the real grid, the half-day box included.
- **G. Grabbing one man's box of a group — hold mine.** A card at the box asks **Change · [6 pax] · [Cutter]** every
  time, for a drag of its body or its edge, before anything moves. Astra's proposal does not speak to it, and its own
  rule ("nothing splits a man off quietly", "viewing never splits") asks for it.
- **H. What an entry means for planning — agree with Astra.** Three meanings, not my one switch: **away** (counts him
  out: leave, a medical downchit, an overseas duty, a course, an Attach, an Other filed as away), **restricted** (he
  is here but may not fly, as ATT B: D998 already keeps it out of manning), **attending** (on the programme, counts
  him in). Leave and medical take theirs from their kind; for a commitment the scheduler chooses between away and
  attending, or rejects it. The meaning sits on the entry and shows in its window, whatever page it was filed from.
- **I. Pending bids and manning — agree with Astra; I was wrong.** Today's rule (D998, and the Leave War page's
  Manning lines): a leave bid not refused already lowers manning. So the admin sees **one figure, pending counted**,
  as today, and approving a bid does not move it. What I keep from mine, turned round: with a request selected, its
  days show what the figure would be **without it**, so a refusal's effect is seen before it is pressed.
- **J. The name "Leave War" — one answer.** Availability is the home. "Leave War" stays as the name of a bidding
  period and of the bidding view while one is open ("Leave War · Mar–Apr · closes 20 Jan"), because the squadron says
  it and the app already calls a period "a leave war". Not a page of its own. Put to him as question 3.
- **K. The order — agree with Astra's.** Draw the destination first; then the window; groups; a bid becoming leave as
  one record; Availability with approvals beside manning; last the schedule's To review. Duration is not a step: the
  one control arrives with the window, and its change to what an overseas duty or a course may store rides with
  step 3, checked there.

**Better in Astra's than mine, taken:** a decided request stays in place for that visit, so the list does not move
under the hand; a man's paper follows him when he is deliberately taken out; a man added to an approved bid never

## 2. The agreed direction as I now see it

**One home called Availability, joined to the schedule. A member records what is happening; an admin decides leave
bids with the manning in view; a scheduler sees what it means for the week. These are views of the same entry. If you
can see it you can open it, and it behaves the same wherever you opened it.**

**The places**
- **Availability → Mine** (a phone's first screen): the member's dated list, pending first, with the month calendar;
  Add at the place "+ Medical" and "+ Input" stand today.
- **Availability → Squadron** (a desk's first screen): the dense year grid, names pinned, manning rows pinned, open
  dates marked; **Approvals** opens beside it. Medical, SANS, Balances and OIL keep their own working views inside.
- **The schedule** stays the scheduler's page: Unavailable (one row per group), a short To review, the same window.
- Gone: the Leave War and the Inputs page as two destinations; three windows for one item; the permanent Personal
  Inputs list; every "change it on the Inputs page" sentence.

**The object:** one entry — kind, people, dates, Duration, remarks, each man's own papers, history — with a decision
(pending, approved, refused, or none needed) and a meaning for planning (away, restricted, attending).

**The rules**
1. A tap opens the entry's own window, the same everywhere, closing back to where you were.
2. What you may do follows who you are and what the entry is, never the page.
3. A bid is one man's, on dates open for bidding. Approval changes that same entry into leave: charged once, its
   request and decision kept. Leave on any other date is recorded as approved and stands.
4. A group is one entry. A change is for everyone unless **Change · [6 pax] · [Cutter]** is pressed, which takes him
   out at the press; one Undo puts him back. Grabbing one man's box asks the same question at the box.
5. One Duration (Whole day · AM · PM · Times) for filed entries; a bid keeps the first three; medical its own rules.
6. A published day changes only by a signed amendment; a change touching it reads pending until then.
7. Manning counts what it counts today, pending bids included. OIL earned and OIL taken stay apart, and an award
   never moves, doubles or goes because an entry was changed or a man taken out.

**The order:** (1) the destination drawn at his phone's and a desk's size; (2) the one window, from today's three
pages, Duration in it; (3) groups: one row, the Change buttons, each man's paper, the quiet splits gone, together;
(4) a bid and its leave one record; (5) Availability, with approvals beside manning; (6) the schedule's To review.
Each step drawn first, each with a full check and a walk; steps 3, 4 and 5 are high risk (balances, OIL, who sees
whose paper).

**On Power Apps and Dataverse:** nothing here needs an outside service. Not proven, to be tried in the hosting trial:
a group change saving wholly or not at all; a paper only its owner and admins can open (the IT side gives one
security group, so today this is the app's rule, not the database's); refusing the later of two saves to one group.
No live push: another person's change shows when the app next asks.

## 3. The smaller direction

**Stop after step 3** (the window, Duration, groups). That is the agreed consistency design. The squadron would be
well served day to day: one window, the same rights everywhere, a group one thing, no quiet splits, no sentence
sending anyone to another page.

What it leaves confusing:
- Two destinations still to choose between when filing, the Leave War and the Inputs page.
- A bid and its approved leave still two different records, so a few oddities stay under the surface (a member's
  change to his approved leave quietly changing what an admin may then do to it on the war) and must be patched one
  by one instead of ceasing to exist.
- Approvals still a page away from the manning they are judged against.
- The schedule's second list for acting on something already in front of the scheduler.

**A middle stop, after step 4,** removes the second of these and changes no page a member knows. If he wants the
least relearning for the most repair, that is the one I would name.

## 4. The questions left for him

1. **How far: the whole direction, or stop after the groups step?** *When:* every day, for everyone. **Recommend the
   whole direction**, drawn first, built in the six steps; the first three are the same either way, so nothing is
   lost by starting.
2. **While dates are open for bidding, is a member's leave on them always a bid?** *When:* January, March is open, a

   **Recommend yes:** an open date means a bid; any other date, "Record approved leave" after word of mouth; an admin
   may record on any date.
3. **Does "Leave War" stay as a name?** *When:* the admin opens March–April and tells the squadron. **Recommend:**
   Availability is the home; "Leave War" names the bidding period and its view while open.
4. **Deciding bids: person by person, or everyone asking for the same days together?** *When:* bidding has closed and
   the admin sits down to decide forty requests (Astra's question, and a good one). **Recommend both in the one list,
   with a "same dates" filter**; his habit sets which it opens on.

**Questions for the host to settle**

These are product choices, not failures already found. The later walk must retain **RECORD** until an answer exists.

1. **Which persistent surfaces count as windows?** In particular: collapsed Changes, Board itself, Show All, clean chart-layout mode, clean Users rows, and unfinished inline editors beyond the named examples.  
   **Recommend:** persistent task windows with their own close action block; ordinary menus and page modes do not. Explicitly classify the disputed cases.

2. **Does a typed Users row block a cross-squadron bell entry, and ask before Logout?**  
   **Recommend:** yes to both, preserving the same typing protection as pick/view while allowing sign-out after an explicit choice.

3. **When a window and failed save coexist, which refusal is named first?**  
   **Recommend:** window first, as the host reads it; after closing it, Retry becomes the next instruction.

4. **Where does the failed-save loss question sit during Logout with a dirty window and dirty Tracker?**  
   **Recommend:** window first, failed-save loss second, Tracker last; one at a time, and every stay answer cancels departure. Several windows should close from the front backwards.

5. **Should role and bell switches proceed while an ordinary save is still going?**  
   **Recommend:** retain the picker’s demonstrated behaviour where the save remains safely attached to its original squadron. If it fails, apply the failed-save rule. Keep the separately ruled shared-computer wait.

6. **When should another tab’s access change become effective in the already-open tab?**  
   **Recommend:** refresh protection on receiving the change, checking again on focus return and before a further write. The walk must first establish what the current app can actually detect; D2405 does not itself establish live delivery.

7. **Which request wins when a second pick or role change arrives during the first?**  
   **Recommend:** one departure at a time, with a clear consistent final destination. A later refused request should not silently run after its blocker disappears.

8. **Does a failed Save/Retry automatically resume the original switch?**  
   **Recommend:** require a fresh press after recovery, avoiding an unexpected departure while the person is repairing the failed save.

9. **What happens to an active drag at a switch?**  
   **Recommend:** finish or cancel it visibly before departure; never carry it into the new squadron. The choice needs a ruling; detailed Tracker drag handling stays in its separate job.

10. **Are other links that return to the home squadron governed by the same guard?** This includes an own-page landing or “Check my quals”, not just bell lines.  
    **Recommend:** the same protection whenever the action changes the squadron in view; the refusal should point to the pressed link.

11. **Does a blocked bell tap mark the notice read? What about Stay at the Tracker question?**  
    **Recommend:** a refused entry remains unread. For Stay, retain the existing behaviour unless the owner changes it; that question is already open in the backlog and must not be presented as newly discovered.

12. **What happens to an unfinished file/document read after its window closes and the person switches?**  
    **Recommend:** cancel uncommitted work on close, or retain its original destination explicitly; never retarget it to the new squadron.

13. **Should greyed Redo explain the other-squadron history just as Undo does?**  
    **Recommend:** yes. D2593 settles the boundary; the exact explanatory wording remains for the planned wording read.

14. **What should a press on the already selected squadron do while work is open?**  
    **Recommend:** an ordinary no-op/menu close, without a false “pick another squadron” refusal.

15. **What happens to non-window typing during role changes, bell entry and Logout beyond the demonstrated picker cases?**  
    **Recommend:** retain harmless searches/filters; retain or explicitly resolve unfinished saved-data edits. Do not extend the special Users-row rule to every text box without deciding which work needs protection.

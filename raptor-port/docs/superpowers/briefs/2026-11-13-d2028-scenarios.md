**Expected results the walker must preserve**

- D2028: changing an assumed length alone leaves the published day’s length, crowd and pending state unchanged.
- D2013: once a real change waits, the working copy previews today’s settings; View-only keeps the issued version. Those two answers may legitimately differ.
- “Any Logic change makes nothing pending” would be a wrong expectation. D592 still requires pending when an OIL setting changes somebody’s stored amount **or worked times**. D186 still covers a changed printed suggested brief.
- Publishing consumes the working sign-offs. To test that a setting change preserves existing sign-offs, sign the working copy again after publication first. Do not mistake already-empty boxes for signatures lost by this change.

For every crowd check, compare the **people**, not just the number. Picture the chip, opened list, assumed interval and pending state together. Drive the cases on phone and desktop, with a scheduler who is an admin; repeat the read-only openings as a member.

**1. Cover every row and placeholder position**

**Start:** An unpublished day with “Any other row” at 60 minutes and “A sim” at 90. Put a named man on another commitment just beyond the original assumed end, so extending the assumption would actually remove him from the crowd.

**Presses:** Create, publish and sign each applicable case; change its assumed length; reopen its count from Edit Schedule, the board and View-only:

| Row | Positions to cover |
|---|---|
| Ground Programme | Main name and extras |
| Common Programme | People list and extras |
| Standard duty desk | Holder and extras |
| AMT and OFT | Front seat, rear seat, passengers and extras |
| Input’s row | Its placeholder alongside named people; also a Personal input |

**EXPECTED:** Ordinary open-ended schedule rows keep their issued interval and membership, with nothing pending and standing working sign-offs preserved. Sims use their own setting. Extras and passengers inherit their row’s interval.

An input is a separate control: its crowd uses the input’s own window. A missing end must retain the input’s established all-day treatment rather than acquire the ordinary row’s guessed hour. Personal still earns nothing.

**Basis:** D2028, D2005, D360; the availability and input-window contracts.

**Tests:** Parameterize publication, title, membership and pending assertions across these positions. Existing `oilopenrow.test.ts` covers several row kinds before publication; it does not establish their D2028 publication transitions.

**2. Enter and leave the pending state through different causes**

**Start:** Publish the 09:00 open-ended row under one hour. Change Logic to two hours; confirm that alone leaves it clean.

**Presses:** Separately introduce, then reverse:

- An unrelated ordinary remark edit.
- A warning hide.
- An OIL Earn decision on another measurable row.
- A real input change.

Keep the ALL AVAIL window open while making an ordinary edit behind it.

**EXPECTED:** Each genuine change makes the working copy use the two-hour interval and corresponding crowd. View-only retains one hour. Reversing the last real difference restores the issued interval, membership and zero pending. The open window updates alongside its chip.

Do not require signatures to return after a deliberate Unpublish; that action has its own clearing rule.

**Basis:** D2013, D98, D103; the warning-hide and OIL-decision contracts.

**Tests:** Assert the actual pending list and displayed count, rather than only `dayDelta().length > 0`. Validate twice without further edits to catch an answer that alternates between kept and live settings.

**3. Prove that real availability changes remain visible**

**Start:** A published open-ended ALL AVAIL row with a known crowd; Logic has subsequently changed.

**Presses:** Through Inputs, file leave for somebody inside the **issued** interval. Inspect the working crowd, issued crowd, pending list and sign-offs. Undo the leave. Repeat by changing an existing timed commitment.

Add an equal-count case: release one man and make another unavailable, leaving the numerical crowd size unchanged.

**EXPECTED:** The issued crowd remains frozen. The real difference appears as pending and invalidates the working sign-offs. Putting the real change back clears it. Equal headcounts must not conceal different membership.

For a single input change whose crowd effect belongs to that input, the pending list must follow the existing one-act counting rule rather than add an unexplained duplicate OIL item.

**Basis:** D44, D45’s surviving freeze-and-pending half, D103, D98; Publishing’s pending-item contract.

**Tests:** Use the real input writer and `availableFor` route. The new test that directly adds a ground row does not prove this connection.

**4. Check earned leave affected indirectly through another row**

**Start:** A Saturday within an existing Leave War period:

- Bane is named on an ordinary row at 09:00 with no end.
- A separate ALL AVAIL row runs 10:15–17:15.
- Nobody has other earning work.
- Set “Full day of OIL” to six hours.

Publish under a one-hour assumption. Bane is free for the timed row and earns a full day from it.

**Presses:** Change the assumption to two hours. Then make an unrelated remark change, inspect OIL Earn and View-only, and publish the amendment. Repeat on a weekday declared a holiday **before** publication.

**EXPECTED:**

| State | Working copy | Issued view and Leave War |
|---|---|---|
| Logic change alone | Kept crowd; Bane still earns from the timed row | Original credit retained |
| Remark pending | Today’s crowd excludes Bane; candidate credit disappears | Original credit retained |
| Amendment published | New crowd and credit | Bane’s credit removed |

The open-ended row itself earns nobody anything throughout.

**Basis:** D2028, D2013, D360, D48, D592, D142; D2029 permits OIL Earn to show the working figure alone.

**Tests:** Assert the actual earned credit, OIL tracker and balance after publication. This is why the draft roll-call’s blanket “Leave War must not, because such a row earns nobody” is too narrow: another timed row can earn differently because of it.

**5. The concrete case for `validate.ts`’s `withLiveRules` question**

The changed crowd calculation can matter inside this block.

**Start:** Use case 4 on a fresh Saturday outside every Leave War period. In OIL Earn, exclude every other potential recipient from the timed row, leaving Bane as its only possible earner. Publish under one hour. The missing-period warning must be present.

**Presses:**

1. Change the assumed length to two hours.
2. Inspect the warning without another edit.
3. Add the unrelated remark.
4. Put the remark back.

**EXPECTED:** The setting change alone must not erase the warning: the untouched published day still has its kept crowd and a person who would earn. With the remark pending, the working candidate uses two hours, loses its sole earner and no longer needs that candidate warning. View-only continues to show the issued document’s missing-period warning. Putting the remark back restores the working warning.

This is my reading of D2028 and D2013 together with the missing-period contract. **D2014 alone does not settle every candidate-crowd calculation.**

**Tests:** Add a day-level assertion using `oilWouldEarn` and the actual warning bundles. The existing `withLiveRules` mechanism test proves settings restoration, but explicitly does not prove the day-level result.

**6. Step, dekit and legitimate Logic exceptions**

**Start:** Published days containing a flying man and a separate timed ALL AVAIL row. Use an explicit brief to isolate the availability setting.

**Presses:** Independently extend step into the placeholder interval, then extend dekit into a later interval. Reverse each change. Repeat with a named open-ended sim affecting another row’s crowd.

**EXPECTED:** A setting change alone preserves the issued crowd and creates no pending item. A real edit makes the working candidate use today’s availability windows.

Also run positive controls:

- Change report lead or debrief so somebody’s recorded OIL worked times change.
- Change the full-day threshold across somebody’s earned amount.
- Change the suggested-brief setting on a published flying line with blank B.

Those must retain their existing pending and signing behaviour. Keep availability’s narrow step-to-dekit window; do not widen it to report or debrief.

**Basis:** D2005, D2028, D36, D592, D186.

**Tests:** Add dekit coverage; the new file covers step only. Sweep the other kept settings with fixtures that isolate checking from printed-brief and OIL-record changes.

**7. Distinguish ORIG, latest amendment and today**

Use three answers:

- ORIG: 60 minutes.
- AL1: 120 minutes.
- Today: 180 minutes.

Against a 09:00 open-ended row, give one man a commitment at 10:15–10:45 and another at 11:15–11:45. The three crowds now differ.

**Presses:** Publish ORIG; change to 120, make a remark edit and publish AL1; change to 180. Look at ORIG, AL1 and the working copy in both viewing orders. Add and reverse another remark.

**EXPECTED:** Clean AL1 and its working copy retain 120. ORIG retains 60. A genuinely pending working copy uses 180. Returning to issued content restores 120, not ORIG’s 60. Looking at an old version must not change the next working reading.

**Basis:** D2028, D2013, D98; D187 for each version’s own warnings.

**Tests:** Assert each version’s title, frozen membership and settings independently, plus `dayPendingItems`. Repeat for `simLen`; its new test covers only a clean published sim after one setting change.

**8. Unpublish, then publish again**

**Start:** Case 7, with AL1 at 120 and today at 180.

**Presses:** Unpublish AL1, inspect both faces, sign afresh and publish AL1 again.

**EXPECTED:** ORIG becomes current at 60. The withdrawn amendment’s remark remains pending, so the working copy previews 180. Republication keeps the AL1 label and freezes 180. The withdrawn record keeps its original 120-minute evidence.

Separately withdraw ORIG on a day with no remaining issued version: the resulting draft must immediately use today’s settings. Publishing it again produces ORIG, using today’s crowd.

**Basis:** D101, D2005, D2028; Publishing’s withdrawal contract.

**Tests:** Check current and retired evidence, pending, fresh signing requirements and saved-plan signatures. This catches settings left attached to a formerly published day.

**9. Load a version and switch a saved plan**

**Start:** Case 7. Make AL1’s content differ from ORIG by one ordinary remark. Save one plan matching AL1 and another differing by a remark.

**Presses:** Load ORIG onto the working copy; then load AL1. Switch between the two plans through both the plans menu and its preview’s “Switch to this plan”.

**EXPECTED:** Loading ORIG does not change the issued version. Its content differs from AL1, so the working copy is pending and uses 180. Loading AL1 restores zero pending and 120. The matching plan uses 120; the differing plan uses 180. Plans keep their own valid sign states.

A replacement must not install frozen OIL evidence as though the working day itself were an issued snapshot. Use ordinary rows here: loading does not reverse a member’s live input changes.

**Basis:** D98, D2013, D2028; the version-load and saved-plan contracts.

**Tests:** Exercise the production replacement and validation paths, using newly created day objects rather than preserving the original object.

**10. Undo and Redo across both boundaries**

**Start/actions:** ORIG at 60 → Logic 120 → remark edit → publish AL1 → Logic 180.

**Presses:** Undo the last setting change, undo publication, redo publication, redo the setting change. Separately undo and redo the remark before publishing.

**EXPECTED:** Undoing Logic leaves AL1 issued at 120. Undoing publication behaves as withdrawal: ORIG at 60, remark pending, working preview using live 120. Redo restores AL1’s recorded 120-minute evidence. Redoing Logic to 180 leaves clean AL1 at 120. Reversing the last remark restores the kept answer; redoing it restores today’s preview.

**Basis:** D98, D2013, D2028; the recorded Undo/Redo and publication contracts.

**Tests:** Use global command-based Undo/Redo, including the settings command. Direct assignments to `VCONF` do not exercise this.

**12. Preserve the boundaries of guessed time**

**Start:** A published open-ended row, with Logic subsequently changed.

**Presses:** Add a real end; clear it again; clear the start and restore it. Separately cancel the row and mark it information-only, reversing each action.

No start produces the existing unknown-time explanation. Cancellation and information-only retain their established exclusions. No guessed interval becomes earned OIL.

**Basis:** D360, D31, D98, D2028.

**Tests:** Check title, list, eligibility and pending together; include a row near midnight and a shortened assumption as well as extensions.

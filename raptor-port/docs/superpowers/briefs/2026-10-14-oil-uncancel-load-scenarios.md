## 2. The readers — what must agree afterwards

After an un-cancel on an answer (and after each line of the load's list is answered):

1. The row on the Scheduler Board and on Edit Schedule's week — CX tag and strike gone (ⓘ gone), on EVERY day the input covers.
2. The input's line under Personal Inputs / Unavailable — the same.
3. View-only Sched — an unpublished day follows; a published day keeps the issued face until the amendment goes out (D178, D179).
4. The Inputs calendar: the opened day's card, the List's row or card, the month's bar and its tooltip — no CX tag, no strike, no "OIL?" tag (D767, D777, D814).
5. The input's own window — no CX tag by the title; its OIL line states the ONE answer (D762) and who gave it (D782).
6. The day's one list — the input is not in it; its count and the bell's count drop.
7. The schedulers' bell — the holiday's line counts one fewer; gone at zero.
8. The answerer's own bell (the input's only person, or its filer) — no OIL question for it.
9. The member's bell list — "Un-cancelled · <who>" to every named person and the filer, never to whoever did it, never to the men behind ALL AVAIL. Not answered → nobody is told (D783).
10. The Leave War grid's HO / FO cell and the OIL tracker's "Earned" — only from the latest PUBLISHED version (D142, D2): nothing before a publish or an amendment; after it, one cell a person in the input (and a crowd man behind ALL AVAIL); a No gives nothing.
11. A PUBLISHED day's pending count, its "Not yet signed / Not yet published" marker and the four sign-offs — un-cancelled reads as ONE change naming its people (D736), the sign-offs fall (D103); back to what was published reads 0 and they return (D98).
12. The changes window and the change history — one item for the un-cancel, with who.
13. The day's issues list and the rings — a cancelled input is silent, a live one is checked: its people's clashes come back only once the un-cancel is really made.
14. The ALL AVAIL count and window — a cancelled input frees its people; un-cancelled, they are busy again.
15. OIL Earn mode on the day — the input's people show as earning (Yes) or not (No).
16. Undo / Redo — one step.

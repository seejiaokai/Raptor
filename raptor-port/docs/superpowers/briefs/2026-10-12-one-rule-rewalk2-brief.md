**What changed in this build, so you know what to expect:**
- **A Personal input is the same as an Event for people (his rulings D776, D778).** It may hold several people; a man
  dropped ON its one person's puck REPLACES him (from a seat the two SWAP), on "+ add" he JOINS; ALL AVAIL / ALL may
  be picked or dropped for a ONE-DAY Personal input; a member may file it for other people while "members may file
  for others" is on. **And it is still not work:** it never asks the OIL question, nobody earns from it, and in OIL
  Earn its pucks say "a personal request earns no OIL". The sentence "A Personal input is one person's own" is gone.
- **The "OIL?" sign (D777).** Where nobody has answered an input's OIL question, its CARD — a day opened on the
  Inputs calendar, and the Inputs List on a phone — wears a small outlined "OIL?" tag on the top line, after LATE and
  left of the hours (a mark: a tap opens the input). The desktop List's row wears the "OIL?" button for an input of
  SEVERAL people too. Only whoever may answer sees it. The month's bars show nothing.
- **Eight fixes** from a second read of the code: a person added to one piece of a cut leave is in that piece only;
  a refused time on one man's Unavailable row changes nothing; a second person on a leave the Leave War approved is
  refused ("A leave approved on the Leave War is one person's — file the other person's leave as its own input");
  the first to join taken out and put back on a published day reads nothing pending; a swap from a flying seat on a
  published day is 2 changes (the input, and the jet); a new leave filed for a man who left a shared one has its own
  line; "By" passes to the admin who deletes or swaps out whoever filed an input; a row always shows the people its
  input holds.

## Card 19

**Eyebrow:** From chat 204 · the guard rail · the "Blocked:" note · 1 of 2

**Title:** 19. A Tracker window you can no longer see: what the note says

**When it happens:** rarely. An admin on a desktop starts setting a student's lull period on the Tracker's Info panel
and has picked the first day. Before finishing he makes the browser window narrow. The Tracker folds into its tabs and
shows the Flow chart, so the half-finished calendar is now out of sight behind the Info tab. Then he picks another
squadron from the list at the top left. It cannot happen on a phone: there the calendar's grey cover blocks every
other button until it is closed. A tablet turned upright may get there too; that was not tried.

**What the note says as built:** the pick is refused, as you ruled for any open Tracker window, with
**"Blocked: close the open window on the Tracker, then pick 149."** He is already on the Tracker and sees no window.

Picture: `q19-info-lull-crop.png` — "The open window: the lull period's calendar with its first day picked, before
the browser window is made narrow."

Picture: `q19-built-crop.png` — "As built. The Flow chart tab is showing; the calendar is behind the Info tab."

**Recommended:** **"Blocked: close the open window on the Tracker’s Info tab, then pick 149."** It names the tab the
hidden window is behind, so he knows where to go.

Picture: `q19-recommended-crop.png` — "Recommended, drawn on the real app, not built."

**The other choice:** keep "on the Tracker".

Choices: A — "on the Tracker’s Info tab" (picked) · B — keep "on the Tracker"

Contents-list line: "A Tracker window you can no longer see: what the note says" / "Recommended: name the tab it is
behind — "…on the Tracker’s Info tab""

## Card 20

**Eyebrow:** From chat 204 · the guard rail · the "Blocked:" note · 2 of 2

**Title:** 20. Something left unfinished on Admin's other section: what the note says

**When it happens:** now and then, while an admin is setting things up. He starts something on Admin's Users or
Squadrons section and does not save it. He clicks the other section, so the unfinished thing is out of sight. Then he
picks another squadron from the list at the top left.

**What you already ruled:** for a person's row left changed on Users, the pick is refused and the note says where the
row is: **"Blocked: save or cancel the open row on Users, then pick 149."**

Picture: `q20twin-built-crop.png` — "Your ruled words, as built. Squadrons is showing; the changed row is on Users."

**What the note says as built, in four more cases:** the same refusal, but the note does not say which section the
unfinished thing is on.

- A new squadron with its number typed, Users showing: **"Blocked: save or cancel the new squadron, then pick 149."**
- A squadron's row with its place changed, Users showing: **"Blocked: save or cancel the open row, then pick 149."**
- A new person with a callsign typed, Squadrons showing: **"Blocked: add or cancel the new person, then pick 149."**
- Give access with its role changed, Squadrons showing: **"Blocked: finish or cancel giving access, then pick 149."**

Pictures, side by side: `q20a-built-crop.png` — "As built: a new squadron left unfinished, Users showing." ·
`q20c-built-crop.png` — "As built: a new person left unfinished, Squadrons showing."

**Recommended:** add the section's name, as your ruled words do. Only while that section is not the one showing; when
he can see the unfinished thing the plain words stay.

- **"Blocked: save or cancel the new squadron on Squadrons, then pick 149."**
- **"Blocked: save or cancel the open row on Squadrons, then pick 149."**
- **"Blocked: add or cancel the new person on Users, then pick 149."**
- **"Blocked: finish or cancel giving access on Users, then pick 149."**

Pictures, in a grid of two: `q20a-recommended-crop.png` — "Recommended: the new squadron." ·
`q20b-recommended-crop.png` — "Recommended: a squadron's open row." · `q20c-recommended-crop.png` — "Recommended: the
new person." · `q20d-recommended-crop.png` — "Recommended: giving access." Each drawn on the real app, not built.

**In the pictures:** the letters showing just right of the shorter notes are the end of the page's "Admin" title. The
note stands over it for the few seconds it shows.

**The other choice:** keep the plain words, with no section named.

Choices: A — name the section (picked) · B — keep the plain words

**The recipes that worked:**
- Sign in, switch role, the war's today: the lib's `L.signIn(w, 'admin' | 'member')`, `L.switchTo(w, …)` after a write.
- The Balances page: `L.press(w, T('lw-tab-balances'))`, then wait for `balances-page` visible (~500 ms).
- **Typing in a box:** `L.press` the box, wait (650 ms on a phone, 150 on a desktop — a second tap inside ~0.5 s is a
  double tap), `Control+A`, `keyboard.type('16')`, `Enter`. The box keeps the keyboard after Enter. Without Enter the
  figure stays typed in the box (`data-dirty="1"`). Proved on both sizes: Ranger 15 → 16, War bal 12.5 → 13.5; Undo back to
  15 with the page's scroll unmoved; Redo 16.
- **Close the person window with Escape on a desktop**, not ✕ — see the fault below: on a desktop the window opens below the
  bottom of the screen and its ✕ cannot be pressed (`closePw` in the probe presses ✕ when it is on the screen, else Escape).
- **`lockViaBridge(w, on)` — the admin's lock flipped while the member is signed in (the stand-in for the other person;
  name it so in your report).** The localhost bridge has NO function for the lock. The helper uses the bridge's two role
  switches (`window.raptorRole`, `window.lwSetRole` — localhost only) around the store's own lock writer, found in the served
  build's store module (`/assets/store-<hash>.js`, the same module the page runs), all in one synchronous call, so the
  screen draws only the end state. It works on both sizes: unlocked → his 7 boxes go and "Locked by admin" shows; off → the
  boxes come back. **Under an open box** (he typed 14, no Enter): the box goes, the figure stays 15 and the app's note says
  "Ranger LVE App bal not saved: Locked by admin" — so there is no box left for a real Enter to be pressed in; record that.
  Its limit: the change history names the person signed in (Ranger) for the lock, not Saber.

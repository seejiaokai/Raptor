## Fable — "should the one-person request's row be made consistent too?" (11 Oct 26, read-only; branch `claude/all-avail-as-member` = `main` at 7c1fcdc0)

### 1. Recommendation
**A — leave the one-person row as it is for this job; build `[ALL-AVAIL-AS-MEMBER]` on shared inputs only.** The consistency he is asking for already holds for everything a scheduler TYPES on any request's row (D740, D742: time, remark, title go to the input) and for everyone on a SHARED row (D734). What is left inconsistent is one thing — WHO stands on a one-man request's row — and it is inconsistent for a reason the code makes concrete: joining a man or a placeholder to a one-man request means turning the member's own filing into a shared input with HIM as its filer. That silently moves OIL (D18's "he should earn" becomes "he takes the member's answer", which may be No or nothing), takes away the member's power over his own request when the members' switch is off, and makes the D470 name-box gesture impossible. None of that is a bug the plan can engineer around; each is a product ruling he would have to change on purpose. B or C are "sound-but-costly" as code, but they change four to six rulings with SILENT effects on OIL, so they are not a consistency tidy-up — they are a second feature, to be put to him as one, after he has used the ALL AVAIL member on shared inputs.

### 2. The candidates

**A — one-person row unchanged. SOUND.** Nothing moves; D18, D46, D470, D734 (3), D740 (1), D753 (4) stand. The scheduler sees what he sees today. The one honest cost: the inconsistency stays, and a man or puck the scheduler adds to Ranger's row shows on the schedule and not on Ranger's input (the design note §3 table is correct against `oilev.ts claimDefault` lines 1168–1173 and `landedExtras` 1131–1148: the extras earn by default; the placeholder on a one-man row is resolved through the request's item `i:<id>` and frozen at publication like any other — D44).

**B — full consistency: anyone dropped on any request's row joins the input. SOUND-BUT-COSTLY as code; UNSOUND as a drop-in, because it changes rulings silently.**
- *Code: small.* `grouprow.ts placeOf` (line 77) answers only a row with `srcg` (`overlay.ts sharedRowOf` 268); it would answer any standing request row (`requestOfBox` 255). `groupPut` → `commitGroup` already makes a group out of a one-man request (`inputedit.tsx` 1807 `regroup`, 1824–1827), and the VIEW already handles that case end to end: the occupant of the name box moves to the extras, the OIL refusal is carried to his own item (`overlay.ts entryPeopleOnce` 396–427, `carryOilRefusal` 428), the published-day count folds it to "the man added" (`entryfold.ts regroupRides` 147; plan §4.2, §4.8), the added man's puck wears the hollow tag (`holderbase.ts requestAddMarks` 90–98). The belt `slots.ts sharedSeatBar` (160) would extend to any standing request row. So the one-row job's plumbing carries B; the build is one door widened, one belt widened, tests first.
- *What it changes, concretely:*
  1. **OIL for the dropped man — D18, D470 reversed, silently.** Today a man under Ranger's Saturday Training earns by default (`claimDefault` 1171: `if (!isSpecial(claim.person)) return true`). Under B he is a member and takes the input's answer (D738/D744, `groupPut` 126–141): Ranger said **No** → the man earns nothing, no question asked (`entryOilAnswer` 756 reads 'same'); Ranger has **not answered** → 'none', the man has no answer, earns nothing, and no sheet opens. The scheduler put him there to work; nothing on screen says he will not be credited until OIL Earn is opened. That is exactly the class of fault this app's checks exist to stop.
  2. **D470's gesture dies.** Replacing Ranger's name with Tally's in the name box = Tally joins AND Ranger leaves; Ranger is the last man → refused (`grouprow.ts leave` 186–189, R5). "Ranger can't make it, Tally covers" becomes: take the request off, file a new one. Keeping the name box as D470 instead leaves B inconsistent again.
  3. **The member's own request becomes a shared input HE filed.** `commitGroup` 1826: an admin's regroup sets `grpBy = rows[0].by`, and a member's own record carries `by = himself` (`state/inputstamp.ts` 51). With the members' switch OFF, Ranger re-timing his own Training is refused — `commitGroup` 1803–1805: the other man's record is in `changed`, `mayEditInput` (`perms.ts` 247–257) fails `filedForOther` on `membersFileOn()`, and he reads "Filing for other people is switched off — an admin can change this"; the same for his own OIL re-answer through the window (1809). With the switch ON, Ranger may change or delete the input for everyone (D655), the scheduler's additions included, and his fresh OIL answer overwrites theirs (D682, line 1840 `forAll`). A member never asked to be a filer gains or loses rights by the scheduler's drop.
  4. **A multi-day one-man request.** Its row stands on its first day only (D739 (2), `overlay.ts landRequests` 439). A man dropped on Monday's row of a Mon–Wed Training joins all three days (`commitGroup` copies the shared fields — `inputentry.ts SHARED_FIELDS` 17). Today he is on Monday's row alone. B needs a refusal there or a rule he has not given (C has one: D753 single-day).
  5. **Kinds.** Leave, medical, upchit, OD never land on the programme (`inputs.ts isPersonal` 468; D737 — Unavailable rows stay a row a man, and a puck moved there is `reassignInput`, unchanged). Course, "Fly with", Personal ARE programme rows (`grp:'act'`, lines 231–262), and an admin may make any of them a group (`perms.ts mayFileGroup` 241–246) — so under B a man dropped on a Course row joins the course and on a "Fly with" row joins the flying; Personal asks no OIL, so nobody earns (D470 reading). Sensible for Course and Fly with; odd for Personal.
  6. *Count, late, bells:* one change waiting (the regroup fold exists); never late (`addInside` 142–143); an unanswered man's question goes to his own bell. All fine — the cost of B is not here.
- *Rulings B changes:* D18, D470 (the default), D46 (the placeholder's default, through D753 (1)), D734 reading 3, D740 reading 1, D753 reading 4; and D655/D660 gain a case nobody ruled (a member made filer by a scheduler's hand).

**C — placeholders only join; named men stay the scheduler's. SOUND-BUT-COSTLY, and it does not give him "one behaviour" either.**
- It is `[ALL-AVAIL-AS-MEMBER]` with one more door (the one-man row), so most of its cost is already in that plan. Added: the same filer problem as B (3) — Ranger becomes filer of a shared input of {Ranger, ALL AVAIL}, and D711 (1) then makes HIS answer the crowd's; and the same multi-day problem — D753 (2) says single day, so on a multi-day request's row the drop must be REFUSED, where today D46 allows it "no exception anywhere".
- **It changes D43/D46 on request rows, silently:** today the crowd under a one-man request earns by default (`claimDefault` 1171–1172); under C it follows the input's answer (D753 (1)) — Ranger's No or nothing → the whole crowd earns nothing. The scheduler who drops ALL AVAIL on a Saturday row to make everyone work would see no credit. Same silent OIL class as B (1), now for twenty men instead of one.
- **Kinds:** a placeholder input is six kinds (`inputs.ts PLACEHOLDER_KINDS` 530; D711 (2), D712). On a Course, "Fly with" or Personal row C must refuse the drop or keep it the scheduler's — either way D46 is cut twice and the row behaves two ways by kind. Named men still differ from pucks. Not consistent.
- *Rulings changed:* D46, D43 (on request rows), D734 (6), D753 (4), D740 (1).

**D — the cheap middle: A, plus the input's card SAYS what stands on its row.** SOUND, display-only, modest. The card and the Inputs calendar already read the request; they could also read its standing row (`overlay.ts standsOn` 237, `landedExtras`) and print "on the programme with: Tally · ALL AVAIL". No write, no OIL change, no ruling changed; the schedule and the input then SHOW the same people even though they are owned differently. Its hole: a request on a week not loaded has its row in the week's saved copy, not in `DAYS` — the card would have to read through `weekctx.ts bundle()` (the crew-rest lookback does) or say nothing for that week, as `leavingNote` (`inputedit.tsx` 1655–1672) already does. Worth filing, not worth doing inside this job.

Read-only scenario design only. **No app runs, tests, or file changes were made.** These are instructions for the walkers, not passing results.

Use three independent demo worlds:

- **Walker A:** scenarios 1–31 — filing, questions, permissions, saved results.
- **Walker B:** scenarios 32–52 — desktop list, cards, remarks, saved windows.
- **Walker C:** scenarios 53–69 — month, other entry points, settings, guest boundary.

With two walkers, give scenarios 1–31 to one and 32–69 to the other. Do not share a world.

**Execution conventions**

- **Both roles** means separate runs as Saber, admin, and Ranger, member.
- **Both sizes** means separate runs at **1440px desktop** and **390px phone**.
- Start through **Inputs → List → dates → All dates** unless a scenario says otherwise. July’s demo inputs are otherwise hidden by today’s date window.
- Use the app’s controls for every preparation and change. Prefix test titles or remarks with the scenario number. Use separate dates or fresh demo copies where medical cases would interfere.
- Photograph each stated result, each refusal, and each intermediate question. Capture the saved-row light immediately and again after it settles.
- A missing prerequisite is **not exercised**, with the reason recorded; it is never a pass. Do not manufacture missing-filer records or protected weeks by changing stored data.

- **C. A member filing word-of-mouth leave on the war — a third way.** Astra is right that my version leaves one "go

## 2. The agreed design as I now see it

**An item is the same thing on every page. What you can see, you can change right there, in the same window, by the
same rights. The pages differ only in what they show and what they add.** The Leave War shows the year, man by man:
leave is bid for and decided there, and manning judged. The Inputs pages show the month and the list: anyone files and
corrects anything. The schedule shows the week: the scheduler places people.

1. **Two kinds of thing.** A bid waits for a decision — leave, one man's, on the war only. Everything else stands.
   Once approved, a leave is an ordinary item and where it came from never decides what can be done to it.
2. **A tap opens the item's own window, the same on every page,** over the page in use and closing back to it: people,
   dates, Duration, remarks, paper, Delete, and for a scheduler where it stands. A day with several entries lists them;
   a tap on one opens its window.
3. **A drag moves it:** a box or bar to other days on the war and the Inputs calendar; a puck to change who on the
   schedule. Each has a tap way too.
4. **A group is one item everywhere.** One card, one row of pucks, one box a man on the war that lights with its group
   when selected or under the mouse. Every direct change is for everyone and every day of it. Nothing splits a man off
   by itself. A man's own paper needs no split.
5. **One man is changed alone by one deliberate action, "Cutter only…"** — a named button on the war, a puck's action
   elsewhere. It opens his own copy; he leaves the group only when a changed version is saved; one Undo.
6. **Rights follow the person and the item, never the page.** A member: his own. An admin: anyone's. A published day
   changes only by a signed amendment, and the window says the change is waiting.
7. **One Duration control: Whole day · AM · PM · Times.** A bid keeps the first three. The war's grid keeps halves,
   and the window shows how an item with times is counted there.

**What each page adds:** the war — bids, the far-ahead overseas duty and course, and on a closed day already-approved
leave; the Inputs page — anything; the schedule — anything for the days in view, for one man or several.

**The order:**
1. The one window from all three pages, dates included, a group plainly named in it; each "change it on the Inputs
   page" sentence goes as its action arrives.
2. Honest groups, together: one row under Unavailable and its drops (the job in flight), no silent splits,
   "Cutter only…", a paper a man, the group lighting on the war.
3. In three parts: (a) moving every standing item on the war, a group together; (b) the one Duration control and the
   war's count shown; (c) adds made alike — several men on the war make one item, the schedule's add takes several
   people and a range, "File approved leave" on a closed day.
4. Last: where a leave was born stops gating — an admin adds a man to a bid-approved leave; "Back to a bid" acts on
   the bidder alone.

Rulings changed, as in round 1 plus one: D2770 (reversed), D2778 (two clauses: the his-only action, and the mark in
the box), D1350, D1365's last clause, the reading under D1177. D1724 is how it is built. Steps 2, 3a and 4 are
high-risk work.

## 3. The questions left for him

1. **"Cutter only".** *When:* a stand-down leave filed for six; Cutter is recalled a day early — a few times a year,
   at short notice. *Recommend:* yes, on every page, reversing D2770: one action instead of take-him-off-and-file-again,
   and never a moment where Cutter is recorded as not away.
2. **What a member may do with approved leave on the war.** *When:* leave approved in January for March; in February
   he gets a verbal yes to shift it a day, or to take a new day, and he is looking at the war. *Recommend:* he drags
   his approved leave and it just moves, as on the Inputs page today; and on a closed day the note offers "File
   approved leave". On open days the war still offers only a bid. If he would rather a moved leave wait for approval
   again, that should hold on both pages, not one.
3. **How a group shows on the war's grid.** *When:* every look at a stand-down week. *Recommend:* no mark in the box;
   the group lights when one box is selected or under the mouse, and the move banner names everyone. It changes his
   word in D2778 that the box is marked, so both versions are drawn side by side, the half-day box included.
4. **Adding a man to a leave that came from a bid.** *When:* Ranger's approved week turns into a stand-down for three
   more men. *Recommend:* yes, for an admin; the added men are recorded as filed by him, Ranger keeps his approval,
   and "Back to a bid" would put only Ranger back. Built last.

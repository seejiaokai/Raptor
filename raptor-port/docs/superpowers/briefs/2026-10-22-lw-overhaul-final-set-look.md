## Where the set disagrees with itself

**Would mislead him**

1. **Required / Available rows lose their tint in part 2.** r7-02, r7-03, r7-20, r7-22 draw the pilots' pair olive and the
   WSOs' pair green (D1194); r7-10, r7-11, r7-12, r7-17, r7-19 draw all four rows plain (checked by sampling the
   colours). He would read D1194 as dropped on the bid and Open windows.
2. **The member's top rows come three ways.** r7-01, r7-05, r7-07, r7-08, r7-09 put My leave on line 1's right half with
   "● 3 new", Manning folded (Q9). r7-10 puts My leave on its own third line, no "3 new", Manning open. r7-20 and r7-23
   use a third line, Manning open, with "3 new" in r7-20 only. Q9's answer is unreadable from the set.
3. **Three looks for "Balances".** In r7-01/02/05–09 it is a faint, unboxed label, and in r7-03/06 blue words with no box.
   r7-10–19 draw a short bordered button, and r7-22/23/25–29 a long outlined pill. D1185 is about exactly this look.
4. **Two zoom pairs.** r7-01–19 show magnifiers (Q12); r7-20–29 show "−" and "+". The helper skipped Q12 there, but
   he sees two designs.
5. **"Under N days" and red days.** In r7-26, Available P is red on Fri 23 and Wed 28, yet the chip reads "0 days". In
   r7-10–19 red days are counted ("5 days"). He cannot tell whether a red Available day counts as under-manned.
6. **Approvals "● 3 new" comes and goes.** It shows in r7-02/03/06/14 and is gone in r7-11–13, r7-15–19 and r7-22–29.
   r7-15 is the same visit as r7-14, armed. This blurs Q1.
7. **Ranger's story differs between his list, the bell and the entry window.** The Mon 19–21 Jan approval is "Tue 6 Jan,
   14:30" in r7-13, "08:40" and new in r7-20, and "1d" ago in r7-28. r7-20 dots three rows as new, but the bell shows
   the same events already read. The bell's lines for Tue 10–Wed 11 Feb taken back, 12→19 Mar moved and 23 Feb
   replaced by a course match nothing in his list (r7-20/21/22) or on the grid. Feb 10–11 put back to waiting would make
   "2 waiting" wrong. All of these are invented, but he checks figures against each other.
8. **Events vs Holidays vs grid.** r7-29-events lists Good Friday (Fri 3 Apr) as a public holiday, but r7-29-holidays
   leaves it out. The events' "SP" on Fri 23 Jan and "EX" on 9–13 Feb never appear in the grid's Event rows (r7-03, r7-11,
   r7-13, r7-22).
9. **Groups.** Settings (r7-24/25) show Management 2 first and SXO 10. Every grid shows no Management group and
   "SXO · 12". The report says Management was not drawn on the grid, but the pictures do not say so.

**Worth a word, probably harmless**

10. **Required names as renamable.** r7-20/23 (a member) give REQ P/W the dotted "renamable" underline, but r7-10/12/17/19
    (an admin) do not. Harmless only if the underline is decoration.
11. **Windows docked or centred.** On desktop, the bid, entry, Approvals and person windows dock right (r7-11/13/14/22).
    The day panel is a bottom-right card (r7-06). Open for bidding, Settings, the row windows, Events and Calendar sit
    centred (r7-18, r7-25–29). On a phone, the bid, person, Open and Approvals windows sit at the foot (r7-08–12,
    r7-16/17, r7-20/23). D997 puts task windows in the middle of a phone screen unless A5 overrides it; say which applies.
12. **Legend's place.** On a phone, Legend is at the far right of line 2. On a desktop it follows Under-manned on the
    left, with My leave at the right. The order is the same, but the position may grate against D1188.
13. **Marks.** Parts 1–2 and the phones use numbered circles. Part 3's desktop pictures use long text labels carrying
    ruling numbers, which sit over the Manning figures in r7-22 and r7-27. Labels hide details: in r7-01-2, mark 6 covers
    Piston's 26–27 Jan boxes. In r7-14, mark 5 hides Reaper's CAT chip. In r7-16, Q1 covers the "3" of "New 3". In
    r7-21, mark 1 clips the 17 box. In r7-01/02/05, marks 3/6/Q6 sit on the MAR and JAN month buttons. The Q banners in
    r7-01 and r7-09 float over grid rows, though only over empty days.
14. **Cut off:** the end of r7-07's Legend ("The corner" is cut off; the helper said it scrolls). Phone short names (REQ P,
    "Open", "Under") against desktop full names (REQUIRED P, "Open for bidding", "UNDER-MANNED") look deliberate.

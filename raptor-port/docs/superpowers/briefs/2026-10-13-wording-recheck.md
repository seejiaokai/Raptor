words are here.

**How it was done.** The writer (this chat) read each note where the app raises it, so that a link word is known and

report is summarised in `2026-10-13-writing-guide-reads-2.md`). Word counts are a check, never a target (§1a test 11), and are counted by a script, not by eye.

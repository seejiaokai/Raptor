**2. The direction I now recommend**

**Availability becomes the one home for leave, medical and commitments, connected to the scheduler’s programme.** A person records something once and finds the same item wherever it matters. The board answers who may be available; the schedule answers who is doing what. Both open the same window.

The member’s phone opens Availability on **Mine**: today and upcoming inputs, pending bids clearly named, a month strip, and direct Medical and Input actions. Filing should not wait for the squadron’s whole year to load.

The administrator’s desktop opens on **Squadron**: the dense board, the whole period available, names and manning held in view, and open bidding dates marked. Month and Week give closer views of that same board.

**Approvals opens beside the board.** Selecting a request shows its dates and relevant manning. On a phone, the request and its manning take the full width together. Decisions stay in place for that visit so the next button does not move.

Medical, SANS, Balances and OIL remain directly reachable within Availability. Their specialised work remains intact. Squadron events and ALL / ALL AVAIL commitments never become individual absences.

The schedule keeps its programme, crew controls and **Unavailable** section. A shared input occupies one row. **To review** contains only unresolved scheduling decisions; it creates no new approval requirement for medical or commitments that already stand automatically.

An input carries its kind, people, dates, Duration, remarks, personal papers and history. Its decision and planning effect remain separate. A bid becomes approved leave within that same item, retaining the request and decision; approval does not charge the same days twice. Individual bids retain individual decisions, including decisions on only part of their dates.

Five rules hold throughout:

- **Same item, same permitted actions.** Rights follow the person and the kind of input. Opening from another page neither grants nor removes permission.
- **A group shares its details.** The group choice changes everyone; the named-person choice separates that person immediately, as you ruled. Closing his window unchanged leaves him separate; Undo can restore the separation.
- **Papers stay personal.** Adding a paper does not split the group. Only its owner and authorised admins may open it. I recommend that it follows its owner when he is separated.
- **Time means the same everywhere.** Filed items offer Whole day, AM, PM and Times where permitted; bids keep their three choices and medical keeps its own rules. Exact times govern conflicts even where manning counts halves.
- **Current planning and the published record stay distinct.** New inputs update planning and warnings. They never silently rearrange the programme or rewrite a published day; that still requires its signed amendment.

For leave on open dates, I recommend the bidding rule in B, subject to your decision. A range crossing open and closed dates must show which portions need approval before submission; the app must not silently treat the whole range as already approved.

For last-minute work, a conflict opens its input directly. **Changes** also shows availability changes that cause no conflict. It is separate from LATE and from an amendment’s pending changes.

Build in this order:

1. Agree phone and desktop drawings of the destination.
2. Introduce the common window through today’s pages, with dates and consistent Duration.
3. Complete group rows, deliberate separation, personal papers and consistent gestures together.
4. Make the bid and its approved leave one continuing item, preserving partial decisions, balances and history.
5. Introduce Availability, the board’s views, Mine and approvals beside manning.
6. Replace the schedule’s duplicate decision list and add Changes.

The main departures from your earlier decisions are the separate navigation and calendar-first opening, the permanent Personal Inputs decision list, page-specific Duration limits, the group shortcut’s availability, and the shared mark. The proposed open-date rule changes who may record verbally approved leave there. None is already authorised merely because the readers recommend it.

This remains a substantial build. Balance deductions, group saves, private papers, Undo and published days need particular care. Each stage needs its own appropriate checks; phone speed and the future platform’s ability to save a complete group change must be demonstrated.

**3. The smaller direction**

A useful stopping point is **the common window, consistent Duration and completed group behaviour**, while keeping today’s destinations and bidding policy.

That would remove the repeated trips elsewhere, inconsistent editing and quiet separation of one person. It is worthwhile in its own right.

It would leave members choosing between Inputs and Leave War, approval work separated from the full manning view, and the schedule’s duplicate decision list. It would also retain the different treatment of a bid and leave recorded after verbal approval.

I recommend it as a dependable first delivery, with Availability as the intended destination if you choose the larger redesign.

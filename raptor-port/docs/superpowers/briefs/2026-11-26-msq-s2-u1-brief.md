## What is built

1. **One predicate, `foreignHere(pid)`** — his home that day is not the page's squadron (`otherSqnOf` already reads
   it). Every rule below hangs on it and on nothing else.
2. **The closed row of such a man is short:** his callsign, his home's grey number, seat and CAT, then his role in
   words — "admin", or "master admin, full access"; a member adds nothing; a man with no sign-in has no role — then
   what he already holds here in words ("also plans 149" as built; the View wording from the words file). **No role
   badge, no Sign-in or Roster dots, no sign-in name.** (The grey number's absence inside a folded squadron group is a
   later step — there is no such group yet.)
3. **A press opens it, as every row opens — to ONE line: the page's squadron's, "149 None · View · Plan", with Save
   and Cancel, and nothing else.** No sign-in box, no Role box, no line for any other squadron, no Suspend, Archive or
   Delete — not greyed: not drawn. `SquadronLines` takes the one squadron to draw. Which of None / View / Plan is live
   is today's `lineState`, unchanged (who may give Plan changes in the next step, not here).
4. **A man with no sign-in opens to words in place of the line — "No sign-in. Ask 142's admin to give him one
   first." — and Cancel alone.** None of today's give-a-sign-in branch is drawn for him (`#accGive`, `#accGiveName`,
   `#accGiveRole`).
5. **The row has its own save.** `saveAccess(a.id, { grants: { [the page's squadron]: line } })` and **nothing else —
   never `name`, `role`, `master` or another squadron's line, whatever the editor's state holds.** It never goes
   through the whole row's `accessWant` / rename path. **At Save the row is judged again:** if his home is by then the
   page's squadron, or the page's squadron is no longer the one the row opened on, **nothing is sent**, the row closes,
   and the app's passing note at the screen's foot says **"<Callsign>’s row changed while it was open, so nothing was
   saved. Open it again."** (his D2730 Q13 — these words exactly, the callsign from the row).
6. **The row reports its unsaved change** the same way the whole row does today (read how an opened row tells the
   guard it holds a change — `useGuardWork` or what the file has; if the whole row has no such report yet, give the
   short row the same "changed and not saved" signal the whole row computes and say in your report what you found).
   Cancel and Escape close it and drop the choice.
7. **Unchanged:** his whole row on his own squadron's page, every button as today; the doors themselves
   (`updateAccount`, `saveAccess`, `archivePerson`, `deletePerson`) — on 142's page Hunter does all of it as today;
   Hunter's own "· also plans 149" note; "Plan is for admins only."; "Only an admin of 149 can give access to 149."
8. **Not in this step:** "Edit on 142 →" and every clause of V1 that names it (a later step, after another section's
   pick door lands); the refusal at a squadron pick; who may give Plan; the page's new order, folds, block, jumps.

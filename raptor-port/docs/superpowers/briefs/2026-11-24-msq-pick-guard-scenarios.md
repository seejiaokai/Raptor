# [MSQ-PICK-GUARD] the walk's scenario list — the two designers' lists combined by the host (D2443; chat 171, 24 Nov 26)

**The two lists, each blind to the other, same brief (`2026-11-24-msq-pick-guard-scenario-brief.md`):** a fresh Fable's,
58 scenarios and 41 questions (`…-scenarios-fable.md`, cited F1–F58, FQ1–FQ41); Astra's, 56 scenarios and 15 questions
(`…-scenarios-astra.md`, A1–A56, AQ1–AQ15). Designed before the build, from the rulings (D2403, D2405, D2590–D2595) and
the press report. This file is the index the walk is sized and briefed from: each row names the scenarios it joins, and
**the walker's text for a row is the fuller of the named scenarios, read whole in its own file** — steps, EXPECTED, its
source and what would disprove it. One walk covers this job and [MSQ-NIGHT-153-OPEN] (D485).

**How an EXPECTED is used (the bug-check order §4, "before a brief leaves"):** a line that cites a ruling, or "as today"
with its press-report case, is judged PASS or FAIL; a line either designer marked RECORD, and every row in §2 below,
stays "RECORD exactly what it says" until its question is answered — never judged against an invented result. The host
re-checks each cited ruling against its full row when the walker's brief is written, after the build.

## 2. What the rulings do not say — for him, each to be put with its picture and when it happens (D2439, D853)

None is decided here. The recommendation is the host's unless a designer's is named.

- **H1 — Logout while a change is not saved: is the change kept or lost?** The draft he read says "lose it"; today's app
  keeps it — the band and Retry stay over the sign-in screen and the next sign-in waits (D2322). Recommended: kept, the
  question reading "Your last changes aren't saved yet. Log out anyway?" (the words read; FQ13).
- **H2 — What counts as "a window".** Agreed by both: anything with its own title or ✕ that stays until closed. Not
  agreed, or not said: Traffic, Jump to a date, Day details and the document viewer, which today close at any press on
  the bar (D1645) — Fable: leave them, nothing is typed in them; Astra: they refuse like the rest. The folded Changes
  window; the Scheduler Board (and its cancel-reason box); the Tracker's "Edit chart layout" with nothing moved, and
  Show All. Recommended: the four keep closing as today; the board alone is not a window, with its cancel-reason box or
  Sort all up it is; an editing mode with nothing unsaved is not.
- **H3 — How far "an opened row with typing" reaches on Admin → Users:** a changed Role or access with nothing typed,
  "Give access", "+ Add person", an archived man's row, "+ New squadron". Recommended: any unsaved change in any of them
  counts (FQ18) — each then needs its own noun in the note.
- **H4 — Logout over a window that holds typing but has no question of its own** (a holiday's name, a bid, the OIL
  list's answers, a Tracker remark). Recommended: one common question, "This window has unsaved changes. Log out and
  lose them?" (the words read; FQ16).
- **H5 — How Logout is reached on a phone with a window open.** No control exists; to be designed and drawn.
- **H6 — The Input window's question at Logout** says only "Close it and lose them?". Recommended: "Log out and lose
  them?" with "Discard and log out" — a change to words D2592 names, so his.
- **H7 — A tap on the bell itself that is refused** (the access alert, his own OIL question): "…then press this line
  again." does not fit. Recommended: "…then press the bell again." (FQ7), read by a fresh Fable.
- **H8 — A save still going when a pick is pressed.** Fable: the pick waits for the save; Astra: it goes, as today, the
  save staying with its own squadron. Recommended: it waits — else a save that fails a moment later raises 142's band
  on 149, the case D2594 was made to stop.
- **H9 — Undo's edges:** the greyed words with steps on two other squadrons; whether a change on 149 clears a Redo
  waiting on 142 (recommended: no); Undo of "+ New squadron" while that squadron is in view (recommended: refused there).
- **H10 — The refresh card's edges:** shown even with nothing open (as ruled — the quiet change at midnight goes); "from
  today" when the app is opened days after the move; a posting out or an archive landing at midnight; Refresh trying a
  waiting save once before it reloads; 149's Tracker failing at every pick.
- **H11 — Where the note stands** on a small tablet and a phone on its side; whether the ☰ menu closes after a refused
  pick (drawn closed; recommended closed); and his D731 (1) against D2590 where an input-type window stands at the foot
  of a phone (the sizing file §5).
- **H12 — A drag in progress when a second finger or the keys press a door.** Recommended: the drag is put back, then
  the door answers.
- **H13 — A phone's caret left in a day's note at a pick; typing in Help's bug box, a calendar "+ Note", a Balances
  figure box.** Recommended: press each on today's app first; words stay where typed, a figure is saved or put back.

## 3. The host's calls — technical, told to him, not asked

- With two reasons to refuse, the note names the window first, then Retry at the next press; a refusal always comes
  before the Tracker's question, so he never discards chart edits only to be refused (both designers).
- A refused bell line stays unread and its list stays open; a refused switch never runs later by itself — a fresh press
  after the window is closed or the save lands.
- The row's press is refused, not the opening of the Schedules list (FQ1).
- A door reached by the keys answers as by the mouse; the note is told to a screen reader.
- Logout's order where three could be asked: the window's, the unsaved change's, the Tracker's.
- "Your access has changed" cannot be raised by a press in this build (no tab hears another until a reload): it is
  proved by a test with a named stand-in, said plainly on the evidence sheet, and walked at the database step.
- The words: `2026-11-24-msq-pick-guard-words2-fable.md` — "Pick 142 to undo your changes there."; "Blocked: save or
  cancel the open row, then pick 149."; "Blocked: press Retry to save your changes, then pick 149."; the switch-back
  form of the row's note has no path and is not built.

## 4. Stand-ins the walk names in its evidence

A failed save (the browser's storage refusing every write, lifted before Retry); his own move at midnight (the move
written as a home row, the page clock run past 00:00); the Tracker failing (reads of 149's Tracker records throw); a
right taken away (a second tab). A stand-in proves what the app does once the state exists, never that a person can
reach it.

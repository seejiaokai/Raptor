## What the app should now do (read once)
- **An input is ONE thing with the list of its people.** Its row on the schedule's Ground Programme is a copy of it:
  whoever stands on the row is in the input — a named person or ALL AVAIL / ALL — and the schedule (Edit Schedule's
  week, the Scheduler Board, View-only Sched, the next-week peek) and the Inputs pages (the month's bar, the opened
  day's card, the List's row or card, the input's own window) always show the same people, time and remark.
- **Drawn order everywhere:** named people A to Z by callsign, then ALL AVAIL / ALL last.
- **The hands on an input's row (a scheduler — the admin — only):**
  - a named person put on "+ add", or on a puck where the row holds several (a named man beside ALL AVAIL counts as
    several): he JOINS — "Tally added to Training";
  - a named person dropped ON THE ONLY ENTRY'S puck from the crew list: he REPLACES him — "Tally is on Training
    instead of Ranger"; the OIL answer is void and, where the input asks one (a weekend or holiday), the OIL question
    opens at once for whoever did it;
  - the same, dragged from a SEAT elsewhere on the schedule: the two SWAP places in one step — "Tally and Ranger
    swapped"; a seat that refuses the man replaced refuses the whole swap;
  - ALL AVAIL / ALL put anywhere on the row JOINS (never replaces) — "ALL AVAIL added to Training";
  - a puck dragged to nowhere, right-clicked, or blanked: that person or placeholder LEAVES — "Ranger taken out of
    Training"; the LAST entry is refused — "Ranger is the last on this input — use ✕ to take it off the programme, or
    delete it in its own window";
  - a person dragged from the row to another place: he leaves and lands there, one step; from one input's row to
    another's: he moves, one step; from a seat that is NOT an input's row onto the row: he joins and the seat keeps him;
  - refused, with the reason, and nothing changed: already in it ("Tally is already on this input"); a second
    placeholder or ALL beside ALL AVAIL ("This input already has ALL AVAIL"); ALL AVAIL on an input longer than a day
    ("ALL AVAIL is for one day"); on a kind that is not one of the six ("ALL AVAIL is for Training, Meeting,
    Appointment, Duty, Event and Other"); anyone onto a Personal input ("A Personal input is one person's own"); a
    second person on a medical kind ("A medical entry is one person's — each needs its own document");
  - a time, a remark or the title typed on the row changes the input for everyone, and never makes it read LATE;
  - the board's CX (it asks a reason), red box and "information only" buttons on an input's row change the INPUT. A
    cancelled input shows on the Inputs pages (below); the red box and information only show on the schedule only;
  - the row's ✕ takes the input off the programme; its people and marks stay in it, so Accept (on its Personal Inputs
    line) or Undo brings the row back as it was.
- **OIL:** ONE answer per input, given by whoever filed it or an admin. Everyone in it — and every man behind its ALL
  AVAIL who is not named in it — follows that answer; only the scheduler leaves one person out, in OIL Earn on the
  day. A person added where the answer is Yes earns with no question; where it is No, the note ends "— not counted for
  OIL"; where there is none and the day is a weekend or holiday, the question opens at once for whoever added him
  (closed unanswered: no dialog is left standing; the question waits on the filer's bell). A scheduler's switch-off
  about a person ends when he leaves the input. Taking ALL AVAIL off ends the switches made for the men behind it;
  dropped back, it starts fresh. A named person leaving does not touch them.
- **A published day:** an input filed, taken off, cancelled or un-cancelled, re-timed or re-worded is ONE change
  waiting; each person or placeholder added or taken out is ONE EACH; a swap of the only person is ONE; a person moved
  between two inputs' rows on one day is ONE ("Ranger: Training → Meeting"); a CX put on and taken off again is
  NOTHING waiting. The published face (View-only Sched) keeps what went out until the day is published again. A load
  of an older version never changes an input: the row is re-made from the input as it is NOW.
- **Who may:** an admin changes any input. The ONLY named person of an input (with nothing beside him) changes it in
  full, whoever filed it. Whoever FILED an input for several changes it for everyone while every person in it was put
  in by him; once a scheduler has put someone in, he no longer does (the window says so) — but he may add people to
  his own one-person input. A man IN an input he did not file reads it and has "Take me out"; he adds nobody. A man
  who is the only named person beside ALL AVAIL reads it and may take himself out. No man gives an OIL answer of his
  own. Only a scheduler cancels or un-cancels.
- **A cancelled input:** on the opened day's card and the List's row or card — a small red "CX" tag before the title,
  the title struck through, the reason after it in red; on the month's bar — its words struck through and the bar
  faded, no tag (its tooltip says "Cancelled — WX"); it keeps its place in the day's order; on the schedule's Personal
  Inputs line — the schedule's red tag with its reason ("CX DUE WX"; "CX" alone on a phone's board) and the name
  struck; the input's own window says it in its title bar (the tag and the red reason, no control). Its people are
  free again: no clash or crew-rest warning from it, and it earns nobody OIL.
- **The bell:** someone ELSE's change to an input a person is in (or filed) puts a line on his bell — the input in
  bold with its date, then what happened and who, its age at the right ("**Range brief** Wed 15 Jul — Cancelled — WX ·
  Saber"). The words: You were added · You were taken off · Deleted · Cancelled — WX · Un-cancelled · Taken off the
  programme · Back on the programme · Now Thu 16 Jul 10:00–11:00 · Tally added · ALL AVAIL taken off · Renamed — was
  Kit check · Remark changed. ONE line for one save. Never to whoever made the change; never to the men behind ALL
  AVAIL. An unread line lights the bell. A tap on the bell still goes FIRST to an access request, a bug report or an
  OIL question where one waits; otherwise it opens the list. A tap on a line opens the input (or says "That input is
  gone") and marks it read; a read line stays, muted; "Mark all read"; "Nothing new" when he has none. The maker's Undo
  takes the notice away with the change. When the APP takes a man out (a delete, a posting on its date) the others
  read "Ranger taken off" with no name after it.
- **The Unavailable list** (leave, an overseas duty, a course) stays ONE ROW A MAN. A puck dropped on one man's row
  replaces HIM only. A time or remark typed on one man's row of an absence filed for several gives him an input of
  his own; the others are untouched.
- **The picker ("Several people")** offers ALL AVAIL and ALL as two pucks of their own — one of them — where the input
  is a single day of the six kinds; otherwise they are greyed with the reason under them.
- **NOT a finding (D56):** harm that lives only in data stored before this build when the app is right going forward.
  **Known and told to the owner, not a finding:** the printed schedule and its PDF list flying lines only (no Ground
  Programme, as before); the next-week peek draws no counter on ALL AVAIL; the CSV export has no column for a cancel;
  a search for a cancel's reason does not find the input; two different devices never see each other's changes in
  this build (one browser's storage).
- **The demo:** the loaded week is Mon 13 – Sun 19 Jul 2026 (Sat 18 and Sun 19 are the weekend — the days that ask the
  OIL question); a shared Meeting of four on Thu 23 Jul; an Event for ALL on Wed 22 Jul; a Duty for ALL AVAIL on Sat

  Tally, Hex, Grit, Drifter, Hunter, Anvil, Blade (check each is on the crew list; use another if one is not).

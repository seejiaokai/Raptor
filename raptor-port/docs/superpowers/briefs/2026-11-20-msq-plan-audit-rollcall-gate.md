Part of the roll-call in `2026-11-20-msq-plan-audit-rollcall-brief.md`. Scope: `raptor-port/src/state/perms.ts` only,
read whole, region by region (l.1–2910). Every place that decides who may act for, see, or be told about a squadron is
one row. Pure readers with no decision of who are left out (listed at the end). Line numbers are those of the branch
`claude/multi-sqn-b1-107` as read on 20 Nov 26.

Seeded people as the brief names them: Saber (master admin, home 142), Hunter (142's admin), Ranger (142's member),
Kestrel (149's admin), Osprey (149's member). Both squadrons sit at one place, Home base (`units.ts` SEED, l.36–42).
Today no screen picks another squadron and no screen gives View or Plan; each row says where that decides reachability.

The gate's refusal texts are not on-screen words: a refused command comes back as `CmdError('invalid')`
(`command/commit.ts` l.284–285), and the screens say "That did not save" or "That couldn't be completed just now".
So a refusal text or a code comment that names only the master is marked WORDS *(code comment / gate text, not on
screen)*, for the host to decide.

## Unsure

1. **Gate texts on screen.** I read a refused command as surfacing only as "That did not save" / "That couldn't be
   completed just now" (`command/commit.ts` l.284–285, `undo/timeline.ts` l.887). If any screen prints the gate's own
   text, rows 39's refusal and the gate texts saying "master" alone (l.1501 "a Delete posting for a master admin",
   l.1502 and l.1837 "by one who is not a master", l.1853) become on-screen WORDS under D2240.
2. **Row 43, taking Plan away.** The brief and D2201's reading say Plan is given and taken by a master admin only. The
   reshape plan (l.503–506) reads "dropping a right that holds is a removal in its squadron, which only that squadron's
   admins or a master admin make (D2191, D2192)" — written for the re-link's drop, of any right. If the host reads the
   removal of Plan as its squadron's admins', the removal half of row 43 becomes HAS IT; the giving half stays WRONG.
3. **Row 49's fix** extends D2196 to "an admin of every squadron at the place" by my reading of D2241; D2196's own words
   name the master admin alone.
4. **Row 38** reads "a re-link that carries Plan to another man" as giving Plan; it disappears with the plan's
   guardrail 1, which is not built.
5. **Rows 2–3 against 6–15.** I judged the role-only doors by where they are called (files located by search, not each
   call read): doors reached only on own pages are HAS IT (those pages show only a squadron he is admin of); doors
   reached on pages that follow the pick (Edit Schedule, the Board, Logic) are WRONG for a View holder who is an admin.
   A helper reading the callers may find an own-page door also reached from a schedule page.
6. **The member view.** `levelFrom` gives a grant's level whatever the role in force, so a Plan holder (or a master
   admin) switched to his member view counts as `'admin'` of 149 in the member branches of §7, §7b, §7f
   (`lv === 'member' or lv === 'admin'`) — e.g. he may change 149's Tracker while viewing as a member. No harm found (as
   an admin he may anyway); not a row.
7. **Plan on a member's sign-in.** §6's WHAT check (`validGrant`, l.1964, 1968–1972) accepts a grant of level `admin` on
   an account whose role is member; D2199's reading gives Plan to an admin. `levelFrom` then reads that member as
   149's admin (and `notices.ts adminThen` would tell him). Not a who-decision of an actor, so not a row.
8. **Access requests** carry no squadron (§7e's comment, l.2152–2153, filed; D2094's per-squadron request not built), so
   any admin approves or declines any request. Plan is not concerned; not a row.
9. **Reading.** §11's matrix has no squadron for R: every admin reads every record the browser holds (the browser
   mirrors, l.20–22). Not judged here.

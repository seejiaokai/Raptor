## Findings, most serious first

### M1 — medium (near high) — D43, with the marks it left on D27, D28, D32, D33: a placeholder on an off-by-default seat

- **Page lines:** §What earns, "One principle instead of an allow-list … the placeholder pucks default on wherever they
  land, except on a row that came from an input …" and "The placeholder pucks (ALL / ALL AVAIL) behave exactly like
  named people: on by default wherever they can land …".
- **What the full rows require:** D43 — "**D24 stands.** The four exempt KINDS — SC SPARE, AVALON lines, AVALON duty
  desks, BB lines, and a block minted from an AVALON template (D35) — still default OFF with the switch offered. Those
  are about the KIND of work, not about who is standing on it … the placeholder needs no default of its own — it
  inherits the seat's". The AMENDED-BY-D43 mark written on D27, D28, D32 and D33 says the same: "the placeholder pucks
  are ON by default wherever they can land, like named people; **only the four exempt KINDS (D24/D35) default off**."
  D33 lets a placeholder land on "a duty desk's own position" — so an AVALON duty desk and a duty block minted from an
  AVALON template are reachable.
- **What the page says:** placeholders are on by default wherever they land, the input's row named as the *only*
  exception ("except on a row that came from an input"). The "Off by default" bullet a few lines lower lists the same
  seats as off, so two lines disagree on a placeholder dropped on an AVALON desk; an agent following the "One
  principle" line would make it earn by default — what D43 ruled against (OIL owed, D25).
- **Fix:** in both lines, say a placeholder takes its seat's default — "on by default wherever they land, except on the
  off-by-default seats below, where they start off like a named man, and on a row that came from an input, where they
  follow its one OIL answer" [D28, D31, D32, D43, D755].
- (The old short lines of D43/D32/D28 carried the same imprecision; the full rows do not.)

### M2 — medium — D606 against the later D1041, D1044, D1045, D1046: how long an SC / standby line's day counts

- **Page lines:** §How long a day counts, "An SC shift's typed B (its in-time), where filled, counts as work …" and "A
  SPARE that only stands by reports nowhere, so his B does nothing, and switched on in OIL Earn he earns the shift's
  written hours; a shift with its B or R typed flies whole, SPARE rows included … [D606, D1044]".
- **What the full rows require (newest wins, D90):**
  - D1041 (19 Oct 26, scheduler): "A rally typed on an SC MAIN line starts that crew's day as SC's in-time box does
    today — crew rest, the long day, work hours and OIL"; reading: where both are typed, the earlier of the in-time box
    and the rally starts the day. The page states only the typed B.
  - D1045 (19 Oct 26, scheduler): "On a standby line with B or R typed every row is a jet that flies — SPARE rows
    included — in … crew rest and OIL; **the line's day ends at its END, with no debrief added**; a rally is never
    typed on a standby line that will not fly, and one that does not fly is cancelled (CX)". Not cited, not pointed to.
  - D1046: a row on such a line that does not come in is cancelled with its own CX — "its crew rest, OIL and count go".
    Not pointed to (the page's "cancelled = nothing to measure" covers the effect, not the rule).
  - D1044 (cited) makes a typed B or R put every row of the line in flight, SPARE included. "His B does nothing" is
    D606 reading (3) of 7 Oct 26; read on its own it says a typed B never affects a SPARE, which D1044/D1045 overturn
    (it now makes him fly and earn by default). The qualifier "that only stands by" rescues it only for a reader who
    already knows the later rulings.
- **Fix:** reword the SPARE bullet — "On a line with no B or R typed and no "Flies" tick, a SPARE reports nowhere and
  earns nothing by default; switched on in OIL Earn he earns the shift's written hours [D606]. A typed B or R makes
  every row of the line a jet, SPARE included, each earning by default, the line's day ending at its END with no
  debrief; a row that does not come in is cancelled (CX) and earns nothing [D1044, D1045, D1046]" — and add to the SC
  line, or as a "From other areas" pointer, that a rally typed on SC MAIN starts the day as the B does, the earlier of
  the two counting [D1041]. Keep the account's open point (whether a typed B starts a flying SPARE's own span) open.

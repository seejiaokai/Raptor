1. HIGH — The take-back still accepts invented days after a real reminder was read.

State: Saber posts Hex out as SANS from Fri 17 Jul. Hex is planned on Sat 18 Jul, without availability filed. The posting runs and Hex reads the reminder. Saber’s posting command then removes that posting and writes a taken-back line naming 5 Jan 2099.

Actual: The command succeeds. Hex receives a saved taken-back line naming January 2099, although his reminder named July 18.

Rule: D1693, D1699 and D1710 require taking back the actual line, keeping what it said. D2338 concerns his actual planned, unfiled days.

Cause: `raptor-port/src/leavewar/sansunfiled.ts`, `sansBackCovered`, checks date format, ordering, the posting’s date and whether the reminder’s ID was read. It never compares the supplied days with the reminder’s actual days. `sansBackOwed` now correctly requires a run posting to be taken away, but that does not establish what its reminder said.

This is a same-squadron admin command-level hole. I did not find a new member or cross-squadron bypass.

Fix:
- Derive the expected reminder from the transaction’s before-state, before the posting or its other inputs change.
- Require that reminder to exist and have been read.
- Compare the complete proposed days with that expected reminder.
- Retain the existing recipient, signature and one-line-per-command checks.

Test R1 below fails: the command is accepted when refusal is required. This leaves Astra #1 and security Hole 1 partly open; Opus #2’s first-posting example is closed.

2. HIGH — Delete makes an archived man count during a period when he was absent.

State: On Mon 13 Jul, Saber ticks Hex SANS by hand, with “+ SANS” off and no Leave War row. Hex is named on briefings on Tue 14 and Sat 18. Saber archives him on Fri 17, then deletes him on Mon 20 without restoring him.

Actual: While archived, Hex correctly stays out of Saturday’s ALL AVAIL crowd. After Delete, he enters that same Saturday’s crowd again. Tuesday still correctly includes him.

Rule: D323 makes Archive “posted out from today”; D327 preserves earlier attendance without filling the absence gap. Delete does not mean he returned.

Cause:
- `raptor-port/src/leavewar/sync.ts`, `deletePersonOnWar`, and `state/store.ts`, `forgetPersonFrom`, retain no posting window for this no-row, no-history case.
- `sync.ts`, `availableFor`, checks either the deletion date or the archive date. Once deleted, only Monday’s deletion cutoff applies, and Friday’s archive cutoff is ignored.
- The new `stintOnArchive` repair runs on Restore, so this route never benefits from it.

This is an adjacent case left open, not a regression introduced by the two repair rounds. It affects newly created data.

Fix:
- Preserve the known archive cutoff independently of the later deletion.
- When no Leave War row supplies the attendance interval, apply that archive cutoff as well as the deletion cutoff.
- Keep the existing post-in and earlier-stint checks.
- Test dates before Archive, inside the gap and after Delete, then check publication and earned leave separately.

Test R2 below fails: Saturday’s crowd contains Hex after Delete. I have not demonstrated a silent change to an already published version or its earned leave.

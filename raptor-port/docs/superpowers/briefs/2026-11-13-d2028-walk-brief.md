## The change being walked

His ruling D2028: a published day's row with a start and no end keeps the assumed length it was published under until
its next amendment; changing that Logic setting ("Any other row" for ordinary rows, "A sim" for sims — the Logic page's
"Assumed length, no end time" and "Assumed sim length, no end time") raises no pending mark and takes no sign-off down.
Once something else waits on the day, Edit Schedule and the Scheduler Board preview the next amendment under today's
setting, while View-only Sched keeps what went out (D2013). The amendment, published, goes out under today's.

Where it shows: an ALL AVAIL (or ALL) puck on such a row carries a count; a tap on the count opens the ALL AVAIL
window, whose title reads e.g. "09:00–10:00 · no end time, an hour assumed", with the list of people behind the puck.

## Known traps (each has cost a false finding)

- The board is drawn twice (desktop and phone twins): every board selector goes through the driver's `tap()` / `B()`
  or `#schedBoard … :visible`.
- In the demo week Monday–Thursday carry hard conflicts and a publish is refused while a day has one: **Friday is the
  clean weekday; Saturday is the day for OIL.** The seed Saturday already has an SDO duty desk (Plasma, 08:00–18:00).
- **Publishing uses up the working copy's four sign-offs.** To test "the four stay", sign all four again on the
  working copy AFTER publishing and BEFORE changing the Logic setting; picture them ticked first.
- Adding a request on a non-working day raises the OIL question on top of the form — answer it or nothing is filed.
- Read words by the element that draws them (its own trimmed text, box wider than zero); typed words by the `value`
  of `input` / `textarea`. Normalise a no-break space and the en dash before comparing.
- A negative check first proves its subject exists; a "nothing changed" check proves the press happened (picture the
  Logic page showing the new value before going back to the schedule).
- For every crowd check record the PEOPLE listed (callsigns), not only the number.
- The demo roster is large: to make the one-hour and two-hour crowds DIFFER, give one named man (say Ranger) a Ground
  Programme row of his own at 10:15–10:45 on the same day BEFORE publishing — he is free for 09:00–10:00 and busy for
  09:00–11:00. Prove the difference on an unpublished day first (D0 below) so the fixture is known to discriminate.

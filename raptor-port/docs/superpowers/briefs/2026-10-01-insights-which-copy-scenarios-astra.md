# Scenario-design report

1. **Scheduler Board — Insights must have a reachable door above the full-screen board.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Scheduler Board, desktop and phone; D478/IN1. **SETUP:** Sign in as admin, open **Edit Schedule**, open Tuesday’s **Scheduler Board**, and leave a seat change waiting on the published day. **ACTION:** On desktop, use **Insights**; at 390×844, use **Menu → Week insights**. **EXPECTED:** A visible, operable door exists in the board’s own chrome; **Week insights** opens above the board and retains the issued figures. **DISPROOF:** No door is reachable, the shell’s door is covered by the board, or the window opens underneath it. Static inspection predicts this scenario will fail.

2. **Logic rule change must not leak into published work hours before an amendment.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Logic → **Edit rules**; D45, D103, D179, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday with a flying line lacking an explicit in-time; record its pilot’s **Work hours** and Tuesday’s warning figures. **ACTION:** On **Logic**, change **Nominal report before T/O** or **Flight debrief after land** enough to alter that span. **EXPECTED:** Tuesday shows the appropriate pending/sign-off state, but Insights retains the Original’s hours and frozen warning counts until **Publish AL1**; then both move together. **DISPROOF:** The Work hours bar changes immediately while Tuesday is still Original, or the warning figures and hours move at different times. Static inspection identifies this as a probable failure.

3. **A draft neighbour may move a warning that is deliberately live on a published face.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Edit Schedule and View-only Sched; D183–D185 over D179. **SETUP:** Leave Monday draft and publish Tuesday; arrange the same aircrew on a late Monday duty and an early Tuesday flight without a breach. **ACTION:** Make Monday’s duty late enough to create Tuesday’s crew-rest breach. **EXPECTED:** Tuesday’s published day list immediately gains the live crew-rest warning and Monday gains the dotted “breaks tomorrow’s crew rest” mark; Insights immediately adds one Tuesday issue and one **Crew rest** conflict. Tuesday gets no pending change from that live warning alone; Monday remains a working-copy change. Undoing the Monday edit clears all three signs. **DISPROOF:** The published face and Insights disagree, the warning waits for a Tuesday amendment, or Tuesday becomes pending solely because of the live warning.

4. **Looking at Original while AL1 is current must not redirect Insights to the preview.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Edit Schedule and, once its door exists, Scheduler Board; D478 over D477. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday Original, change its programme and publish AL1 so at least one visible Insights number differs. **ACTION:** From Tuesday’s **Plans/version** picker, open **Original** read-only, then open Insights. **EXPECTED:** The day visibly says it is showing Original, while Insights continues to count current AL1. Return to the live copy and the window stays identical. **DISPROOF:** Insights follows Original, the hidden working copy, or changes merely because the preview opened.

5. **Switching a saved plan into a published day remains invisible until issued.**  
   **Surface / rule:** **Plans → Switch to this plan** on Edit Schedule and Scheduler Board; D45, D98, D103, D478. **SETUP:** Save two materially different Tuesday plans; publish Plan A and record every Insights section. **ACTION:** Switch the working copy to Plan B. **EXPECTED:** The edit surface shows Plan B with pending changes and cleared sign-offs, while Insights remains Plan A on every page; after signing and **Publish AL1**, all affected figures become Plan B together. **DISPROOF:** Any tile, bar, idle chip, warning count, or By-day number moves on the plan switch itself.

6. **Hidden warnings follow the version that carried the hide.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Tuesday’s issues list, View-only Sched and Insights; D469, D471, D472, D477, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday with four shown issues and record the issues tile, its “N warning” subtitle, **Conflicts by type**, and Tuesday **By day**. **ACTION:** Hide one warning through its ✕ control, reload, then publish AL1; unhide it, reload, then publish AL2. **EXPECTED:** While each hide/unhide waits, the published day and Insights retain their current count. AL1 reads three, with the hidden struck-out line reachable in Tuesday’s list; AL2 reads four again. **DISPROOF:** The waiting hide changes Insights, the issued hide is still counted, or the tile/type/day totals disagree.

7. **Taking a man off, putting him back, and replacing him must move all crew figures together.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Scheduler Board crew seat; D45, D98, D103, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday with a pilot who has no other sortie and note **Aircrew flying**, his **Flying load**, and both men’s **Not on the flying programme** state. **ACTION:** Remove him; put him back to prove the no-op round trip; then replace him with an idle pilot. **EXPECTED:** Each waiting state leaves Insights at the issued assignment; the exact put-back clears pending and restores sign-offs. Publishing the replacement swaps the two men’s load/idle signs while Sorties and Formations stay fixed. **DISPROOF:** Crew figures move before AL, fail to move after AL, or the no-op round trip remains pending.

8. **Cancelled aircraft line and cancelled formation obey the same boundary.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Scheduler Board’s line/formation **CX** controls; D45, D103, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday and record Sorties, Formations, the affected flyer’s load, and Tuesday **By day**. **ACTION:** Cancel one aircraft line, then a whole formation, leaving both pending; reload and issue AL1. **EXPECTED:** Nothing moves while pending. AL1 removes the aircraft from Sorties and the formation cancellation from both Sorties and Formations, with the affected load and Tuesday row matching. **DISPROOF:** A cancelled item counts after AL, disappears before AL, or the weekly tiles disagree with Tuesday.

9. **Moving take-off time freezes Work hours as well as the day.**  
   **Surface / rule:** Board time box; D45, D103, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday with a pilot whose report-to-debrief span can be changed clearly. **ACTION:** Move **T/O** by at least an hour, confirm Tuesday is pending, reload, then publish AL1. **EXPECTED:** The working day shows the new time, but Insights retains the issued Work hours and LONGDAY status until AL1; afterward the pilot’s hours and all warning figures move together. **DISPROOF:** Hours move while pending, the warning count moves alone, or the revised time never reaches Insights after AL1.

10. **Duty and ground rows added after publication remain outside Insights until issued.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Scheduler Board **+ Block/+ row** and Ground **+ Item**; D45, D103, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday; identify a person not otherwise scheduled and record their absence from Work hours. **ACTION:** Add a timed duty row for that person and a timed ground row for another person. **EXPECTED:** The board shows both rows and pending changes, but Insights’ hours and warning figures remain issued. After AL1, both people appear with the correct spans and any resulting conflicts arrive together. **DISPROOF:** Either row contributes while pending or remains absent after issue.

11. **Inputs-page leave and activity request use their issued filing/details.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Inputs; D177–D179, D103, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday with a scheduled person. **ACTION:** File Tuesday leave through **Inputs → Add input**, then file a timed Duty & commitments/activity request for another person; edit or delete one before publication. **EXPECTED:** Tuesday shows pending and loses its sign-offs, but its published schedule and Insights remain unchanged. The final state—not intermediate edits—appears after AL1; a complete round trip back to issued reads zero pending. **DISPROOF:** The leave/request changes hours, idle names, or conflicts before AL, or Insights reflects an abandoned intermediate state.

12. **Change first, then publish: the only copy moves immediately and becomes the Original unchanged.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Draft Wednesday beside published Tuesday; D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday but leave Wednesday unpublished. **ACTION:** Change Wednesday’s seat, cancel a formation, move a time, and hide a Wednesday warning. **EXPECTED:** Wednesday and every affected Insights figure move immediately because its working copy is its only copy; Tuesday remains issued. Sign and **Publish day** Wednesday: the same figures remain, now backed by Original. **DISPROOF:** Wednesday waits for publication, publishing it changes its figures a second time, or Tuesday’s pending work contaminates Wednesday.

13. **View-only “Working draft” never changes which copy Insights counts.**  
    **Surface / rule:** View-only Sched’s version selector; D478 setting aside D477. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday and make a visible pending seat/time change. **ACTION:** Switch Tuesday to **Working draft** on View-only Sched and open Insights beside it. **EXPECTED:** The day visibly shows the working draft, but Insights still counts current Original/AL; switching back changes no Insights text. **DISPROOF:** The modal follows the version displayed by the page.

14. **Undo/Redo follows the publication boundary, including Undo of AL1.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Top-bar and board Undo/Redo; D98, D103, D148, D478. **SETUP:** Publish Tuesday, make one waiting change, and record Insights. **ACTION:** Undo, Redo, then publish AL1 and press Undo for the publication. **EXPECTED:** The waiting change never moves Insights; Undo clears pending, Redo restores it, and both leave Insights issued. AL1 moves the window. Undo of AL1 retracts it and returns Insights to Original while reopening the change as pending. **DISPROOF:** Undo/Redo affects figures before issue, Undo of publication leaves AL1 counted, or the buttons on the board and shell produce different results.

15. **Unpublish and Load onto working copy keep “latest published” literal.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Version picker and **Unpublish**; D98, D101, D478. **SETUP:** Create materially different Original and AL1 figures. **ACTION:** While AL1 is current, preview Original and choose **Load onto working copy**; reload. Then Unpublish AL1. **EXPECTED:** Loading Original changes only the working copy: Insights remains AL1 and the day is pending. Unpublishing AL1 makes Original the latest published version, so Insights reverts to Original despite the AL1-shaped working edits remaining pending. **DISPROOF:** Loading changes Insights, Unpublish keeps AL1 counted, or the window follows whichever content is editable.

16. **A second week must replace every row and title, not reuse the previous issued world.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Week picker/arrows and Scheduler Board week step; D478. **SETUP:** Give the current week a distinctive published Tuesday/AL1 and leave the next week mostly draft with different totals. **ACTION:** Open Insights, switch to the next week through **Pick a date** or week navigation, reopen, then return. **EXPECTED:** The title dates, all seven By-day rows, tiles, bars, types, and idle chips follow the loaded week and return exactly. **DISPROOF:** Any figure or date survives from the prior week, or the board’s week step differs from the ordinary picker.

17. **Reload at every publication state must preserve the same answer.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Saved state; D56 applies only to legacy-only records, not new writes. **SETUP/ACTION:** In one fresh-data flow, reload after Original, after a pending change, after AL1, after loading Original onto the working copy, and after Unpublishing AL1; wait for **Saved** before each reload. **EXPECTED:** Original stays Original; pending stays invisible to Insights; AL1 stays counted; loading the older version remains working-only; Unpublish returns the count to Original. **DISPROOF:** A reload adopts the working copy, loses the issued version, restores stale Insights values, or changes pending/sign-off state. Do not add migration cases for old demo records.

18. **Every ordinary page, width and eligible role must open the identical window.**  
    **Surface / rule:** D478/IN1 and the overlay roll-call. **SETUP:** Create one published day with a pending change and record the expected Insights text. **ACTION:** At 1440×900 use **Insights** over View-only Sched, Edit Schedule, Inputs, Quals, Logic, Leave War, Tracker, Help and Admin. At 390×844 repeat through **Menu → Week insights**. Repeat as admin, member, and the admin’s **Switch to the member view**. **EXPECTED:** Exact identical contents, correct week title, scrollable modal/sheet, and the modal is topmost at its centre. **DISPROOF:** Any page/role changes the figures, lacks its width-appropriate door, clips the window, or paints another overlay over it. A guest is conditional: the separate Guest view currently has no Insights door, so record that as excluded rather than inventing one.

19. **Opening Insights must not mutate any downstream schedule or entitlement reader.**  
    **Surface / rule:** Regression boundary around IN1; D45, D177–D185. **SETUP:** Keep a published Tuesday with a pending edit and known OIL/Leave War outcome. Record the day bar, opened issues list, ⓘ day popup, pending count, sign-offs, Print/CSV, Leave War award/balance and OIL Earn figures. **ACTION:** Open, scroll and close Insights repeatedly on both widths and after a reload. **EXPECTED:** Every recorded non-Insights value is byte-for-byte or visibly unchanged; only publishing/unpublishing may move the relevant issued outputs. **DISPROOF:** Merely computing or viewing Insights changes a bar/list, pending/signature state, export, Leave War number or OIL result.

## Missing call sites / likely defects

1. **Confirmed missing production door — Scheduler Board.** [SchedBoard.tsx](C:/Users/User/projects/Raptor/raptor-port/src/ui/SchedBoard.tsx:406) builds the full-screen board’s own action bar, but imports neither `setInsights` nor `setDrawer` and renders no Insights control. The shell’s desktop button at [Shell.tsx](C:/Users/User/projects/Raptor/raptor-port/src/ui/Shell.tsx:414) and phone drawer door at [Drawer.tsx](C:/Users/User/projects/Raptor/raptor-port/src/ui/Drawer.tsx:81) sit beneath the fixed board (`z-index:400`). A person therefore cannot open Insights while on the board. The modal’s `z-index:470` is already sufficient once a board-local door is wired.

2. **Probable missing issued-world dependency — Logic-rule changes affecting Work hours.** [officialDiverges](C:/Users/User/projects/Raptor/raptor-port/src/engine/validate.ts:1662) rebuilds the issued world for day-content/input filing divergence, but not for a rule-only change; [issuedWorld](C:/Users/User/projects/Raptor/raptor-port/src/engine/validate.ts:1696) then falls back to current `EVD`, which [computeInsights](C:/Users/User/projects/Raptor/raptor-port/src/engine/insights.ts:37) uses for Work hours. A person can see this as scenario 2: change **Nominal report before T/O** after publication and the Work hours bar is likely to move before the amendment, even though the published face remains Original. This needs the runtime walk before it is called confirmed.

3. **Missing real-browser behavioural coverage.** The new test drives engine writers directly and checks only View-only Sched, Edit Schedule and Inputs. The existing browser check at `geometry.spec.ts` verifies that Insights bars draw, not publication/version behaviour, page doors, roles, overlays or the board. That gap explains why the board door omission is not caught.

## Explicit negatives — checked and apparently wired

- The ordinary-page door is shared correctly: `Shell` mounts one `InsightsModal` outside every page section; desktop and phone both toggle the same `INSIGHTS` state.
- All visible figures now derive from one `computeInsights()` result. The issues tile no longer separately reads working `WARN`, and its total/type/day figures share the same warning bundle.
- `computeInsights()` calls `validate()` on every open, then reads issued days, issued events and `faceWarn()`. It does not inspect `CURPAGE`, the View-only **Working draft** switch or an older-version preview, which is correct under D478.
- `faceWarn()` appears correctly wired for version-owned hides and the D183–D185 live exceptions.
- Normal week loading replaces `DAYS`, publication state and view state before revalidation, so no obvious second-week cache call site is missing.
- The generic modal stacks above the board; layering is ready even though the board lacks a trigger.
- Guest exclusion is explicit rather than accidental: `GuestApp` is a separate tree and the existing account test asserts that `#insightBtn` is not mounted. Under the brief’s “if he can open it” qualification, this is not a finding.
- No migration or old-demo-record scenario is proposed. Every potential failure above can recur with newly created data, satisfying D56.
- Historical memory was used only for initial repository orientation; the report’s conclusions were rechecked against branch `claude/insights-which-copy` at `b739c54c`.

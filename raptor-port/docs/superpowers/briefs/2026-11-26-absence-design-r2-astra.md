# Round 2 — Astra

**Keep the three pages and make existing items behave consistently across them.** Fable’s distinction between each page’s purpose and an item’s editing rules is useful. Below is the combined design I would agree to; points retained from my proposal still need Fable’s response.

**1. A–H: where I move, and why**

- **A. Shared mark at rest — agree with Fable.** People should know before dragging that a box belongs to a group. Use a small, non-numeric shared mark, plus group highlighting on selection. My objection remains to squeezing a count into a half-day box. The mark must be demonstrated at the smallest grid size without obscuring the leave code or other marks.

- **B. “Cutter only” — hold mine.** When Cutter’s box was tapped, show “Change for Cutter only” directly beside “Change for all 3 people”, within the window. Asking the user to tap Cutter again repeats the step he objected to. From an Inputs card or combined schedule row, no individual has been chosen: select a puck there to reach the same action. This is one action with context carried forward.

- **C. Filing verbally approved leave on the war — agree with Fable.** I withdraw the extra filing action. Inputs remains the everyday filing place; the war remains primarily for bidding and its existing direct additions. Existing leave can still be corrected wherever it appears. This keeps one clear way to start each task without making correction depend on birthplace.

- **D. A member dragging his own approved leave — agree with Fable.** Yes: it moves, retaining its approval, under the same rights and checks as on Inputs. Management’s verbal approval remains the real-life prerequisite; the app does not send the leave back for another decision. A member inside somebody else’s group cannot thereby move everyone.

- **E. Adding someone to leave approved from a bid — hold mine.** Yes, an authorised admin should be able to add him. His addition is a direct filing, while the original bidder’s approval history stays his alone. Returning that bidder to pending must never return the added person too. This is a proposed change to the earlier one-person restriction, requiring the owner’s agreement.

- **F. Several people entered together for OD or course — agree with Fable.** One deliberate filing for several people makes one group item, provided their details match. Otherwise identical work requires several later corrections merely because it began on the war. Leave bids remain individual.

- **G. Leave by the hour — a third way.** Keep Times for filed leave, as both proposals ultimately recommend, and show its actual hours alongside the leave and manning counts before Save. Do not make this redesign depend on a speculative removal of an existing ability. Bids keep Whole day, AM and PM; the grid keeps halves.

- **H. Build order — a third way.** Combine the honest group display and deliberate individual change in one delivery, then use Fable’s smaller steps for Duration, movement and creation. Leave the approval-history extension until last. The single order is below.

**Other changes to the proposals**

“Whatever is dragged, the whole item moves” needs more precise wording. A calendar drag changes the whole item’s dates; a schedule puck changes membership. Removing Cutter removes Cutter across the item’s dates, not everyone.

“What you can see, you can change” is also too broad. Members can see other people’s absences without gaining editing rights. Existing group permissions, squadron access and the scheduler’s placement restrictions remain.

Approval history remains meaningful even when it no longer limits ordinary editing. “Back to a bid” belongs to the person whose bid was approved; it cannot become a group-wide reversal.

I would not add a second tiny mark for exact hours without proving that it remains distinguishable beside the shared mark. The day list and item window must show exact hours regardless.

**2. The agreed design as I now see it**

The Leave War is for bidding and judging future manning. Inputs is for members’ everyday filings. The schedule is for schedulers’ planning. Each keeps those jobs. An existing item opens in the same window wherever it appears, with its dates and the actions that person may use. A bid awaits a decision; approved or directly filed leave stands as an input. A group has one shared set of details. Changing one person deliberately gives him his own input.

1. **One window, over the current page.** It shows people, dates, Duration, remarks and permitted documents and actions. Closing returns to the same place. A day holding several entries lists each separately and retains its permitted Add actions. Page-based “change it elsewhere” instructions disappear as their replacements become available.

2. **One set of rights.** A member corrects his own input; whole-group editing remains with those entitled to change everyone. A scheduler’s placement, rejection or cancellation cannot be cleared by making a personal copy. View-only Sched stays read-only. Existing approved leave does not become pending merely because its dates change.

3. **A group looks and behaves like a group.** One card on Inputs and one row on the schedule; one marked box per person on the war. Selecting or beginning a drag identifies the whole affected group before a change happens, including people outside the view. Shared dates, times and remarks change everyone. Membership drops retain the agreed add, replace and remove rules.

4. **One-person changes are deliberate and complete.** “Change for Cutter only” prepares his full existing details for correction. Only a successful, changed Save separates him; Cancel, unchanged Save and a blocked Save leave the group intact. One Undo restores the whole operation. His private paper needs no separation and remains visible only to him and admins.

5. **One Duration control.** Whole day, AM, PM and Times for filed inputs where duration applies; the first three for bids. Moving dates preserves duration. Exact hours remain exact on the schedule, while the war explains their half-day counting. Medical keeps its own counting and overlap rules.

Medical remains individual. ALL / ALL AVAIL retains its crowd controls. Delete and Reject remain different actions. OIL awards are never carried along by an absence move. Published schedules change only through signed amendments.

The build order is:

1. **Common window:** local opening, full dates and consistent permitted editing from all three pages.
2. **Groups:** combined rows, shared marking, deliberate one-person changes, personal documents and one Undo; remove the silent separations together.
3. **Duration:** the common choices and clear counting beside exact hours.
4. **Movement:** eligible items draggable on the war, whole groups moving together, with a tap-and-keyboard route and clear refusals.
5. **Creation:** one group OD/course filing on the war; several people and full date ranges when adding from the schedule.
6. **Approval history:** adding people to previously bid-approved leave, with each person’s history and later decisions kept correct.

Draw each delivery before building it. Group changes, documents and approval history carry the greatest risk. Their checks must include Cancel, blocked Save, Undo, medical overlaps and changes spanning published days.

**3. Questions left for him**

1. **When Cutter returns earlier than his group, should “Change for Cutter only” work wherever the group is opened?**  
   **Recommend yes**, directly available when Cutter is already known, separating him only on a successful changed Save. This changes the earlier decision against a one-person shortcut.

2. **When an admin enters several people together for one OD or course, should they become one group item?**  
   **Recommend yes**, where their dates and details match. Separate filings remain separate and never join themselves.

3. **When an OD or course occupies only a morning, afternoon or stated hours, should the same Duration choices be available on every page?**  
   **Recommend yes**, with Whole day initially selected for OD and course. This changes the war’s whole-day-only rule and its requirement to visit Inputs for other hours; counting rules remain unchanged.

4. **When someone joins leave that originally came from another person’s approved bid, should an admin add him to that item?**  
   **Recommend yes**, recording his addition as directly filed leave and preserving the bidder’s own approval history. A later bid decision affects only its original person.

This is a design response, not a browser check; the proposed marks and button arrangement still need pictures at phone and desktop sizes.

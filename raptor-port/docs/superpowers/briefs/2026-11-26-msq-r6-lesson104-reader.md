## Verdict

Mostly faithful. Five changes recommended: one in the lesson's rule, two in its readings, one on each page line.
One record issue outside the brief.

## 1. The rule: "never as a made-up plain box" is not his and is not shown true

- He said the drawing was ugly and the real list is fine. He did not say "made-up". Round 6's own page says round 5's
  drawing "showed the app as it really is today" (the browser's own plain list), and D2645's row says chat 188's
  drawer checked this. Round 5's D12 pictures are desktop pictures (`e11-d-plannedby`). On a desktop browser that list
  really is white and plain. So "made-up" states, unmarked, something the record contradicts. "Box" is also wrong:
  it was an open list.
- "His phone" is fair as the case he corrected: he judged it by his iPhone. But schedulers sign PLANNED BY mostly at a
  desk (D820), and the rule as written would make a desktop picture draw the iPhone's list.
- His second words, "No need for an app version", are only in the readings. Fine as a lesson, but the rule can carry
  them in a few words.

Replacement rule:
> A list of people to pick from is drawn as it really shows on his phone, never in a look his phone does not show; no
> app-drawn version is drawn in its place.

## 2. The readings

- "which he is happy with": he said "ok". Use **"which he is ok with"**. Round 6's page says "happy" too; the same fix
  applies there if it is touched again.
- "it holds for any control the browser or phone draws itself — a mock-up shows it as on his device": it is marked as
  the agent's reading, so it is allowed (lesson 103 widens the same way). It is a widening he did not correct, so keep
  it marked. It should also say what a **desktop** picture does, or it leaves the D820 gap open. Suggested:
  *"it holds for any control the browser or phone draws itself: a phone picture shows it as his iPhone does, a desktop
  picture as the desktop browser does."*
- **The lesson-68 sentence is not accurate as written.** "Found on the running app" is not lesson 68's rule. It is the
  agent's reading in lesson 68 (D1496 reading 2). His rule there is only "standardised to the rest of the app". The
  phone's own list *is* the app's list on his phone. So lesson 104 is lesson 68 applied, the way lesson 103 calls
  itself, and narrows only that reading. Also, lesson 68's tail says it is narrowed by 104 "for a list of people", but
  104's sentence narrows it for every control. The two disagree.
  Replacement (inside the readings):
  > it is lesson 68 applied to a list the phone draws itself; it narrows lesson 68's reading "found on the running app"
  > to mean as it shows on his phone, not on the test browser on his PC.

  Lesson 68's tail can then stay as it is: "lesson 104 for a list of people".
- Head: "then, shown both," is fair. N10 offered the app-drawn list and "Keep the plain list".

## 3. The two page lines

- **How we work §Design:** "a list of people to pick from as his phone really shows it, never a made-up plain box
  (lesson 104) [D2645, D2646]". It has the same "made-up plain box" problem. Replacement:
  > a list of people to pick from as his phone really shows it, never a look his phone does not show (lesson 104)
  > [D2645, D2646].
- **People & accounts §Many squadrons:** "these lists stay the browser's own, as his iPhone shows them [D2646]". This
  line also names "+ Commitment"'s list of SANS aircrew, so "these lists" takes in a list D2646 does not cover. D2646
  covers only the sign-off boxes and "+ Input"'s Person list (and calls even that the agent's reading). It also drops
  "no app version". Replacement:
  > the sign-off boxes and "+ Input"'s Person list stay the browser's own list, as his iPhone shows them — no app-drawn
  > drop-down [D2646].

## 4. Outside the brief: the record calls D2646 a narrowing when it is a reversal

D2645's rule was "drawn in the app's own drop-down look, never as the browser's plain list". D2646 makes the drawing
the browser's or phone's own list. That reverses D2645's rule, not narrows it. Under D136 and D201, D2645 is replaced
and should be marked and archived, with D2646 carrying lesson 104. The alternative is to rewrite D2645's mark to
"REPLACED". The host should decide which and say so.

## The K0 settlement — where I built it, and why

**As a second named clause of `letOpenOn`**, not in the `held` formula. `letOpenOn` already means "the one squadron this
window may stand live on although it is not the input's own", and is worked out **once, when the note is taken** — which is
exactly the brief's "the page is his home on the day the window opens". Put in `held`, the clause would be judged on every
draw, so it would read the day at each draw (the F12 fault in another shape), or need a third field in the note. The
clause: the input names him (`holds`) **and** the page the window stands on is his own home today (`pageSqn ===
myUnit(todayIso())` — K9's `pageIsHome` asked of the window's own page; on his own pages the same question, and on a
schedule page the squadron in view). `inputSqn` gained nothing.

Its width, as the brief draws it: it also lets open, on a moved master admin's new home page in the admin view, his old
input of the squadron he left (Saber moved 142 → 149: his old 142 duty on his 149 page). The list never shows it there
(K9's reach test, D2729 Q8), so no press of the list reaches it; the Medical tab (finding 1) and a raw open do. If the
orchestrator wants it as narrow as the list — "a squadron he is moving to" only — it is K9's `aheadAt` test added to the
clause.

# Cold read of the warning lines

Read alone, as a scheduler meets each line in the list ("Ranger — <line>"). a) what happened, b) why it is a problem, c) what to do, d) two readings / unclear words, e) would a person say it across a desk. Then the verdict.

---

**1.** Clash: RU (T/O 13:00, landing …) & BRIEFING 12:40–13:00
a) Ranger is on RU taking off at 13:00 and in a BRIEFING 12:40–13:00. b) Not stated: the briefing ends exactly at T/O, so the overlap is not visible; presumably the time before T/O is needed. c) Not stated. d) "landing …" reads as text cut off: is the landing unknown, unset, or truncated? e) The bare "…" is odd.
**UNCLEAR.** Suggest: "Clash: RU (T/O 13:00, no landing yet) & BRIEFING 12:40–13:00" (and see 6: the reason the two clash is still not visible).

**2.** Clash: VL BFM (T/O 12:00, landing assumed 13:00) & BRIEFING 12:40–13:00
a) Airborne 12:00–13:00 (landing filled in by the app) while a BRIEFING runs 12:40–13:00. b) He cannot be in the air and in a briefing. c) Implied: move one. d) None. e) Fine.
**CLEAR.**

**3.** Tight turn: VL BFM → RU, 0 min (needs 120). VL BFM landing assumed 13:00 (T/O + 1h), RU T/O 13:00
a) Lands VL BFM 13:00 (assumed, T/O + 1h) and takes off RU at 13:00. b) No gap; 120 needed. c) Implied. d) "needs 120" — units only implied by "0 min"; elsewhere the app says "2h". e) A little telegram-like but readable.
**CLEAR BUT.** "Tight turn: VL BFM → RU, 0 min (needs 2h). VL BFM landing assumed 13:00 (T/O + 1h), RU T/O 13:00"

**4.** Misses VL BFM debrief 13:00–15:00 (landing assumed): MEETING 13:30–14:00
a) The VL BFM debrief is 13:00–15:00 (worked from an assumed landing); a MEETING 13:30–14:00 sits inside it. b) He would miss part of the debrief. c) Not stated. d) The colon does the work of "because of" — a reader can take MEETING as the thing missed, not the cause. e) Stiff.
**CLEAR BUT.** "Misses VL BFM debrief 13:00–15:00 (landing assumed) for MEETING 13:30–14:00"

**5.** Misses RU brief 10:40–13:00: BRIEFING 12:40–13:00
a) RU's brief runs 10:40–13:00; a BRIEFING 12:40–13:00 overlaps its end. b) Misses the end of the brief. d) "brief" (the flight's) and "BRIEFING" (another item) are near-identical words for different things; same colon issue as 4. A 2h20 brief also looks odd cold.
**CLEAR BUT.** "Misses the end of RU brief 10:40–13:00 for BRIEFING 12:40–13:00"

**6.** Clash: RU (T/O 13:00, landing 14:30) & BRIEFING 12:40–13:00

**CLEAR BUT.** "Clash: BRIEFING 12:40–13:00 runs up to RU T/O 13:00 (landing 14:30)"

## Families worded inconsistently
- **Clash vs Misses for the same shape:** 5 "Misses RU brief 10:40–13:00: BRIEFING 12:40–13:00" and 6 "Clash: RU (T/O 13:00, landing 14:30) & BRIEFING 12:40–13:00" — same briefing, same T/O, two different headings.
- **Clash vs On standby:** 7 "Clash: SC AM 07:00–13:00 & OFT 1 10:00–11:30" vs 14 "On standby: SC AM 07:00–13:00 & LECTURE 09:00–10:00" — same layout, different heading, the difference unexplained.
- **in-time vs rally:** 16 "SC AM in-time 06:00–07:00" vs 33 "SC AM rally 06:00" (and P1/P2 "start" for the rally).
- **Front seat vs FCP:** 50 / 54 "in the front seat of" vs 51 / 52 "in FCP on".
- **DAAR vs day AAR:** 57 "Not DAAR current … call for day AAR".
- **Capitals:** 10 / 21 "AVALON DUTY" vs 13 / 19 "AVALON duty"; "MEETING" (4, 16, 25) vs "Meeting" (15, 28).
- **Downchit lines:** 19 "Downchit + planned: AVALON duty …" vs 21 "Downchit + planned: AVALON DUTY … (ATT C, medically down)" — same case, one with a bracket.
- **Bracket after a debrief:** 4 "(landing assumed)" vs 25 "(landing + 2h)" — same slot, different kind of fact.
- **Crew-rest head:** 30–32 "Crew rest:", 39 "Tight crew rest:", 33–35 no head at all.
- **Turn needs:** 3 / 29 "needs 120" vs elsewhere "2h".
- **No-OIL lines:** 63 / 64 "until it is fixed", 65 "Type it so…", 66 no action.
- **Approval:** 45 "CO approval needed" vs 46 "Approval needed" (no approver).

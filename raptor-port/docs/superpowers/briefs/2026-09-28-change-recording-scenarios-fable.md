**Headline (what is MISSING, ranked):**
1. Cutting over `people` + `settings` does NOT make "Add a person", Archive, Restore, Restore as, Delete, Post out or Undo post out undoable — every one of those commands also writes the war's `lw.postouts/all` record (D308/D320 stints), and `lw.postouts` is a deferred collection that makes the whole entry ineligible (`timeline.ts:346-354`). Only Quals row edits, callsign renames, accounts, the guest switch and the Logic/template/arrangement settings would become undoable. The owner has to choose (Q1).
2. Three "seen" commands (`changes.seen`, `access.seen`, `person.backSeen`) become undo steps the moment settings/people are cut over; a MEMBER's `changes.seen` step is one his Undo can never take (the commit gate refuses the restore as "an admin's record", `perms.ts:425-430`), so it is never marked undone and his Undo is STUCK behind it with the wrong message.
3. No roster rule is re-checked on restore: a rename or an un-archive undo can put two roster men on one callsign; undoing an add can delete a man who is on the schedule; undoing an account change can put a sign-in ON for an archived man (D322) or leave no admin (D148's named guard); a Delete's undo is dead only for schedule/input records, not for the person or his account (D287).
4. D148's "says who": today no refusal ever names a person. A `remote` envelope is ignored by the timeline (`timeline.ts:156-158`) so the refusal comes from the transaction's stale-revision check and reads "…something else changed on this week. Try again." — wrong for a war/roster record and false ("Try again" can never succeed). The design's stateless pre-check (`revisionOf` vs `expected`, §8.1) is imported at `timeline.ts:20` and never called.
5. The Quals page keeps its own copy of the LoX column list (`QualsPage.tsx:284-285`) — an undo of a column removal will repaint the Leave War's catalogue but not the Quals page, and the page's next column edit writes the stale list back over the undo.

## What changed in this build (so your EXPECTED lines make sense)

- With the admin's own "view as member" switch on, the bell has NO holiday line and is not lit by one; switched back,
  the line is there again.
- A passing note ("Input added", "Schedule updated"…) never lands on the bar "Not swapped — OIL not answered": while
  the bar is up the note sits just above it.
- "Both pucks flash" when the question is left unanswered — also the puck that was DRAGGED where the man keeps his
  seat (the Unavailable row from a seat; an add from a seat onto a row's open place), and again after "Answer OIL"
  and a second "Don’t swap".
- A holiday with no name of its own (the Leave War's "PH" button): the list says "Fri 17 Jul is now a public
  holiday." and its second line is "Public holiday"; the bell's line reads "Public holiday" in bold.
- Behind the question, and after "Don’t swap", the day's issue count, its warning list and the rings on the pucks
  are those of the day AS IT STANDS — never of the swap that was not made.
- "Answer OIL" never makes a different change from the one that was waiting: if the row has changed in any way
  meanwhile (another man added to it), it says "That row has changed — not swapped" and makes nothing.
- Switching OIL Earn on takes a waiting change's bar away.
- On the Unavailable row an armed TAP keeps the row's ring while its question is up and after it is left
  unanswered; the ring goes when the hand-over is made.
- ONE bell line a holiday, whatever is done to its dates: lengthened → one line for the whole holiday; shortened →
  the line and the list name the days that are still a holiday; taken away and made again over other dates → one
  line; one holiday entered Friday to Monday → one line and one list for the whole of it.
- On a phone held sideways (844 × 390) the question's two buttons stay in sight, and in the one list Later and Save
  stay in sight at the window's foot while the cards scroll (at every size).

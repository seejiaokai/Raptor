## Finding 1 — P2: the fallback branch of `crowdSide` disagrees with the screen and with `daySnap`

**The case, step by step** (weekday harness, the same stand-in roster change the fix's own "gone from the roster" test uses):
1. Tuesday "Briefing" 09:00, no end, ALL AVAIL. Published with "Any other row" at 1h. The crowd is all four men.
2. "Any other row" changed 1h → 2h, then a check. Nothing waits — correct.
3. A man "nova" becomes available to the crowd resolver. He is busy 10:30–10:45 from something that is not on this day's schedule or filing, so he is free over the kept hour and busy over 2h.
4. Kept comparison: nova joins, so there is a crowd entry. Today's comparison: the crowd equals the issued one, so there is no crowd entry. Nothing else waits, so `crowdSide` returns the kept comparison (its last line).
5. `dayDeltaCore` is therefore non-empty, so `untouchedKept` leaves the day out and the working copy is drawn under today's 2h. The window shows the issued four, with no nova.
6. The list has 1 OIL item and `daySigned` is false. `pendItemWords` reads `{"where":"What this day earns","to":"changed"}`. `crowdChange` counts under the settings laid on the day, which are today's, finds no difference, and falls through to the generic words.
7. Sign and publish the amendment. The issued crowd is still `[bane, ignite, pump, stiff]` with no nova, and `dayDelta` is then empty. The amendment did not make the change the list announced.

**Cause:** `raptor-port/src/engine/publish.ts`, `crowdSide`, the last line: `return other||t.oil.some(isCrowdEntry)?t:k;`. It returns `k`, the kept comparison. But `validate.ts` `untouchedKept` decides from `dayDeltaCore(di).length>0`, which includes `k`'s crowd entry, so the day is drawn under today's settings. `daySnap` always freezes under today's (`oilEvidence(di,undefined,null)`). This is exactly where `crowdSide`'s "other" and "`untouchedKept` did not lay the kept settings" stop meaning the same thing (question 2).

**Recommended fix — needs the owner's word, because there is no fully consistent answer under D2028 as worded.** Either choice in this state breaks one rule: reading clean breaks D45 (the kept-settings screen would show nova with no pending mark); pending breaks D2013 (the line names a change that neither the screen nor the amendment makes). My recommendation:
1. When `crowdSide` returns `k` because of a crowd-only change under the kept settings, have `untouchedKept` lay the kept settings too. Expose the choice as one function both sides ask, e.g. `crowdSideRv(di)`, instead of `dayDeltaCore().length>0`. The window and `crowdChange` then show and name nova.
2. Have `daySnap` / `freezeWarn` freeze that amendment under the kept `rv`, not today's, when it was born only from such a change. The list, the screen and the record then agree.
3. Put to the owner whether such an amendment should keep the old settings. Step 2 narrows D2028's "reaches the day from its next amendment".

The smaller alternative is to fix only the words (`crowdChange` counts under the comparison's `rv`). It leaves the screen and the amendment disagreeing with the list.

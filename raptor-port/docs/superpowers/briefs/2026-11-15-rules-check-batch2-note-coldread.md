# The Logic page's note — the cold read (design guide §1a test 9; chat 79, 15 Nov 26)

The note under the changed-settings strip (D2015, D2044). Each reading by a fresh helper given the wording ALONE.

**The writer's facts, listed before the read:** F1 a published day stays on the Logic settings that stood when it was published, all of them · F2 a setting changed now does not reach it until it is published again · F3 where a changed OIL setting would change that day's OIL, the day reads pending at once · F4 the day's OIL stays as published meanwhile · F5 the remedy: publish it again.

**Round 1** — "A published day keeps the settings it was published under until it is published again. If a changed OIL setting would change a published day's OIL, that day reads pending." The reader got F1–F3; F4 was LOST: "the day's OIL no longer shows a firm value … It is not clear whether OIL is lost"; F5 guessed ("I would guess re-publishing"). Two readings it named that the screen settles: "settings" all or only OIL (the strip's first line names them), and "OIL" undefined (the app's own word). The wording went back.

**Round 2** — "A published day keeps the settings it was published under, and the OIL they gave it, until it is published again. If a changed OIL setting would change that OIL, the day reads pending." The reader got F1–F4 ("keeps the OIL those settings gave it … until the day is published again"); F5 again a guess from "until it is published again". The same two context-only readings. Built (`src/ui/LogicPage.tsx`, its test `logic.test.tsx`); the words remain his to change on his look (D2015).

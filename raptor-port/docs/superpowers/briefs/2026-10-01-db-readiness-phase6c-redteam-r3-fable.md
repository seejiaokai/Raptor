(3) `acceptInput` `onDay`: same predicate (a `kept` row of an 'r' request on a covered day still stands, so [REQ-ORPHAN-ROW] 3's adopt keeps working). (4) `acceptedDay`, `reconcileDayFiling`, `reconcileLandedAcc`: same. (5) `landedStanding`: skip rows that do not stand (`continue`), so a dead `cx` row is not the claim's standing. (6) `unacceptInput`: leave as is (✕ removing a dead row is the holder's own act). (7) Tests, red first: the load scenario above (R's Wednesday row put back, nothing pending, no "on Monday's programme"); Accept after ✕ with a dead row elsewhere lands on the covered day; the OIL case (a dead cancelled Friday row, the live Saturday landing pays).

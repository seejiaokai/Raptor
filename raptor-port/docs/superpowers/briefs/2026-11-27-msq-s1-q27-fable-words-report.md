## The three questions

(a) 13's "clear" with no Clear button. It reads right. "Clear" is the ordinary word for emptying a box, and the approved sister note already says "add or clear the new qualification"; a different verb here would give one box two names. Keep it. If he wants a real button named instead, the best true alternative is "Blocked: press Done, then press Undo again." — true, shorter, but it also ends editing on the Quals page, so I rank it second. A third, longer but most literal: "Blocked: delete the words in “Add qualification”, then press Undo again."

(b) "try again" in 15 and 16. In 16 yes: the press cannot be named, and "try again" is the plain phrase anyone would use. Its one weak spot is the "Retry" first half ("press Retry …, then try again"), which echoes; in a safety net no press reaches today, that is acceptable. In 15 the press can be named — it is a card — so name it (adjusted above) and the echo goes.

The Tracker's rules page: before and after

The trial you approved: the page keeps your standing rules and what you like; what is already built and checked by the app's own tests leaves it. Nothing is deleted — your words stay in the full record.

Before
20,007 characters — 73 of your Tracker rulings written out
After
10,631 characters — about half: 34 rulings still written out, 39 listed by number only

How a rule was judged

One question for each: if this line were gone, would the next AI working on the Tracker likely get it wrong — and would nothing in the app's checks catch it?

  • If a check in the app fails the moment the thing breaks, the rule leaves the page and is listed by number at the bottom.
  • If no check catches it, or a check only covers part of it, the rule stays.
  • Your standing wishes, things you said to leave alone, and work not yet built always stay.
  • A new safety check stops anyone listing a rule as "held by tests" when no check actually names it. I tried it with a rule that has no check: it refused, naming the rule.

Four that left the page

Left the page

The import's refusal message must read, word for word: "Student marks not brought in: they belong to a chart this file doesn't carry…"

A check compares the message on screen with your exact wording. Change one word and it fails.

Left the page

Logging out with unsaved chart changes asks first: Save them, Discard them, or Stay.

A check presses each of the three answers and confirms what happens, and another confirms nothing is asked when nothing is unsaved.

Left the page

The Tracker uses Raptor's colours fully — backgrounds, text and the event colours, sim turned to amber.

A check goes through every colour the Tracker draws and fails if one is not Raptor's, or if an old Tracker colour comes back.

Left the page

Every control on the Tracker's top bar is as tall as its dropdowns — 30 on a computer, 26 on a phone.

A check opens the real app at phone and computer sizes and measures each control's height.

Two that stayed, and why

Stayed

Your charts, syllabi and ball details are kept: nothing wipes them without putting them back, and you are told first so you can export a copy.

"Tell him first" is something the AI must do, not something the app does — no check can catch it being skipped.

Stayed

On a phone a finger scroll glides — it speeds up with the finger and slows to a stop after you lift, never stopping dead.

No check covers how the scroll feels, so without this line a later change could quietly make it stop dead.

What is not settled yet

  • This is a trial on the Tracker alone. No other area's page has changed, and nothing is live.
  • One rule (fixing the stray scroll bars before two other jobs) is finished work, not a built feature. It sits on the "held by tests" list for now; moving it to the archive waits for your word.
  • Before any of this goes live, two readers still check that no rule's meaning changed.

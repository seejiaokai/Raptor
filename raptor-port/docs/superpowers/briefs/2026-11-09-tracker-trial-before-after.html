The Tracker's rules page: before and after

The trial you approved (9 Nov): the page keeps your standing rules and what you like; what is built and fully checked by the app's own tests leaves it. Nothing is deleted. Your words stay in the full record.

Before
20,007 bytes — 73 of your Tracker rulings written out
After
15,593 bytes — 54 written out, 19 listed by number only

How a rule was judged

One question for each: if this line were gone, would the next AI working on the Tracker likely get it wrong, and would nothing in the app's checks catch it?

  • If a check fails the moment any part of the rule breaks, the rule leaves the page and is listed by number at the bottom.
  • If no check catches it, or a check covers only part of it, the rule stays written out.
  • Your standing wishes, things you said to leave alone, and work not yet built always stay.
  • A new safety check refuses any rule listed as "held by tests" when no check names it. Tried with a rule that has none: it refused, naming the rule.

Three that left the page

Left the page

The import's refusal message reads, word for word: "Student marks not brought in: they belong to a chart this file doesn't carry…"

A check compares the message on screen with your exact wording. Change one word and it fails.

Left the page

Logging out with unsaved chart changes asks first: Save them, Discard them, or Stay.

A check presses each of the three answers and confirms what happens, and another confirms nothing is asked when nothing is unsaved.

Left the page

The Tracker uses Raptor's colours fully: backgrounds, text and the event colours, sim turned to amber.

A check goes through every colour the Tracker draws and fails if one is not Raptor's, or if an old Tracker colour comes back.

Two that stayed, and why

Stayed

Your charts, syllabi and ball details are kept: nothing wipes them without putting them back, and you are told first so you can export a copy.

"Tell him first" is something the AI must do, not something the app does. No check can catch it being skipped.

Stayed

On a phone a finger scroll glides: it speeds up with the finger and slows to a stop after you lift, never stopping dead.

No check covers how the scroll feels, so without this line a later change could quietly make it stop dead.

What the two readers changed

Two separate readers (Astra and a fresh Opus) opened every test the first pass relied on. Neither found a rule of yours cut outright. Both found that 20 of the 39 "fully checked" rulings were checked only in part, plus one listed by number whose only "check" is a comment. All 21 went back on the page in their old words, under a heading that says their tests hold only part.

Back on the page

A change to a student's pace, end dates or lull periods can be undone; adding, removing or renaming students, courses and syllabi, event details, and an Import cannot.

The checks test the first half only. Nothing would fail if Import became undoable, so the "cannot" list must stay written out.

Back on the page

Every control on the Tracker's top bar is as tall as its dropdowns (30 on a computer, 26 on a phone), the bar no taller and nothing moving.

A check measures each control, but never the bar itself, so a taller bar would pass.

What is not settled yet

  • This is a trial on the Tracker alone. No other area's page has changed, and nothing is live.
  • Each of the 21 could leave the page later, once a check covering the missing part is written. The readers named each missing check; writing them is a job for later, on your word.
  • One rule (fixing the stray scroll bars before two other jobs) is finished work, not a feature. It is written out again for now; moving it to the archive waits for your word.

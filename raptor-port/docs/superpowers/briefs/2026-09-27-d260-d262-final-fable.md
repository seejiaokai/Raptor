## Findings, most severe first

### F1 · D262 · The mouse's edge scroll runs over the card's own controls, the frozen name column and the early month buttons — the grid runs off toward January while the admin is nowhere near the days' edge. NEW.
**Where:** `src/leavewar/ui/select.ts:848-852` (`band()` tests x only, against `wrap`'s rect, with no floor at the days' left edge),
`:871` (`overGrid` is true for anything `isGrid` accepts), and `src/leavewar/ui/Matrix.tsx:1175-1176` (`inGrid` = the whole `.card`,
which holds the `card-hd` row — Manning · ⚙ · Rearrange · OIL · zoom — as well as the wrap).
**Why it happens:** `leftEdge()` is `wrap.left + frozenWidth` (the days' left edge). `band()` returns −1 for EVERY `mx < left + 36`,
so the whole region left of the days counts as the left band: the frozen name/counter cells, the JAN/FEB month buttons (the
strip is sticky in the two frozen columns), and the header row's left cluster (the card is `inGrid`, and no y is checked at all).
Once `bandsLive` is true (the mouse has been in the middle of the days once — always, in practice), resting the mouse on any
of those scrolls the days 18 px/frame toward January; at `scrollLeft` 0 the loop keeps re-requesting frames (`:861` re-arms
whether or not it moved) until the mouse leaves the region. The walk's C6 could not see it: the demo war opens on JAN, so
`scrollLeft` was already 0 when the mouse went to Manning / Legend (C5b).
**Scenario:** admin, JAN–DEC 26, press MAR, tap SLASH's LL (or any chip drawn in March) → Move → move the mouse up to the
**Manning** button (or ⚙, OIL, a callsign in the frozen column, the JAN button) and rest it. **What happens:** the days scroll
left under the header until January fills the screen; the ghost still reads "Move 1 entry". **Expected:** nothing moves until the
mouse is in the 36 px band at the days' own left edge (or the right edge); D262 says "when I DRAG to the EDGES … auto scroll".
**Fix — `select.ts wireMove`:**
1. In `band()` (`:848`) add the geometry of the days: `const r = wrap.getBoundingClientRect(); if (my < r.top || my > r.bottom) return 0`
   (the header row and the banner are out), then `return mx >= left && mx < left + EDGE ? -1 : mx > r.right - EDGE && mx <= r.right ? 1 : 0`
   — the left band is `[left, left+EDGE)`, never the frozen columns or the sticky month buttons under them.
2. Narrow `overGrid` (`:871`) to the scroller itself, not the card: `wrap.contains(e.target) || e.target.closest('[data-testid="sticky-head"], [data-testid="hscroll"]')`
   (keep `opts.isGrid` for the click-cancel, where the wider "the card" is the right meaning).
3. In `edgeStep` (`:854-862`), when `wrap.scrollLeft === before` (the end of the year reached) do NOT re-request a frame — return;
   `onMouseMove` re-arms it on the next movement. Ends the hot loop at 0 / max.
4. Pin in `movewire.test.ts`: a mousemove on an element inside the card but outside the wrap's rect (y above `r.top`) scrolls nothing;
   a mousemove at `x < leftEdge` (over the names) scrolls nothing; the existing right/left-band cases stay green. Re-walk C6 with the
   grid parked on March, not January, and add "mouse rests on Manning / a callsign / JAN" to the roll-call.

### F2 · D262 · Phone: a tap that closes a sheet opened MID-move is read by the move first — it stages the chip on the day under the finger, or ends the move when the tap falls on the page. Cancel half NEW; stage half on `9f7814d9` already.
**Where:** `select.ts:906-923` (`onClick`, a document CAPTURE listener registered when the move began);
`src/leavewar/ui/Sheet.tsx:102` (on a coarse pointer the scrim is `pointer-events: none` — a tap outside the panel lands on
whatever is UNDER it) and `:103-120` (the sheet's tap shield is a document capture `click` listener registered when the SHEET
mounts — later than the move's, so it runs second; its `stopPropagation` cannot reach the move's listener).
**Scenario (phone, admin):** pick a chip up → tap a callsign in the frozen column (the person sheet opens) or ⚙ → tap the grid
beside the panel to close it. **What happens:** if the finger landed on a day cell, `onPick(date)` runs before the shield: the
landing is STAGED there, the banner turns to "Move 1 entry here? Confirm / Cancel" — a landing nobody chose, one tap from
Confirm; if the finger landed on the empty page, `onCancel()` runs: the move ends silently while the sheet closes. On desktop
the scrim is a real element and `[data-testid$="scrim"]` protects it — this is the phone only. Not walked: C5b covered the
Legend / under-manned shades, which are real elements (`umscrim`, `role="dialog"`), not a `Sheet`.
**Fix:** at the top of `wireMove.onClick` (`:907`) add `if (document.querySelector('.bidsheet')) return` — a tap while a sheet is
up belongs to the sheet and its shield, never to the move (Matrix's own figure-select Escape guard, `Matrix.tsx ~1163`, uses the
same test). Do the same at the top of the gesture's `hit` (`:889`: `t => document.querySelector('.bidsheet') ? null : dateAt(t)`) so a
hold-drag through a transparent scrim cannot arm either. Pin in `movewire.test.ts`: mount a `div.bidsheet`, click `b` → no pick;
click `outside` → no cancel; remove it → both work again. Walk on the phone: pick up → tap a name → tap a day beside the panel.

### F3 · D262 · A press-and-drag released OFF the grid lands the chip on the last day the pointer crossed. NEW (the press-and-drag is new).
**Where:** `select.ts:890-895` — `current` keeps `dragDay` sticky (`if (d) dragDay = d`) and returns it whatever is under the
pointer now, so `finish(true)` at the release hands that day to `onSelect` → `onPick` → a desktop LANDS it, a phone stages it.
**Scenario:** admin, chip picked up → press on 9 Feb, drag (arms at 4 px) across 10–12 Feb, then up off the card onto the page
header and release, meaning "never mind". **What happens:** the chip lands on 12 Feb (the last day crossed) — the pointer was
not over it. A CLICK on that same empty spot cancels; a RELEASE there lands. **Expected:** a release off the grid lands nothing
(the move stays on, or ends — either is honest); the two gestures on "an empty area outside the grid" should agree.
**Fix:** in `current` (`:890`): `const el = document.elementFromPoint(x, y); const d = dateAt(el); if (d) dragDay = d; else if (!el || (opts.isGrid && !opts.isGrid(el))) dragDay = null;`
— sticky over the grid's own gaps (a heading row, the counts) as the drag-select is, dropped the moment the pointer leaves the
grid. With `dragDay` null the payload is null, `finish` calls no `onSelect`, and the stale amber ring is repainted by the next
hover. Pin in `movewire.test.ts` ("a press-and-drag carries it" block): `elementFromPoint` → `outside` on the last move, up → `picks`
stays `[]`.

### F4 · D262 · The "leave the band first" rule (S2) is reset by a held button or a pass over a non-grid element. NEW, low.
**Where:** `select.ts:871-873`. `inBand` reads `band()`, which is 0 whenever `overGrid` is false — and `overGrid` is forced false
while `e.buttons !== 0` or over anything not `isGrid`. So `else if (!inBand) bandsLive = true` fires when the mouse never left the
band. **Scenario:** chip picked up, mouse resting in the right band (correctly still); press-and-drag to a day that is OCCUPIED
→ refused, move stays on, mouse still in the band → the next 1 px movement starts the hover scroll. **Fix:** compute a
geometry-only `geo = bandAt(mx, my)` (no `overGrid` term) and drive `seen`/`bandsLive` from `geo`; scroll only when `overGrid && geo`.

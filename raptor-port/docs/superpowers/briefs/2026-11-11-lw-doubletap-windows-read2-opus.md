**No blocking findings.** Two small notes, neither needing a fix before merge:

1. **Note (minor, not a regression).** The `cancel` listener (Matrix.tsx, the `lastPressRef` effect, ~1582) forgets the remembered press whatever finger sent the cancel. Case: one finger rests on the screen and the browser takes it over for a scroll, while another finger taps a window's ✕. The ✕ press is forgotten, the window closes, and a fast second tap reaches the grid. That is how it behaved before this job, and it is rare. If wanted: `const cancel = (e) => { if (lastPressRef.current?.id === e.pointerId) lastPressRef.current = null }`. Test: down on the OIL sheet with pointerId 3, cancel with pointerId 9, close the sheet → the second tap is still swallowed.

The guard only swallows clicks on the grid, and in these tests no window closes, or the press lands on a window.

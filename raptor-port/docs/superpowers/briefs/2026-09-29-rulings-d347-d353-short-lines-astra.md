## Dispositions (the build chat)

A short line is the rule alone, by design (D390: "its number, its date, the rule as it stands"); the detail, readings and
conditions stay in the full row, which a chat opens before acting on a ruling's detail. So an OMISSION is not a defect;
a short line that would lead a reader to a DIFFERENT rule is.

- **D347 — changed.** "Every Undo / Redo pair sits in the top bar" read without D349 says the board's pair moves too; the
  board keeps its own bar. Short line and heading now end "; the scheduler board keeps its own bar (D349)".
- **D353 — changed.** "Risky work" was undefined in the line; the full row defines it. Short line and heading now name it
  (money / earned leave, permissions, the published record, saved data) and add "everything else one".
- **D348, D349, D350, D351, D352 — kept.** Each states its rule truly; what Astra adds (the board's full order, the popup's
  closing, Clear edit history's safeguards, the stage outcomes, the phone-picture retake — done) is detail its full row
  carries. D350's "all other roster / settings edits are undoable" is the 16 Sep 26 rule (register AM39d), not D350's.

## The naming question

Recommend keeping "the open window", not naming it.
- An input's title ("Drifter · 1 Jan") makes a clumsy sentence: "close Drifter · 1 Jan first".
- A mix of named and unnamed windows would give several forms of one note.
- D2403's own wording is general ("a note saying to close it first").
- The window is in front of him when the note appears, so the name adds length and no information.
- If the R3 check shows the bell-list confusion is real, fix that case alone with "close the window behind this list", not by naming everywhere.

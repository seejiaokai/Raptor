Bidding with no stages
Before

D1177: The Leave War's stages (draft, open for bidding, bidding closed, published) go: members bid only on the open dates the admin sets in the "Open for bidding" window; nothing is open until he opens it, and with nothing open the chip reads "Bidding closed"; a member may still delete his own pending bid on a closed day.

After

The Leave War has no stages: members bid only on the dates the admin opens in the "Open for bidding" window — nothing is open until he opens it, and with nothing open the chip reads "Bidding closed"; the admin decides bids at any time (the agent's reading of D1177; your D418 had "in every stage but Draft"); a day not open stops a member's new bid, though he may still delete his own pending bid there, the day's note saying so.

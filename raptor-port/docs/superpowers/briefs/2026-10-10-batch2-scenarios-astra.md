**Explicit negatives**

- I found no current controls-only route for Ranger himself to change the admin filing switch while retaining his session. The live-window sequence therefore uses Saber’s supported member/admin switch; it must not be simulated by changing hidden state.
- A blocked bar deliberately does not lift. Its drop-time refusal cannot be tested by a normal drag in that state; the absence of the gesture is the correct visible check.
- The note people picker traps keyboard focus. Opening another note editor underneath it is not a valid normal-user setup. Its Escape/Cancel also differs deliberately from cancelling an active text edit.
- I found no separate shared OIL standing printed on the month bar, phone card or desktop shared row. Those surfaces lead to the window; the schedule’s dialog still concerns an individual record.
- Leave War OIL tracker dates already use a year-bearing format. A different formatter alone is not evidence of a missing year.
- I found no basis to require top-positioned toasts for the schedule’s blocking dialogs, Leave War sheets, or older windows outside this shell.
- No scenarios depend on repairing old demo records, resizing unrelated buttons, reopening the settled choices, or including the separately filed landscape work.

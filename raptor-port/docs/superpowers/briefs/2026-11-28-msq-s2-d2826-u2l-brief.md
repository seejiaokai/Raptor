## 3. The rule
1. **The add form becomes the third entry of the one record.** `OpenWork = { row, give, add: AddWork | null }`.
   - `AddWork = { on: AddOn; typed: AddTyped; drawn: { onScreen: boolean } }`.
   - `AddOn = StartedOn & { mine: string | null }`, where `mine` is his own home that day at the press. **Add no field to `StartedOn` itself**, nor to `Shown`, `Typed`, `GiveOn` or `GiveTyped` (U2's rule).
   - `AddTyped = { name, np, role, postIn, set: { name?, np?, role?, postIn? } }`, the marks as in plan §1.2.
   - `adding` becomes `const adding = !!work.add`, keeping that name (see §6).
2. **When an entry is taken:**
   - at the press of "+ Add a person";
   - at `useOpenRequest`'s open (Quals' "+ Add person");
   - again after an add that succeeds, because the form stays open and empty for the next man.
3. **The judge, `judgeAdd(w, at, day?)`, works in this order:**
   1. `pick`: his own pick (as `byHisPick`), silent (D2825).
   2. `moved`: his home today is not `on.mine`. This means his own move landed. The form closes with **D2826's note**.
   3. `changed`: any other started-on fact differs (`startedOnChanged`). The form closes **silently** (a reading, open point d).
   4. `ok` otherwise.
   - At a press, an unset box whose day is gone (`dayGone`) with no move: see open point f.
4. **When the note is owed:**
   - `moved`, AND the form was on his screen at the last draw (`drawn.onScreen`, re-held from `shown` as Give access is), AND something is typed (`addFormDiffers(work.add)`, by the marks).
   - With nothing typed the form goes without a word (open point c).
   - At a press (`add()` judges before it calls `addPersonAndAccount`), `moved` always says the note. Nothing is sent and the form closes.
5. **One write of `work` per draw.**
   - `useOpenWorkJudged` judges `add` with the rest and writes once.
   - `useOpenRequest` must **not** write `work` during the draw. Hand its "open now" into `useOpenWorkJudged`, as `closedNow` is handed. Otherwise the judged `setWork(next)` value replaces the opening queued before it in the same draw.
   - Every other write is functional `setWork(w => …)`.
6. **As the page goes**, `useNoteAsPageGoes` and `takenAwayUnder` carry the add entry by the same rule. Hunter's case may close on the last draw (the `startedOnChanged`/`adminThere` route proof 2 shows) or as the page goes; build both, and let the whole-app test decide.
7. **The notes owed are sentences.**
   - `noteOwed` and `sayChanged` today carry callsigns only. Make the owed list hold finished sentences: a row or Give access maps through `changedWhileOpenWords`; the add form gives D2826's sentence.
   - All of them are said in one `HOOKS.toastBatch`, in order: row, Give access, add.
   - Keep the name `takenAwayUnder` (`docs/data-schema.md` names it).
8. **The date** follows plan §8 / §1.3.
   - The box shows `day = typed.set.postIn ? typed.postIn : effectiveToday()`, computed in its own draw.
   - `PostInField` gets `onSelect`, which sets the value and the mark; `onChange` does nothing more.
   - Add sends `day`.
   - `postIn0` goes, and the mark replaces it in `addFormDiffers`.
9. **Closing by his own act stays silent, and the names stay.**
   - `shutAdd` (Cancel, Escape, and KR's future closer) stays callable with no argument, silent and writing nothing. It lets go `work.add` and moves the keys back, as today.
   - `closeRow()` and `closeGive()` are unchanged.
10. **The page's squadron is never re-read for the add.** The door `addPersonAndAccount` adds to `pageUnit('own')` at the press, so the press must judge first. That press-time judge is the whole safety of proof 3.

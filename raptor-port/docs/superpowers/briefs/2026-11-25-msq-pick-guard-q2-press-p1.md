## What surprised me
1. **On a desktop, the document viewer over an Input window leaves the bar free.** The pick goes through, and the 142 band is drawn but scrolled out of sight inside the window. On a phone the same viewer covers every door.
2. **Logic and Quals carry their edit mode onto 149's page**, with Done drawn. The change already made is saved into 142's record without a question.
3. **Quals keeps "Done" in the member view.**
4. **The Scheduler Board is a whole-screen window.** Its only doors are its own bell and ✓ Done. Its bell's 142 line closes the board and changes squadron without a word.
5. The ⓘ info mode turns off at a pick but stays on at the view switch. Edit chart layout stays open in the member view, as the first report found.
6. Traffic's typed airspace line is saved into 142 as it is typed. Nothing in it is lost at a pick.

## Stand-ins
- p1-boardbell: chat 166's bell set-up. Saber gives Hunter Plan of 149 and files his 8 Dec input, all by presses. The saved data is then copied into each fresh browser.
- On the phone (WebKit), a box's words are selected by script before typing over them, because WebKit takes no select-all key. This was used for Remarks, the airspace line and Crew rest.
- Nothing else; every other step is a press.

## Not pressed, and why
- **An armed "Discard N edits & load".** The demo has no issued day. Publishing one by presses (four sign-offs, Publish day, then edits, the plan's issued version, then Load) was started in a probe but not finished in the time.
- **Show All and Reorder on a phone.** Neither is drawn on the phone's Tracker page as it opens (Show All is folded into ⓘ there; Reorder sits in the side panel), so they were not reached.
- **OIL Earn with the board's bell.** The bell is free, but Saber's list is empty ("Nothing new"), so there was no line to press.
- **The Changes window's folded form on a desktop**: it does not exist there.

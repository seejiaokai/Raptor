### F1 — An archive takes a SANS-posted man out of the ALL AVAIL crowd on the days between his posting and his archive; a published day of those days reads pending and loses his OIL
- **Concrete failure** (`zz-proof-1.test.ts`): demo week, today Wed 15 Jul. Tue 14 Jul holds a squadron event for ALL AVAIL 08:00–15:00 and Hex (`rocky`) named on a 16:00 briefing. Post Hex out as SANS from Mon 13 Jul (it runs at once). The crowd of Tue 14 Jul has him (correct — SANS, planned with us). Archive him on Admin → Users (`archivePerson`). The crowd of Tue 14 Jul no longer has him — with "+ SANS" off AND on (both tests fail: `expected [ 'bane', 'stiff', 'slipway', …(40) ] to include 'rocky'`).
- With the day published first (signed, approved, OIL pass run, 0 pending), archiving him makes the published Tue read pending: 2 items, one of them the OIL item whose crowd went from `…+riddler+rocky+shaft…` to `…+riddler+shaft…`. A control (same day, Hex an ordinary man with no posting, archived the same way) keeps him in the crowd; only a warning item appears there, so the OIL item is the SANS case's own.
- Against D327 ("an archived man stays in the ALL AVAIL crowd on every day before his archive") and D44/D142 (the published day reopens and its OIL moves).
- **Cause:** `raptor-port/src/leavewar/state/store.ts` `stintClosed` (about line 2406): an archive rewrites the closed stint as `poOutcome: 'overseas'`, `poDone: to+1` even when the stint was closed by a SANS posting that had already run (record after archive: `"to":"2026-07-12","poArchive":true,"poOutcome":"overseas","poDone":"2026-07-13"`). The changed reader, `sync.ts availableFor` lines 2092–2095, then lays `windowFor(posting, true)`; the outcome is no longer `'sans'`, so the window closes from Mon 13 Jul and `inSquadron` drops him on Tue 14 Jul although `sansOnP` still says he was SANS that day (archive's `sansStop(body, today)` keeps Mon–Tue as SANS days).
- **New or older:** the cause is older. Before this change the same flip happened with "+ SANS" on. With it off he was already missing from Tue's crowd (the fault this change fixes), so the archive changed nothing. **With this change the flip now also happens with "+ SANS" off, and a published day of that kind reads pending on archive, which is new.**
- **Fix, step by step** (one rule that also closes F2):
  1. In `sync.ts availableFor`, import `atHome` from `./engine/people` next to `inSquadron`.
  2. Replace line 2095 `if (lw && !inSquadron(lw, iso)) continue` with:
     ```ts
     /* a day he is SANS and named on our programme, he is ours whatever his posting window says (D27, D283):
        only his squadron stints, and his archive, close it */
     const sansHere = !!engaged && engaged.has(id) && sansOnP(p, iso)
     const archivedBy = p.archived && p.archivedOn ? iso >= String(p.archivedOn) : false
     if (lw && !(sansHere ? atHome(lw, iso) && !archivedBy : inSquadron(lw, iso))) continue
     ```
     The `windowFor(posting, true)` line stays: it still covers reading (2), the hand untick after a SANS posting ran, when `sansOnP` is false and the window must be open.
  3. Add to `allavail-sans-posted.test.ts` the two cases of `zz-proof-1.test.ts`: in the crowd of Tue 14 Jul after the archive, both ways; and a published Tue 14 Jul with 0 pending after the archive. Add one more: Thu 16 Jul (after the archive date), named, gives "out".
  4. Run `allavail-sans-posted.test.ts`, `sans-from-date.test.ts` and the archive/one-door test files.
  - Put to the owner with it: step 2 means a SANS man named on our programme counts on that day even when a posting window says he is not ours (before a post-in date to come, or after a non-SANS posting). That is D27's reading ("a SANS man only when planned with us"), but it is his call. The narrower alternative is to keep the outcome `'sans'` in `stintClosed` and close the window at `archivedOn`. Changing `stintClosed` alone, without the archive date, is wrong: `windowFor(…, true)` would then leave him open-ended after his archive.

### F2 — With a post-in date still to come, a SANS man (ticked by hand) named on our programme counts in ALL AVAIL with "+ SANS" off and not with it on
- **Concrete failure** (`zz-proof-2.test.ts`): Thu 16 Jul holds the ALL AVAIL event and Hex named on a 16:00 briefing. Set Hex's post-in from Fri 17 Jul (`setPostIn`). Tick SANS on Quals by hand (Wed 15 Jul). With "+ SANS" off the war has no row for him: the projection drops a SANS man, and `setPeople` / `reprojectRoster` put back a kept row only when the record has a `to`. He is then IN the crowd of Thu 16 Jul. With it on he has a row (`from: 2026-07-17`) and is OUT. Output: `{ rowOff: false, off: true, rowOn: true, on: false }`; `the same answer both ways: expected true to be false`.
- The display switch decides who is counted, and so OIL and a published day's crowd. This is the host's reading (1) in a state this change does not reach.
- **Cause:** `sync.ts availableFor` line 2093, `const posting = lw0 ? postings[id] : undefined`. The saved posting is read only when the switch-shaped roster has a row, so with no row nothing is checked. The change kept this guard from the old code.
- **New or older:** older. The old code had the same gap (`const lw = lwById.get(id)`), and this change kept it, so the switch can still change the answer.
- **Fix, step by step:** the F1 fix's step 2 settles it. The man is SANS and named, so he counts both ways (the owner's question under F1 covers whether that is right). If the owner rules the other way (a man not yet posted in is never counted), do this instead:
  1. Line 2093: `const posting = postings[id]`.
  2. Line 2094: `const base = lw0 ?? (posting ? stripHome(posting) : undefined)`, then `const lw = base && posting ? { ...base, ...windowFor(posting, true) } : base`. Import `stripHome` from `./state/store`. With no row there are no squadron stints, so `atHome` is true.
  3. Add the `zz-proof-2.test.ts` case to `allavail-sans-posted.test.ts`, asserting the same answer both ways.

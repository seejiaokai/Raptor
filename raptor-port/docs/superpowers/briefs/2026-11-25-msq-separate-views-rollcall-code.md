# Roll-call: where one squadron's things show on another's pages (D2622) — read of `claude/msq-b1-screens-135`, 25 Nov 26

Read only, nothing changed. Worked example throughout: Hunter (home 142, holds Plan of 149), Saber (master admin, home 142), Ranger (member of 142, holds View of 149). "On 149" = 149 picked in the top bar.

One background fact explains most rows. `pageUnit('own')` (state/perms.ts) makes the own pages (Inputs, Quals, SANS, Leave War, Tracker, Admin → Users) follow the pick only for a Plan holder or a master admin in the admin view. For a View holder, or anyone in the member view, a "View only" pick offers only View-only Sched and Help (`viewOnlyPick` / `pageOffered`, D2381). So most crossings below reach a **Plan holder or master admin**. The ones a **View holder** meets come through the bell or a card drawn on every page.

## D. Checked and found clean (each keeps to the squadron in view, or the page's own squadron)

- Schedule week and day board: pucks, the AIRCREW and personal panels, OIL Earn. They read the day's roster of the squadron in view.
- ALL AVAIL "Who's available": the event's own people. It closes at a pick (D2596).
- Insights: the schedule's roster.
- Changes window and edit history: they hold only the lines of the squadron in view (`engine/editlog.ts` `elogLoad`). A line about his own 142 input goes to 142's history (`push` → `homeUnitOf`).
- Inputs page Person filter, look-ahead, deleted and archived groups, the Inputs and SANS settings windows: page's squadron (`pageUnit`). The windows close when the squadron changes (`state/cutoff.ts` `winStale`).
- Medical tab: `onRosterOf(person, pageUnit('own'))`. One caveat, guessed: a moved man's document pager (`medEpisode`) may join his old-squadron episode.
- SANS calendar month, day, Highlight and settings: page's squadron.
- Quals: list `rosterOf(page squadron)`; an admin's edits only on men of his squadrons (`ofMySquadron`). Columns are the squadron's own.
- Logic: the squadron picked. Its doors stamp the settings window with that squadron.
- Calendar / Days window: closes when the squadron changes.
- Leave War, every tab (grid, Approvals, Balances, OIL, day panel, postings): the war loaded is the page's own squadron's. My leave is drawn only when he is on that war's grid. Approvals' "New" is per squadron.
- Tracker: follows the page's own squadron. People and medical days are that squadron's (`tracker/peoplewire.ts`). Its Undo is parked per squadron. In the member view it is covered.
- Admin → Users own list, Waiting list, the link picker, the Admin tab's waiting count: page's squadron. The tab is hidden on a "View only" pick.
- Exports: the squadron number by kind (`ui/export.ts` `sqnNum`).
- Documents viewer: only what its input reaches (see A1).
- A "View only" pick hides every own page (D2381). So for a View holder the Inputs, Quals, SANS, Leave War, Tracker and Admin crossings above cannot be reached; his only crossings are A5, A7–A9 and A13.
- The page's own bell mark (`markBell`): no callers.
- Schedules menu, ☰ squadron list: his readable squadrons only.

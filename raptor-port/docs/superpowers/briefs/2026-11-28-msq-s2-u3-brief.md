## 3. The rule

**R1. One folded group for each other squadron, live men only** (D2629, D2652 N13 c, D2635; §19.3 "After").
- On a squadron's Admin → Users, every squadron other than the page's own is one heading at the page's foot.
  - Until U5 builds the block, the foot is **after the Archived fold**, as `s2-21-*-master-foot` draws the order: People, Archived, (U5's groups), the folds.
  - Each heading is folded until he opens it.
- A group holds that squadron's men on its roster that day, read through `usersListOf`'s own body, never a second walk:
  - not archived, not deleted, not a placeholder, nobody waiting for access;
  - so a group has no Restore and no Delete;
  - and the men already listed on the page are left out (open point 1).
- The heading's count is that squadron's live count, the same body: `usersListOf(u, iso, '').count`.
  - The count stays whole while searching or ticking (D2652 N13).
  - It counts the same people as the group, save open point 1's Plan holders.
- **Who sees the headings:** whoever may give access to the page's squadron (D2635). Today only such a man reaches the page at all (D2381), so this adds no check. A pin holds that Ranger (142's member) never reaches it.
- **A squadron with nobody live:** no heading (a reading, open point 4).
- **A tap** opens the group ("▾"), its men A to Z, each row with §19.1's short face.
  - Inside a fold no row wears the grey number (D2652 N4).
  - A second tap folds it.
  - Each heading's fold is the admin's own, kept while the page is mounted, as the Archived fold's is.

**R2. Order** (D2730 Q1).
- The headings come in the one order: `state/perms.ts inSquadronOrder` (home first, then by number), over `state/units-model.ts squadronOrder`.
- The order already exists and already has its tests (§"Where the plan no longer fits", item 1). **Write no sort of your own.**
- The example: on 149's page, Mako (150's own admin, holding Plan of 149) sees "▸ 150" before "▸ 142"; Saber (master admin, home 142) sees "▸ 142", then "▸ 150".

**R3. The "All squadrons" / Squadron tick is retired** (D2654 E8).
- `UsersFilter.sqn`, `sqnTicks`, the Squadron chips, `filterCount`'s squadron part, "Showing both squadrons, grouped", and `useUsersFilter`'s clearing at a pick all go.
- Seat, CAT and Role stay (D2335, D2306, D2307, D2401; D2400: "Plans 149" stays as built).
- The ticks hide rows inside an open group as they do on the list (§19.3, V14).
- "People · N" and "Showing n of m" count the page's own list, as today: the folds are left out of both.

**R4. The search** (D2730 Q9; §19.3 V13; D2647's reading for the fold).
- **Callsign only for another squadron's man:**
  - A man who is not on the page's squadron's roster that day is matched by his callsign only. That covers the Plan holders listed on People, and every man of a fold.
  - The page's own men are matched by callsign or sign-in, as today.
  - The search box's words stay.
- **The search opens and folds headings:**
  - While a search is typed, a heading whose group holds a match is drawn open on its matches.
  - The other headings stay as he left them. Hiding a heading with no match is U4's (D2647); in U3 it stays, closed.
  - Today's one flat list of finds goes.
  - Cleared, each heading is again as he left it (his own taps, kept under the search).
  - A press on a heading the search opened folds it until the search changes (the Archived fold's pattern, `archOpen`).

**R5. A heading folded over an open row** (D2654 E9h, his answer as recommended).
- Where the open row stands in a group and that heading folds by his press, the row closes as its Cancel does: nothing written, no note, what was picked dropped.
- **The planner's reading, named for open point 3:** the same holds when his clearing of the search folds the group the open row stands in.
- A group holding the open row is drawn open while the row stays open. This covers a regrouping by no act of his, so §21's "a regrouping never drops what he picked" holds for folds as it did for the ticks.

**R6. The Archived fold holds the page's own squadron's men only** (the security read's finding B; its pins become this rule's tests).

**R7. "Edit on 142 →"** (D2652 N5; §19.1; drawn `u6-*-edit`, pressed `e8j2-d-after`).

## Finding 6.1 — DRIFT (low): a hidden "takes off and lands at the same time" warning leaves the amber time box on a second, identically worded line

- **Ruling:** D469 — "if it's hidden, the pucks shouldn't have flagging for that specific item" (the amber time box of a nought-minute line is that warning's flag, per the code's own comment over `fltNoLenShown`).
- **Kind:** DRIFT.
- **Where:** `ui/html.ts` fltNoLenShown asks whether a hidden FLT_NO_LEN warning carries THIS line's slot key (`w.key === ff:<di>.<gi>.<li>.ld`). The validator (`engine/validate.ts` `add`, verbatim from the original) keeps only ONE warning when two have the same rule, men and words — first one wins, and keeps the FIRST line's key.
- **The exact case:** Tuesday, WAVE 1, two lines added with "+ Line" and no callsign yet; Ranger on the first, Saber on the second; both typed take-off 15:00 and landing 15:00. The list has one advisory, "WAVE 1 takes off and lands at the same time (15:00) …". The scheduler presses ✕ on it: the day reads no issues, the line is struck — the first line's time boxes go plain, the second line's stay amber.
- **What he would see:** an amber box on a line with nothing in the list explaining it (the list shows the one struck line), and no way to clear it short of flagging the warning again. Rare (needs two lines worded identically: same callsign — or none — in the same wave, or the same callsign at the same time in two waves).
- **Confidence:** high that it happens (reproduced); low impact. Settled by a fix and the test turning red.
- **Red line seen:** `hidden: neither line flags: expected [ false, true ] to deeply equal [ false, false ]`.
- **Suggested fix (not built):** in `fltNoLenShown`, treat the line as hidden when a hidden FLT_NO_LEN warning of that day carries the same words as this line's own `fltNoLenMsg` (the hide key's words), not only the same slot key. (The original's dedupe stays as it is — D1537.)

## Question 6.U1 — UNCLEAR: what makes a hidden warning "the same one" once the schedule under it changes

- **Rulings:** D469 (his words: "it can be hidden until another person unhides it"); kept from Aug 26 as the agent's reading: "if things change that warning will appear again — the hide is tied to that exact warning"; D479 (told to him: a clash recreated by a put-back "returns already struck").
- **As built:** a hide is keyed by the day, the rule, the men named and the warning's WORDS (`engine/warnhide.ts` hideKey). A clash's words name the two events ("VL BFM & BRIEFING clash") but no time.
- **The exact case (test "as built (6.U1 …)"):** Tuesday, Ranger on VL BFM 12:00, and on a BRIEFING 12:00–12:30; the clash hidden. (a) The brief slid to 12:30–13:00 — still a clash, same words — stays hidden. (b) Ranger taken off VL; the brief moved to 15:00; a NEW wave added with another VL BFM line at 15:00 and Ranger put on it — a clash of a different line at a different hour, worded the same — arrives already struck, and his puck on the new line carries no flag. Nothing in the app tells the scheduler; only the amber "already on …" note at the drop (D479).
- **What it costs:** a scheduler who acknowledged one clash may unknowingly have acknowledged a later, different one on the same day. A hide also never expires: its key stays on the day for as long as the week is kept.
- **To settle (his call):** is a hide meant to cover "the same words on the same day" (as built), or that one line/event — e.g. coming back once the hidden warning has been gone after an edit? D479's "returns already struck" was put to him for a put-back of the same man on the same seat, not for a different line.
- **Confidence:** high on what the app does; whether it is wrong is his.

## The concept (both)
One home called **Availability**, joined to the schedule. A member records what is happening; an admin decides leave
bids with the manning in view; a scheduler sees what it means for the week. These are views of the same entry: if you
can see it you can open it, and it behaves the same wherever you opened it.

## The places (agreed)
- **Availability → Mine** — a phone's first screen: the member's own dated list (pending first, then what is coming;
  entries others filed for him included — Astra's correction), with the month calendar; Add where "+ Medical" and
  "+ Input" stand today. It must not wait for the squadron's year to load.
- **Availability → Squadron** — a desk's first screen: the dense year grid (today's war grid), names and manning rows
  pinned, open bidding dates marked; **Approvals** opens beside it, so a bid is judged with its manning in view.
  Medical, SANS, Balances and OIL keep their own working views inside Availability.
- **The schedule** stays the scheduler's working page: Unavailable (the name kept; one row per group), a short **To
  review** in place of the permanent Personal Inputs list, the same window.
- **Gone:** the Leave War and the Inputs page as two destinations; three windows for one item; the permanent Personal
  Inputs list; every "change it on the Inputs page" sentence. **"Leave War"** stays as the name of a bidding period
  and its view while open ("Leave War · Mar–Apr · closes 20 Jan").

## The object (agreed)
One **entry**: its kind, people, dates, Duration, remarks, each man's own papers (D2781), history — with a
**decision** (pending, approved, refused, or none needed) and a **meaning for planning**: *away* (counts him out:
leave, a medical downchit, an OD, a course, an Attach, an Other filed as away), *restricted* (here but may not fly,
as ATT B), *attending* (on the programme). Leave and medical take theirs from their kind; for a commitment the
scheduler chooses away or attending, or rejects it.

## The rules (agreed)
1. A tap opens the entry's own window, the same everywhere, closing back to where you were.
2. What you may do follows who you are and what the entry is, never the page.
   **His D2885 (28 Nov 26, F5):** the Leave War's ⋯ keeps only Attach, Other, Post out and Post in — no other input
   type is added to the war; a tap on the war opens the war's window (a day may hold several entries), where an entry
   filed on the Inputs page offers a button to it there and a way back to the same place on the war. Such an entry keeps
   its Move and Delete on the war beside that button (his D2886, D1724 standing).
3. **A bid becomes leave by changing state — the same entry before and after**, charged once, its request and
   decision kept; a decision may cover only part of a bid's dates, as today.
4. **NOT TAKEN — his D2882 (28 Nov 26, F2 "no"): on a date open for bidding a member may bid OR file leave that
   stands at once, as today.** The readers' proposal, kept for the record: leave on a date open for bidding is a bid;
   on any other date it is recorded as approved and stands; one Leave action, the date deciding (no second button
   beside Bid while bidding is open); an admin may record approved leave on any date; a range crossing open and closed
   dates shows which part needs approval. The open-date window therefore offers both — its look drawn before any build.
5. **A group is one entry.** A change is for everyone unless **Change · [6 pax] · [Cutter]** is pressed (D2783,
   D2784), on every page; it takes him out at the press, his paper going with him, one Undo putting him back.
   **Grabbing one man's box of a group** (a drag of its body or edge) asks the same question at the box, every time,
   before anything moves. A man added to an approved bid never inherits its approval history. Two people saving one
   group at once: the later save is refused with its reason.
6. **A group's box at rest carries no mark**; under the mouse, the keyboard or a tap the group lights together and the
   window names everyone. (Both, after round 2 — Astra moved to it; the consistency design's question 3, answer B.)
7. One Duration (Whole day · AM · PM · Times) for filed entries, arriving with the window; a bid keeps its three;
   medical its own rules; exact times decide clashes even where manning counts halves.
8. Manning counts what it counts today, pending bids included (D998); with a request selected, its days also show the
   figure without it, so a refusal's effect is seen first. Approving a counted bid does not lower it again.
9. A published day changes only by a signed amendment. OIL earned and OIL taken stay apart: an award never moves,
   doubles or goes because an entry changed or a man was taken out.

## The order (agreed)
1. The destination drawn at his iPhone's size and a desk's (this page's sketches are the first pass).
2. **The one window**, from today's three pages, dates included, Duration in it. Medium risk.
3. **Groups**: one row under Unavailable, the Change buttons everywhere, each man's paper, the quiet splits gone —
   together. High risk.
4. **A bid and its leave one record.** Changes no page a member knows. High risk (balances, OIL).
5. **Availability**: Mine, Squadron, Approvals beside manning; the Leave War and Inputs tabs fold in. High risk.
6. **The schedule's To review** in place of Personal Inputs (his D2883: yes, named "To review", holding what is not
   placed — rejected inputs, each acceptable again); Reject on the item's own row.
Each step drawn first and given a full check with a walk.

**Stopping points:** after step 3 (the consistency design — one window, one set of rights, a group one thing) the
squadron is well served day to day, but still chooses between two pages to file, a bid and its leave stay two records
(their oddities patched one by one), approvals stay a page away from manning, and the schedule keeps its second list.
After step 4 (a middle stop) the second of those goes, and no page a member knows has moved.

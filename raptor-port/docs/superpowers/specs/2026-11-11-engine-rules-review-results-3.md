## Findings in full

### 3.1 — W33: a day on a row with no times yet is read as a break day (DRIFT)

- **The rule:** `engine-rules.md` §Chip ranking: "A break day is due after `VCONF.maxRun` (6) consecutive days (owner, Aug 26) … counted … off `day.events`, which is every kind of tasking there is: a flight, a duty post, a sim, a ground item, a programme row. Leave and downchits are NOT tasking, so a clear day resets the count". The Logic page's own fold (approved lines, D928): "Any tasking counts: a flight, a duty, a sim, a ground or programme row." His original words for the run are not quoted in the doc; "on the programme" is its word (Logic: "On the programme more than 6 days in a row").
- **Kind:** DRIFT. **Where:** `validate.ts` RUNLEN (reads `day.events`); `events.ts buildDay` sends any duty, sim, ground or programme row with no start to `day.blank` (D605), never to `events`. A flying line with no times DOES go into `events` (NaN times), so it counts.
- **The case:** limit 6. Hawk is on a ground row 09:00–10:00 Monday to Saturday (six days). On Sunday the scheduler seats him on a ground row ("+ row", or a duty desk from a template whose times were cleared) and has not typed its times yet. The app: no break-day warning — Sunday reads as a clear day. Had the Sunday task been a flying line with no times yet, the red "No break day" warning lands on Sunday. The ruling: a day with any tasking is a working day.
- **What he loses:** a seventh day in a row goes unflagged until a time is typed; the crew list's pre-drop question (runIfPlaced, the same RUNLEN) says nothing either. Small window (times are usually typed), but silent.
- **Confidence:** medium — the rule says "any tasking"; what settles it is his word on whether a row with no times yet is a day on the programme (the D605 build kept time-less rows out of `events` for the clash loop, Insights and OIL, and never named the run).
- **Red line seen:** `AssertionError: expected [] to deeply equal [ 4 ]` (DAYS_RUN days for him, Friday expected, limit set to 3 in the test).
- **Suggested fix (not built):** count `day.blank` entries (the time-less rows, which already carry the man) in RUNLEN's on-set, and in seedRunIn / nextMondayWorked the same way.

- **Suggested fix:** after W27 is ruled: either "…but his in-time, or the suggested B where none is typed, is later, so it clears", or no change if crew rest moves to the default rally.

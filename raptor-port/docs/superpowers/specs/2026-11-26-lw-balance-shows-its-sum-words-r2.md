# Question 16 — the grey line under App bal, round 2 (a fresh Fable, 26 Nov 26)

"typed" names the act, not the source, so it carries nothing. The line must say where the figure came from.

| State | Line | Characters |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing since | `from official leave app on 26 Nov` | 33, one line |
| Something since | `from official leave app on 26 Nov: 24` then `less 1 day of leave approved since` | 37 and 34, two lines |
| Half day | second line `less 0.5 day of leave approved since` | 36 |
| Never typed (box only) | `Type what the official leave app shows` | 38, one line |

**Why each reads cold**
- Nothing since: under "App bal 24" it says the 24 is the official leave app's figure as of that date, which also explains "App bal".
- Something since: read downward it is the sum, 23 in the box, 24 from the official leave app, less the 1 day approved after, and that day is the "−1" on the leave row.
- Never typed: says what to do and where the figure comes from. A member with no box sees "Not typed" alone.

**Against the idea to beat:** "from leave app, 26 Nov" is right in kind. This keeps the app's one name ("official leave app") and "on" so the date reads as the day of the figure. Its second state on one line is 56 characters and cannot fit.

**One line not possible for "something since"** with the source named. A one-line fallback, `24 on 26 Nov, less 1 day approved since` (39; 41 with 0.5), drops the source, so the question he asked returns. Not recommended.

**Not checked:** counts are characters, not measured on his iPhone, whose letters are wider. If the 37 wraps, drop "on" (35). Lesson 36 says balance tiles carry no "−"; "less" is a word, the row's "−1" is outside the tile.

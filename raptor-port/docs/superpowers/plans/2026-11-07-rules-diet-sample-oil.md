# OIL (off in lieu) — how it works now

Loads with OIL, Leave War, placeholder-puck and publishing files. **This page states each rule as it stands today; the
number after it is the ruling that set it.** His words, the readings he was told and the history are in the full rows,
`.claude/decisions-full/oil.md` (search: `grep -h '^| D142 |' .claude/decisions-full/*.md`) — open the row before acting
on a rule's detail or asking him about it. A ruling of another area that bears on OIL is listed at the end.

## What OIL is
- Earned leave — time off banked, never pay or money; say it that way in every report and screen [D25].

## What earns, and from what
- Only the issued (published) schedule earns, in both directions: a holiday declared after publication waits for a
  republication and the day says so; revoking one never removes OIL silently [D2].
- A day's OIL comes from its latest published version — the latest amendment, or the EOD if that is the latest — however
  long ago the day was; no lock, no clock [D142]. An issued day keeps what it went out with when a rule changes under it
  [D48]; a later time or Logic change reads as pending until it is published again [D592].
- Every seat can earn; its default decides whether it does; the admin can always override in OIL Earn [D28, D31, D43].
  On by default: flying lines, SC MAIN, Common Programme, Standard and SC-shift duty desks, ground rows, sims, sim
  passengers, the extra-people line [D15, D24, D43, D755]. Off by default but offering the switch: SC SPARE, AVALON flying
  lines and duty desks, BB flying lines, and a duty block minted from an AVALON template — an activated one can be
  credited, on a published day through an ordinary amendment; one that flies counts as a real flight and earns by default
  (scheduler page) [D24, D35, D1042]. An ⓘ row does not earn [D15].
- When a standby line counts as flying — its B or R typed, or a row ticked "Flies" — is the scheduler page's [D1042–D1044].
- The placeholder pucks (ALL / ALL AVAIL) behave like named people: on by default wherever they can land, and wherever
  one can land it offers the switch [D32, D43, D755]. On a row that came from an input, a placeholder is one of the input's
  people and follows its one OIL answer [D43, D755].
- A man the scheduler puts in the name box of another man's request row replaces him in the input, or joins it beside
  him, and follows the input's OIL answer; a request that never asks the OIL question (Personal) earns nobody anything,
  whoever is on its row [D470, D755].
- ALL / ALL AVAIL never include ground crew; a ground crewman named on a weekend duty earns; a placeholder there earns
  for the men it stands for unless switched off [D52].
- A seat with nothing to measure offers no switch and says why [D31].
- An OIL award and a worked day add up [D82]. OIL may be credited by hand on any day [D79].

## What does not earn
- An "Off day" — settled, do not reopen [D21].
- Leave and medical inputs: never earn, never asked [D804].
- A cancelled input, or an information-only one: never asked — not at a save, a put-back or a filing; no "OIL?" tag, not
  in the one list nor the bell [D814].
- An ALL AVAIL / ALL row with a start and no end earns nobody — the scheduler page's [D360].
- A worked weekend that earns nobody any OIL says so [D81].
- The OIL batch's words stand as built: when an amendment is withdrawn, the Unpublish warning, its done note and the armed
  button's title; and the same-time line's "earns nobody any OIL" sentence [D1094].

## How long a day counts
- A flying line: from its earliest applicable entered in-time / rally for the formation — the nominal report time only
  where none is entered [D591, D592]; an evening-before report lengthens that line's own day only; the day counts first
  event to last end, breaks included [D592].
- An SC shift: a typed B (in-time) counts as work — its OIL and work hours run from it to the shift's end; a blank B leaves
  the written start to end [D606].
- A flying line whose written take-off and landing are the same still earns [D49]. An overnight line earns on the day it
  sits on; hours past midnight earn the next day nothing [D42].

## The OIL question (inputs on non-working days)
- Every input kind that can earn is asked the OIL question and its answer decides, wherever it is filed, under Unavailable
  included [D804]. "→ Unavail", "Accept", a put-back after the day became a holiday, and a swap or add that raises the
  question all put the question FIRST — nothing moves or is put back until it is answered [D783, D793, D804, D831]. Not answered:
  both pucks flash and a one-line bar stays in the band under the top bar — "Not swapped — OIL not answered · Answer OIL ·
  OK" — until tapped [D783, D831, D840].
- Taking the cancel off an input with no answer for a non-working day asks the question first; already answered or nothing
  to ask → un-cancelled at once and the answer counts again [D816]. With the bar up, "Answer OIL" un-cancels at once where
  nothing is left to ask [D865].
- A day made a holiday afterwards: the admin declaring it is told at once; what he answers there notifies nobody; only
  what is left unanswered goes to the schedulers and to whoever answers for each input [D781, D783]. The one list opens by
  itself when a holiday is saved, and the schedulers' bell carries one line a holiday, counting what is left [D783, D831].
- The admin answers a day's open questions from one list — each input with yes / no, plus "Yes to all" / "No to all" [D782];
  the list a load opens shows the day's whole open list [D865].
- A published version loaded back onto the working copy is not held: the note says, in short plain sentences, what the
  day is back to, what viewers still see, what else came back or stayed out and how many inputs need an OIL answer (not
  the edits thrown away) [D818, D863], and the OIL list opens for the day that needs the answer — another day of a
  several-day input included [D862]. "Switch to this plan" that brings back an unanswered input gets the same short note
  and list [D865].
- The "Only some days…" calendar is the app's one date calendar: a tap turns each separate day on or off, only the input's
  non-working days tappable [D923]; on a phone up to 400 wide it has no box of its own and its count reads "No days
  selected — tap a day to credit it" [D978]. The ⓘ mark's words stay as built [D865].
- A weekend or holiday no Leave War period covers names the reason and offers the way out, never a bare refusal [D19]; an
  input filed for such a weekend asks and saves its OIL answer as on any other day — the Inputs page's [D1578].

## Earned and awarded
- "Earned" counts only what the app credited itself — the published schedule or an accepted duty input; every OIL an
  admin gives by hand, typed on the war grid or credited from the OIL Tracker, shows apart as "awarded" [D400].
- Every award by hand shows on the Leave War grid on its date as an FO or HO box; a tap says who gave it and why [D402]. A
  member opens his own award read-only at every stage [D261]. Every member may see every man's tracker entries, figures
  and grants with their reasons [D1008]. An award does not flag a leave day [D80].
- A dragged block's delete removes everything in it, awards included — its confirm names each award first [D260].
- At the database step earned OIL is a table of its own — one row a person and a date, not tied to a Leave War period —
  read by every screen; until then the Inputs page's D1578 stands [D1579].

## How it shows
- The green OIL bar shows only on the events that counted towards a man's day; the ALL AVAIL count chip agrees [D1].

## Settled — leave as they are
- The OIL mode's phone tap targets: he settles OIL on his phone easily — not reopened, not filed as a defect [D26].
- Stored awards in the old shape: demo data, not converted — only the demo seed is rewritten, and an old-shape record must
  still not break a load [D401, D54, D56]. The award dates and post-out losses seen in the demo data are left — demo data
  only, wiped before the database [D163, D54, D56].

## From other areas (each stated on its own page)
- Scheduler: whoever files an input for other people answers its OIL question for all of them at the save [D660]; one
  OIL answer for everyone in a shared input, given by its filer or an admin; only the scheduler leaves one person out, in
  OIL Earn on the day [D762]; the answer given again is written for everyone, the scheduler's refusal still
  winning [D682]; a split answer counts "credited for 2 of 3 — Ranger: no" [D731]; ALL AVAIL / ALL among an input's people
  follow its answer [D753]; whoever stands on an input's row is in the input [D755]; OIL Earn shows his own puck's green
  ring [D272]; where the placeholders may land [D27, D33, D47]; who counts as available and its window [D36–D41, D51];
  frozen at publication, the pending mark [D44, D45].
- Leave War: a bid sharing its day with an award moves alone [D265]. People: an archived man stays in the crowd before his
  archive [D327]. How we work: D54, D56.
- `[OIL-AUTO-REMOVE]` (the OIL Earn mode) went live with his look waived: its evidence is the walk,
  `raptor-port/docs/handpass/2026-09-22-oil-walk.md` — the defects it found are fixed red-first, do not re-do them — never
  assume he saw the walked Saturday; design of record `raptor-port/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-21-oil-auto-remove-decisions.md`
  (§8/§9 not relitigated).

## What the numbers mean — and how far to trust them

**Faults you found (17).** Counted from two places: the bug-check order's list of the faults you found that a walk
missed (kept since 24 Oct 26), and every backlog item marked as your find or your report. Two things make this rough:
- The records seldom say whether you saw the fault on the **live app** or on a **preview link** before "merge live". Only
  3 say preview. So 17 is the most it can be; some of them were surely previews (your look at new work is usually on its preview
  link).
- The list of your finds only started on 24 Oct 26. Before that only backlog items are read, and they were not always
  marked as yours. So **the two older windows are too low** — 0 for late September is not true, it is unrecorded (you
  found three faults in the OIL build on 21 Sep 26 alone).
So the jump from 5 to 17 mostly shows that your finds are now written down, not that more went wrong.

**Changes undone or redone (1).** No change was taken back (no "revert") in any of the six weeks. One pull request
fixed a fault that an earlier one, live for under two weeks, had brought: the shaky-tests batch (20 Oct) fixed a time
typed into one man's row of a shared input, which showed wrong since the standby batch went live the same day. This is
counted only where the fix names the pull request it fixes — so it is a **floor**: most fixes, yours included, do not
name what brought the fault. The "possible" figure is every fix that touches mostly the same files as a change of the
two weeks before; it is listed, not counted, because in this app many changes touch the same large files.

**Merges (61).** Pull requests that went live on main. Checked against GitHub's own list: the same, but for two that GitHub
counts as merged though they reached main by another route (one in late September, one now); they are not counted here. Late September also had 30 changes put straight onto main,
mostly notes (and 7 in mid-October); those are not merges and are not counted.

the two numbers compare like for like, since both windows will have your finds written down. After four weeks

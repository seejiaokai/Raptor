## 3. M5 in two merges, not one — his D1475 (28 Oct 26, "All as recommended")

Seven jobs, about two and a half to three times M4, every one touching saved data or who may write it. D1087 puts two to
four related jobs in one merge; D485 sizes one check by the riskiest change — a single M5 would be one very long FULL
check over seven surfaces. **The split, as he ruled it (D1475 — D1250's five merges become six):**

- **M5a — balances** (jobs 1–5): the person window with a member's new rows, the balances page with its lock, a member's
  own writes, the leave year. One subject — a person's leave figures — and one storage change, checked once.
- **M5b — the admin's set-up** (jobs 6–7): the new Settings with "+ SANS", Management first, holidays and events by file.

Each its own branch from `main` once the one before is live, its own FULL check, its own "merge live". M5a first: it is
what members use, and its open questions (§4) are the ones that change how leave is counted. While he answers M5a's, M5b's
drawings can be made.

What was put to him:
1. **War bal after an approval.** D1217 rules War bal = App bal less the days *pending*. Once a bid is approved it is no
   longer pending, so its days come back into War bal until he types a new App bal — Ranger types App bal 20, bids 3 days
   (War bal 17), the bid is approved, War bal shows 20 again. Today's code takes approved leave off too. *Recommended:*
   War bal = App bal less every leave of his, pending or approved, dated on or after the day App bal was last typed — so
   approval changes nothing, and re-typing App bal from the official app starts the count afresh (the date App bal was
   typed becomes a saved field).
2. **A leave across 31 Dec** ([LEAVE-YEAR], still to rule). *Recommended:* each day is charged to the year it falls in.
3. **D1235's three readings**, to confirm before the 1 Jan step: a person with no grade gets only the roll-over; days
   pending on 1 Jan count in the year they fall in; a balance below zero carries nothing forward. *Recommended:* as read.
4. **The split** (§3). *Recommended:* two merges.

## 5. Risks the code survey found (each carried into the build spec)

1. Every ⚙ setting and the balance write go through the war's general edit command, whose permission row is a bid's; a
   member is stopped only because the opening balance is listed as an admin's record. M5a needs its own balance command
   with its permission row and §11 row (D200), and that listing changed for a member's own row while unlocked.
2. The balance write takes any decimal, leaves no history line, and still accepts OIL at the store (only the screen hides
   it) — B6's fix closes all three.
3. EL has a counter but no figure anywhere: "every kind" (D1201) has to show it.
4. Two ways change a balance today — the typed opening and the FIGURES drawer's grants (a drag down a column); the
   drawer goes (D1245), so where a grant is made from must be settled in the build spec.
5. A member's seen-moment is a new own-row record with its permission row (M4's admin one is the pattern).
6. The grid's day-cell code must not change (`docs/performance.md` §E); removing the drawer is measured before and after.

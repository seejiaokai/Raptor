## 2. The rulings it builds and must not break

**Builds:** D400 (earned vs awarded), D401 (no stored award converted; an old record never breaks a load), D402 (every
hand award on the grid on its date), D200 (2) (who entered it and when). **Must not break:** D79 (any day), D80 (no flag),
D82 (an award and a worked day add up), D142 (a day's OIL from its latest published version), D260 (Delete takes awards
and names them first; an award never moves), D261 (a member opens his own award read only, every stage), D265 (a bid
beside an award moves alone), D299 / D321 / D323 (a deleted man's past OIL stays; an archive takes nothing), D25 (OIL is
earned leave), N13 (an award is not attendance), N17 (an award leaves the manning alone), N19 (the label follows the
worth), D148 (undo per person), the 6 Sep 26 "reason only for OIL" on the tracker and the figures bar.

  man's single award opens nothing), which passes.

## 5. Sizing the walk (D607)
1. **Type of change:** A (a rule inside one shared calculation — the R box joins `resolveReporting`), C (a new control and
   gesture — the R box, a tap that places a suggestion, the button-press hold), D (the time cells drawn by three builders
   — the week, the peek, the board — plus the export and the print), E (layout — R/B · TO/LD on the week, one line or
   stacked on the board), G (the publish path — a changed rally on a published day).
2. **What only a walk could find here:** each surface that draws the time cells separately (week edit and view, the peek,
   the version look, the board at its two layouts and the read-only board); the tap / type / Escape / Tab orders by mouse
   AND finger (the phone's selection behaviour is the known risk); the first-press hold across many buttons; the
   published-day orders around OIL (type → publish → change → amend; publish → change → undo; reload); the warning
   list's readers (crew rest, long day, order) on screen. The arithmetic is carried by `engine/rallybox.test.ts`; the
   markup by `ui/brieftime-ui.test.tsx`; the geometry by `e2e/rallybox.spec.ts` and the rewritten geometry tests.
3. **What the ledger says** (§The figures): D — 10 walks, all 10 found a real fault (61 faults, 57 in the change); C — 15
   walks, 8 found one (31 faults); G — 7 walks, 4 found one (22 faults, 20 in the change); E — 10 walks, 4 found one (6);
   A — 9 walks, 5 found one (14 faults, only 2 in the change). So: the surfaces drawn separately and the gesture and
   publish-path orders are where the walk's value is; the arithmetic is left to the tests, its readers seen once each.
4. **The walk chosen:** two Sonnet 5.5 walkers (D787) in parallel, one served build (port 4298, bundle `index-CU-49a0e.js`),
   each its own browser world. **A — desktop 1440** (plus 821 / 820 / 768 wide, a 700-tall window and a browser started
   at 125%): the designer's scenarios 1, 2, 4, 5, 8 (desktop), 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 19, 20, 22. **B — phone 402 by
   finger** (iPhone-like touch; plus a member): 3, 6, 7, 8 (phone), 11, 16, 18, 19 (phone), 21. About 25 scenarios. The
   designer's list is `docs/handpass/parts/rally-box/scenarios.md`; none set aside. Carried by a test, not walked
   again: the RB1–RB3 arithmetic (each reader on screen once in scenarios 1–3, 17); the D505 / D506 precedence cases
   beyond scenario 2's four.
5. **After the walk:** the ledger row — written at the close.

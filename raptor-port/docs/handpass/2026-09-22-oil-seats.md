## 6. What the walk found

### Finding 1 — a count that says everyone earns is captioned "some of them do not"

**Where:** every count chip, on every surface, mode on or off. Seen on a duty
desk ("30 of 30 earn"), a sim passenger row ("27 of 27 earn") and a Common
Programme row ("39 of 39 earn").

**What happens:** when the men behind a placeholder all earn but at *different
amounts* — some a full day, some half — the chip reads "30 of 30 earn" and its
own caption reads *"Some of these men earn OIL and some do not"*. The two
sentences contradict each other on the same puck. The money is right; the words
are not.

**Why it happens:** the summary reports a single amount only when every man gets
the same one. A mixed-amount crowd returns "no single amount", and the wording
reads that as "not everyone earns". The case where *all* of them earn at two
different rates has no sentence of its own.

**Consequence:** a scheduler reading a published day is told some of those
thirty men are not being paid, when all thirty are. That is a wrong statement
about money on the face of an issued document.

**New to this branch?** The caption is. Before this change the chip could not
appear on most of these seats at all.

**Picture:** `GAP-01-count-captions.png`.

### Finding 2 — an exempt line's earn switch is painted green while its own words say it earns nothing

**Where:** every AVALON line and AVALON duty desk, in the earn mode.

**What happens:** the switch is drawn in the *earning* style — green fill, green
border — and its own tooltip reads *"This kind of event earns nothing — tap to
make it earn"*. The money is off (every man behind it shows as not earning), so
only the paint is wrong.

**Why it happens:** the switch has three states in its words (earns · earns
nothing by default · switched off by hand) but only two in its paint. "Switched
off by hand" gets the struck-through grey; *everything else* gets the green,
including "this kind of event earns nothing".

**Consequence:** D24 says the four exempt kinds "all start OFF". On screen they
start green. An admin scanning the board sees green and reads it as earning —
the one thing the colour exists to say.

**New to this branch?** **Yes.** On `main` the switch was a clean two-state
control and exempt kinds never drew one at all — step 4 is what brought them
into the mode.

**Picture:** `GAP-02-switch-states.png`.

### Finding 3 — a sim row that is exactly full offers no way to add another body

**Where:** any sim row whose seats are full — an OFT with both seats taken and
no extras, or an AMT box with an even number of passengers.

**What happens:** there is no empty slot to drop into, and the "+ add" strip
every other people-cell carries is not drawn on a sim row at all. A drag onto
the row therefore lands on a seated man and **replaces him** — the rear-seat
pilot silently becomes the placeholder.

**Why it happens:** the sim seat grid pads itself to an even number of seats, so
an already-even row grows no spare slot; and the "+ add" strip is deliberately
switched off for that grid, on the reasoning that it "auto-adds rows as pucks
land, so it never packs edge-to-edge and swaps". That reasoning holds for odd
counts and fails for even ones.

**Consequence:** the register promises that a placeholder on a sim row's extras
counts the people it stands for, and the roll-call says the switch must be
offered wherever a puck may land. The engine does all of that correctly — but a
scheduler cannot put the puck there. And the gesture he *will* try loses a man
off the sortie without saying so.

**New to this branch?** **No — this is pre-existing**, identical on `main`. But
it matters more now: before this change a placeholder on a sim row credited
nobody, so the missing door led nowhere. This change made that seat pay real
people.

**Pictures:** `DOOR-06-append-truth.png`, `DOOR-07-addz.png`.

### A false alarm, recorded so it is not re-found

Tapping a count chip appeared to open nothing, on both the working copy and the
issued page. It was the walk's own instrument: the app's message element carries
no class, and the reader was matching class names. The tap works and lists every
man with his amount. Fixed in `seat-lib.mjs` with the reason written beside it.

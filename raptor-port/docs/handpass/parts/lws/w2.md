## Notes on the three FAILs and on the set-up

1. **Set-up the app's own controls cannot do while the page is away.** Posting a man out from a chosen date (22c, 22h, 27 edges) and filing a leave for a posted-out man (22h, 27 edge 3) were done through the developer bridge (`lwSetPostOut`, `fileInput` — the real inputs door), because the Post out button and the Inputs form sit on pages where the Leave War is on screen or take many steps; the war's own PI button and Archive on Admin → Users were used where they fit (27 edge 2; 21, 22c archive, 23). There is no control that moves an EXISTING man's post-in while the war is away (Admin → Users edits only his sign-in, role, Archive, Delete); so scenario 22(a) was done as "a man added with a later post-in" and scenario 21 covers the archive-and-restore move. A clash with the schedule (Item 8) needs a published day; it was made with the project's own fixture (the e2e `twoClashes` calls: a programme row on Sat 18 Jul, the day signed and published), the bids by the Leave War sheet. The "different leave filed on the Inputs page" of the brief cannot make a clash: the door refuses it ("Basher already has LL on 10 Nov — OL can't overlap it"), so the reachable clash is the weekend/holiday-work kind.
2. **30, Undo then Redo while armed (desktop).** With the sheet open the scrim covers the top bar, so a person cannot press Undo without first pressing the scrim (which closes the sheet and takes the arm back). Only a scripted click reaches the buttons; then an armed "Really reset?" survives Undo and Redo (the order went default and came back, and the question is still live). Not reachable by a person with the app as it stands; filed as a latent edge, the host to judge.
3. **33 tap, a SANS man with Show SANS off.** The note's instruction "Decide the bid ›" opens nothing: the day sheet opens from the man's own row, and he has none. It is silent, no error. A scheduler is left with a clash he cannot decide from the note unless he turns Show SANS on first. This is how the tap always worked for a man with no row (the callsign fix did not change it) — probably older behaviour — but it contradicts the scenario's "goes where its instruction says". The same tap for an archived man whose row is still drawn works (34).

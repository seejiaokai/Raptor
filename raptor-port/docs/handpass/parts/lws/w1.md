## Notes — scenario designer's open questions, answered

- Scenario 6 (the open question): a future date picked while the question is up is SAVED at once, no ask, no delete (the contract's wording). The sheet, line, Admin tag and grid all moved to the new date.
- The ask shows for a date the app then refuses (scenario 17, noted by the designer): "Tap again to delete Probey" was drawn for a date before his post-in, and only the tap produced the refusal sentence. Not a regression of D1102's rule (nothing was deleted, the box went back), but a button that promises a delete the app will not do.
- A man with no post-in date of his own (Hex, in an earlier try on a fresh world): picking Mon 5 Jan 26, 3.5 months in the past, asked and the tap deleted him dated that day. No refusal. That try was also why scenario 17 was re-run on a made-up man with a post-in date; Hex's delete in that run ended his world, so scenario 4 was run in a fresh world.
- The scenario's "top-bar Undo puts the Delete posting back" (9) and "the top-bar Undo does NOT bring him back" (4): the top-bar Undo is greyed out after a posting and after a delete, with a tooltip explaining why (D350); it could not be pressed. Not a fault.

## Odd things in passing (marked as older behaviour where it is)

- Delete leaves his row drawn for the months up to the delete date: after deleting Hex "today", October still shows his row with struck days from today, November does not. The scenario's "H's row leaves the war" holds from the first month after the date only. Probably the intended display span (the past keeps his record, D299); flagged because the scenario text reads as if the whole row goes.
- A future schedule day he sat on: none exists to check. The demo schedule's only week is Jul 13 – 19 (past), so "a future day shows the seat empty / pending" was NOT WALKED; the past day kept his puck on both sizes (older rule D297, confirmed).
- Pressing a month button on the Leave War drops the rows of men posted out before that month (older, by design: it is what Item 6 is about); a script that tapped Hex's struck day after pressing NOV found no cell until it used Oct. A real user pressing NOV cannot tap an Oct 31 day either, so reaching the sheet again means choosing the month he was posted out in.
- After a page switch the one-day sheet and the drag-selection sheet on a desktop stay open, and keep the date last picked in them (for example Tue 10 Nov 26); only the "Tap again" is taken back. On a phone the one-day sheet closes at the switch while the drag-selection sheet stays. Not asked about; fine for D1102.
- Older behaviour: in the one-day sheet's PO the preselected "Overseas Sqn" chip is a toggle; tapping it unselects it and the line reads "off the manpower, nothing else" (D303). That is how Ryder's posting ended up outcome-less.
- Older behaviour: Delete posting line wording differs by whether the man has an account: "deleted with his account…" (Hex, Probey is "deleted." — no account).
- After the delete the person list still keeps Hex under the hood as deleted + archived, but Admin → Users does not list him in Archived (the archive list is not shown at all).
- Sign-in as a deleted man: stays on the sign-in card; I did not capture an error sentence (none looked for). If a message is wanted when the delete is in, that is a separate check.

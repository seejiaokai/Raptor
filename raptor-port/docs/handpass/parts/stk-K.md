## Findings
1. **L-09:** press inside any of the four confirmation windows, drag out, release on the surround → window closes (desktop and phone). Steps above. D538 expected it to stay open.
2. **L-11:** a published day with a remark containing two spaces: tabbing through the day alone creates "1 pending", opens "Publish AL1" and a change line that looks identical (the Ground row's text collapses to one space).
3. **L-14:** an OIL window opened by a Tab leaves the caret in the box behind it; typed text lands there. The request is already saved before the window is answered.
4. **L-16:** on the phone board's Desktop layout the failed-save band scrolls with the board; Retry sits 1000+ px from the warning words.
5. Observations (no verdict): after changing a box, the first click into another box gives it no caret (focus goes to the page body, L-10, L-12); while tabbing, the day's issue count, warning list, puck rings and "N changes/pending" chip stay stale until Escape or a click (L-13); the last Tab out of a day lands on the page body first (L-12).

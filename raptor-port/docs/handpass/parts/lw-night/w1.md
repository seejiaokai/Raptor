# Walker 1 — the manning bubble (scenarios 1–21, break tests 1–3, the state table)

The night of 25 Oct 26, branch `claude/lw-m3-placing-leave`, the final build served at `http://localhost:4180/` (bundle `index-Cw8BzgyR.js`, equal to the local `dist`).
Driven with scripted real browsers from `raptor-port/scripts/handpass/lw-night-w1-*.mjs` (library `lw-night-w1-lib.mjs`; one script per group of scenarios, run once per size). Pictures: `raptor-port/docs/img/handpass/2026-10-25-lw-night/w1/` (file names below are given without the folder and without ".png"). Raw records of every run: `w1/w1-*.json` in the same folder.
Sizes: phone 402 × 874 at 3×, touch, iPhone user agent — in Chromium and in WebKit (Safari's engine); phone on its side 874 × 402; desktop 1440 × 900 at 1×. On a phone every tap got a picture about 0.1 s after the finger lifted (D1281; the name ends "-after-tap") besides the settled picture; the bubble reading is taken at the same moment.
Colours read from the screen on every bubble: red = fill rgb(46,20,24), edge rgba(240,85,95,.7), words rgb(255,170,176), the little arrow the same fill; amber = fill rgb(42,34,16), edge rgba(229,168,59,.7), words rgb(242,200,121). Every bubble step also read where it stands, its words as seen, its box against the screen (never past an edge), no sideways page scroll, and — for the foot note — that the note's own centre is the note and the bid just placed is not under it. Each "nothing raised" step also asserts proof the press happened (the cell's own state and the figure).

## Counts

229 recorded checks (the scenarios' steps and sub-steps, each size and engine on its own line): **194 PASS, 5 FAIL, 28 RECORD, 2 NOT DONE**. Besides these, 42 browser-world checks of the console, page errors and failed requests: all clean (none in any world).
The FAILs are two findings (each on every size it was run at): the bubble of an overseas duty filed over a weekend stands at the foot, behind the OIL question; and a second quick press on "Show ›" opens a stray bid window. NOT DONE: scenario 2 on a phone (the list puts it on the desktop: keys). The locked week of scenario 8 cannot be made on screen; the nearest reachable pending day was walked (said on its line).

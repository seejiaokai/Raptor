## (c) Findings

### F1 — "Undo post out (PO)" leaves the man archived: his row, bids and leave VANISH from the war (survives a reload)

**What happened.** A Post out made through the bid sheet has "Archive on PO date" ON by default, and the man is archived at
once (the date is already past on the app's calendar). The posting sheet's own "Undo post out (PO)" removes the date but
NOT the archive. An archived man with no posting dates is dropped by the war's roster (the "one limit" CURRENT-STATE §B
names), so his row disappears in EVERY month — including July, when he was in the squadron, and September, where his
clearing leave sits. A reload does not bring him back; the Quals page as it opens does not list him either.

**Steps from a fresh world (admin `ad`).** (1) Inputs page: LL for Cobra, 10–11 Sep 2026. (2) Leave War, July: tap Cobra's
28 Jul → PO → date 2026-08-01 → leave "Archive on PO date" as it is → "Post out from 2026-08-01". (3) September: 10–11 Sep
read `LL PO` — correct. Tap the blank hatched 14 Sep → the posting sheet → "Undo post out (PO)". (4) Look at July, August,
September: no Cobra. Reload: still none. The same with Drifter posted out from 15 Jul and his leave on 17 Jul (the SXO
group reads 11, no Drifter). Scripts `w5-05b-postout-row-probe.mjs` (check 4), `w5-05-postout-file.mjs` (A-sheet-undo).

**Rule.** The posting sheet's Undo should put him back as he was before the Post out (the archive the same Post out made
included); answer C / N8 and CURRENT-STATE item 13 (his leave is held, SHOWN and charged).

**Who it hurts.** The admin who corrects a mistaken Post out loses the man from the Leave War entirely: his leave, bids and
notices can no longer be seen, decided or moved there, and the only way back is to restore him from the Quals archive —
nothing on screen says so. His leave stays on the Inputs page.

**Pictures:** `w5-05b-desktop-3-drifter-after-undo-postout`, `w5-05b-desktop-4-cobra-after-undo-postout-sep`,
`w5-05-desktop-A4-after-undo-post-out` (and the phone twin `w5-05-phone-A4-after-undo-post-out`).

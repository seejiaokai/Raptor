5. **A pick made outside the mode stays lit in the mode,** also after a refused tap (A23).

7. **The passing-note element keeps its last words after it fades** (`#toastEl` still holds the text, opacity 0). A script that reads it without emptying it first reads the previous note. Not a fault of the app a person can see.

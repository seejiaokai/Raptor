Two new questions from the fixes

15

Narrow phones: a date breaks, or the buttons get narrower

Narrower buttons on a phone

On phones narrower than yours (390 and 360 wide), and with an iPhone's wider letters, a line has no room for the name, the box, "23 Jan AM ›" and both buttons, so the › drops to a second line (first picture). And a lit "Approved" is wider than "Approve", so its left edge moves under your finger (second picture). Making each button as wide as its longer word fixes the second but makes the first worse, even at your 402. Narrowing Approve and Refuse a little on a phone (smaller side padding, same height) fixes both. You ruled that no button changes size without your word, so this is yours to say.

16

Approve leave for days after a man posts out?

Refuse it, unless he becomes SANS

Today Approve is refused only once a man's posting has gone through. Before that, his leave for days after his posting-out date can still be approved, as here: Trident posts out on 1 Mar and his 13–16 Apr leave was approved. My recommendation: refuse it with "He posts out on 1 Mar: refuse or delete it", except when he is posted out as SANS, since a SANS man's leave is still kept.

Fixed without asking

These were plain faults, not design choices, so I fixed them as you ruled. Each has a test that failed first, and each is walked again before your look.

The bell went on saying "bidding open" for dates long gone

Once the dates had passed, the "its dates ended" line could lose to the older "bidding open" line, and two weeks on the "open" line came back. Now a man told that bidding opened is always told it ended.

A long bell hides "Mark all read"

With 12 lines it stopped at the 9th with no sign of more, "Mark all read" out of sight. Now "Mark all read" stays at the foot, with "↓ 3 more" until you reach the end.

The manning bubble hides behind the slim bar

A bid that turns a row amber or red raised its bubble under the slim bar. It now stands above the bar, on a phone and a desktop.

A person's window covers the slim bar

A window opened half over the slim bar, cutting "Back". While the bar is up, windows now open above it.

At 390 and 360 wide the "8" poked out past the lit outline. Each tab now takes at least its own width.

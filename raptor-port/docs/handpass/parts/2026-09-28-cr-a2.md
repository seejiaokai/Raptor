## Findings

**F1 — Every Leave War undo says "a change to the leave board".** (both widths; known — plan B8 and D352 change it)
Repro: fresh world, admin → Leave War → any bid / Approve / Move / Delete / +OIL / ⚙ switch / stage move → Undo.
Showed: the bubble, the button's hover and the history line all read "a change to the leave board", for seven different
acts; the history puts them under "The day" with no name, so a reader of Wednesday's window sees three identical
"Undo — a change to the leave board" lines and cannot tell a bid from an approval from a removal
(`lw-24-H-changes-Wed.png`). Should: the app's word ("Leave War") and, ideally, the act and the man. Already planned:
B8 ("a change on the Leave War") and D352 (the stage's own words) — B8 as written still leaves the six non-stage acts
identical to each other.

**F2 — Filing ONE input on the Inputs page is described as "a batch of inputs".** (both widths; NEW)
Repro: sign in as Ranger → Inputs → file one LL on Wed 15 Jul → Leave War → look at Undo's hover → Undo → Redo.
Showed: hover "Undo — a batch of inputs", bubbles "Undid: a batch of inputs" / "Redid: a batch of inputs", and the
same words in the changes window (`inputs-03-I1-lw-undo.png`, `inputs-06-I1-lw-redo.png`, `roles-06`, `roles-07`).
Should: "a personal input" (Fable S9's expectation; the describer's own word for `inputs.write`). Cause, read not run:
the Inputs page files through the batch door (`writeInputsBatch`, `InputsPage.tsx` — its edit and OIL-answer saves too,
not walked), whose type is described as "a batch of inputs". The
plan (§ list review, "Clear old clutter … today 'a batch of inputs'") names only Clear old clutter — a single filing is
not mentioned. Every filing a member makes hits this, and for a member the war's Undo is the only door.

**F3 — With only a posting left, the greyed Undo's hover says nothing.** (both widths; known — plan B3, D350)
Repro: admin → Leave War → Echo, any day → PO → Post out (14 Oct) → press Undo until it greys. Showed: greyed, hover
"Undo the last change". And while an older step exists, Undo's hover names THAT step ("Undo — a change to the leave
board") and pressing it takes back Echo's earlier bid while the posting stays (`inputs-23-I4-app-undo.png`) — a person
who has just posted a man out and presses Undo sees his bid vanish and the PO remain. Should (plan B3): the sentence
saying a posting isn't undone here. Suggest the look card also covers the "skips the posting and takes the older step"
case — B3's sentence only appears when nothing older is left.

**F4 — An undo pressed on one page does not take you to the other page where the change was.** (both widths; partly
known — GU-E5, Fable Q3, plan B7)
Repro A: admin → +OIL award on the war → Edit Schedule → top-bar Undo: the award is gone, the view stays on Edit
Schedule, only the bubble says so (`lw-16`). Repro B: a schedule note and a planning-calendar title, then the war's
Undo twice: the view stays on the war both times (`inputs-14`, `inputs-16`); the member's Inputs filing undone from the
war likewise (`inputs-03`). AM39b says Undo "takes you to where the change was". Plan B7 covers roster/settings pages
and (its item 6) inputs/plan closures → the Inputs page; nothing in it takes a SCHEDULE change undone from the war to
the schedule, or a Leave War change undone from Edit Schedule to the war. Once [UNDO-TOPBAR] puts the pair on every
page, this is the common case.

**F5 — A ⚙ setting or a stage move on the war: its Undo / Redo lines are dated today, with no line for the change
itself.** (both widths; NEW)
Repro: admin → Leave War → ⚙ → Show SANS → Undo → Redo (or the stage → Undo → Redo) → Edit Schedule's clock. Showed:
nothing in the demo week; the four lines exist only in the week of 28 Sep (today, a week nobody opens), under "Mon ·
The day", and there is no line for the Show SANS switch or the stage move they undo (`lw-28`). Should: consistent
with plan item 9's principle for settings (no undo line when the forward change writes none), or both written where
someone will see them. The change-history rules (D263) name only absence changes, so this is the plan's call — flagged
because the undo line currently stands alone.

## What I could not walk, and why

1. **The redo-time clash check itself** (Astra 23, "medical / bid constraints bypassed"): the route through the app —
   undo a leave, file a medical on its day, redo — withdraws the Redo when the medical is filed, so the re-check that
   refuses a redo ("leave over a medical") is never reached from the screen. Needs a unit model.
2. **True two-user interleaving** (D148's "someone else changed the same thing — says who"): one browser can only take
   turns via sign-out / sign-in, which empties the list. Unit-only, as Fable S8 / Astra 11–13 say.
3. **Fable S15's lapse path**: skipped, unit-only (Fable's own note).
4. **Getting to a week**: week 2 on the phone, and the week of 28 Sep on both widths, were loaded through the probe bridge
   (the phone bar has no week buttons; the calendar door exists but I did not drive it). The desktop week 2 used the
   app's own week button. Navigation only — no gesture under test went through the bridge.
5. **The member's own changes window** (opened from a day's count on View-only Sched): not walked; I checked the admin's
   clock only, as the brief asked.
6. **Short screens** (a phone on its side, a 700px-tall laptop): not walked.

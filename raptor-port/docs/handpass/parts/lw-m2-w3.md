## Every FAIL, with the steps to reproduce

1. **36 — a long period name breaks the top row (desktop overlap, phone and 431–500 sideways page scroll).** Admin → period picker → "+ New period" → type a name (e.g. "Annual Leave Period For The Whole Squadron 2028", 47 characters) and pick any free dates → Create. On a phone (390) the picker grows to 321 px, My leave is pushed to x 381 (9 px on screen) and the whole page scrolls sideways (document 536 on a 390 screen); at 431 and 500 the same (My leave squeezed or half off, document 536 wide); at 600 and on a desktop a 47-character name is fine. With a 183-character name (the window has no maximum) the picker is 1109–1116 px wide on every size: on a desktop it overlaps My leave, on a phone/431/500/600 the page is 1324 wide and My leave is off screen. Expected by the scenario: nothing overflows, My leave stays visible or shrinks gracefully. Pictures s36-phone390-med, s36-phone390-long, s36-desk1536-long, s36-431-med, s36-500-med.
2. **38 — the Grid/OIL tab list's arrow keys do not move the focus.** Any role, any size: Tab to the Grid tab (or focus it), press ArrowRight: the OIL tab becomes selected (aria-selected) but document.activeElement stays on the Grid tab; ArrowLeft from there selects OIL again instead of Grid. The scenario's "the focus follows". Also (same scenario) with the OIL tracker up the whole screen, including the tab row, is covered, so the lit OIL tab and the unlit Grid tab cannot be seen (the selected state itself is right). Pictures s38-*-after-keys, s38-*-oil-up.
3. **60 — the Manning bubble cannot be dismissed from the keyboard and never times out.** Member, phone: bid LL on a day that turns Available P amber; the bubble shows; press Tab and Escape, wait 9 s: the bubble is still there. Only a pointer tap removes it. Pictures s60-bubble, s60-after-keys-and-9s.

Not FAIL but worth his look (each recorded in its row): 42 phone — "Done" is the ⇅ icon alone and the Manning caret still reads ▸ while the rows are forced open (M9); 39 — a pending bid already shows "next 10 Nov" in My leave; 58 — a Move onto a day where the bid turns a row red raises no bubble (M7); 59 — the bubble also shows on a desktop (M8); 61 — a bid placed with the page scrolled far down shows the bubble out of view; R14 — the red list prints "Available P 27", not "27 of 28" (M11); the bubble covers the month buttons while it is up; the bid window still says "HOW MANY / HOW MUCH" (D1212's "Date / Duration" is not in this build).

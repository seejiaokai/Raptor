## What the screen said, and what the money did

### E1 — exactly six hours, three ways

Sunday, built from nothing but the SDO desk.

- **One desk.** SDO 08:00–14:00 → the man's puck reads "Dash earns half a day of OIL" and wears the
  short pale bar. End moved to 14:01 → "earns a full day", full bar.
- **One sortie.** A line taking off 10:00 and landing 11:00 — a working day of 07:00 to 13:00, six
  hours to the minute → both seats read half a day. Landing moved to 11:01 → both read a full day.
- **Two desks with a gap.** The same man on 07:00–08:00 and 12:00–13:00 → six hours end to end → half
  a day on BOTH his pucks, so the gap is paid. The second desk to 13:01 → a full day on both. Then
  the early desk switched off inside the OIL Earn mode → that puck loses its bar entirely and he
  drops to half a day on the desk he still has, because the far end of his day went with it.
- **The money.** Published once at the end. The Leave War Sunday cells read FO, FO, FO and HO, and
  the OIL tracker boxes read "+1 · AUTO · Weekend/PH · Duty", "+1 … FLT" twice, and "+0.5 … Duty" —
  exactly what the pucks had said.

### E2 — midnight, no times, zero length

- **Across midnight.** A shift 20:00–02:00 → half a day on both crew; 20:00–02:01 → a full day. A
  night sortie taking off 23:00 and landing 00:30 → a full day (a working day of 20:00 to 02:30).
- **The money stays on the right day.** Both pay Sunday 19 Jul. Monday 20 Jul is empty for every one
  of them.
- **No times, and zero length.** A desk with a man and no times at all, and a desk 09:00–09:00. The
  warning list carries one line naming both: *"BLIND DESK and ZERO DESK have no times — nobody on
  them earns OIL for this day."* It survives publishing. Neither row offers a switch in the OIL Earn
  mode and neither man is tappable there. Tapping the man's puck anyway only selects him — he gains
  no bar and no money. Their Leave War cells stay empty.

### E3 — four rows, two of them count

Forge put on a desk 08:00–10:00, a sim 11:00–12:00, the info-only ADMIN row 13:00–14:00 and a
15:00–16:00 briefing.

- **Start.** Three glowing pucks reading "Forge earns a full day" (08:00 to 16:00 is eight hours) and
  a fourth, on the info-only row, that is inert and wears no bar.
- **Switch the late briefing off.** He drops to half a day on the desk and the sim; the briefing puck
  loses its bar and stops being tappable.
- **Back on, then take him off that one row only.** His briefing puck reads "Forge earns nothing from
  this event — tap to put him back on it" and the other two drop to half a day. Same result as
  switching the whole row off, for him.
- **The money.** Published in that state: Leave War cell HO, tracker "+0.5 · AUTO · Weekend/PH · SIM +
  Duty", and the day's detail reads *"half a day — worked 08:00–10:00, 11:00–12:00"*. The info-only
  row and the briefing are correctly absent from the worked hours.

### E4 — two men on one line, one denied

## FINDINGS

### 1. On the scheduler board, an ALL AVAIL row never says how many men it stands for

**What I did.** Put an ALL AVAIL row on a day, then read it on all three places the app draws it:
the scheduler board, the Edit Schedule week and the View-only page. Then denied one of the men
inside the OIL Earn mode and read all three again.

**What happened.** On both week surfaces the puck carries a small count beside it — "45", or
"44 of 45 earn" once someone is denied — and hovering says what it means, and tapping it lists every
man with "full day", "half day" or "nothing" beside his name. On the **scheduler board that count is
not drawn at all**, in any of the four states. The board draws the puck and nothing else.

Pictures: `EF-F2-03-one-denied-board.png` (bare puck) beside `EF-F2-03-one-denied-viewonly.png`
(the same row with "44 of 45 earn"). Also `EF-F2B-01-board-sentinel.png`.

**What should have happened.** The count should be beside the puck on the board too. It is the only
thing that tells a scheduler how many men a row pays, and the only way to ask who they are.

**How bad this is for a real squadron.** This is the worst of the four states, not the best. When
some of the men behind a row earn and some have been denied, the puck deliberately wears **no green
bar** — the count is what carries that fact instead. On the board there is no count, so a row that is
paying 44 men and refusing one looks identical to a row that pays nobody and to a row nobody is free
for. The board is where the scheduler works, where he edits the day, and where the OIL Earn button
lives — so the one screen he is on when he makes the decision is the one screen that will not tell
him its consequence. He would have to leave the board for the week view, or enter the OIL Earn mode
and count pucks by hand. Nothing is mis-paid: the money and the week views are right. This is a
missing reading on the working surface.

Same shape as the three defects the owner found on 21 Sep: a surface the feature was never connected
to. The board's people cell for a Common Programme row draws the puck and the "+ add" strip and drops
the count the shared seat builder produces.

## OBSERVATIONS — not defects, but worth a line

**2. A crew still inside the working day of their own sortie counts as "available" for a muster in
that window.** Ranger and Echo flew 10:00–11:00. The OIL measure counts their working day as 07:00
to 13:00 — three hours before take-off, two after landing — and pays them on that basis. But an
ALL AVAIL row at 12:00–13:00 lists both of them as available and would put them on it. So the app
uses two different ideas of when a man is busy: his event's own clock for availability, and his
working day for money. It moves no money (they earn from the sortie anyway, and that muster falls
inside the day they are already paid for), and the availability rule almost certainly predates this
build — I did not compare against `main`. But the row's membership is wrong on screen: two men are
offered for a muster they cannot attend. Evidence: `EF-F1B-02-musters-mode.png` and the table under
F1 above.

**3. On a day whose OIL came only from the published schedule, the day sheet still offers a "Clear"
button, and pressing it does nothing.** I pressed it (Codex 23 asks for exactly this) and the earned
day survived — cell, tracker and balance all unchanged, which is the correct outcome. But the button
is there and reads as though it would remove something. A scheduler who wants that day's OIL gone
would press it, see nothing happen, and have no idea he is supposed to change the schedule instead.
The sheet does say "change the schedule and the OIL follows" a few lines below.
Evidence: `EF-H-14-after-clearing-the-auto-credit-day.png`.

**4. An SC wave drew eight "Earns OIL — tap to stop this item earning" switches on one day, seven of
them on lines with nobody on them.** Seen in passing while building E2; it belongs to the scenario
about switches on rows that can never earn (Fable S3), which another worker is covering. Recording
it only so it is not lost. Also: the SC shift crew's credit reads "FLT" in the tracker, which reads
oddly for a shift rather than a sortie — same owner, worth one glance.

## What was NOT walked, and why

- **Phone width.** Everything here was driven at desktop width. The phone has its own OIL Earn button
  and its own board layout, covered by another worker's scenario.
- **The fifth state of the ALL AVAIL puck — a window nobody at all is free for.** There is no way to
  reach it through the app's own controls without first making all forty-five men busy, which is not
  a realistic day. Not walked; recorded rather than faked.
- **Publish-boundary orders, amendment numbering, the holiday cases and the mode's exits.** Other
  workers' scope.

## One thing that looked like a defect and was not

Adding a THIRD man to a Common Programme row that already holds two appeared to fail. It was my own
driving — the row's people cell is one target, so a click at its centre lands on a man's puck and
selects him instead of arming the cell. Clicking the "+ add" strip at the end of the cell works every
time. No defect. `EF-probe-massbrief-third.png`.

## Findings

**F-1 — a Tab commit leaves the warning list stale (Board and week).** Steps: Friday wave with an In-time line at 08:00 and a second line; type "10:00 RALLY" in the second line and press Tab.
Expected (D509): the warning list shows the red Rally line. Happened: the app holds it (the red message appears under the line), but the Board's list still says
"No conflicts flagged for Friday ✓" (u-001) and the week's Friday bar says "2 issues" instead of 3 (u-004); committed with Enter or by clicking away the same edit updates both (u-002, u-005); correcting the Rally to 09:00 by Tab leaves the old warning showing (u-003). Same for a take-off typed on the Board and committed by Tab. Only the display is stale — the model is right. Reopening the board refreshes it.

**F-2 — phone Desktop-layout Board: the sideways pan and the ⋯ menu.** (a) A sideways wheel/pan takes only when it starts over the Board's top bar; over the schedule body nothing moves (the Board's body scroller holds the gesture). (b) Panned so ⋯ sits at the right edge (x 320 of 390) its menu opens at 320–496 and the Insights item's middle is off screen (q-007). With the Board panned further left everything works (q-008…q-010).

**F-3 — an issued day's earned leave moves when a Logic value changes (X-11, P2-01's case).** Steps: publish a Saturday flight 10:00–11:00 for Ranger and Echo (Leave War HO*, figure 0.5 — w-001); Logic → "Nominal report before T/O" 3h → 4h; no amendment. Leave War now FO*, figure 1 (w-002), and the OIL tracker lists "+1 · 18 Jul · AUTO · FLT 06:00–13:00" (r-002) — the day's work now starts at the new nominal 06:00, so it clears the six-hour line. D48 says an issued day keeps its money when a rule changes under it.

Observations: the Tracker's own corner chip reads "● saved" while the top bar says "Not saved" (b-002); the OIL tracker's ✕ is 19×17px; Insights hours did not move when the nominal lead changed (RECORDED).

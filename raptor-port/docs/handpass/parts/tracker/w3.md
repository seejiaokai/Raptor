## 2. Findings (all CONFIRMED — seen happening)

### w3-F1 — On a phone, the Details bubble outlives the chart: over the Info tab, over other pages, and over the NEXT person's schedule after a logout (Fable #10)

**What a person sees.** On a phone, with ⓘ Details on, tapping a ball shows its bubble. The bubble then
stays: over the Info tab when they switch to it; over the Leave War grid when they go there through the
menu; and after Logout — hidden behind the sign-in card — it reappears over the next person's
View-only schedule the moment they sign in (the member saw the admin's "ACG-02 Propulsion System").

**Steps (phone, finger).** Sign in `ad`/`a` → menu → Tracker → ⓘ → tap ST-01 → tap "ⓘ Info" → tap
"Flow chart" → tap ACG-01 → menu → Leave War → menu → Tracker → tap ACG-02 → menu → Logout → sign in
`us`/`us`.

**Should have seen.** The bubble goes when its chart leaves the screen, and never crosses a logout.

**Where.** The bubble is one element on the page body (`core.js` `showDetailBubble`, ~line 3652); only
a chart redraw, the mouse leaving the ball, a press on the chart or the ⓘ toggle hide it. The phone's
Flow/Info switch (`App.jsx` `ViewTabs` → `setTab`), the Tracker page going inactive
(`TrackerPage.tsx`) and the logout (`resetSession`) never do. On a desktop it happens to go because
the mouse "leaves" the ball when the page changes. Fable's §5 fix 10 names the three places.

### w3-F2 — A resize or a turn of the phone made while the Info tab shows leaves the chart un-fitted — scrolling sideways — until "reset"

**What a person sees.** On a phone, on the ⓘ Info tab, turn the phone upright after having used the
chart sideways, then tap "Flow chart": the chart is stuck at the sideways zoom (92%) — 842px of chart
in a 390px screen, scrolling sideways, one ball in view at the edge. The same on a desktop window
narrowed from over 1050px while on the Info tab (the chart comes back at 100%, 912px in 390px), and
the first event can no longer be centred (the half-view of empty space above it is gone). Turned
sideways on the Info tab, the chart keeps the upright fit (40%) instead of filling the sideways screen
(92%). The same turns made on the Flow tab re-fit correctly; "reset" puts it right.

**Should have seen (R101).** On a phone the chart fits the screen width and only scrolls up and down.

**Where.** `core.js` — the resize listener (~5319) and `fitPhoneWidth` (~2736) measure the chart's box,
which measures 0 while the Info tab hides it, so the fit is skipped (and above 1050px the zoom is set
to 100% regardless); the centring space (`padBoard`) is measured the same way. Nothing re-fits when
the Flow tab is picked again (`App.jsx` `setTab`). A person can still scroll sideways to a ball and tap it.

### w3-F3 — A phone turned sideways cannot grade: the pop-up's grade buttons are above the top of the screen; the chart's zoom control is below the bottom (Fable #43, predicted there; worse than predicted)

**What a person sees.** At 844×390 (a phone on its side) a tap on a ball opens the grading pop-up
starting 73px above the top edge: the title ("ST-01 · STUDENT A") and the **Not done** and **DCO**
buttons are off the top, DPCO and Marginal half cut. The pop-up does not scroll and the page does not

desktop menu in two rows at this width), so the chart gets about 164px.

**Steps.** A phone (or an 844×390 touch window) → sign in → menu → Tracker → tap the top-most ball.

**Should have seen.** Every part of the pop-up reachable (Fable §5 fix 43: a height cap with its own
scrolling, and never placed above the top), and the column fitting the screen (R68).

**Where.** `Pop.jsx` places the pop-up above the ball when it does not fit below
(`y = p.y - r.height - 12`) with no floor at the top; `.pop` has no height cap or scrolling
(`tracker.css` ~323). The column is the screen minus the measured top bar (`TrackerPage.tsx`,
`--tr-top`); at 844×390 the bar, tabs, chart and zoom control do not fit what is left.

### w3-F4 — "Reset to doc" on a ball the user made wipes everything typed — no question, no undo, Cancel does not bring it back (Fable #44, predicted there)

**What a person sees.** A ball they added themselves (arrange → + Acad) has no source document. They

undo", and Show All shows the row empty. The same happens in Show All's own Edit → Reset to doc.

**Steps.** Syllabus ✎ → ✎ Edit chart layout → + Acad → name `MY-01` → ✓ Done editing chart → ✓ Save
changes → tap MY-01 → ✎ Edit details → fill Name, Hours, Crew → Save → tap MY-01 → ✎ Edit details →
Reset to doc → Cancel → ☰ Show All.

**Should have seen.** No "Reset to doc" where there is no document (Fable §5 fix 44: show the button
only when the event has shipped details), or at least a question first. Separately, for every event the
reset is written the moment it is pressed, so the window's Cancel beside it no longer cancels anything.

**Where.** `Modals.jsx` `InfoModalInner` (`#ifReset`) and `ShowAllPanel.jsx` `SaEdit` → `core.js`
`resetInfoFor` (~3785: deletes the details and saves at once).

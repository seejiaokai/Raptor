## Findings

### F1: after deleting a man who signed a published day, Undo is stuck and gives an instruction nobody can follow (new)
**Steps, from a fresh world (admin):**
1. Edit Schedule → calendar → Sat 3 Oct.
2. Open the board. On the Common Programme press "+ Item" and put Hex on it.
3. Quals → Enable editing → WSOs → tick SCHEDULER on Hex.
4. Back on the board, sign the four with Hex as PLANNED BY, then press Publish day.
5. Admin → Users → Hex → Delete, then "Tap again to delete Hex".
6. Edit Schedule → the top bar's Undo. The button reads "Undo — a sign-off".

**What showed:** "A later change touches the same thing — undo that first.", the same on every press, with the button still
on. The "later change" is Saturday's publish. Undo passes over it for good, because taking it back would put the deleted
Hex's signature back (D287). So "undo that first" cannot be done.

Following AM39c's own advice makes it worse: after Unpublish, the next Undo takes back the Unpublish (republishing the
day), and the one after that is stuck again. Nothing behind the publish can ever be reached. AM39c's sentence ("A day on
this week was published after that change — tap Unpublish on that day first…") never appears on this route, which is the
route Fable's S13 predicted would show it.

**What should have shown:** a message that is true. It should say the change can't be undone because a later change can't
be taken back, and never tell the person to undo it. The post-out-outcomes plan (its line 713) meant a step that is passed
over to block with "…can't be undone yet". The build says "undo that first" instead.

**Why, from the code (for the builder):** in `src/undo/timeline.ts`, `undoConflict` picks its words with `isEligible(o)`
only. A newer entry that `deadFor` passes over still counts as eligible, so it gets "undo that first".

Separately, the Unpublish → Undo loop means AM39c's advice can never work in last-in-first-out order. Put that to the
builder: the advice may need rewording, or the Unpublish may need handling.

**Pictures:** d/s13-05, d/s13-06, d/s13-07 and the p/ twins. **Rare route** (it needs a deleted man whose signature is on a
published day still to come), but it is a dead end with a false instruction. **New:** not in `OUTSTANDING.md`. Nothing
under `raptor-port/src` has changed on this branch, so `main` behaves the same.

### F2: the board's Undo that crosses to another week closes the board (new)
**Steps:**
1. Open the board on Mon 13 Jul and change its first overall note.
2. Calendar → Thu 23 Jul, open the board on Thursday.
3. Press the **board's** Undo.

**What showed:** the note reverted and week 13 Jul loaded, but **the board vanished**. You land on the edit week, on
Thursday (not Monday). The board's Redo went with it, so the Redo you can reach is the top bar's.

**What should have shown:** the board staying open on Monday 13 Jul, where the change was. AM39b: "takes you to where the
change was". The code means to do it: `snapView` in `src/state/undo-wire.ts` says "if the board is open, bring the changed
day onto it". But `loadWeek` closes the board first, so by then there is no board to bring the day onto.

**Pictures:** d/land-04, p/land-04, d/nav-10, p/nav-10. **New.**

### F3: the top bar's Undo never brings the changed day into view on the edit week (new, worst on a phone)
**Steps:**
1. Edit Monday's first note (on the board or the week), then close the board.
2. Step the edit week to Friday.
3. Press the top bar's Undo.

**What showed:** the note reverted, but the week stayed on Friday/Saturday. On a phone, which shows one day, the change is
never on screen, and the only sign of it is the bubble "Undid: a change to the schedule". Across weeks
(Mon 13 Jul undone from Thu 23 Jul) the right week loads, but on **Thursday**, the day index you were on, not Monday.

The off-week S32 case passed only because the change and the view were both on Tuesday.

**What should have shown:** the changed day on screen, as the board already does (landing B passed). AM39b, and the owner's
13 Sep 26 "[XWEEK-UNDO]: snaps you to that week and shows what the undo did".

**Code note:** `snapView` does nothing for the edit week ("best-effort — the shell's week view already shows the loaded
week"). The change-recording plan's B7 covers the page-level snap for the roster and settings only, not the day on the
week.

**Pictures:** d/land-01, p/land-01, d/land-03, p/land-03. **New:** not filed. The nearest item, GU-E5, is an input-only undo
not jumping weeks.

## Smaller observations (not failures; for the look card)
- **O1: OIL Earn ends when the board closes** (`setBoardDay` clears it). So the top bar's own "Left OIL Earn" guard
  (`Shell.tsx`) can never be met: the top bar sits behind the board while the mode is on. With the board closed, the top
  bar's Undo takes back OIL decisions with the mode already shut. This is safe (nothing wrong changes). Pictures: d/oil-07,
  d/oil-08.
- **O2: signing another day drops an undone sign-off's Redo.** Sign Fri, Undo, then sign Sat, and Friday's Redo is gone.
  All the week's sign-offs are one record, so any new sign-off drops the redo tail. That is the F-w3-2 fix working as
  built, with no stall, but Fable's S14 expected Friday's Redo to survive. Pictures: d/nav-05, p/nav-05.
- **O3: bubble words.** Undoing one LL filed on the Inputs page says "Undid: **a batch of inputs**" (S16; Fable S9 expects
  "a personal input"). Undoing "Discard 2 edits & load" says only "a change to the schedule", and so do wave add, row
  delete and wave drag. Nothing says what came back. This is A2's S23 ground; I record only what I saw.
- **O4: no "Discard marks" on a phone.** The Amendments panel is hidden below 820px (`scheduler.css`), so a phone cannot
  clear draft marks at all. Probably by design, but it is undocumented. Flag it for the host to judge as a missing door.
- **O5: the section-order offer outlives the Undo.** After a section drag and its Undo, "Use this section order as the
  default for every day? Set as default / Not now" stays on screen, offering to make the order just undone the default.
  Pressing "Set as default" would promote the restored order. On a phone the Undo/Redo bubble sits on top of the offer's
  buttons. Minor. Pictures: d/struct-20, p/struct-20.

## The two things the walk found that no unit test can see

**F1 — D1359's joined bar is not painted (scenarios 33, 34, 35, A4; also every multi-day leave and course on the war).** The app marks the days
of one entry as joined (`jr` on the first, `jl jr` in the middle, `jl` on the last — read on every day), but on screen every day is its own rounded
box with its own code, approved or pending, a leave, a course or an Attach, on a phone and on a desktop (`s33base-1-three-day-bar`,
`s33b-1-approved-three-day`, `s33-3-across-the-edge`, `a4b-1-attach-bar`). Cause, read from the page: the join rules (`:is(.mx td) .c.jr
{right:-1px; squared corners}`, `.c.jl {left:0; color:transparent}`) lose to rules that start with `#page-leavewar` (`… .c.whole {left:2px;
right:2px}`, `… .c.s-w {color: rgb(var(--h))}`, `… .mx td .c {border-radius:4px}`): computed on a middle day, `right 2px, left 2px,
border-radius 4px, colour = the leave's own`. A jsdom unit test cannot see a cascade. **The same cause** makes the D1370 outlined box (OTH, ATCH,
TRG…) draw with the generic approved edge: computed fill `rgba(200,212,228,.05)` and edge `inset 1px rgba(200,212,228,.85)` where D1370 says
`rgba(232,237,242,.05)` and `1.5px rgba(200,212,228,.78)` (the rule `.c.k-unav.s-a …` loses to `#page-leavewar .c.s-a`). Words colour is right
(#E8EDF2). The difference is small to the eye; the box reads outlined.

**F2 — logic under F1 (scenario 34a):** two SEPARATE next-door filings with no remark (Mon 2 and Tue 3 Nov, each its own bid) are marked `jr` / `jl`,
i.e. treated as one entry. On screen they look separate only because nothing joins; once F1 is fixed they would join, against the brief's
"two boxes, not joined" (D1346: entries never join by themselves). Mon–Fri with a second entry on Wednesday breaks correctly (Mon–Tue `jr`/`jl`,
Wednesday's two entries no marks, Thu–Fri `jr`/`jl`), and a half-day run joins top to top (`top jr`, `top jl jr`, `top jl`).

## 5. Each finding in full

### AB6 — an overnight leave's tail ambers the next day, whose list does not show it (CONFIRMED)
- **What happens.** A leave that runs past midnight clashes, on the NEXT day, with published work that starts before
  it ends. The day turns amber and the list opens with the clash sentence — admin: *"Two of these can't both stand on
  the same time — an admin needs to change one."*; the member himself: *"Two things on this day cover the same time.
  An admin has to decide between them — nothing has been thrown out."* — and then lists ONE thing, the OIL credit.
  The leave that is the other half of the clash is not on that day's list at all (it is on Friday's).
- **Steps, fresh world.** (1) Inputs page: Ranger, LL, Fri 17 Jul, custom 20:00–06:00 (stored as 20:00 → 06:00).
  (2) Edit Schedule → Saturday's board: the SDO desk's start → 05:00; add Ranger to its extras line (the board's own
  toast: *"Ranger — On leave but tasked — SDO duty"*). (3) Sign the four, Publish day. (4) Leave War → July → tap
  Ranger's Saturday. Script: `w4-06-saturday.mjs` (`AB6-list-shows-what-clashes`, `AB6-phone-list`,
  `AB6-member-reads-the-tail`).
- **Rule.** Fable S6 / F6; R6 — the list is where every record behind a mark is read (B1, B9); N10 — the member is
  told what a clash means for HIS leave, which needs the leave named.
- **Who it hurts.** The admin is told to change one of two things and shown one; the member is told his day has two
  things covering the same time and cannot see his own leave in it. Money is right (the credit lands, the leave
  charges Friday's afternoon only) — this is a reading problem, not a figure one.

### W4-1 — on the phone a finger cannot select ONE day (NEW)
- **What happens.** Held on one day and lifted (300, 600 or 900 ms; still, or wobbling 2–14 px inside the 19 px day),
  the day lights while held — the selection has armed — and the selection sheet mounts on the lift. About 20 ms later
  the finger's own tap arrives, lands on the day under the sheet, and the sheet is gone. Nothing is left open. The
  code's guard against that tap (the "swallow the next click" in the drag-select, removed again after 0 ms) is gone
  before a touch's tap arrives; a mouse's click comes at once, so the desktop's one-day drag works, and a finger that
  drags over two or more days makes no tap at all, so that works too.
- **Steps, fresh world.** Phone (390, touch), Leave War, August; hold a finger on any empty day for ~0.3 s, lift.
  Script: `w4-08-hold-why.mjs` (logs the sheet mounting at the lift and gone 20 ms after the tap; the two-day drag as
  the control) and `w4-07-hold-probe.mjs` (six hold lengths / wobbles, all the same).
- **Rule.** G1 — the drag-select on the phone (owner, 27 Aug 26: "I can't select a range … stuck with 1 input"; the
  long-press "take" he asked to see); R8.
- **Who it hurts.** Anyone on a phone adding to a day that already carries a mark: the tap then opens the LIST, which
  has no "place a bid" door, so the drag-select is the only way in — and by finger it only works across two days or
  more, which writes the neighbouring day too. G2 on the phone had to put its afternoon bid in over 6–7 Aug for that
  reason. Caveat: Chromium's touch emulation; a real phone's long-press may behave differently past ~0.5 s, but a
  short hold (0.2–0.4 s) is a tap on both iPhone and Android.

## 2. Findings

**F1 — the next-week peek shows the working copy of a published day (P2-17).**
Steps: Edit Schedule, load week Jul 20, add a wave PB (BFM, 12:00) on Monday, publish it (Original); then change its Mission to ACM and its Rally, do not amend ("2 pending"). Go back to week Jul 13 and look at the peek column for Monday 20 Jul, as the admin on Edit Schedule and as the member on View-only Sched (`dk-04-p17-d-peek`, `dk-03-p17-c2-peek-member`). Expected (P2-17): issued readers keep the issued wording until the amendment. Happened: the peek reads "PB ACM" - the unamended change - while View-only on Jul 20 reads BFM, print and CSV read BFM, and the Original reads BFM. After AL1 they all read ACM. (The peek's own header says it shows next week's "planned programme", so this may be intended; the scenario and D45 say otherwise - the host decides.)

**F2 — an unrecognised clock spelling is ignored without a word (P2-13).** A line "8h00 VL IN TIME" or "8.00 VL IN TIME" is kept as typed, resolves to no clock and raises no message, while "2400" and "25:99" do warn. The crew simply start at the default. Cosmetic/edge; not a scenario failure.

**Observations (not defects):** P2-15 - Common Programme rows have no CX (only the delete cross). P2-09.d - pressing "+ In-time / Rally" a second time on a wave that already holds a line adds the same text and the same 09:00 again (for the host's P2-08). H-05 - the line text itself carries no day word; the heading right above it does ("22:30 (prev day)"). H-01 - the unchanged evening clock now gives 21h-25h work spans for the crew, flagged only as grey long-day notes.

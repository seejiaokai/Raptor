# W1 — the Calendar window on a phone, by touch (13 Oct 26)

Walker W1, Sonnet 5.5. Build served at `http://localhost:4187/`, admin `ad`, touch phone (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, 2x), real finger events (touch start, six moves, rest 90 ms, lift). Fresh browser context per script. The clock was left running (today in the app: Tue 13 Oct 2026). All pictures: `raptor-port/docs/img/handpass/2026-10-13-one-date-calendar/w1/`. Scripts and logs: `raptor-port/scripts/handpass/odc/w1/` (`a.mjs` scenarios 1-10, `b1.mjs` 11 and 15, `b2.mjs` 12 and 16, `c.mjs` 13 and 14, `d.mjs` a second look at 14, `lib.mjs` shared).

Counts, by scenario (16 in all; 13 counts once for its three sizes): **PASS 13 (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16) · FAIL 0 · RECORD 3 (6, 14, 15) · NOT WALKED 0**; plus one extra RECORD of my own (8b). Scenarios 3, 10, 11 and 12 carry a RECORD part inside their PASS.

Two choices of mine, so the table reads true:
- Scenarios 11 and 12 both save a "Every Thursday" rule that makes NF. To read the month cleanly, **11 (and 15) ran in one context and 12 (and 16) in a fresh one**, so the two rules do not overlap on the month. Both reached the same Every Thursday window by the same taps.
- On this build the Calendar window is a floating window that starts tall (bottom at y=838 at 390x844) and **shrinks to fit its content after a slide that starts on its top bar** (see 6). So I ran 8 and 9 (which need empty room under the content) BEFORE 6, and ran 8 again after (8b).

Leads from the read (each has a scenario below):
- L-A. The decided ring is drawn with `outline`, the same property the keyboard ring uses. In Approvals `.appr-ok:focus-visible` is the identical 2px cyan / 2px offset, so the keyboard on a lit button changes nothing on screen, and the keyboard on the greyed one gives it the very ring that means "decided". In the bid windows `.dchip` has no focus rule of its own, so `.dec-ring` overrides the browser's ring on the lit one (focus invisible) and the greyed one gets the browser's own ring, not the app's cyan (D842, D1771).
- L-B. `.bidsheet .daylist` is `overflow: hidden` with a rounded edge (only `.daylist.flat` is visible). A ring standing 4px outside the button may be cut at the list's left edge or foot. Same question in Approvals' scroller (`.appr-scroll` overflow auto, `.appr-card` overflow hidden, a 28px fade at the foot) for the first and last line.
- L-C. Approvals phone gap between the two buttons is 6px; the ring takes 4px of it. Window grid gap is 6px too.
- L-D. A stale Approvals line ("Already approved by Saber", "No longer there") is `disabled`; `.appr-ok:disabled:not(.lit)` fades only the unlit one. What does a stale line wear now — ring, grey, both?
- L-E. Approve held by a medical or leave (D1544) is `disabled` at 45%; on a refused bid it is also `dec-off`. "Stays pressable" does not hold there by design, but it must read as disabled, not as the ordinary grey.
- L-F. A whole leave of several days opened whole and a picked range pass `span`, so nothing is lit even when every day is approved. That was so before; the walk should picture it beside D1330 (lesson 23).
- L-G. The phone rule `.bidsheet .dchip.dec { font-size: 11.5px; align-self: stretch }` — the day's list row is a wrapping flex (`.dl-acts`), the window row a 4-column grid. The two may not come out the same height or wrap the same.
- L-H. "Approve" → "Approved" changes the word's length. Approvals holds the longer word's width; the window is a fixed grid; the day's list (non-grid `.dl-acts`) is content-sized, so Refuse, Move and Delete may shift under a repeated tap.

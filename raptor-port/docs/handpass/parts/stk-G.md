## Findings
1. **L-01.** After publishing, any of the three Logic changes flips the published Saturday between FO and HO at once, with "No pending changes" and the sign-offs standing; value put back, it flips back. Expected (D48, D142): it keeps what it went out with.
2. **L-02.** Typing new words for the button alone raises "RULES MODIFIED" on both banners and the Logic "1 rule changed ..." strip. Expected: no stamp.
3. **L-03.** With `08:00H: IN TIME + WX/NOTAMS` and `08:30H: VL RALLY AFTER IN TIME` both crews' reporting does not start 08:00: VL's work hours are 30 minutes shorter than RU's, in either order.
4. **L-04 side finding.** A person on a flying line with no times shows "NaN min" in Insights' Work hours, with or without any reporting line.
5. **L-06.** The changes window lists a Blue/Red answer under "Leave War - <a code like rmuv8bvw885wj10>", not under the formation. A template-copied answer is listed the same way; after Undo of the template its lines are still listed.
6. **L-07 (a) and (c).** An answer on a day never saved is lost on reload and on week change and back; it holds once the day has been saved (b).
7. **L-08.** With another question open, clicking into a second line's Remarks gives no role button.

## Anything else noticed
- **Driver trap (affects older walks):** in CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`, a `touchMove` that leaves a point out does NOT lift it; a `touchEnd` that names a point lifts just that finger (`touchEnd` with an empty list lifts all). Checked with a small probe (pointerup events logged). The brief's "a later touchMove/touchEnd whose list leaves a point out releases it" is half right: only touchEnd does. `trk-fps-walk.mjs` W4 ("the second finger lifts by leaving the move") therefore did not lift a finger at all, so its D134 check was weaker than it reads. Worth a line in `docs/walk-scripts.md` section 4.
- My first run of P7 failed for exactly that reason (my script's own fault; the chart scrolled by the pinch's settle at the final lift). Fixed by lifting finger 1 with a named touchEnd; the rerun passed. R1 first failed because I read the dialog's whole text (it includes the OK button); fixed to read the message element only.
- The pinch cap: after P3 the zoom sat at the 300% cap, so P4/P5 would have started with a pinch that could not move the zoom. I added a real tap on "reset" first (versions 4 and 5) so the pinch before the switch has room to move the zoom (P4 started at 0.4, P5 at 0.633). P6 and D3 likewise reset first so "about double" is not hidden by the cap.
- P4/P5 menu: the "✎" menu panel on a phone covers the upper-left of the chart; the pinch spot is chosen clear of it. The menu stayed open while two fingers were down on the chart (touch does not send the mouse-down that closes it), which is what made a button press under the pinch reachable at all.
- The phone Tracker undo/redo sit in the app's top bar (disabled in R1 both before and after).
- Not walked and not in scope: a real lost lift on a device (Safari), the phone's own glide.

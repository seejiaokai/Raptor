## What the walk found, and each disposition

- **A. The pinned dates row still blinks for one painted frame** — Leave War desktop wheel (was two frames before the
  change, now one), a phone flick in Chromium (one), Quals on a phone flick (one). The walker judged the desktop PASS; the
  host's look at the painted frames overturns it. **Cause:** the browser moves the page on its own and paints that frame
  before the page's code hears of the scroll; the pinned row is drawn by the page's code, so it can be no earlier than the
  next frame. The fix took away the extra frame React added; it cannot reach the browser's own frame. **Confirmed,
  pre-existing in kind, halved by this change.** Removing the last frame means letting the browser keep the row pinned
  itself (a pinned copy always there, laid under what scrolls above it, or the browser's own pinning) — a design change
  to the grid's layering with its own risk, not this small job (lesson 7: the design question before a model). **Put to
  him** with the pictures; filed `[LW-PIN-LAST-FRAME]`. Scrolling is not slower.
- **B. The date heading is not ringed while its day window is open** — the host's own expected line in the brief, with
  no ruling behind it; `main` draws no ring there either (the grid does not read which day is open). **False positive**
  (the brief's line, not the app).
- **C. On a phone a tap on a grid cell behind the open day window did not open the bid window** — the cell lay under the
  window (`ph-chromium-s13-a-cell-pressed-with-day-open.png`): the tap landed on the window. On the desktop the grid
  behind it took the tap. Unchanged by this job. **Not a finding.**
- The drawing check's early differences (the foot 16px low, its words 4px off) were the walker's own model of the
  drawing; measured again against the drawing, no difference over 2px. **Not a finding.**

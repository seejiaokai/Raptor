**The agent's readings — say if any is wrong:**
- The admin's role switch is gone with "View as" (your "no preview as a member", D166).
- One person, one account; an account stays when its callsign is archived (posted out).
- An admin cannot change his own account — so the app always has someone else to put him right, and nobody can lock
  every admin out.
- The guest sees the published programme only — no warning list, no ⓘ day panel, no medical detail.
- Until the Microsoft sign-in, an account you add signs in with any password, and anyone at a shared computer could type
  another person's sign-in name — so "who changed it" can be claimed. That ends at the database step.

  that browser at his next sign-in; a tab he already has open keeps what it had, and another person's phone never
  hears of it. At the database step it takes effect at once, everywhere (it is in the IT brief).

- A guest sees the published day's ordinary inputs as members do — a meeting, an appointment and its remark; only a

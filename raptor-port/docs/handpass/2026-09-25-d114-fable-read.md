### Older (not caused by this commit) — the two doors §9.4 does not list

**O1 — The mirror's round trip reads 1, not 0 (S14).** Steps: publish Monday. As a member, file a Meeting on Monday (it auto-lands as pending, 16 Sep 26 rule) — or as the scheduler press Accept. Day head reads "1 pending" (paired, right). Now ✕ that row. The row is gone and Monday's content is byte-identical to the issued Original, but the day still reads **"1 pending"** with the line *"Bane · Meeting · not on the programme → taken off"*, and stays so until an AL is published carrying "1 item · 1 input filing".
Why: `unacceptInput` always parks the request `r` (owner, 26 Aug 26 — dormant, flags nothing), and the filing fingerprint (`publish.ts` P2-R3-01, 15 Sep 26) deliberately treats `''` and `r` as different states because the issued face still flags a fresh request while the working copy silences a dormant one. So it is a real difference in what the issued day says, by design — not a pairing miscount, and identical on `main`.
Why it belongs on the sheet: D98's own words are *"if the change results in going back to the same as the published schedule … it shouldn't show as pending"*, and the §9.4 row "✕ then Accept again → 0" walks only the *other* order. The owner will meet this one the first time a member files something on a published day and he takes it off. **This is his call, not a fix I would prescribe** (it collides with his 26 Aug dormancy rule). Recommendation: walk it, record the reading in §9.4, and put it to him as one line — or, if he wants 0, the change is in `unacceptInput`: when the request has no landed row in the *current issued* snapshot (`daySnapOf(di, dayCurVer(di)).fil[id]` is `''`/absent) park it `''` instead of `r` on that day — with the cost that the removed request flags again. Test that would pin whichever he picks: `amendbatch.test.tsx` — publish, `acceptInput`, `unacceptInput`, assert `dayShownPendCount(MON)` is 0 (or 1 with the line's words).

**O2 — Orphan row in another week after an Inputs-page delete (S15).** Steps: on week 2 accept Bane's Meeting onto Monday and publish it; go to week 1; on the Inputs page delete that request; go back to week 2. The row is still on Monday's programme (nothing removed it), and the day reads **"1 pending · A request · on the programme → not on the programme"** while the schedule shows no change; the ⓘ, board and Amendments panel agree with it.
Why: `dropInputRow` → `unacceptInput(acceptedDay(r), r)`; `acceptedDay` and `unacceptInput` search only the *loaded* `DAYS`, so the row in the stashed week is never spliced, and the filing entry (`g→''`, the id gone from `INPUTS`) has no `delete` to pair with. Pre-existing on `main` (the filing counted 1 there too). Rare and cross-week; rank low. Fix, if wanted: in `unacceptInput`, when no loaded day holds the row, also sweep the stash (`weekstash` snapshots' `ground` by `src`, through the stash's own write path so the stashed week re-persists), and only then park `r`. Test: `loadweek.test.ts` — accept on week B, `loadWeek(A)`, `dropInputRow`, `loadWeek(B)`, assert no ground row carries that `src` and `dayShownPendCount` is 0.

**O3 — Weekend ✕ is two lines (S13).** By D109/F5 the OIL block is one item of its own; a request row that earns, taken off a published Saturday, reads "2 pending". Consistent with every other removal on an earning day; not D114's. No action — noted so nobody files it as a D114 miss.

## 3. Explicit negatives — what I checked and found right

- **The pair test itself.** Delete side reads the *issued* day's row at the delete entry's own index (`enumRows(prevD)` mints `gr:di.i.prog` from the issued index; `grow(u, snap.d)` reads the same array) and requires the filing to *leave* `g`; add side reads the *live* day at the add's index and requires the filing to *arrive* at `g`. No wrong-row pairing is possible: `src` is unique across loaded days (`acceptInput`'s guard), and `!u.inp` stops a second filing taking a row already paired.
- **Id forms agree.** `filingDelta` writes `inp:${di}.${iid}` raw; `dayPendingItemsIn` splits it raw and compares to raw `row.src`; `pendlist` decodes (`newId` is `[0-9a-z]` only, so a no-op). The URI-escaped form is the *pending-key* (`markInputFiling`), a different thing, not consulted here.
- **The invariant** "items empty ⇔ `dayDelta` empty" survives the pairing (S16).
- **Sign-off binding (D103)** is untouched — it reads `filingKey` + the digest, not the count.
- **The stored diff** is unchanged — `alIssue` still stores `dayDelta(di)` whole; only `units`/`ukinds` are added, both optional in `schema.ts` and `schema.test.ts` (`$opt`).
- **Persistence and undo.** `SCHED.als` rides `schedFields` whole (`ukinds` with it); the command layer restores records whole; the history snapshot carries `INPUTS`, so the top-bar undo of ✕/Accept restores the filing with the row.
- **The Original as baseline** carries `fil` (`SCHED.orig[di] = {…daySnap(di)}`), so the pairing works against the Original and against any AL alike.
- **Every counting surface** (S17) reads the one body; the only raw-mark read left is dead under a preview.
- **Doors.** Both ✕ doors (board row `grdel`, Personal-Inputs `data-acc="x"` on week and board) and the Inputs-page delete all go through `unacceptInput`; the edit week has no separate ground-row delete. Plan switch, load and template apply all run `reconcileDayFiling` before any snapshot can freeze a dangling `g`; a template is refused on a published day (D96).
- **Words.** Paired delete: "*Who* · *what* · on the programme → taken off / under Unavailable / not on the programme"; paired add: "… not on the programme (or taken off / under Unavailable) → on the programme", tappable to the row. Unpaired filing keeps its old words. `RANK` puts a paired removal with the ground section (5), not with filings (8) — sensible.
- **Amendments panel.** Waiting line "Mon · 1 change · 1 removal" for ✕, "Mon · 1 change" for Accept; issued line from `ukinds`; `c.inp` now counts only unpaired filings, so "input filing" never appears beside a paired removal.
- **Docs.** `engine-rules.md` (§the pending count's unit), `ui-contracts.md` (§The counting unit) and the register AM23 carry D114 and `ukinds`; `schema.ts` is the declared home of the stored field and was updated with its test.

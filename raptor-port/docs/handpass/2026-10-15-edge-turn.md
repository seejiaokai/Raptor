## 2. What was decided (D898 — "U know how it work … u can build it") — told to him in the morning
- **Which edge:** the month's SIDES — the right side turns to the next month, the left to the last. The month already turns
  sideways (its ‹ › arrows, a finger's swipe, its slide), so the edge that turns it is the one it moves along; the top and
  the foot are where the page itself scrolls on a phone.
- **The band:** a mouse's 36 px from the side (the app's one mouse edge number); a finger's 24 px — the outer HALF of the
  48 px finger band, because a phone's date is about 50 px wide and the whole band would be most of a Sunday: a run ended
  on a Sunday and held a moment would have turned the month under it. Past the side counts, while level with the month.
- **The hold:** 600 ms in the band before the first turn; held on, again every second. A run carried THROUGH the band is
  not a wish to turn. The turn itself is the sign the hold has taken (no new mark — the guide §5a, D850).
- **After a turn:** the run keeps its first date; once the new month has slid in, the date under the still pointer is
  read again, so what is lit is what a release would pick. Let go, nothing turns on.
- **Also the bar** (an input or a planning note moved by days): the same side, the same hold — the guide's "carried to the
  edge, the view follows" (D895). Dropped in the new month it moves there; one undo puts it back and turns the month back
  to it (D672).
- **Not the SANS calendar** (it shares the pick machine): the ruling names the Inputs month; named to him, not changed (D852).
- **The Tracker (D899):** Edit chart layout's canvas pans (it neither scrolls nor turns): a ball, a selected group, an
  arrow's orange end or its bend square, a drawn line's end or its corner square carried to the canvas's edge pans it
  that way at the app's one edge feel, and the thing stays under the finger. Not the box drawn with ▣ Select, nor a pan of
  empty space — named to him.

## 7. What the walk found, and each disposition
1. **The canvas's foot could not be reached on a desktop** (T2 failed): Edit chart layout's box runs on below the window,
   so its bottom band was off the screen. **Fixed** — the pan's edges are the canvas's as seen, cut to the screen
   (`edgescroll.ts edgePan seen`); **red first**: `edgeturn.test.ts` "a canvas taller than the window…" failed on the
   old code, passes now.
2. **An arrow's bend square stayed behind while the canvas panned** (T6): the square was drawn again only on the release
   (older behaviour; with the pan it slid off with the chart and looked dropped). **Fixed** — it rides the finger
   (`core.js`, the `.linehandle` drag); walked T6 both sizes.
3. **Safari (found while writing this sheet, §7.9):** the turn draws away the date or bar under a held finger; Safari sends
   that finger's later events to the element it landed on even after it has left the page, and from there they reach
   neither the month nor the window — the drag would go dead and the turn turn on. **Fixed** — the landed-on element hears
   the gesture too (`caldays.ts heldOn`, `once`); **red first**: `edgeturn.test.ts` "Safari: …" and `caldrag.test.tsx`
   "Safari: …" failed on the old code. Only his iPhone proves it on a real device — on his look card.
4. **The code reads' six (F1–F4, R1, R2)** — §11, each fixed.
Not faults (the walk script's own, fixed in the script): a hold past the repeat turned twice; the input window's title
read from the wrong element; October has no inputs to carry (the walk steps back to July); a "SANS" highlight chip taken
for the SANS tab; after a pan no ball left on screen (⤢ Fit, or on a phone leaving and re-opening the editor — Fit there
shrinks the chart until the handles are a pixel wide); the test browser's dropped tap after a drag (D158).

## What the walk found

**A defect already on `main`:** a first boot saved the Leave War's demo world BEFORE it was dressed onto the roster, so
after the first reload the demo bids sat on the seed's invented callsigns (ramp, splice …), on nobody's row. It is the
symptom `bug-check-order.md` §7.7 told walkers to steer round ("a world nobody has written to yet comes back WITHOUT its
Leave War OIL story"). Fixed red-first (`leavewar/bootseal.test.ts`; the identical probe on `main` showed the undressed
names — step 2 above would fail there). That §7.7 caution can be relaxed once this merges.

## Not walked, and why

- Deleting every input through the screens, then reloading — proven in the unit suite on the real boot path
  (`state/bootinit.test.ts`); nothing in phase 0 changed the delete flow.
- The fold — no converter ships in phase 0, so it can never run in the app; proven in `storage/fold.test.ts`
  (incomplete manifest writes nothing; one atomic group; crash after the journal).
- A Leave War holding no period at all — cannot render today; phase 5's test.

## 3. The finding

**N1 — a squadron picked while the Leave War's Approvals is open leaves the old squadron's requests on screen.**

- **Who, and what was pressed:** Saber (master admin). Leave War → Approvals on 142 (his first visit to the war this
  sign-in); View-only Sched; Schedules menu → 149; Leave War → Approvals (149's requests); then, with Approvals still
  open, Schedules menu → 142.
- **What happened:** the top bar reads 142, but the page keeps 149's Approvals: Osprey's request (a 149 man) with its
  "New 1" and "Approve all 1"; the grid's rows behind it are still 149's ten. Waiting changes nothing (read again after
  4 s). Pressing **Grid** shows 142's war at once; leaving for another page and coming back shows 142's Approvals. The
  same the other way: a pick of 149 with 142's Approvals open kept 142's twelve lines under "149". An **Approve** pressed
  on the stale Osprey line does nothing and says nothing — no toast; the line's leave figure changes from −1 to 0 and its
  "Lowest avail" goes blank, and the header gains 142's "+2 pending in JAN - DEC 27"; back on 149 the request is still
  waiting (nothing wrong was saved).
- **What should happen:** a master admin's every page follows the pick (D2197): the moment 142 is picked, Approvals shows
  142's requests — or, if the page is meant to hold, it says so as a window held at a pick does (D2323). As it is, the
  screen shows one squadron's requests under another's number, and a press on them is silent.
- **When it happens:** only after the war was opened on the first squadron earlier in the same sign-in. A clean sign-in
  that picks 149 first, opens Approvals and picks 142 switches at once (probe `r3c`, both with and without a request in
  149). On the Grid tab a pick switches at once on the same route (probe `r3d`, `PICK_ON=grid`). No Leave War window was
  open at any pick (none mounted), and no band showed.
- **Reproduced:** in seven runs — the R2 script three times (desktop twice, phone once), the probe `r3d` with and without a
  149 request, on Approvals, and with the Approve press (desktop).
- **Is it the night's fix?** Not proved either way. The R2 fix changed only which squadron's "seen" moment Approvals
  writes (it now reads the war loaded as it draws). The page not redrawing after the war is swapped under it looks older
  than that, from the step that gave each squadron its own war; a build of the commit before the fix was not walked.
- **Severity:** medium — what an admin decides on, wrong squadron under the number he picked; the press that fails is
  silent. Nothing wrong was saved in the walk.
- **Pictures:** `n1-desktop-pick-142-on-approvals-shows-149.png`, `n1-phone-pick-142-on-approvals-shows-149.png`,
  `n1-desktop-approve-on-stale-line-silent.png`, `n1-desktop-149-request-still-waiting.png`.
- **Disposition:** OPEN — to the host. Not fixed here.

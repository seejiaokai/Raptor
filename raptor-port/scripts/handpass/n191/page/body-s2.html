The open questions

Q1. Home squadron first: in every list of squadrons, or on Admin → Users only?

Everywhere: your home squadron first, then the rest by number, in every list.

When this happens: every time an admin of more than one squadron opens a list of squadrons. It changes nothing for anyone whose home is the lowest number (Saber, Hunter: 142). It matters for Mako, home 150, who plans 149: many times a week.

Today every list of squadrons goes by number: the squadron menu at the top, the menu under ☰, the bell's Show buttons. You ruled "home squadron first, then by number, wherever squadrons are listed"; the plan had built it on Admin → Users alone.

Recommended: everywhere. Mako sees 150 first in the squadron menu, the ☰ menu, the bell's Show buttons, the Display page's list, and the folded squadrons at the foot of Admin → Users ("▸ 150" before "▸ 142" on 149's page). One order everywhere, as your words say. Its cost: those lists reorder for anyone whose home is not 142.

The other choice: Admin → Users only; every other list stays by number.

Not drawn: the lists look the same, only their order changes.

When this happens: an admin on a smaller iPhone (a mini or an SE, narrower than yours) opens Admin → Users while ten or more of his squadron are posting out at once, say a detachment rotating. A few times a year.

Q8. A Plan holder making a man an admin of the squadron he plans

No longer: only 149's own admins or a master admin make a man an admin of 149.

When this happens: a few times a year, at a desk. Hunter (142's admin, Plan of 149) posts Viper, a 142 man, to 149. From that day Hunter, through his Plan, makes Viper an admin of 149. Viper, now 149's own admin, gives Plan of 149 to Basher, another 142 admin. Nobody who was 149's own admin was asked.

Today this cannot happen, because only a master admin gives Plan. Your ruling that a squadron's own admins give Plan opens this two-step road, while a Plan holder may still make 149's men admins of 149.

Recommended: whoever may give Plan of 149 is who may make a man an admin of 149: its own admins or a master admin. One rule for both, so the road is closed. Its cost: a squadron whose only admin is a Plan holder needs a master admin to make an admin of its own.

Other choices: keep it, and tell 149's own admins and master admins on the bell when a Plan holder makes a man an admin of 149 (new words, drawn first); or leave it as it would be built.

The other choice: as today, hidden on the row but still found by it.

Q10. Which admins can give Plan. Osprey, 149's admin, has had his sign-in switched off, but the app is still open on his PC. He presses Plan on Hunter's row. Rare. I would build: "149's own admins" means admins who can still sign in. One switched off, archived or posted out gives no Plan, even with the app still open; he sees Plan greyed. Q11. Undo of a Plan change by one who has lost the right. Kestrel ends Hunter's Plan of 149, then is posted out of 149. Back at his desk he presses Undo. Rare. I would build: that step is blocked for good, in Undo's words today, naming nobody; nothing half done, no bell line saying Plan came back. The other way: a new message saying why.

Only 149's own admins or a master admin can link it to another person." Today's sentence says only a master admin can; it changes to match who now gives Plan. Shown where today's refusal shows.

I would build: "Cinch’s row changed while it was open, so nothing was saved. Open it again." (pictured under group 6). It says the Save did not happen.

Long text wraps instead of covering the timings. A programme item whose name ran on without a space — a mashed-together callsign, a pasted string, a hand resting on the keyboard — had nowhere to break, so it did not wrap: it ran straight out of the NAME column and painted over the START time beside it. On a phone the worst case reached 252px into the next column, hiding the time completely, and widened the whole day into a sideways swipe it should never have had. Every free-text cell in a day now breaks mid-word when it has to and the row simply grows taller: programme names and their sub-lines, the EP and ORDERS notes, duty and ground and sim labels, remarks, personal-input rows, the sim planning notes, and the same fields on the scheduler board. Two things had to change together — the text needs permission to break, and the cell needs permission to shrink to its column; either one alone leaves the overflow. Crew pucks are deliberately left as they were: a name on a puck still stays on one line and trims with an ellipsis, because a puck is a fixed 74px tile and wrapping one would break every row it sits in. Checked by measuring the ink itself rather than the cell, at 390, 430, 820 and 1500 wide, on the view page, the edit page and the board — 36 measurements, and each one fails on the previous build. Regression suite 728.

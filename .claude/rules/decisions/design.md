# Screen design — how it works now

<!-- shape: page -->

Loads by itself with any file of screen work (the `paths:` above). Each line is a rule as it stands, its rulings after it; his words, the agent's readings told to him (never stated here as his rule) and the history are in the full rows, `.claude/decisions-full/design.md` — open the row before acting on a rule's detail or asking him about it. Moved here 1 Nov 26 (D1554, D1735). Other areas' rulings are pointed to at the end, each stated on its own file; the map: `DECISIONS.md`.

## The top bar — Undo and Redo, the bell, the page names
- Every Undo / Redo pair sits in the app's top bar, in the same place, order and look as Edit Schedule's, on a desktop and a phone, on every page where a change the one Undo takes back is made; the Tracker's own pair sits there too, taking back only the Tracker's changes; on a phone the group runs in the desktop's order — Undo · Redo · (the changes clock, Edit Schedule only) · the sync dot · the bell at the far right; the Scheduler Board keeps a bar of its own (below) [D347, D348, D349, D854, D481].
- Up to 1599 wide every page's top bar takes its compact look — Undo and Redo as arrows only, names on hover, the sync pill its dot alone — so Edit Schedule's bar, with the changes clock, stays one line at his PC's 1536 [D1126, D347, D348].
- Undo and Redo, everywhere, leave the screen where it is when what they change is in view, moving to the change only when it is out of view [D672].
- The top bar stays pressable over "Jump to a date", the day-details window and the document viewer, as over the Traffic window: the grey starts at the bar's foot, Undo and Redo work with the window open, any other bar button closes it first; an input's own window and the question boxes keep the bar covered [D1645].
- Help's and Admin's titles take the size and weight of Logic's page name and keep their place; the pages without a title stay without one [D1138].
- The board covers the top bar, so its own bar carries the same group laid out as Edit Schedule's — Undo · Redo · History · Sync · the bell — then one way out, ✓ Done, no ✕ Close; Done raises no "Schedule updated" note, nothing in its place; on a desktop Sort all stays a button, before Undo, and Insights stands beside the bell; on a phone Sort all, the Phone / Desktop layout switch and Insights sit behind one ⋯ button in the bar's second row, beside the highlighter [D349, D854, D481].
- The page names, Edit Schedule to Admin, stand on the top bar's first row beside TALON wherever they all fit, the bar's buttons after them or on a row of their own to the right; where they do not all fit they go into the side menu, unchanged, and the bar is one row, as the mock-up of 4 Nov 26 draws them [D1641, D1642, D1643].
- On a phone held sideways the names always go into the side menu and the bar is one row, for everyone, even where a member's fewer names would fit [D1643, D1642].

## Icons and marks — one drawn icon, one thing
- One thing has one icon throughout the app — one pencil, one gear, one calendar — each drawn by the app in its button's colour so every device shows it alike, never the emoji his iPhone would draw for ✏, ⚙ or 📅 [D1134, D1509, D1707, D1508].
- Every pencil is the solid pencil of his picture (filled, eraser at the top left, tip at the bottom right), the line pencil gone too; only a ✎ inside help text, or after an edited chart's name in the Tracker's Syllabus list, stays typed [D1134].
- The ✕ that hides a warning, the ⇄ of Move and a ✎ inside help text stay typed (the Tracker's ✕ marks are drawn), and any other icon drawn two ways is put to him with pictures before it changes (D1124) [D1703, D1793, D1792, D1134, D1124, D1508].
- The one settings gear everywhere is the Leave War's drawn line gear, in its button's colour — white on the Inputs, SANS, Tracker, OIL tracker and board rows, grey on the Leave War; the OIL tracker's 📅 becomes Edit Schedule's line calendar (D1508) [D1509, D1707, D1508].
- The board's and the Leave War's typed marks are drawn line icons: every ⇅ (Sort all, Auto sort, Rearrange the grid, Done) takes the Tracker's Reorder mark, Templates a page of blocks, Reset order an arrow turning back, the phone menu's layout switch a phone and a screen [D1703, D1793].
- OIL has one mark of its own, a ticket — a day off held — on the board's OIL Earn and the Leave War's OIL tab, in place of ◧ and the plain clock, so a clock means only History; on a phone the board's day bar shows the desktop's marks before Templates and OIL Earn — the page of blocks and the ticket — on one row, the buttons keeping their height [D1704, D1703, D1793].
- Four more typed marks are drawn line marks, built as drawn: the 👁 on a look at a published version the wave templates window's line eye; the Inputs calendar's ⤢ and ⤡ two new marks, arrows out and arrows in; the Tracker's ↺ Restore and a hidden warning's ↺ the Reset arrow; the ✕ that hides a warning stays typed [D1792].
- The board's OIL Earn wears no clock while History's clock stands on its bar [D1700, D1704, D1792, D1793].
- Every search box looks like the Edit Schedule week's — a grey line magnifier inside the box at its left, the box as tall as the buttons beside it — each keeping its own words since each finds something different [D1127].

## Controls, arrows, rows and words
- Arrows on either side of words that change — a day, a month, a count — keep their place: the words get the room their longest value needs, so the arrow pressed again and again never moves; Edit Schedule's crew panel ‹ day › first, then every such place in the app [D1122].
- A page's controls are one row (§3 of the guide): the page's own buttons in the one row under the top bar and the page's tabs, at its button height (36 where it holds only the page's own buttons), even one button; a fold is not a button; not in it: a window's or sheet's own buttons, one item's controls, a bar shown for a while; on the narrowest phone a button keeps its size and place, shorter words drawn for him first, else the layout drawn and put to him; the Inputs and SANS calendars' tabs and controls one row on a desktop (the Inputs' D1125) [D926, D1125].
- The boxes and buttons whose font line the browser had dropped show the app's letters at the size and weight each line meant, as drawn [D1680].
- On a touch screen, a phone or a tablet, the page itself never zooms: only the grids that zoom by their own pinch — the Leave War grid, its OIL tab and its Balances page, Quals, the board's desktop layout and the Tracker's chart — zoom, and a pinch anywhere else does nothing [D1567].
- The app's screens are not wordy: a label, a hint or a line of small print earns its place or goes — fewer words on screen, the same information — though a sentence a person would say may cost a word or two, by the writing guide's order: every fact first, then a sentence a person would say, then as short as those two allow (the scheduler's D836) [D726, D836].

- On the board its small menus, its day and week chips and Edit Schedule's day name are reached and pressed by the keyboard: a menu opened by Enter or the down arrow puts the keys on its first item, the arrows moving through it [D1646].
- The keyboard's ring is the app's cyan focus ring; under the keys an item of the board's or the week's ⋯ menu keeps its raised row and wears the cyan ring, and the board's + Block and Templates buttons wear it in place of the browser's own — built; + Wave, the plans selector and a jet's stores wear it too, the duty role box (a text box) the boxes' cyan edge [D1646, D1771].

(its ✕ and Escape), "Open the other input and lose them?", "Delete this input for all N people?" and "Take yourself out of this input?"; the Delete key's "Delete this input for all N people?" on an input's card in the opened day; and the Leave War posting's delete [D1626, D1627, D1628].

## Saving — "Not saved" and "Blocked:"
- A failed save's warning is a band of its own along the bottom of the top bar — the page moves down one line, nothing is covered — one design across the whole app, in look 1, the strip: a drawn warning sign, "Not saved" in bold and "keep this page open" quieter, read from the left, Retry a solid amber button at the right; the line under it is the band's own amber, never the top bar's cyan edge; the passing "Saving…" note stays as it is; a refusal of one change takes a look of its own (below) [D587, D841, D1655, D1663, D1665, D1667].
- The same band sits under the bar of every full-screen surface that covers the top bar — the board, and the Leave War's full-screen OIL tracker (its grid and settings), a working surface whatever its kind; a window, a sheet or the phone's menu is a short visit and gets none; the Inputs calendar and the Medical view, under the top bar as part of the Inputs page, carry none; a window he has resized but never moved moves down below the band, as one not yet placed does, so the band and its Retry are never covered; a moved window keeps its place [D587, D841, D574, D620, D626, D1523].
- When a save has failed the band shows on the screen after asking for access, the guest page and the sign-in screen too, the page moved down under it, so a stranger whose request was not saved is never told it was sent; the pass there reads "Not sent yet" in the band's amber — its mark and its first step — its look back by itself once the save lands [D1657, D1664].
- In a browser that refuses to store anything, every page shows a "Not kept" band under the top bar — the drawn warning sign, "Not kept" in bold, "this browser loses your work on a reload" quieter, no Retry, in the "Not saved" strip's look — the page moved down under it, the Tracker's corner showing nothing while it is up; "Not saved" stays the failed-save band's own words; its words fit one line at his iPhone's width; built [D1901, D1655, D1667].
- The test switch `?savefail` on the app's address refuses every save in the one tab opened with it, so the band, its Retry and the pass's "Not sent yet" can be seen on his iPhone; without it the app saves as always, and nobody else is touched [D1666].
- A refusal of one change is not a failed save: the schedule's refused change, a Leave War figure box, the Grades window and a document too big to store take one look of their own, apart from the band's: the app's plain grey note, only the "no entry" sign in red and "Blocked:" in bold white — not a red box, so red keeps meaning a change kept that clashes [D1665, D1667, D1668].
- It opens with the bold word "Blocked:", the rest after the colon in quieter words — never "Not saved", the band's alone, and never "Refused" (a leave bid or access request turned down); a document too big to store keeps its own words, in the same note [D1667, D1668, D1665].
- The schedule's refusal of a change carrying its own reason takes the grey note too — "Blocked: <reason>. Nothing was saved…" — the words after the reason as they were, made a sentence ("for anyone: take that person off the list, or change it" where the input holds several people) [D1669, D1667, D1668].

## Loading — the claw
- While the app starts it shows the blue talon logo drawn as a claw scratch — the left claw first, top to bottom, then the middle and the right, then all three whole, repeating — each claw along its own line, 0.42 s each, 0.16 s apart, held whole 0.55 s; the sign-in's light round it, 72px; still for less motion; drawn by the browser from the logo's own lines, no library, adding nothing to the load; shown on every start, no 0.3 s wait, even when the app is up a moment later, and gone the moment it is up [D1591, D1605, D1607, D1610, D1611, D1615].
- The claw also shows over a page that opens slowly, in the blank moment before it is drawn, never adding to its time: one overlay over the page area under the top bar, the bar in reach; the same claw, from the tap with no wait; only the pages measured to go blank raise it — the Leave War and the Tracker, on their first open after sign-in — so a page that opens at once never shows it; every page's background is the claw's plain dark, the faint blue wash gone everywhere, so the claw simply appears; nothing raises it early, so no time is added [D1607, D1610, D1611, D1614, D887].
- While the Leave War or the Tracker is still downloading after 8 seconds, a quiet grey line "Still loading…" shows under the claw, going with it when the page arrives [D1632].
- When the Leave War's or the Tracker's own download fails, only that page shows a card — "Couldn't open the Leave War" (or the Tracker), "It didn't download. Check your signal, then retry." and Retry — the top bar staying; the whole app never goes blank; a failed background fetch on a desktop shows nothing until the page is opened, then the card [D1631, D1633, D1638].
- Retry fetches the page's files again and, once they have come, reloads the app — everything saved kept, the sign-in asked again until the database step, the Undo list empty — then reopens the page that failed by itself once he is signed in, never through the address [D1633, D1638, D1635].
- When Retry cannot fetch the files — no signal, a file cut off, or a save still waiting — it does not reload: the claw holds at least one full round, about 1.3 s, and the card comes back, still signed in, the keyboard back on Retry in the app's cyan focus ring, so the press reads as tried [D1638, D1636].

## The design guide's standing rules (`raptor-port/docs/design-practices.md` holds the wording)
- Feedback for an action is designed into the screen itself — the result is the feedback — and a passing note is raised only for what is important and not intuitive, or complicated (§1: the result not shown on screen, a press that did more or less than expected or carries a warning, a failure or refusal); a screen reader is told the same in words, the mark steady for less motion, the keyboard back on the window's opener, never on the new thing; "Saving…" and "Not saved" are not this rule's; a built note saying only what the screen shows is put to him to delete, never deleted unasked [D850].
- One mark, one look (§4): the just-added mark: the plain blue ring, 2px, no glow, about six seconds, its last half-second a fade (steady for less motion), on the edge of the thing added — a row's controls outside it; a line, row or wave round itself; a table row one box; a new block round its heading and each row; a puck in a seat none; no other flash over it (one inside it is that thing's own); a new kind of mark is his to rule; a taken suggested brief or rally flashes the ring about a second (the scheduler's D1038); a Leave War day brought into view its cyan [D876, D854, D1038, D1449].
- No mark in this app glows (§4): a ring, stripe, box, line, panel, dot or button is drawn as it is — an own-colour shadow or a halo is a glow, a solid ring drawn as a shadow a line, a dark shadow under a window or a menu depth; the balance bar's, the move banner's, the fast sync dot's and Admin → Users' dots' bands are gone; a new on/off control takes the filter chip's "on" look unless he rules otherwise, other "on" looks staying; a new glow is his to rule; the app's own sign is not a mark — the sign-in logo keeps its light, as the claw does; the page's faint wash is gone [D887, D1614].
- Dragging (§5A): one landing line in one place — the top edge of the row under, the bottom edge only under a list's last row or above a heading it cannot land on; a drop onto a place marks that one place, once; carried near its box's edge it scrolls the box the way the thing can move, at the app's edge distance and speed, on for less motion too; a built drag that differs is named to him, never changed without his word [D895].
- Nothing pinned leaks: a bar, or any part that stays put while what is under it scrolls, shows none of that content behind it or through a gap at its edges, at every zoom and scroll position; the desktop week's ‹ › arrows are its one exception (the scheduler's D1522) [D1518, D1522].

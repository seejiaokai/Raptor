# The Leave War — how it works now

<!-- shape: page -->
<!-- trial: built rulings by test -->

Loads by itself with any Leave War file (the `paths:` above), and the OIL page, `oil.md`, loads with it — save with the `*arch-stack-4*` documents, where it is opened by hand; OIL is leave the war banks, so a line here points there for an OIL rule rather than restating it. It holds his standing rules, what he likes and what a fresh model would get wrong if not told, each line's rulings after it.

What is built lives in the app and its tests, listed by number on the "Built, held by tests" line, each named by a test that holds it whole; one its tests hold only in part stays written out. The built detail is in `raptor-port/docs/`: `ui-contracts.md` (its Leave War sections), `performance.md` §C and §E, `data-schema.md` §World 2, `leavewar/known-gaps.md`, the one-absence register and clash check (`superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-*`), `file-map.md`.

His words, the agent's readings (never stated here as his rule) and the history are in the full rows, `.claude/decisions-full/leave-war.md`: open the row before acting on a rule's detail or asking him about it. The map: `DECISIONS.md`. The war's architecture — its own store, what it saves, the one absence record and the seams where it meets the rest of the app — is `.claude/rules/seams/leave-war.md`.

## What it keeps
- This page is the rules diet's step for the Leave War's rulings: one page in the Tracker, Inputs and scheduler trials' shape, keeping his standing rules, what he likes and what a fresh model would get wrong; a built ruling leaves for the "Built, held by tests" line only when a test holds it whole; each full row is kept whole and searched; the page stays under the size of the list it replaced; read by Astra and a fresh Opus reader, blind; merged on his "merge live" [D1947, D1944, D1943, D1942, D1941, D1932, D390, D1938, D1735].
- The Leave War keeps its year grid — the whole period in one grid, not a month at a time; on a phone the close look is the day panel and pinch to zoom [D1157].
- The URHO who runs the Leave War is given an admin account; the app has no Leave-War-only role [D1223].
- The Leave War has no stages: members bid only on the dates the admin opens in the "Open for bidding" window — nothing is open until he opens it, and with nothing open the chip reads "Bidding closed"; the admin decides bids at any time (the agent's reading of D1177; his D418 had "in every stage but Draft"); a day not open stops a member's new bid, though he may still delete his own pending bid there, the day's note saying so [D1177, D1225, D418].
- Every build and walk of the war's entries — [LW-CELL-COMBOS], [LW-FULL-EDITING] and any other — covers every combination of entries on a day (whole days, halves, two or more, each kind) for how it looks on the grid, what a tap opens, how that window looks, and how to reach and make each change, pictured at both sizes [D1725].
- His open Leave War design questions are put to him in the order in which one answer shapes the next — the colour of each kind first, then how an overseas duty and a course look in a window, then how the window is made shorter, then the rest [D1301].

## Every screen
- A button stands in the same place on a phone as on a desktop, never on the left of one and the right of the other — save "+ Medical" and "+ Input", which stand left on a phone and right on a desktop; it holds for the whole app (the agent's reading, the design guide's lesson 28) [D1188, D1300, D1787, D1841, D1840].
- A button's words always stand inside the button, in every state it shows: the Leave War's Delete, once pressed, reads "Delete — sure?" on two lines inside its edge, on a phone with an iPhone's wider letters too [D1387, D1395, D1397].
- Wherever a Leave War window lists what a day holds, each entry leads with its code drawn as its coloured box, as the grid draws it, never as plain words — and the same wherever else the app names a kind of entry in words [D1327, D1328].
- In the Leave War's day list an entry's box has beside it only what the box does not say — its half of the day, its state, and where an entry filed on the Inputs page is changed — never the kind's long description, which the Legend holds; the other places D1327 names (the day panel's Away lines, My leave, the person window) the same (the agent's reading); a drawing made with the design skill cuts words as well as boxes [D1328].
- Every input, on the Leave War and the Inputs page, can carry photos and PDFs, added or seen at any time, even once its dates are closed, so a leave shows it was applied and approved in the official leave system — each through the app's one Document button, as the input window has it: the upload arrow and "Document", "Add" once a file is there, each file a chip with its ✕; a leave filed on the Inputs page asks for its document as a medical one does, never as a hard stop [D1150, D1159].
- Nothing glows, each place keeping its line: on the Leave War the green open-bidding box, the ring on his own name and the landing line; elsewhere, filed here, Edit Schedule's green "he can take this seat" ring, the light under today's day card, a SANS puck's stripe and the Tracker's gold "can fly next" and turquoise "search found" rings; the Leave War's Rearrange button shows "on" by the filter chip's pale blue fill, blue words and blue edge, never a halo [D881, D882, D1221, D1782].
- OML is ordinary medical leave wherever the app spells it out, the Leave War's medical label included; "outpatient medical leave" is not used [D1950].
- Wherever the Leave War writes a half day in words it reads AM or PM after the code — "LL (AM)", "HO* (PM)" — and a whole day the code alone: in the clash note, the credited-OIL window, the day's list, a member's approved-leave window and the bell [D1525].
- On the Leave War a half day is written as .5 everywhere — 14.5, 2.5, −1.5 — never as ½: the person window's tiles, the Balances page, Approvals, My leave, the day panel and the grid alike [D1246].
- One document, the notifications register, lists every place in the app that should send a notification and how each works, starting with the reminder to attach a leave's document a day before it starts; the agent adds to it by itself whenever work shows a place that needs one, tells him, and he may strike any [D1151].

## Periods, stages and bidding
- On the Leave War a member never bids on a day before today, even inside an open month — an admin may; a member may still correct a wrong input of his for a past day on the Inputs page, and the scheduler then publishes another end-of-day version to bring the published schedule up to date [D1211].

## The grid
- The Leave War's top takes as little height as it can — fewer rows or fewer buttons — and Manning keeps its place at the left of the grid's bar, never pushed off to the right [D1240, D1243, D1244, D1245].
- Every kind on the Leave War has a colour clearly its own, told apart from every other at a glance on a phone — an OIL taken, the other leave and an overseas duty above all — and no kind is pink: medical red (238,82,82), course purple (160,100,240), overseas duty a solid slate (128,142,160, filled .58), an OIL taken chartreuse (170,230,40) on the grid, in the bid window and on the Inputs calendar, never FO and HO's green, so a box that spends OIL is never read as one that gives it; the leaves and FO / HO keep their colours [D1323, D1322, D1316, D1309, D1291, D1175].
- SANS men are off the Leave War unless the admin puts them on — by "+ SANS" among the groups in Settings, taken off by that group's ✕ — the squadron's main way of working; they never fall into "Everyone else", and leaving SANS out stays the default and one tap away [D1215, D1221].

## The bid windows and the day's list
- A leave placed over several days at once, by a drag or a picked range, is one entry with one remark for all its days: a tap on any of its days shows that remark and a change there changes it for all; words typed in the drag window apply to every day dragged, whatever is pressed, replacing the remarks the dragged days held, so they end with one [D1339, D1341, D1347, D1362].
- Entries on the Leave War never join by themselves: next-door bids of one leave with one remark already act as one and become one leave when approved; two approved leaves are joined by extending one and deleting the other, which keeps the kept leave's document and approval; there is no Join button [D1346, D1341, D1347, D1362].

## Manning
- The Leave War's manning is lowered only by leave, a medical downchit (not ATT B), a course or an overseas duty a scheduler has not rejected, any commitment under the schedule's Unavailable list — an Other its filer put there or one a scheduler moved there — and a leave bid not refused; a commitment on the Ground Programme does not [D998, D1356, D1357, D1381, D1388, D1365, D1390, D1372, D1374, D1389, D1398, D1393].
- Any commitment under the schedule's Unavailable list counts him out on the Leave War, whatever its kind — an Other, Training, Meeting, Duty, Event, Appointment, Personal, an Attach, a course, an overseas duty — by 1, or 0.5 for a half day, lowering the manning, the Available rows and so the SANS calendar's need [D1357, D1381, D1389, D1390, D1393, D1352, D1356, D1372, D1374, D998, D1388, D1365, D1398].
- A commitment on the Ground Programme, or rejected, counts him in on the Leave War — a course or an overseas duty a scheduler rejects (Reject, then → Ground or → Unavail, both offered to the scheduler, the schedule's crew list counting him as the war does) included; a course, an overseas duty and an Attach land under Unavailable by themselves, an Other where its filer picks, the rest on the Ground Programme until a scheduler moves one [D1357, D1381, D1389, D1390, D1393, D1352, D1356, D1372, D1374, D998, D1388, D1365, D1398].

## Approvals and decisions
- The Approvals tab lists everything awaiting approval, its count rising with each new request: Approve on each line and an "Approve all" that approves exactly what the list shows, its date choices covering the whole period in view — there is no quick-approve switch, and the grid's drag-select stays [D1152, D1178, D1180, D1181, D1184, D1189, D1192, D1197].
- Each Approvals line shows the request's kind as the grid's coloured box and the person as his own puck — seat by its colour, his CAT on its chip, a SANS man's purple edge — its figures in columns lining up from line to line (how long, "LVE Bal", the lowest Available — every Manning row the request turns amber or red, each in its colour — and when it was placed), and a document icon where it carries one, a tap on it opening the document for the admin to review [D1152, D1178, D1180, D1181, D1184, D1189, D1192, D1197].
- There is no Ack on the Leave War: a bid simply waits until it is approved or refused, the bid windows' Decide offering Approve · Refuse, and purple in the Legend means only "this is you" [D1158].

## Balances and leave grades
- The Leave War has one Balances page, a tab of its own, for every person's balances and leave grade: an admin enters them all and sets the grades; a member sees the page and, while the balances are unlocked, edits his own row, every other row and the grades reading only; there is no second balances list — the grid's fold is gone, each name keeping only its LVE figure, and the person window's line is called "Balances" [D1185, D1230, D1242, D1245].
- A member edits his own balances, every kind but OIL, in his person window and on the Balances page until the admin locks them; the admin locks every member's balances at once with one switch, "Lock for members", on the Balances page — there is no lock per person — and once locked every person window shows a small padlock, a member's figures read only and only the admin changes them [D1203, D1209, D1220, D1242, D1247, D1485].
- On the Leave War an undecided request is called "pending", never "waiting"; a bid comes off his balance the moment it is placed, and the grid shows that one figure, War bal, only the person window also naming the official app's balance, App bal, beside it, as the next line says; the days pending are counted in days, written 2.5, never in requests [D1153, D1217, D1246, D1472, D1479].
- App bal is typed from the official leave app and then comes down by itself whenever a leave of his is approved, carried-forward days first; pending days stay in Pending until approved, and War bal is App bal less them, so an approval leaves War bal unchanged; typing App bal again only corrects it and starts the count afresh, the app keeping the date it was typed; CCL, FCL, PL, EL and CL come down by themselves as approved too [D1472, D1473, D1479, D1491, D1492].
- A leave balance changes only by its box on the Balances page or the person window, by approved leave and by 1 Jan — the grid's drag down the LVE figures and the bar it raised to give several people extra LVE are gone, as the FIGURES drawer is; the other readings of the M5a build spec stand as put to him, none struck [D1245, D1472, D1479, D1491, D1492].
- Every 1 January the Leave War rolls each person's leave over by itself: what is left of his carried-forward days is gone, what is left of the year just ended becomes his carried-forward leave, and his grade's days are added — 37 left, 4 of them carried forward, as a junior pilot becomes 33 carried forward and 66 in all [D1218, D1224, D1235, D1474].
- A year's unused annual leave carries forward one year only, as the official leave system keeps it, and leave taken always comes off the carried-forward days first, then the year's; with no grade nothing is added on 1 January, a leave pending over the new year counts in the year its days fall in, and a balance below zero carries nothing forward, its overdrawn days still coming off [D1218, D1224, D1235, D1474].
- On 1 January only LVE rolls over by itself; CCL, FCL, PL, EL and CL are left as they stand, each person's new figure typed from the official leave app by an admin or by the member on his own row while unlocked; a leave grade given after 1 January, or a grade's "+ days" changed during the year, counts from the next 1 January, the days that 1 January already added staying as they were, and a wrong grade put right after 1 January is corrected for that year by typing his App bal [D1489, D1490].

## OIL on the war
- Every OIL rule — what earns, what a hand-typed award or a credited FO and HO is, what Grant OIL gives — is the OIL page's (`oil.md`, loaded with this page); the lines here state only how the war shows and gives it [D1209, D1392, D1397].
- Grant OIL gives any amount on one day — half a day, a day or more than a day — its design keeping every amount easy to give: an "HO · 0.5" and an "FO · 1" pick and a box for any other amount hinting "other: 1.5, 2…", the button naming the amount, a half written in figures, never ½ [D1392, D1395, D1397, D1246].
- The admin is not asked which half a granted HO is, since it does not matter — it is drawn in a half of its day's cell, the morning unless the morning is held, and the half an earned HO is drawn in stays as built, a nicety, never a rule to build around (his words in D1391's row); from the drag window Grant OIL gives one grant on each day picked, an FO for a whole-day Duration and an HO for a morning or an afternoon, with one reason for all of them [D1391, D1392, D1394, D1164, D1397].

## Members, the bell and undo
- The person running the Leave War is not flooded with notifications of new requests — not while bidding is open, when he closes the bids then approves, nor afterwards, when ad-hoc leave is daily operations: there is no setting to turn them on, no bell line and no phone ping; what waits shows only on the Approvals count when he opens the Leave War [D1208, D1219].
- A member's bell tells him when the Leave War decides his leave — one line a decision, naming who made it: a bid approved or refused, an approved leave put back to a bid, moved or deleted, a bid replaced by a clashing input, days decided together one line, a tap taking the grid to it; every member's bell is told when bidding opens or closes, naming the dates and who, a tap opening the Leave War with that bidding period in view; and an admin's Undo of a decision the member has read leaves a struck-through "taken back · <who>" line, an unread one going [D1142, D1214, D1216, D1693, D1699, D1710].

## Inputs, postings and the schedule
- The Leave War's closed dates stop only bidding on the Leave War: leave filed or changed on the Inputs page is never stopped by them and lands approved — once his dates are closed a member gets management's verbal approval, then files or edits his leave there himself, and the Leave War follows by itself [D1166, D1177, D1225].
- UNA is gone: an Other filed on the Inputs page goes where its filer picks — the Ground Programme (something he is doing) or Unavailable (he is away) — and one under Unavailable shows on the Leave War, its remarks on the schedule's Unavailable list, the Inputs page and the Leave War alike; an Other filed from the Leave War's bid window, an admin's from its ⋯, goes straight under Unavailable with no choice and takes the window's Duration — whole day, AM or PM — any other time changed in its input on the Inputs page [D1356, D1365, D1388, D1357, D1398, D1390, D1381, D1372, D1374, D1393, D1389].
- Once a scheduler has moved a member's input — any kind a scheduler can move: an Other, Training, Meeting, Appointment, Duty, Event, Personal, Attach, course or overseas duty, never leave, a medical, an upchit or SANS availability — only a scheduler changes where it stands, as the Inputs page states too [D1356, D1388, D1398, D1357, D1365, D1390, D1381, D1372, D1374, D1393, D1389].
- Once a scheduler has moved it, the member's own window shows where his input now sits and does not let him pick again, and a member's change of its kind leaves it where the scheduler put it, a kind that cannot stand there refused; only where it stands is locked — its times, dates, remarks, title, document, taking himself out and deleting stay his [D1356, D1388, D1398, D1357, D1365, D1390, D1381, D1372, D1374, D1393, D1389].
- A member's change of an input's kind never brings back one a scheduler rejected: it stays rejected until a scheduler accepts it, and a change into a leave or a medical, which cannot stay rejected, is refused with its reason [D1399].
- "Load onto working copy" is not a scheduler's move: putting a request back where the published version had it does not lock where it goes, so a member whose own pick the load undid may pick again; a scheduler who wants it held presses Reject, → Ground or → Unavail [D1401].
- The war approves a man's leave on days from his posting-out date as on any other day — Approve, Approve all, the bid windows' Auto-approve and Undo / Redo alike, before or after the posting has run; he tells his superior himself, and the next squadron may still refuse it [D1541, D1545, D1555].
- A posting with the Delete outcome refuses approving his leave from its date in all of those ways, because he leaves flying for good and has no next squadron to refuse it; even then Move and the drag window may carry his already approved leave onto those days, since a move changes its days, not whether it was approved, and it can still be deleted [D1541, D1545, D1555].

# The scheduler — how it works now

<!-- shape: page -->
<!-- trial: built rulings by test -->

Loads by itself with a scheduler, board, engine, amendment or storage file (the `paths:` above). It holds his standing rules, what he likes and what a fresh model would get wrong if not told, each line's rulings after it. What is built lives in the app and its tests, listed by number on the "Built, held by tests" line, each named by a test that holds it whole; one its tests hold only in part stays written out. The built detail is in `raptor-port/docs/`: `engine-rules.md`, `ui-contracts.md`, `feature-impact.md`, `performance.md`, `data-schema.md`, `undo-contract.md`, `file-map.md`.

His words, the agent's readings (never stated here as his rule) and the history are in the full rows, `.claude/decisions-full/scheduler.md`: open the row before acting on a rule's detail or asking him about it. The map: `DECISIONS.md`. A ruling of an Inputs page alone lives in `inputs.md`; one that governs both an Inputs page and the schedule lives here (the agent's reading of D802, told to him). The rules every area shares (the store, the funnels, what persists) stay in `raptor-port/CLAUDE.md`.

## What it keeps
- This page is the rules diet's step for the scheduler's rulings, in the shape he approved for the Tracker and the Inputs pages: one page, by screen inside it, not split by screen; it keeps his standing rules, what he likes and what a fresh model would get wrong; a built ruling leaves for the "Built, held by tests" line only when a test holds it whole; each full row is kept whole and searched; the page stays under the size of the list it replaced; read by Astra and a fresh Opus reader, blind [D1944, D1943, D1942, D1941, D1932, D390, D1735, D1938].
- The Inputs pages — the Inputs list and calendar, the SANS calendar, the Calendar window, an input's own window and card — have their own page, `inputs.md`, opened before planning or changing anything of an input, those calendars or the late cut-off; a ruling that governs both an Inputs page and the schedule stays on this page (the agent's reading, told to him) [D802].
- Nothing on a published schedule may change without the scheduler acknowledging it — the standing test for any change that touches an issued day: who was available, and who stood behind a placeholder, is frozen at publication on every day, earning or not, and a later change shows as pending, cleared by an amendment or the EOD version; any change that shows as pending wipes the four sign-offs — something waiting means sign again — and putting it back restores them [D45, D44, D103].

## Every screen
- The writing guide is a standing companion to the design guide and the design skill, used every time text in the app is written or changed, its aim intuitive, clean, simple, smooth, less verbose and more human, with fewer dashes [D833, D834, D836, D839].
- The writing guide is approved as written, and when its aims pull apart the order is every fact first, then a sentence a person would say, then as short as those two allow — so a wording may end a word or two longer [D836, D825, D833, D834, D839].
- A shorter wording keeps every key word — what happened, why, what it costs and where to put it right — and only filler is cut; one that reads as a summary of the old is redone, and one that cannot lose a word without losing a fact is left as it is [D825, D833, D834, D836, D839].
- The long dash is not the app's ordinary way of joining two thoughts, because it reads as machine-written; a colon may take its place, and a dash may still be used where it makes sense [D834].
- The app says "OIL earned", never "pays": "it must be published again before OIL can be earned" [D2000].

## The week on Edit Schedule
- Cross-week dragging is not built and the work is not filed: on Edit Schedule a carried man stops at Monday and Sunday and is never carried into another week [D1573, D1529].
- The desktop week's ‹ › arrows stay see-through, never solid — an exception to D1518, because they cover the day's information at times — and are a slim tab, 14 wide and 70 tall, flush on the window's edge, that slides out to the full arrow when the pointer comes near and back when it leaves, see-through at rest and still slightly see-through when open; slid out it looks as the old arrow did at rest — the same frosted glass at the same see-through level, grey edge and grey arrow, never lit blue nor more solid [D1522, D1526, D1527].
- Faster keyboard entry of times and notes keeps the existing Enter and Escape meanings; the Tab route covers Edit Schedule's week and the Scheduler Board, view-only and issued read-only surfaces staying outside it [D544, D550].
- A wave's reporting runs in-time, rally, brief, take-off, landing, never the other way: a squadron may report with a rally only, an in-time with the rally straight after, or an in-time then a rally; with no in-time the rally starts the report; a rally may share the brief's time with no new gap demanded, the existing brief-before-take-off and exception checks staying; each formation's rally is its own box, the typed lines above the wave for the in-time [D497, D507, D502, D509, D1026, D1029, D1036, D1781, D1040, D1041, D1083, D1085].
- The earliest applicable entered time is a flying wave's report start — of several lines for one formation and one activity the earliest counts, whatever their order — and a person's own earlier qualifying event may start his day sooner; OIL is worked out from the entered in-time and rally too [D498, D506, D592, D501, D502, D509].
- A formation's own instruction replaces the whole-wave one of the same activity only: a formation's rally never cancels the whole-wave in-time, nor the reverse, so a whole-wave in-time of 08:00 and VL's rally of 08:30 still mean VL reports at 08:00 — the earliest applicable stage starts the report [D505, D498, D501, D502, D592, D509].
- A reporting line applies to the formation it names — that wave's own formation callsigns, read case-insensitively, in remarks too — or, naming none, to the whole wave, never to a named person; the typed lines above the wave keep their free-text format, one operative clock a line with its activity words and remarks, and a typed line is an in-time whatever its words — one saying RALLY is read as a rally only until [RULES-CHECK-FIXES] is built [D500, D501, D504, D1026, D1034, D1995, D1029, D1036, D1781, D1040, D1041, D1083, D1085].

## Crew and pucks
- ALL AVAIL and ALL are a scheduling feature, not an OIL feature: dropped anywhere, on every seat they can land on and with OIL Earn off too, they work out who would be available and show the count — worded as what it is (who has nothing else on the programme then), a starting point and never a promise; where they earn is the OIL page's rule [D27, D37, D43, D755, D776].
- The two placeholder pucks are refused on flying lines only — both cockpit seats of every flying line, at every door, with the reason on screen — and land everywhere else (sim seats, duty desks, passengers, extras, ground and programme rows), where a placeholder still arms its seat; tapping an empty cockpit seat still narrows the crew list; where they earn is the OIL page's rule [D33, D47, D43, D755, D776].

## Warnings and the rules engine

## Publishing, amendments and signing
- Everything on a published day freezes for now, and a change to what it shows reads pending for the admin, a medical downchit included, except what stays live on its face: the dotted "breaks tomorrow's crew rest" mark and its RUN twin, a crew-rest or 7-day breach on the day with the crew-rest tight-turn note, and the Qualification-flag warnings (an illegal seat, SC currency, AAR currency, an AAR instructor not cleared); the crew-pairing warnings stay frozen [D179, D183, D184, D185, D188].
- The freeze is for now — provisional: put to him again when the build shows it, never re-decided without him; the live warnings are drawn from today's judgement and never make the day pending by themselves (the agent's reading, told to him) [D179, D183, D184, D185, D188].
- No "Reissue AL1" button: after an Unpublish the withdrawn amendment's changes stay on the working copy as pending, and "Publish AL1" brings the day back [D101].

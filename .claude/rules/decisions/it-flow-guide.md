# The IT flow guide — how it works now

<!-- shape: page -->

Loads by itself with any IT flow guide file. Each line is a rule as it stands, its rulings after it; his words and the agent's readings told to him (never stated here as his rule) are in the full rows, `.claude/decisions-full/it-flow-guide.md` — open the row before acting on a rule's detail or asking him about it. The Leave War's rulings on it are pointed to at the end; the map: `DECISIONS.md`.

## The guide
- For the IT team and the next developer: a PowerPoint deck plus a PDF — a map slide of every journey, then one slide per journey: real screenshots, numbered click marks, arrows, a short caption per step, a "What to test" box naming the checks and the automated test covering it; no rules-engine detail; few words, pictures first [D410, D412, D414].
- His sample approved as drawn — four pictures a slide, the style kept — its map and slide style stand for every slide since; also the alternate-plan flow (saved plans: making one, switching, bringing one out) and the work flow — the journeys following one another, from inputs filed to a day published and amended [D410, D411, D412].
- How a schedule is made, step by step — the day, wave and duty templates and the duty blocks each with their steps — and each way wherever the app offers more than one (a puck brought onto a seat; every other such case the agent finds — his examples are not the limit) [D412].
- The schedule's two editing modes and when to use each: the Scheduler Board — one day, more functions dedicated to a day — and Edit Schedule's week — the big picture, smaller edits [D416].
- What happens by itself: for one action (one input filed, and each other that spreads the same way) every place in the app that changes with nobody touching it, and what it shows there; screens, not the rules' arithmetic [D415, D410].
- The Leave War's flow, in his order: the admin creates the period, opens its dates for bidding; members bid; he closes the bids, then decides them, and texts the squadron; members edit remarks at any time; later changes mainly by the member through inputs; the admin may still edit on the war, as he sees everything there at once [D413, D1177, D1208].
- The Leave War's manning, set up and customised — where the rows come from, how an admin adds, changes or removes one, what the grid shows after — as screens, not the count's arithmetic; his "All I can think of" closed his list for now; the agent still adds what else it finds [D417, D412, D414].
- The Tracker in full: making a flow chart (each tool and what it does, joining events, editing an event's details), how a member updates it and what he sees, every other way to update, what it refuses (an NA cannot have fails), making students and courses, export and import [D414].
- Each stage, step and way drawn is checked in the running app first; where the app differs from his description it is put to him, never drawn over [D413, D1177, D414, D415, D416].
- Re-shot and rebuilt only when he says so — not after each app change, not on a schedule; a changed screen it pictures files and re-shoots nothing by itself (a chat may say once, in its closing report, that it shows an older screen); refreshes already filed wait for his word too [D403].

# The IT flow guide — how it works now

<!-- shape: page -->

Loads with any IT flow guide file. Each line is a rule as it stands, its rulings after it; his words and the agent's readings told to him are in the full rows, `.claude/decisions-full/it-flow-guide.md` — open the row before acting on a detail or asking him about it. The Leave War's rulings on it are pointed to at the end, each stated on its page; the map: `DECISIONS.md`.

## The guide
- For the IT team and the next developer: a PowerPoint deck plus a PDF — a map slide of every journey, then one slide per journey, or several in order where one needs them: real screenshots, numbered click marks, arrows, a short caption per step, a "What to test" box naming the checks and the automated test covering it; no rules-engine detail; few words, pictures first [D410, D412, D414].
- His sample approved as drawn — four pictures a slide, the style kept — its map and slide style stand for every slide since; also the alternate-plan flow (saved plans: making one, switching, bringing one out) and the work flow — the journeys following one another, from inputs filed to a day published and amended [D410, D411, D412].
- How a schedule is made, step by step — the day, wave and duty templates and the duty blocks each with their steps — and each way wherever the app offers more than one (a puck brought onto a seat; every other such case the agent finds — his examples are not the limit), each naming what to test [D412].
- The schedule's two editing modes and when to use each: the Scheduler Board — one day, more functions for a day — and Edit Schedule's week — the big picture, smaller edits [D416].
- What happens by itself: for one action (one input filed, and each other that spreads the same way) every place that changes with nobody touching it, and what it shows there; screens, not the rules' arithmetic [D415, D410].
- The Leave War's flow, in his order: the admin creates the period, opens its dates for bidding; members bid; he closes the bids, then decides them (his best order), and texts the squadron; members edit remarks at any time; later changes mainly by the member through inputs; the admin may still edit on the war, as he sees everything there at once [D413, D1177, D1225, D1208].
- The Leave War's manning, set up and customised — where the rows come from, how an admin adds, changes or removes one, what the grid shows after — as screens, not the count's arithmetic; his "All I can think of" closed his list for now; the agent still adds what else it finds [D417, D412, D414].
- The Tracker in full: making a flow chart (each tool and what it does, joining events, editing an event's details), how a member updates it and what he sees, every other way to update, what it refuses (an NA cannot have fails), making students and courses, export and import, and what else the agent finds [D414].
- Each stage, step and way drawn is checked in the running app first [D414, D415, D416]; a Leave War stage the app does differently from his description is put to him, never drawn over [D413, D1177, D1225].
- Re-shot and rebuilt only when he says so — not after each app change, not on a schedule; a changed screen it pictures files and re-shoots nothing by itself (a closing report may say once that it shows an older screen); refreshes already filed wait for his word too [D403].

On 8 Nov 26 (chat 3) he agreed on his phone to five parallel build chats. The app refused every start three times:
first on a turn a finished background wait had woken (as on the night of 21 Oct 26), then on his own reply "Start them"
sent from his phone — over Remote Control it opens no chat at all. He had to go to his PC and type the go there; the five
then started at once.

**How to apply:** when a plan proposes side chats, say in the same message that the app can open them only on a message
he types at the PC, and offer the other way at once — helpers inside this chat (D1416) — so he chooses before the work
that comes first. Never put the start on a turn a background wait or timer will wake. Related:
[[new-chat-screen-no-branch-picker]].
